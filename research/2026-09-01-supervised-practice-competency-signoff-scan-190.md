# Research Scan 190 — Supervised Practice and Competency

Status: RESEARCH / PROVENANCE ONLY
Date: 2026-09-01
Canon impact: none.

## Purpose

Existing Narrative systems cover institutional authority, delegation, field work, records, drills and public demonstrations. This scan isolates the progression from observing a task to performing a narrow responsibility with less supervision. It must not create PTU Features, ranks, titles or authority.

## Public references

### Wigglytuff's Guild
Bulbapedia describes apprentices doing ordinary jobs and chores, receiving harder or simpler assignments according to performance, and graduating through a separate process.
Sources:
- https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Wigglytuff%27s_Guild
- https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Exploration_Team_Kit

Reusable lesson: useful work, training evidence, task difficulty and graduation can remain separate states.

### Ranger School — Shadows of Almia
The protagonist trains at Ranger School, graduates, and only afterward transitions into professional Ranger work. A live incident can occur during graduation without making that incident the sole definition of qualification.
Sources:
- https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Appendix%3APok%C3%A9mon_Ranger_2_walkthrough/Section_1
- https://gamefaqs.gamespot.com/ds/944533-pokemon-ranger-shadows-of-almia/faqs/55434

Reusable lesson: practice, formal transition, role issuance and independent duty can be represented independently.

### Legends: Arceus — Survey reports
Research-task progress is credited through survey reports, while Galaxy Team rank is a separate institutional state.
Sources:
- https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Research_task_(Legends:_Arceus)
- https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Rank_(Galaxy_Team)

Reusable lesson: field activity, recorded evidence and status advancement need not happen at the same moment.

### PTU Mentor
The public PTU rules list Mentor as a real Trainer Class with concrete mechanical effects involving Tutor Points, Moves, Move Lists, Natures, Poké Edges and Abilities.
Sources:
- https://pturpg.wikidot.com/classes
- https://pturpg.wikidot.com/mentor

Boundary: ordinary narrative mentoring cannot imply the PTU Mentor Class. Training scenes cannot grant Tutor Points, Moves, Edges, Skill Ranks, Abilities, Features, levels or Experience unless authoritative PTU state and engine contracts produce those effects.

## Extracted structures

- observe a task;
- assist inside a defined scope;
- perform under direct supervision;
- repeat routine work with supervisor available;
- identify an exception and escalate it correctly;
- receive a recorded scope decision from an already-authorized role;
- revisit the decision when procedures materially change.

One successful scene is weak evidence for unrestricted independence. Record task scope, procedure version, supervision level, context, observer and caveats.

## Ouros direction

Use a generic evidence-and-supervision architecture. Institutions may opt into it using authority already established by canon. The architecture records performance and scope; it never creates the authority to issue that scope.
