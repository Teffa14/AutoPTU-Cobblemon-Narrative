## Question

What reusable structures can help Ouros decide when a persistent NPC should tell the truth, remain silent, or deliberately deceive, without turning deception into a random personality roll or omniscient plot device?

## New sources processed

### Deception and reciprocity — Experimental Economics
https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/experimental-economics/article/deception-and-reciprocity/D5C0287C840C831D763C3AE59A9B7A88

Reusable lesson: lying behavior can depend on relational context and perceived prior conduct rather than a fixed universal cost. Ouros transforms this only into explicit relationship-sensitive decision inputs. It does not import measured human rates or moral claims.

### Enjoy the Silence: An Experiment on Truth-telling — University of Edinburgh
https://www.research.ed.ac.uk/en/publications/enjoy-the-silence-an-experiment-on-truth-telling/

Reusable lesson: truth, deception and silence can be separate strategic options. This directly supports a three-posture world-agent decision instead of forcing every non-truthful situation to become a lie.

### Deception and Its Detection: Effects of Monetary Incentives and Personal Relationship History
https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0093650210396868

Reusable lesson: relationship history can affect deception decisions and suspicion. Ouros uses only the high-level idea that directional trust/affinity can affect communication policy; no experimental coefficient becomes game math.

### Deception and Communication in Autonomous Multi-Agent Systems: An Experimental Study with Among Us
https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2603.26635

Reusable lesson: strategic agents may prefer equivocation or lower-risk ambiguity under pressure rather than outright falsehood. Ouros does not implement linguistic equivocation here, but preserves silence as a distinct lower-commitment option and keeps explicit exposure risk in the policy input.

### Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Explorers of Time / Darkness — Bulbapedia plot summary
https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon%3A_Explorers_of_Time

Reusable structure only: actors may deliberately circulate false information toward a concrete objective while later events test whether the information worked. Ouros does not copy the characters, locations, rumor, objective or plot resolution.

Reusable lesson: communication choices can remain meaningful across non-combat scenes and jobs instead of existing only as pre-battle exposition. No episode plot, NPC or dialogue is imported.

The long-running campaign reached its hundredth numbered episode in 2026. Reusable lesson: persistent motives, relationships and information state can support very long arcs. No proprietary story element is adopted.

## Transformation into Ouros

The research supports a world-agent choice among disclosure, withholding and deliberate deception. Ouros makes that choice inspectable through explicit values: goal pressure, utility gain, secrecy value, truthful cost, silence cost, exposure risk, third-party harm, duty conflict and directional relationship state.

These values are game-policy inputs. They are not claims about real human psychology.
