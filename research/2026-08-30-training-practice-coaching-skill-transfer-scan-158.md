# Ouros Narrative Research — Training, Practice, Coaching & Skill Transfer — Pass 158

Status: RESEARCH ONLY. This file is provenance and design evidence. It does not establish Ouros canon or new PTU mechanics.
Date: 2026-08-30

## Research question

How can Ouros support recurring training, coaching, sparring, tutoring and deliberate practice as persistent world activity without turning narrative attendance into automatic mechanical progression, duplicating formal education, or letting Minecraft/Cobblemon invent combat competence?

## Existing-repository gap check

The complete recursive Narrative tree was inspected before this pass. Adjacent owners already exist:

- `design/social-bonds-mentorship-clubs-layer.md` owns mentorship relationships and explicitly permits supervised practice, sparring and teaching opportunities while forbidding free Skill Ranks, Edges, Features or Tutor Moves.
- `design/battle-institutions-challenge-circuits-layer.md` owns formal challenges, exhibitions, post-battle review and narrow training-exercise definitions.
- `design/competitive-scouting-replay-analysis-extension.md` owns evidence-bounded scouting and preparation knowledge.
- `design/formal-education-enrollment-course-assessment-continuity-extension.md` owns courses, attendance, practical assignments and institutional assessment while separating academic results from PTU advancement.
- `design/downtime-hobbies-personal-projects-layer.md` owns general downtime activity framing.

The missing continuity is the multi-session practice thread between those systems: what the learner meant to work on, which exercises actually happened, who observed them, what feedback was given, what was demonstrated, what changed in the plan, and whether a separate governing PTU progression transaction was actually authorized.

## Internal PTU/Caelo cross-check

The project source scan identifies PTU Core, Caelo Player's Guide, character creation material, errata/extra material and Pokédex material as governing sources for mechanical claims. Existing Narrative layers already preserve the hard rule that learning opportunities do not directly grant PTU benefits.

Public PTU reference material reinforces that boundary. The Mentor class and Move Tutor progression use specific prerequisites, Tutor Points, action frequencies and target restrictions. These are explicit mechanics rather than a generic reward for spending narrative time with a coach.

Source: https://pturpg.wikidot.com/mentor

The public Character Advancement reference also treats Retraining as a specific transaction with Trainer Experience costs, prerequisite restrictions and in-character timing guidance. A practice montage cannot silently become a respec.

Source: https://pturpg.wikidot.com/character-advancement

Reusable lesson for Ouros: narrative training can create context, evidence, relationships and opportunities, but any Move learning, Feature/Edge change, Skill change, stat change, Tutor Point expenditure or retraining outcome must be a separate source-backed mechanical transaction.

## Pokémon source pattern — coach exercise followed by application

The official Pokémon episode page for “Kabu's Battle Training!” describes Kabu first leading a training exercise and then challenging the learners to a Multi Battle. This provides a useful high-level structure:

1. identify a purpose for practice;
2. run a bounded exercise;
3. apply the lesson in a different context;
4. let the outcome create new feedback rather than declare permanent mastery.

Source: https://www.pokemon.com/us/animation/horizons/1/kabus-battle-training

Ouros adaptation: a coach can define a practice objective and later observe an application battle. The battle result remains authoritative from AutoPTU. Narrative records what was attempted and what the coach actually observed. It does not convert one win into a permanent bonus.

## Pokémon source pattern — persistent training venue with several modes

Pokémon Legends: Arceus uses Jubilife's training grounds as a recurring location for ordinary Trainer battles, move tutoring and specialized challenge formats. The venue supports different kinds of improvement activity without requiring every visit to be the same encounter.

Source: https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Training_Grounds

Reusable lesson: a training place can become a durable social and gameplay anchor. Different instructors, learners, exercises and service modes can use the same physical site over time. The venue's continuity matters independently from any single battle.

The Path of Solitude also demonstrates a deliberately constrained challenge centered on one Pokémon. Ouros can borrow the structural idea that a practice test may isolate one question or constraint. It must not import Legends: Arceus rewards, opponent tables or rules.

Source: https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Path_of_Solitude

## Pokémon source pattern — practice battle as relationship/understanding event

In Legends: Arceus, an early training-ground battle is framed as helping another Trainer work on coordination with their partner Pokémon. The useful abstraction is that a practice battle can matter because of what participants are trying to learn, not only because of win/loss.

Source: https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Walkthrough%3APok%C3%A9mon_Legends%3A_Arceus/Part_3

Ouros adaptation: a practice session may generate observable evidence such as timing problems, coordination successes, repeated positioning errors or a new communication routine. Hidden emotions, bond values and mechanical Loyalty effects remain separate unless explicitly authorized.

## PTU living-world community pattern — reward caps and agreed sparring formats

The public Kairos Isles PTU living-world wiki documents community-specific sparring downtime. Players can spar more often than the campaign's rewarded weekly limit, while additional matches stop producing those campaign rewards. Participants may also choose among agreed sparring rulesets.

Source: https://kairosptu.wiki.gg/wiki/Character_Downtime

This is not PTU rules authority. It is useful campaign-design evidence for two principles:

- practice frequency and reward eligibility are separate state;
- repeated sparring can continue for roleplay or testing even after progression rewards are exhausted.

Ouros should therefore avoid an infinite “train repeatedly for power” loop. Repeated low-information drills can compress into history after their narrative value is exhausted unless a governing progression system explicitly needs another transaction.

## PTU community pattern — downtime training is commonly customized

A public Pokémon Tabletop discussion about school downtime shows GMs experimenting with study/practice allocations and customized progression. This is evidence that training is a common campaign need but also that homebrew approaches vary widely.

Source: https://www.reddit.com/r/PokemonTabletop/comments/ideygf/

Reusable lesson: do not infer one community's downtime-point system as PTU canon. Ouros should preserve a neutral continuity model that can host an approved Caelo/PTU rule later.

## Design synthesis

A strong Ouros training thread should preserve four different layers of fact:

1. Intent: what the learner or coach says they want to work on.
2. Activity: which drill, lesson, spar, review or field exercise actually occurred.
3. Evidence: what was observable during the activity and what feedback was recorded.
4. Mechanical transaction: any separate PTU/Caelo-authorized advancement, Tutor, retraining or other rules effect.

These layers must remain linkable but non-equivalent.

A character can practice without receiving a rules benefit. A legal tutoring transaction can happen within a larger training story. A learner can demonstrate a concept once and later fail to reproduce it. A coach can be wrong. A battle can expose a different problem than the one the session was designed to address.

## Reusable encounter and quest patterns

### Narrow drill, changed application

A learner practices one visible tactical question in a controlled exercise. A later ordinary battle creates a different context where the same question may or may not matter.

### Coach disagreement

Two credible instructors propose different practice priorities based on different evidence. Neither needs to be secretly incompetent. The player can choose what to test and later compare outcomes.

### Old lesson, new roster

A practice plan was reasonable for an earlier team composition. Evolution, roster change or a new challenge contract makes some parts stale. Historical sessions stay true while their relevance changes.

### Training venue continuity

A dojo, Gym annex, school court, club room, field station or public practice ground accumulates learners, instructors, notices, worn equipment, archived drills and remembered sessions. It can close temporarily, change staff or repurpose a space without deleting training history.

### Teaching as late-game content

An experienced Trainer supervises a novice. The interesting outcome is whether the learner could perform the bounded exercise and what evidence exists afterward. The player does not automatically grant the novice a Feature or Move.

### Interrupted practice

A world event interrupts a session. The continuity layer records where the plan stopped. Returning later can resume, revise or retire the plan instead of pretending the session completed.

## Authority safeguards derived from research

- `PRACTICE_ATTENDED != MECHANICAL_PROGRESSION`
- `PRACTICE_COMPLETED != MOVE_LEARNED`
- `COACH_FEEDBACK != CANONICAL_OPTIMAL_TACTIC`
- `DEMONSTRATED_ONCE != MASTERED`
- `REPEATED_SUCCESS != FEATURE_GRANTED`
- `SPARRING_WIN != FORMAL_QUALIFICATION`
- `TRAINING_PLAN != AI_TACTICAL_POLICY`
- `MENTOR_PRESENT != MENTOR_FEATURE_AVAILABLE`
- `MOVE_TUTOR_NPC != EVERY_MOVE_LEGAL`
- `RETRAINING_INTENT != RETRAINING_COMPLETED`
- `TRAINING_BATTLE != FORMAL_BATTLE_RECORD`
- `PRACTICE_ROSTER != FORMAL_CHALLENGE_ROSTER`
- `SESSION_INTERRUPTED != TRAINING_FAILED`

## Battle implementation implications

A static sparring battle can use current verified primitives if every selected Move, Ability, Item and Trainer Feature is individually audited.

Richer drills that rely on protect/escort objectives, dynamic reaction windows, forced movement, timed partner rotation, changing terrain, tactical withdrawal or an AI that understands the pedagogical objective remain dependent on the corresponding capability families.

Training cannot be implemented by scripting missing PTU mechanics in Minecraft. If a drill requires Intercept, the authoritative AutoPTU Intercept path must resolve it. If a practice scenario requires dynamic zones or reactions, that dependency remains visible until the engine verifies the family.

## Source-use exclusions

This pass does not copy dialogue, characters, plots, opponent tables, reward tables or proprietary prose from Pokémon media. It uses only high-level patterns.

Community-specific downtime and sparring rules remain attributed examples, not Ouros canon and not PTU authority.

The PTU public rules references are used to identify mechanical boundaries. Exact Ouros adoption still requires cross-check against the project's authoritative PTU/Caelo source material and engine contracts.

## Research conclusion

The useful new surface is not another generic “training system.” Ouros needs continuity for practice itself: goals, sessions, evidence, feedback, revision and handoff to separately authorized progression. That structure fills the gap between mentorship, formal education, battle institutions, scouting and AutoPTU while preventing repeated practice from becoming an unbounded power faucet.
