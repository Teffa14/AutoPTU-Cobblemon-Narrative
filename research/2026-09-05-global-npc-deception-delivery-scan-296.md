# Research scan — selective deception delivery — Pass 296

Status: RESEARCH / PROVENANCE ONLY
Date: 2026-09-05

This pass searched new public material after checking the existing research inventory. It intentionally avoids reusing the Pass 295 deception sources as its primary basis.

## New sources

T2MAC: Targeted and Trusted Multi-Agent Communication through Selective Engagement and Evidence-Driven Integration — Sun et al., 2024. https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.10973

Reusable structure: targeted communication is preferable to indiscriminate broadcast when different agents have different relevance and reliability. Ouros applies only the high-level separation between recipient selection, message transport and evidence integration. It does not import the learning algorithm, benchmarks or cooperative-agent assumptions.

Formulating Manipulable Argumentation with Intra-/Inter-Agent Preferences — Arisaka, Hagiwara and Ito, 2019. https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.03616

Reusable structure: selective communication can be strategically deceptive, while detection and trust response are separate processes. Ouros applies that separation to authored statements, delivery and later trust consequences. It does not adopt the paper's argumentation formalism or detection model.

Misinformation Propagation in Benign Multi-Agent Systems — Becker et al., 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.16710

Reusable structure: misinformation can persist through multi-agent exchange and outcomes depend on who is exposed and how received information is combined. For Ouros this supports recipient-specific delivery and preserving downstream provenance. No LLM prompting, debate protocol or evaluation benchmark is imported.

PokeU Panic! — publicly listed Pokémon Tabletop United campaign, StartPlaying, accessed 2026-09-05. https://startplaying.games/adventure/cmr6r2xws01ecl604wm1bf5qr

Reusable structure: ordinary social life, rumors, disappearances and escalating investigation can coexist with battles across a medium-length PTU campaign. Ouros takes only the pacing lesson that incomplete information can move from local rumor to wider consequence over multiple scenes. The university, conspiracy, Project Halo, characters and scenario details are not copied.

Tales of Visiwa: A Retrospective — Pokémon Tabletop RPG community site. https://pokemontabletop.com/tales-of-visiwa-a-retrospective/

Reusable structure: a long PTU campaign can sustain regional exploration, dangerous wilderness, history and supernatural mysteries over a persistent setting. Ouros takes the durable-world lesson only. Visiwa, its cultures, chronology, gods, explorers and plots are not imported.

## Transformation into Ouros material

The research supports a narrow design rule: false or strategically incomplete information should travel through explicit recipient selection and ordinary world channels. A lie should therefore have latency, reachability, backlog and provenance rather than becoming a global quest flag. Detection, motive and trust consequences remain separate layers.

No protected prose, dialogue, characters, factions, locations or plots were copied. No source rule became an Ouros rule merely because it appeared in public material.

## PTU / Caelo / Kairos boundary

This pass adds no combat mechanic. PTU remains the deep mechanical authority used for structured battle assumptions. Caelo and Kairos remain reference material where available to the project and are not silently promoted into canon. The implementation is world-simulation policy in the narrative repository.
