# Sports, Racing & Athletic Culture Research — Pass 39

Status: RESEARCH ONLY. Nothing in this file is Ouros canon.

Date: 2026-08-19

## Why this pass exists

The repository already covers formal battles, Contests, performance culture, travel, transport, clubs, public events, professions, seasonal events and social institutions. It does not yet have a dedicated model for non-battle athletic activity: racing, relays, obstacle courses, endurance events, navigation competitions, team sports, mounted sport, recreational leagues or community athletic festivals.

That gap matters because a living Pokémon world should give physically oriented Trainers and Pokémon something meaningful to do that is neither a Gym battle nor a Contest.

## Source set and reusable structures

### Pokéathlon / Pokéathlon Dome

Source:
https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9athlon

High-level pattern only:
- a permanent athletic institution can support several event families;
- one Trainer may field several Pokémon across a meet;
- different events reward different kinds of physical performance;
- records, medals and recurring participation can persist across visits;
- team composition matters because no single Pokémon is naturally ideal for every event;
- an athletic venue can be a social destination, record archive and career path rather than a one-off minigame.

Copyright/mechanics boundary:
Do not copy Pokéathlon event scoring, performance-stat formulas, Aprijuice mechanics, point economy, records or rewards into Ouros. Those belong to HeartGold/SoulSilver and are not PTU rules.

### Mantine Surf

Reusable pattern:

### PTU Skills: Acrobatics and Athletics

Source:
https://pturpg.wikidot.com/skills

Relevant rules-discovery points:
- Acrobatics covers precision movement, balance and coordinated physical tasks;
- Opposed Acrobatics Checks are explicitly cited for races through obstacle courses or difficult terrain;
- Athletics represents physical training and endurance used for running, climbing and other strenuous activity.

This is strong evidence that PTU already has a legal rules vocabulary for some athletic challenges. The narrative system should reference those governing Skill rules rather than inventing a separate generic “sport stat.”

### PTU Rider and travel gear

## Design lessons for Ouros

### Athletic institutions should be distinct from battle institutions

A Gym answers questions about battle philosophy, League progression and tactical competition. An athletic club or racing circuit can care about endurance, navigation, teamwork, control, precision, route choice or physical coordination.

The same NPC may participate in both, but the institutions should not collapse into one system.

### The world should support several scales of sport

Small scale:
- neighborhood races;
- school field days;
- club practice;
- informal climbing challenges;
- local fishing or paddling meets.

Regional scale:
- annual road race;
- mounted circuit;
- relay league;
- surf championship;
- endurance expedition race.

Large event:
- multi-discipline festival;
- invitational circuit final;
- interregional athletic meet.

### Course state should come from the real world graph

A race course should reference actual routes, roads, bridges, terrain, weather, transport closures, construction, conservation zones and settlement state.

If a bridge is closed in world state, a race should not silently use it unless the event has an explicit temporary arrangement.

### Competition should not imply battle

Two Pokémon racing beside one another are not automatically using Struggle Attacks. Contact, blocking, interference or combat maneuvers require explicit rules if a sport allows them.

### Training should not create unsupported mechanical buffs

Practice sessions can create narrative familiarity, route knowledge, coaching relationships and career state. They cannot invent Speed bonuses, Skill Ranks, Combat Stages, Features, Edges or Pokémon stat increases outside governing PTU/Caelo rules.

### Records need context

A “course record” should record:
- course version;
- route state;
- event ruleset version;
- participants;
- timing/scoring authority;
- environmental conditions if relevant;
- whether the result was official, exhibition or practice.

This prevents an old record from being compared blindly against a later course with different terrain or rules.

### Safety and welfare matter without becoming bureaucracy

The existing care, crisis and conservation layers can provide:
- temporary suspension after hazards;
- veterinary/medical review where governing rules require it;
- course closures;
- wildlife protection windows;
- recovery periods.

Routine events should not become paperwork simulators.

## PTU/Caelo cross-check status

Public PTU rules strongly support using Athletics and Acrobatics for relevant physical challenges and confirm that mounted play has explicit mechanics.

The project’s full Caelo PDFs were not exposed as directly searchable files in this runtime. No Caelo-specific race procedure, Skill modifier, mount rule, reward, fatigue rule or event classification is asserted here.

Before implementation, verify against the project source corpus:
- exact Athletics/Acrobatics check procedures;
- opposed/team/assisted checks used by Caelo;
- Mountable and Rider interactions;
- movement capabilities;
- Skill Stunts and Features;
- any Caelo event or downtime restrictions;
- recovery/injury handling after physical competition.

## Copyright and originality rule

Use external sources to extract event architecture, pacing, institution design and world-state relationships. Do not reproduce proprietary minigame mechanics, scoring tables, course names, characters, scripted incidents or dialogue.

Ouros sport should be authored from its own geography, institutions, cultures and PTU/Caelo mechanics.
