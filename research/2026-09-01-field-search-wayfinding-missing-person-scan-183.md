# Field search, wayfinding and missing-person scan 183

Status: RESEARCH / PROVENANCE ONLY / NOT CANON
Date: 2026-09-01

## Research question

How can Ouros support overdue travelers, lost people, field searches, route-finding, trace interpretation and rescue continuity without turning every case into an omniscient map marker or a combat quest?

This pass was selected after inspecting the current Narrative tree and Marea canon. Existing layers already cover route condition, communications, claim propagation, ecology/phenology, access restrictions, institutional delegation, aftermath, memorials and pilgrimage. The uncovered seam is operational search: converting an overdue or missing report into bounded, evidence-driven field work and then closing that work without inventing facts about injury, death or wrongdoing.

No source below is copied for characters, dialogue, proprietary plot, exact puzzle presentation or distinctive setting content. Only reusable structural lessons are extracted.

## Public sources

### Pokémon Legends: Arceus — Gone Astray request chain

Source: Bulbapedia task/request documentation.
https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Task_(Legends:_Arceus)

Observed structure:

- the same civilian can become lost in several different field regions over a longer relationship arc;
- a requester reports the absence rather than the game treating the target's exact position as common knowledge;
- Ursaluna's tracking signal narrows the search in-world;
- different areas change traversal demands while preserving the core rescue premise;
- successful location is followed by reporting back to the concerned person.

Reusable lesson for Ouros:

A recurring search thread can reveal both the subject and the district. Search can be a legitimate exploration loop when location knowledge is progressively earned. Ouros should avoid copying the exact ride-signal UI. A trace-capable Pokémon, Skill, Feature, witness report, instrument or physical clue must have explicit provenance before it narrows a search.

### Pokémon Legends: Arceus — A Nosepass to Guide the Way

Source: Bulbapedia task documentation.
https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Task_(Legends:_Arceus)

Observed structure:

- wayfinding is treated as a practical field problem;
- local saying and Pokémon behavior motivate a navigation solution;
- the request connects terrain knowledge, equipment/companionship and safe traversal.

Reusable lesson:

Ouros can make navigation competence visible without inventing a universal minimap oracle. Historical route knowledge, landmarks, instruments and Pokémon capabilities can each contribute, but folklore about a species must remain separate from verified mechanical or behavioral capability.

### Pokémon Mystery Dungeon — rescue and escort jobs

Source: Bulbapedia Job documentation.
https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Job_(Mystery_Dungeon)

Observed structure:

- rescue and escort are distinct job types;
- a rescue requires finding a lost target;
- an escort adds responsibility for moving a vulnerable client through dangerous space;
- target survival and successful arrival are separate operational concerns from simply defeating enemies.

Reusable lesson:

Ouros should never collapse FIND, CONTACT, STABILIZE, ESCORT and RETURN into one generic quest-complete flag. A target can be located but still need assistance. A safe contact can end a search even when no escort is required. Combat victory alone cannot imply successful recovery.

### Pokémon Mystery Dungeon — expedition/job variation

Source: Bulbapedia Expedition documentation.
https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Expedition_(Mystery_Dungeon)

Observed structure:

- field assignments can ask for lost objects, rendezvous, escorts or battles within the same exploration framework;
- the operational objective determines success, not the mere presence of a dungeon.

Reusable lesson:

A Marea search route can support several objectives without becoming a special-purpose dungeon. The same Sendero segment can host a check-in failure one day, instrument recovery later and an ecological transect on another date.

### Tales of Visiwa — PTU campaign retrospective

Source: Pokémon Tabletop official/community site campaign retrospective.
https://pokemontabletop.com/tales-of-visiwa-a-retrospective/

Observed structure:

- wilderness is dangerous enough that certified Explorers have meaningful social value;
- exploration, dangerous wilds and regional institutions coexist in a long-running PTU campaign;
- access to the wild is part of setting structure rather than only encounter generation.

Reusable lesson:

Field competence can have institutional context in a PTU campaign. Ouros should not import Visiwa's certification rules, history, deities or colonial setting. The high-level lesson is that a search may involve jurisdiction, prepared personnel and risk assessment before anyone enters the field.

### National Park Service — Outdoor Emergency Plan

Source: United States National Park Service.
https://www.nps.gov/articles/gtgemergencyplan.htm

Observed structure:

- trip plans and intended routes matter before a person becomes overdue;
- last known location, landmarks, equipment and route information are important inputs;
- groups are advised to regroup at the last known place rather than scatter into an uncontrolled search;
- self-rescue, waiting safely and contacting organized responders are legitimate outcomes.

Reusable lesson:

The strongest gameable search state is not an exact hidden coordinate. It is a chain of increasingly useful bounded facts: expected route, last verified point, time window, known equipment, witness observations and safe/unsafe sectors. Searchers can create secondary incidents if they ignore coordination.

### Yosemite Search and Rescue — Lost at Hetch Hetchy

Source: National Park Service/Yosemite SAR case account.
https://www.nps.gov/yose/blogs/lost-at-hetch-hetchy.htm

Observed structure:

- an overdue traveler can become stranded after leaving the expected route;
- once a safe exit is unavailable, remaining in place can be the rational action;
- search duration and outcome emerge from route uncertainty and terrain, not from villainy.

Reusable lesson:

A missing-person story can remain consequential without requiring kidnapping, conspiracy or a hostile faction. Weather, route choice, injury risk, communication failure and conservative self-rescue can create sufficient narrative pressure.

## Extracted structures

### Missing is a report state, not a truth about cause

Suggested epistemic chain:

EXPECTED PRESENCE / CHECK-IN
-> MISSED EXPECTATION
-> VERIFICATION ATTEMPTS
-> OVERDUE OR MISSING REPORT
-> LAST-KNOWN EVIDENCE
-> SEARCH PLAN
-> FIELD OBSERVATIONS
-> CONTACT / SELF-RETURN / SUSPENSION / UNRESOLVED CLOSURE

The report must not silently imply injury, death, abduction, criminality or incompetence.

### Last-known information should be versioned

Useful fields:

- subject identifier;
- reporter;
- expected destination/check-in;
- planned route if known;
- last verified place;
- last verified time or interval;
- evidence source;
- clothing/equipment or Pokémon companions when relevant and already known;
- communication attempts;
- known route closures or weather observations;
- uncertainty/confidence;
- later corrections.

A later witness can move the last-known point forward. The old version remains in provenance.

### Search space should narrow through evidence

Search gameplay can operate on named sectors and route segments rather than giving the player a magic coordinate.

Examples:

- south trailhead to lower shelf;
- lower shelf to seasonal crossing;
- Mirador branch;
- upper junction to Loma Clara;
- known alternate service route only if canon later establishes one.

A sector being searched does not prove the subject was never there. Search quality depends on timing, visibility, method and accessibility.

### Trace observations remain observations

Possible traces:

- a dropped item with uncertain ownership;
- fresh damage to vegetation;
- footprints or wheel marks;
- a witness sighting;
- an opened gate or disturbed marker;
- a companion Pokémon's repeated behavior;
- an instrument record that bounds time;
- a route register or sign-in entry.

Each needs provenance. A trace can be genuine but unrelated. Pokémon behavior can suggest where to inspect without becoming universal translation.

### Search closure needs explicit outcomes

Useful terminal or pause states:

- CONTACT_ESTABLISHED_SAFE;
- FOUND_ASSISTANCE_REQUIRED;
- FOUND_ESCORT_REQUIRED;
- SAFE_SELF_RETURN;
- FOUND_BY_OTHER_PARTY;
- REPORT_RETRACTED_OR_RESOLVED;
- SEARCH_SUSPENDED_CONDITIONS;
- CLOSED_UNRESOLVED;
- TRANSFERRED_TO_OTHER_AUTHORITY.

`CLOSED_UNRESOLVED` is valid. Narrative does not owe every historical or current absence a convenient answer.

## Design invariants proposed for Ouros

`NOT_AT_EXPECTED_LOCATION != MISSING_CONFIRMED`

`MISSING_REPORT != DANGER_CONFIRMED`

`OVERDUE != INJURED`

`TRACE_FOUND != SUBJECT_IDENTIFIED`

`SEARCH_SECTOR_CLEARED != SUBJECT_ABSENT_FROM_REGION`

`MAP_MARKER != WORLD_POSITION_TRUTH`

`POKEMON_BEHAVIOR != UNIVERSAL_TRACKING_POWER`

`COBBLEMON_ENTITY_DESPAWNED != SUBJECT_MISSING`

`BATTLE_VICTORY != SEARCH_COMPLETE`

`SUBJECT_FOUND != SUBJECT_SAFE_TO_TRAVEL`

`NO_CONTACT != DEATH`

## PTU/Caelo boundary

The project contains PTU trainer content where Survival, Researcher, Backpacker and related concepts have rules surfaces. Search design must therefore avoid creating a free generic 'tracking skill' that bypasses PTU Skills, Features, Capabilities or Pokémon-specific content.

A narrative clue may be available because anyone can see it. A mechanical advantage, automatic detection, supernatural sensing, special movement or Trainer Feature interrupt needs exact PTU/Caelo provenance and engine parity before becoming authoritative.

No indexed Caelo source was located in this pass that defines search-and-rescue authority, navigation practice, emergency signalling, ranger jurisdiction or tracking rules. This remains an evidence gap, not permission to invent regional law.

## Recommended Ouros direction

Use search as a composition layer over existing systems rather than a new global quest engine. Communications owns reports and updates. Claims/provenance own uncertain observations. Route/access layers own closures. Ecology owns wildlife facts. Institutional roles own who can coordinate or authorize field work. Care owns medical consequences. Battle remains a narrow handoff when a genuine confrontation occurs.

The most valuable first implementation should be a non-combat search with multiple plausible explanations and a self-return or contact outcome. That validates epistemic discipline before adding tactical rescue complexity.
