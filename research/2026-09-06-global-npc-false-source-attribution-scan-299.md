# Research scan — false source attribution and borrowed authority — Pass 299

Status: RESEARCH / PROVENANCE ONLY; NOT CANON
Date: 2026-09-06

## Question

How can Ouros support investigations where a speaker borrows a trusted person's or institution's authority without treating factual contradiction, source verification and deceptive intent as the same discovery?

## New public sources

### Vaccari, Chadwick, Hall & Lawson — deceptive source misattribution

Source: Journalism & Mass Communication Quarterly, first published online 2025-07-13; 2026 issue.
https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/10776990251350563

The study directly separates false information from deceptive attribution to a reputable source. Its experiment found that attaching a trusted source could increase perceived credibility of a message. Reusable Ouros lesson: the source named in a message is gameplay-relevant state and may need independent verification. Do not treat source reputation as proof of authorship.

No human-effect magnitude becomes an Ouros rule.

### Pena, Klemfuss, Loftus & Mindthoff — source monitoring and misinformation

Source: Psychology of Consciousness 4(4), 2017.
https://doi.org/10.1037/cns0000137

The study examines misinformation exposure, source credibility and source-monitoring accuracy. Reusable Ouros lesson: content memory and attribution can diverge, reinforcing the existing separation between `KnowledgeLedger` history and subjective `SourceAttributionStore` state.

No cognitive rate, threshold or probability is imported.

### Liu, Qi, Wang & Metzger — fact-check source credibility

Source: Communication Research, first published 2023-10-27.
https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/00936502231206419

The experiment finds that perceived credibility of a correcting source can mediate correction effectiveness. Reusable lesson: verification evidence should itself retain source/provenance rather than becoming an omniscient `FALSE` flag.

### Pokémon XD: Gale of Darkness — Mayor's Note / disguised occupation structure

Source: Bulbapedia summary of the Mayor's Note.
https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Mayor%27s_Note

The item provides an incomplete authenticated message that changes how the player can interpret disguised actors occupying a city. Reusable high-level pattern: a partial record from a known authority can unlock verification of identity/authority claims. Ouros does not import Phenac City, Cipher, Mayor Trest, Justy, dialogue, item text or plot sequence.

### Pokémon Tabletop United — Strike Team Delta recruitment post

Source: public r/pbp PTU one-shot post, 2026-08-08.
https://www.reddit.com/r/pbp/comments/1vj0tgx/pokemon_strike_team_delta_ptuoneshot/

The scenario frames an investigation around conflicting reports, a disappearance, environmental evidence and an institutional response. Reusable lesson: a PTU mission can begin from reports whose reliability must be tested against physical evidence before tactical resolution. No region, observatory, police force, plot or encounter is copied.

### Pokémon Undercover PTU campaign recruitment

Source: public r/PokemonTabletop post, 2022-11-22.
https://www.reddit.com/r/PokemonTabletop/comments/z24ni1

The campaign premise uses maintained cover identity, heists and information gathering across sessions. Reusable lesson: identity and attribution can remain meaningful campaign state across many scenes rather than resolving through one check. This source was encountered in earlier deception research, so Pass 299 does not treat it as a new primary design source; it is retained only as a cross-check that the new verification seam remains compatible with PTU-style long-form intrigue.

## Transformation into Ouros

Pass 299 introduces source-verification phases, not a lie detector. A direct denial opens a dispute. A provenance-independent authorship/dispatch record may corroborate that the named authority was not the source. Explicit evidence of the speaker's intention is still required before a default trust penalty can target the speaker.

A missing archive row is deliberately insufficient evidence. A copied rumor cannot multiply its own evidentiary weight. The actual delivery source and provenance remain immutable even when a character remembers or was told a different attribution.
