# Research scan — disclosure expectations, omission and rescue dispatch — Pass 301

Status: RESEARCH / PROVENANCE ONLY
Date: 2026-09-06

Purpose: find reusable structures for distinguishing ordinary silence from failure to disclose when a recipient had a justified expectation. Nothing in this note becomes Ouros canon or PTU rules merely by being recorded here.

## New sources reviewed

Roggensack, Katlyn Elise; Sillars, Alan. “Agreement and understanding about honesty and deception rules in romantic relationships.” Journal of Social and Personal Relationships, 2014. https://doi.org/10.1177/0265407513489914

Reusable abstraction: expectations around honesty can include obligatory rules and discretionary rules. Ouros uses only that structural distinction. It does not import relationship findings, human rates or moral judgments. A world-agent expectation must be explicit and provenance-backed before silence can have a breach consequence.

Metts, Sandra. “An Exploratory Investigation of Deception in Close Relationships.” Journal of Social and Personal Relationships, 1989. https://doi.org/10.1177/026540758900600202

Reusable abstraction: omission is a distinct communicative strategy from explicit falsification and appears under different relational pressures. Ouros translates this into separate TRUTHFUL/SILENT/DECEPTIVE postures and does not classify all withholding as lying.

Vrij et al. “Omission Lies: The Effect of Omitting Little or Much Information on Verbal Veracity Cues.” European Journal of Psychology Applied to Legal Context, 2025. https://journals.copmadrid.org/ejpalc/art/ejpalc2025a3

Reusable abstraction: deliberate omission can preserve mostly true utterances while withholding relevant content. This supports keeping silence/omission separate from false-content statement provenance. Ouros does not adopt behavioral lie-detection cues from this study.

Bulbapedia. “Friend Rescue.” https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Friend_Rescue

Reusable Pokémon structure: a rescue request is a concrete, addressed call for help with a recoverable mission state. For Ouros this suggests explicit rescue/disclosure obligations should be represented by durable events rather than inferred because someone happened to be nearby. No passwords, dungeon rules, penalties, characters or text are copied.

Bulbapedia. “Wonder Mail.” https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Wonder_Mail

Reusable Pokémon structure: missions can be carried as explicit messages with identity, destination and task context. Ouros uses only the message-to-obligation pattern. Wonder Mail encoding and game-specific mission rules are not adopted.

Public PTU community discussion, “Pokemon meets Watership Down,” r/PokemonTabletop, 6 March 2026. https://www.reddit.com/r/PokemonTabletop/comments/1rmpd42/pokemon_meets_watership_down/

Reusable PTU/Kairos evidence: community members describe using Kairos PTU for Mystery-Dungeon-style rescue play, reinforcing that rescue obligations and persistent social stakes can coexist with PTU-derived characters. This is community evidence only; no system recommendation or homebrew rule becomes Ouros authority.

StartPlaying, “Pokémon Villains - Neo Plasma,” active 2026 PTU campaign listing. https://startplaying.games/adventure/cmgx0o7nw000njm04m9bw8glz

Reusable structure: recurring faction missions can combine rescue, sabotage, recruitment and ideological obligations. Ouros takes only the general faction-duty pattern. The named faction, ideology, mission text and campaign story are protected/external material and are not imported.

## Transformation into Ouros

The safe reusable pattern is `knowledge + explicit expectation + communication opportunity + observable response + consequence`. The expectation must have a source such as a request, role, faction duty, promise or emergency protocol. Silence without that expectation remains ordinary non-disclosure.

This supports original Ouros structures such as a route dispatcher expected to warn registered travelers, a ranger obligated to escalate a habitat closure, a rescue coordinator who must acknowledge an SOS, or a faction courier who promised to relay a safety notice. Whether the NPC fulfills, withholds or falsifies the information can then produce different histories without an omniscient morality flag.
