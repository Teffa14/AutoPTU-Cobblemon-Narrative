Reusable structure:

Ouros transformation:

A durable world timeline should preserve a message delivery, the fact that it affected an NPC's evidence, a planner wake-up and a later planning decision as separate records. Later resolution cannot safely stand in for those earlier transitions.

## New reusable design lessons

Coherent recovery should preserve causal edges rather than only endpoint state. A completed trigger ID is too weak when later investigation needs to know which actor, delivery and evidence woke the planner.

Transport, cognition and decision are different ownership domains. The save model should retain their relationship without collapsing them.

A field consequence becomes more believable when it intersects an already useful site. The player can discover an outdated order while visiting for another reason, rather than being summoned by a bespoke quest flag.

Historical evidence supports disagreement without deception. One NPC may correctly prove that a message arrived and another may correctly point out that the requested route was never adopted.
