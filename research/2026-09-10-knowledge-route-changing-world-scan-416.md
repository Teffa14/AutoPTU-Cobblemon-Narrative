### Outer Wilds design interviews

Sources:
- https://mcvuk.com/business-news/we-didnt-think-it-would-have-tremendously-broad-appeal-mobius-digital-on-the-multi-bafta-award-winning-outer-wilds/
- https://www.gamesradar.com/the-making-of-outer-wilds/

Reusable high-level structures:
- curiosity can direct exploration without a waypoint being the primary motivator;
- learned information can function as a progression gate without changing the character sheet;
- the environment can continue changing independently of player attention;
- a clue can teach where or when to investigate rather than merely award a conventional key.

Ouros transformation:
- knowledge must remain per-agent and provenance-backed rather than globally unlocked;
- physical access remains dependent on current world state, permissions, travel and actual conditions;
- learning a route does not freeze that route or create an omniscient quest flag;
- an NPC may exploit the same knowledge only if they legitimately possess it and can act on it.

No Outer Wilds locations, characters, loop premise, alien history, puzzles, dialogue or plot are imported.

### Pokémon Shadowside — Chapter 4 public release post

Source:
- https://www.reddit.com/r/PokemonRMXP/comments/1v1gik6/pokemon_shadowside_chapter_4_out_now/

Public release date: 2026-07-20.

The public project post describes a traditional-RPG-oriented Pokémon fangame without a Gym Challenge, and Chapter 4 presents a single island divided into strongly contrasted environmental halves. The useful abstraction is spatial legibility: a route network can communicate different histories, risks and traversal expectations through authored environmental contrast rather than through a menu label alone.

Ouros transformation:
- contrasting environmental evidence can help an informed traveler recognize which route description applies;
- visual presentation may support observation, but the adapter cannot infer hidden facts or PTU mechanics from blocks;
- separate physical revisions of the same route can remain distinguishable through authored world state.

No Shadowside region, island, factions, characters, battle format, map, story or named environments are imported.

## Design synthesis

Candidate produced by this scan: `proposals/2026-09-10-the-route-is-learned-not-unlocked-pass-416.md`.

The central reusable structure is a route whose existence is a persistent world fact, whose safe/useful traversal depends on changing conditions, and whose practical use depends on what a particular actor has learned. Discovery changes knowledge. It does not toggle geography into existence.
