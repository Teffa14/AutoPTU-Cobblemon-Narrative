# Regime-transition and hysteresis evidence scan — Pass 273

Status: RESEARCH / PROVENANCE ONLY. No canon effect.
Date: 2026-09-04

## Question

What evidence should Ouros require before a persistent post-disturbance ecological shift can be promoted beyond the Pass 272 `PERSISTENT_SHIFT_HYPOTHESIS` into a narrowly supported regime-transition or hysteresis interpretation?

## New public sources

1. Scheffer, Carpenter, Foley, Folke & Walker (2001), “Catastrophic shifts in ecosystems”, Nature 413:591–596, DOI 10.1038/35098000. The review distinguishes gradual external change from abrupt switches between contrasting states and emphasizes loss of resilience as a precursor. Reusable lesson: abrupt-looking observations alone do not identify the mechanism; resilience, thresholds and state persistence need separate evidence.

2. Suding, Gross & Houseman (2004), “Alternative states and positive feedbacks in restoration ecology”, Trends in Ecology & Evolution 19:46–53, DOI 10.1016/j.tree.2003.10.005. Strong biotic/abiotic feedbacks can keep degraded systems from responding to ordinary restoration. Reusable lesson: restoring the original driver may be insufficient when a self-maintaining feedback has developed.

3. Sha et al. (2026), “Alternative stable states: Evidence from natural ecosystems and implications for ecological restoration”, Ecological Solutions and Evidence, DOI 10.1002/2688-8319.70239. The synthesis treats critical thresholds, positive feedbacks and non-retracing recovery paths as central evidence classes. Reusable lesson: hysteresis requires more than slow return and recovery may need intervention against the maintaining feedback rather than only reversal of the initiating pressure.

4. PTU Survival reference, pturpg.wikidot.com/skills. Survival explicitly supports scouting ecosystems and learning common Pokémon/resources, with environment-specific difficulty. Reusable lesson: the reduced Ouros investigation can expose evidence through field observation without inventing hidden combat outcomes.

5. Pokémon Legends: Arceus public game material from Nintendo describes repeated field survey of wild Pokémon and behavior that varies with conditions such as time and weather. Reusable lesson: repeated visits under controlled conditions are a natural player-facing way to reveal ecological state, provided Ouros keeps observation separate from world truth.

## Transformable design lessons

A visually persistent altered site should first create an investigation question. The player can compare the same site across semantic windows, compare a reference location, document whether the initiating pressure has actually ceased, look for a maintaining feedback, and test whether reversing the original pressure retraces the departure path.

A useful mystery structure is therefore: apparent non-recovery -> alternative explanations -> evidence for a maintaining process -> targeted intervention -> return-path comparison. The answer is earned from evidence instead of a quest flag.

An intervention can have asymmetric outcomes. Removing the initiating pressure may do little, while disrupting a feedback can permit recovery. This creates consequence-rich ecological quests without requiring a demographic change or a battle.

## Ouros evidence ladder proposed

`PERSISTENT_SHIFT_OBSERVED` records durable departure only.

`ALTERNATE_STATE_HYPOTHESIS` requires repeated comparable evidence that the shifted configuration persists after verified cessation while obvious secondary pressures remain unresolved or excluded.

`FEEDBACK_MECHANISM_SUPPORTED` requires source-backed evidence for a specific process capable of maintaining the shifted configuration.

`RETURN_PATH_ASYMMETRY_SUPPORTED` requires evidence that recovery under comparable driver conditions follows a materially different path or threshold from degradation.

`REGIME_TRANSITION_SUPPORTED_NARROWLY` requires a declared reference, a persistent contrasting configuration, adequate comparison/detectability, and evidence that simple lag/ongoing pressure is insufficient.

`HYSTERESIS_SUPPORTED_NARROWLY` additionally requires supported return-path asymmetry and a plausible maintaining feedback. It remains scoped to the tested site/process/window and does not become a species-global rule.

## Guardrails

Persistence alone does not prove an alternative stable state. A single threshold-like observation does not prove a critical threshold. A feedback hypothesis does not prove that the feedback caused the observed state. Delayed recovery does not prove hysteresis. A successful restoration does not retrospectively prove every proposed mechanism. Population size remains owned by the demographic ledger.

Minecraft block state, entity visibility, particles or elapsed ticks are presentation/runtime evidence only unless an Ouros authority contract promotes them. Observed environmental geometry has no PTU cover/hazard/zone semantics without an admitted mapping.
