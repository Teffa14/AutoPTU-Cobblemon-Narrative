# Selective Corrective Action and Consequence Repair Scan — Pass 312

Status: RESEARCH / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-06

## Research question

How can Ouros represent a corrected decision changing only the effects that actually depend on that decision, while preserving unrelated consequences, historical memory and independent hazards?

This note is provenance only. It does not establish canon, PTU mechanics or Caelo overlays.

## Source: NIST corrective-action terminology

Source: NIST, `corrective action`, lexicon entry added 2025-05-27.
https://www.nist.gov/glossary-term/42361

Reusable lesson:
- corrective action is directed at eliminating or mitigating a cause and preventing recurrence;
- correction can therefore be modeled as an explicit action against a defined problem instead of a generic rollback.

Ouros transformation:
- a decision review identifies that a historical basis changed;
- each downstream operational effect remains independently addressable;
- the repair runtime targets one consequence at a time.

Not imported:
- laboratory standards;
- legal effect;
- forensic procedure;
- any real-world compliance requirement.

## Source: U.S. GAO — Identity One, LLC

Source: U.S. Government Accountability Office, B-424040.2/B-424040.3, 2026-04-13.
https://www.gao.gov/products/b-424040.2%2Cb-424040.3

Reusable lesson:
- corrective action is assessed in relation to the concern that caused it;
- the scope of the remedy can be specific rather than automatically reopening every part of a prior process.

Ouros transformation:
- a review can cease or amend the particular consequence created by the faulty basis;
- unrelated effects require separate provenance;
- an effect that survives rescission needs a new independent basis rather than continued reliance on the invalidated one.

This is only a structural design analogy. Procurement law does not become Ouros law.

## Source: U.S. GAO — Veterans Medical Supply, Inc.

Source: U.S. Government Accountability Office, B-422168.4/B-422168.5, 2025-07-11.
https://www.gao.gov/products/b-422168.4%2Cb-422168.5

Reusable lesson:
- a corrective action can be evaluated by whether it reasonably remedies the identified error;
- remedy scope can include a concrete operational change rather than a purely documentary correction.

Ouros transformation:
- a `RESCIND` review may authorize cessation of a concrete access restriction;
- that cessation is recorded separately from the historical decision and from other consequences.

## Source: Project Escapade

Source: public Pokémon fan-game design page.

Observed reusable structure:
- world lore is intended to live in locations and mechanics rather than interrupting play with constant exposition;
- open progression and side areas support discovery through world interaction.

Ouros transformation:
- the state of a gate, notice board, checkpoint, service road or work site can communicate the current consequence of an old decision;
- revisiting the same location after a review can visibly show which effects changed and which persisted.

No region, encounters, custom species, rules or authored lore are imported.

## Resulting design principles

1. Preserve the historical decision even after review.
2. Represent each downstream effect as its own durable consequence.
3. Repair one effect at a time.
4. Require a new basis when an effect survives rescission for a different reason.
5. Keep documentary correction, public correction, relationship repair, economic restitution and physical world changes as separate seams.
6. Let revisited locations expose those differences through environmental state.
