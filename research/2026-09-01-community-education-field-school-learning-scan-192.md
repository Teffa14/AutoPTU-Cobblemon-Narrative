# Community Education, Field-School Learning, and Assessment Scan

Status: RESEARCH / PROVENANCE ONLY
Pass: 192
Date: 2026-09-01
Canon authority: none

## Purpose

Identify reusable structures for Ouros around ordinary teaching, field-school sessions, public instruction, course continuity, assessment, and learning records without turning narrative education into PTU character advancement or silently duplicating the supervised-practice/competency layer.

## Existing Ouros seam

The current Marea canon already establishes a physical field school at Loma Clara and Jo Venn as its instructor. Jo's canon duties are practical instruction in observation, cultivation records, and safe field practice. Oren connects to the field school for prevention education, while Nerea, local producers, and other residents provide plausible subject-matter links.

Pass 190 already covers supervised practice, contextual evidence, and scoped operational responsibility. This pass therefore treats education as knowledge transmission and learning history. It does not issue work authority, promotions, licenses, PTU Features, Skill Ranks, Moves, Abilities, levels, or Experience.

Reusable structure:

- classes and examinations are separate states;

- instruction can be revisited over time rather than being a one-scene tutorial;
- academic relationships with instructors can continue outside individual lessons.

Do not import:
- Paldea's curriculum as Caelo curriculum;
- exam question sets;
- Exp. Candy or other rewards;
- badge-gated inventory;
- academy hierarchy, school year, uniforms, admissions, or legal status.

Design lesson:
A Marea field-school session should know what subject it covers, which source/procedure version it teaches, whether attendance occurred, and what evidence exists that a learner understood the material. None of those facts automatically modify a PTU sheet.

### Ranger School — Pokémon Ranger: Shadows of Almia

Sources:
- https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Ranger_School
- https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Appendix:Pok%C3%A9mon_Ranger_2_walkthrough/Section_1

Reusable structure:
- one school can prepare people for distinct roles;
- classroom, library, outdoor instruction, staff space, dormitory/social life, and practical grounds create different learning surfaces;
- students can receive limited tools appropriate to their current status;
- an emergency can interrupt a ceremony or lesson without becoming proof that every part of training was mastered;

Do not import:
- Ranger licensing, Capture Styler progression, school rank, graduation requirements, dormitory structure, or Almia institutions;
- the Tangrowth incident or any distinctive plot beat.

Design lesson:
Ouros can use location-based teaching and restricted instructional tools without treating equipment possession as qualification. A lesson interrupted by a real incident should preserve both the interrupted lesson state and the incident state.

### PTU community academy discussion

Sources:
- https://www.reddit.com/r/PokemonTabletop/comments/9nrfy4/help_need_campaign_ideas_for_a_pokemon_academy/

Observed community patterns:
- GMs use academy campaigns to mix ordinary classes, clubs, field trips, practical exercises, and larger adventures;
- suggested subjects often map to existing Pokémon-world concerns such as weather, wilderness safety, Pokémon knowledge, items, coordination, and team roles;
- one recent commenter proposed simulated rescue exercises where defeating a panicked Pokémon is not necessarily the core objective.

Authority warning:
These are community ideas, not PTU rules. No proposed class structure, DC, reward, graduation requirement, or mechanical benefit may be imported without separate authority.

Useful pacing lesson:
Education works better as varied recurring activity than as repeated quizzes. Lectures, observation walks, artifact/source comparison, practical demonstrations, peer explanation, and correction of old notes can all express learning while reusing world systems already present in Marea.

## PTU cross-check

## Reusable Ouros structures

1. Subject continuity
A learning subject can recur over seasons and revisions instead of resetting after each quest.

2. Source-aware teaching
A lesson can cite a procedure, field note, archive edition, observation set, or local record. When the source changes, the lesson can be updated without erasing the old session.

3. Learning evidence without mechanical advancement
A learner can demonstrate recall, comparison, correct uncertainty, safe refusal, or corrected understanding. This evidence is narrative knowledge history only.

4. Guest expertise
Nerea, Oren, Taro, Alba, Ivo, Teo, Sela, Lia, or other canon residents can contribute within their existing authority. Guest instruction does not transfer their institutional powers to Jo or the learner.

5. Error as continuity
A wrong answer or outdated note can persist, be corrected, and later become evidence of growth. The world should not silently rewrite earlier understanding.

6. Independent world activity
A lesson may occur without the player. The player can later encounter changed boards, revised handouts, learner questions, or a record of a session already held.

## Research boundary summary

This pass supports a narrative education layer for Marea because a canon field school and instructor already exist. It does not establish Caelo schooling law, PTU advancement, certification, or a new institutional hierarchy.
