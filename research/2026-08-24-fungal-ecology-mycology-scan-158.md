## New public sources inspected

### Official Pokémon — Paras / Parasect

- https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokedex/paras
- https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokedex/parasect

Paras/Parasect provide a species-specific host/fungus relationship. The reusable lesson is that a fungal relationship can change with life stage and can be tightly bound to one Pokémon lineage without becoming a generic rule for all fungi or all Grass-types.

### Official Pokémon — Shiinotic

- https://www.pokemon.com/uk/pokedex/shiinotic

Shiinotic provides an explicit species case where luminous spores and sleep are connected. Ouros uses this as a guardrail: ambient glowing fungi or spores do not create Sleep. The exact Pokémon Move/Ability/rules contract must do that.

### Official Pokémon — Amoonguss

- https://www.pokemon.com/el/pokedex/amoonguss

Amoonguss provides a species-specific link between spore release and later mushroom-like growth. The useful structure is `spore observation -> later growth -> causal assessment`, not automatic attribution.

### Kairos Isles PTU living world

- https://kairosptu.wiki.gg/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Encounter_Tables

Kairos maintains a Mushroom Forest as a distinct persistent habitat with a different encounter composition. Pass 158 takes only the structural lesson: fungal habitat identity can recur across a living world rather than existing only as one dungeon gimmick. No rates, relic species or campaign-specific content are copied.

### NPS — visible mushrooms versus fungal organism

- https://www.nps.gov/shen/learn/nature/mushrooms.htm

NPS distinguishes visible fruiting bodies from a larger fungal organism/mycelial stage in the substrate. Moist conditions influence visible fruiting. This strongly supports Pass 72's existing rule that absence of visible mushrooms does not prove fungal absence.

### U.S. Forest Service — fungal roles

- https://research.fs.usda.gov/treesearch/38089

The Forest Service distinguishes decomposer, disease/pathogenic and symbiotic roles. Ouros should therefore version role assessments and avoid assigning function from mushroom appearance alone.

### U.S. Forest Service — mycorrhizae

- https://research.fs.usda.gov/treesearch/9995

Mycorrhizae are specific fungus-root associations with resource exchange. This supports scoped host/site evidence, not a generic tree/fungus buff.

### U.S. Forest Service — difficult detection and repeated surveys

- https://research.fs.usda.gov/treesearch/54715

Forest fungal detection can be difficult; repeated multi-year surveys and below-ground sampling improve detection. This is the strongest source for Pass 158's new monitoring extension: method, effort, season and below-ground coverage must travel with `NOT_DETECTED` observations.

### U.S. Forest Service — wild mushroom harvest

- https://research.fs.usda.gov/treesearch/5632

Wild mushroom collection can create ecological, recreational and commercial management questions. Pass 158 therefore records harvest pressure separately from fruiting observations and population interpretation.

## Reusable Ouros design lessons

Pass 72 already supplies fungal occurrence and ecological-role authority. Pass 158 deepens five areas:

- repeated survey effort and detectability;
- visible fruiting as versioned episodes;
- uncertainty over whether separate observations refer to the same persistent fungal system;
- sample/custody provenance before Taxonomy or research interpretation;
- harvest records that remain separate from abundance claims and PTU Item acquisition.

This supports long Chronicle arcs where a patch disappears visually, returns elsewhere, is sampled under different methods, enters a harvest dispute or is reidentified years later without retconning the original observations.

## Rules and canon guardrails

Do not infer mushroom count from population size, fruiting absence from organism absence, ambient spores from PTU `Spore`, physical contact from `Effect Spore`, visible fungi from Poisoned/Sleep/Paralysis, mycorrhizae from stat/healing bonuses, deadwood fungi from structural failure, market supply from local abundance or Minecraft harvesting from PTU Item acquisition.

The complete primary Caelo corpus was not recovered reliably in this run. Super PTU Online Helper was not exposed as an invocable capability. No Caelo-specific mushroom, foraging, powder, forest or spore rule is invented.
