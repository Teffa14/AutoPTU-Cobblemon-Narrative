## Public source A — Suzerain

Source: Torpor Games, public Suzerain site.
https://www.suzeraingame.com/
https://www.suzeraingame.com/suzerain

Observed high-level structure

Suzerain describes a world of actors with different ideals and agendas where decisions have later political, institutional and personal consequences. Reports and conversations are part of the information surface through which the player understands that changing world.

Reusable Ouros lesson

A durable choice and another actor learning that choice should be separate events. Consequences should attach to the communicated decision and later world reactions rather than assuming every relevant actor instantly knows a choice when it is made.

Transformation boundary

Ouros imports no country, cabinet, policy, character, dialogue, ideology, plot, ending, statistic or event from Suzerain. The reusable pattern is only decision -> communication/observation -> later actor response.
