## Transformation for Ouros

The useful combined pattern is continuity across absence. A named actor can promise future help, leave the current scene, encounter new information, and still carry the earlier obligation until it is fulfilled, revised, deferred or missed.

Ouros should preserve separately:

- the decision that created the promise;
- the communication that made it known;
- the scheduled window owned by the responder;
- later evidence that may change what the responder intends to do;
- the final result of acting, deferring or missing the window.

A restart, chunk unload, player departure or Minecraft presentation transition must not erase the causal chain.

## Original Ouros hooks

### The morning-after repair

A field technician accepted a morning inspection before the player left the district. Overnight, a second report changes the suspected failure mode. The technician still appears because the appointment survived, but their plan can change after receiving the new evidence.

### Two promises, one crossing

A responder has one ordinary work obligation and one accepted assistance window. The player can observe which one wins from the NPC's real priority and timing state. The system should not invent a personality explanation after the fact.

### The outdated briefing

A helper reaches the rendezvous with a valid commitment but an old understanding of the problem. The scene can become investigation, clarification or replanning before any tactical resolution begins.
