# Ecology pressure, activity and projection visibility scan — Pass 248

Status: RESEARCH / PROVENANCE ONLY
Canon effect: NONE

## Question

How can Ouros let scarcity and disturbance change where and when already-counted wild Pokémon become visible through Cobblemon without treating visibility as abundance, emigration or mortality?

## New public-source evidence

Official Pokémon material provides a strong presentation precedent. Pokémon Legends: Arceus states that some species or behaviors are observable only under particular times of day or weather and that wild Pokémon react differently by species, including approaching, attacking or fleeing. Pokémon Sword/Shield likewise varies wild encounters by weather and location. These are useful precedents for conditional field visibility, but their encounter tables are gameplay abstractions rather than literal Ouros population biology.

USGS movement research shows that daily bird movement timing varies with foraging mode and habitat. A separate USGS beach study found that human proximity often caused birds to move or fly, while much of that displacement was short enough not to alter large-scale habitat use. This distinction is directly reusable: a visible actor can shift microhabitat, timing or exposure without producing a demographic event.

USGS work on artificial light and migrant stopover use also demonstrates that environmental context can change where animals concentrate at multiple spatial scales. Ouros should therefore keep projection eligibility separate from the underlying member ledger and from permanent migration state.

## Reusable design structure

Persistent ecology should produce a projection envelope rather than a spawn count. The envelope can describe eligible microhabitats, activity windows, search-radius band, visibility/exposure band and a presentation-capacity limit. Cobblemon then receives already-counted eligible sources through the Pass 239 lease mechanism.

Resource pressure may broaden search or alter timing. Disturbance may reduce exposure or move activity toward sheltered places or quieter periods. Neither mechanism changes population abundance unless a separate Pass 238 demographic event occurs.

## PTU/Caelo/Kairos cross-check

PTU mechanics remain authoritative only when structured mechanical adjudication begins. Ordinary ecological availability, field observation and projection do not need AutoPTU. Caelo and Kairos remain evidence for living-world operation, not automatic rules overlays. No external campaign rule is adopted by this pass.

## Ouros proposal extracted from the research

Add a deterministic projection-policy evaluator between persistent ecology state and Cobblemon materialization:

persistent population/member state
→ local pressure/context
→ projection envelope
→ already-counted source selection
→ Pass 239 lease reservation
→ Cobblemon materialization

Projection changes may affect presence probability, activity window, microhabitat eligibility and search radius. They may not create members, erase members, change demographic totals or promote identities by themselves.

## Encounter implications

Reduced version: no battle. The same persistent Fletchling appears in a different eligible microhabitat/time window after resource pressure rises, then becomes less exposed during a disturbance spike. This depends only on Ouros state, lease reconciliation and Minecraft/Cobblemon projection.

Rich version: a player follows or intercepts a pressure-displaced Pokémon across terrain. That version additionally depends on complete movement, lifecycle, terrain/zones/reactions when authored, AI tactical policy and adapter/playback. If attacks occur, damage/status and the exact Move/Ability/Item/Trainer Feature families used must also be verified.

## Sources

Pokémon Legends: Arceus official gameplay page: conditional behavior by time/weather and species-specific reactions.
Pokémon Sword and Shield official Wild Area page: encounter availability varies by weather and location.
USGS, Diurnal Timing of Nonmigratory Movement by Birds (2020): foraging mode and habitat influence timing of daily movement.
USGS, Birds at a Southern California beach (2001): human disturbance often causes short displacement without necessarily changing large-scale habitat use.
USGS, Artificial light at night confounds broad-scale habitat use by migrating birds (2018): environmental context changes stopover habitat concentration at multiple scales.

## Unresolved questions

The current thresholds and projection bands remain fixture-only. Ouros still needs species-specific functions that translate resource pressure, disturbance, time, weather, nesting state and individual temperament into presentation eligibility. The lower-Sendero physical resource is still not canon-approved, so this pass does not name it.
