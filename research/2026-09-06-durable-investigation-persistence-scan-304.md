# Research provenance — durable investigations and persistent evidence — Pass 304

Status: RESEARCH / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-06

Purpose: enrich Ouros with investigation structures that can span long periods, restarts and changing conclusions without confusing research inspiration with canon or engine authority.

## Source: Azure Architecture Center — Event Sourcing pattern

URL: https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/architecture/patterns/event-sourcing
Accessed: 2026-09-06

Reusable lesson: intent-bearing historical events and durable snapshots support later reconstruction, auditing and alternate read models. The source explicitly treats snapshots as an optimization rather than a replacement for the underlying event history.

Ouros transformation: infrastructure-attribution findings may be snapshotted for recovery, but their cited evidence claims remain the causal basis. A restored conclusion cannot stand if the corresponding investigator ledger or evidence claims are absent from the same world checkpoint. No Azure implementation detail is adopted as runtime architecture.

## Source: Pokémon Burning Scales

URL: https://github.com/Benitex/Pokemon-Burning-Scales
Accessed: 2026-09-06

Reusable lesson: a compact open area densely populated with interactions and side quests can support deeper local consequence chains than a route that exists only to move the player between major objectives. The project also frames research into unusual Pokémon phenomena as a central exploration motive rather than requiring the standard badge loop.

Ouros transformation: a damaged relay, inspection site or isolated settlement can remain revisitable after the immediate incident. Later evidence, repairs and local testimony can change how the same place is understood. No characters, plot, regional forms, dialogue, map layout or specific quest is copied.

## Source: Pokémon Adventures in the Millennium actual play

URL: https://castbox.fm/channel/Pokemon-Adventures-in-the-Millennium-id2882526
Accessed: 2026-09-06

Reusable lesson: a PTU campaign can organize long-running play into named acts whose consequences and unresolved problems persist across many episodes. Public episode metadata shows an extended Sinnoh campaign with multiple acts and later missing-person stakes.

Ouros transformation: an investigation can produce an early finding, pause for travel or another arc, then reopen when new evidence appears. The historical finding remains queryable instead of being overwritten by the latest answer. No characters, episode plots, scenes or dialogue are imported.

## Source: Pokémon Tabletop exploration discussion

URL: https://www.reddit.com/r/PokemonTabletop/comments/1gx1cz9
Accessed: 2026-09-06

Reusable lesson: one community GM describes travel maps where wild Pokémon perform small ongoing behaviors rather than waiting as static encounter tokens. The useful structural point is environmental evidence emerging from ordinary activity.

Ouros transformation: inspection clues may be embedded in persistent environmental activity: displaced nesting material, repeated traffic patterns, damaged vegetation, scavenged cable sheathing or maintenance behavior. These are authored evidence opportunities, not automatic truth flags. Species behavior must still be validated against project ecology/canon before use.

## Mechanical and canon boundary

No PTU, Caelo, Kairos, AutoPTU or Cobblemon mechanic is adopted by this research note. Pass 304 concerns persistence and provenance of investigation state only. Any future scene that uses Perception, Technology Education, Survival, Pokémon capabilities, Trainer Features, Moves, Abilities, Items, weather, hazards or tactical movement must be validated separately against authoritative project sources and the live capability audit.

No source above is canonized. All derived material remains proposed until accepted through the project's canon process.
