# Pass 168 research scan — risk sharing, coverage, loss, and claims

Status: RESEARCH ONLY / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-08-31

## Internal gap check

The repository tree was inspected before drafting. Existing Finance, ownership/material-culture, logistics/courier, repair/reconstruction, archives/records, health/care, employment, estate, public-memory, and disaster/event continuity remain authoritative for their own facts. No dedicated insurance/coverage/claim continuity layer was found by repository search for insurance, coverage, claim, premium, underwriter, indemnity, risk pool, mutual aid, loss compensation, or equivalent terminology.

This pass therefore studies a narrower missing question: when canon explicitly creates a mutual-aid fund, coverage contract, guild guarantee, transport guarantee, communal reserve, institutional protection plan, or similar arrangement, how can Ouros preserve the arrangement and later loss/claim history without inventing modern insurance law or allowing a battle result to settle an economic dispute?

## Public research

### Pokémon recovery and reconstruction structures

Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Blue/Red Rescue Team uses a world repeatedly affected by natural disasters and makes rescue work an ongoing institutional activity rather than a single exceptional incident. Reusable lesson: loss events, rescue activity, recovery, and later social continuity can be separate episodes. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon%3A_Blue_Rescue_Team_and_Red_Rescue_Team

Pokémon Pokopia material and contemporary analysis describe damaged settlements, restoration of Pokémon Centers, rebuilding decisions, and a useful distinction between restoring former function and rebuilding differently. Reusable lesson: compensation or resources supplied after a loss must not imply that the old object, service, settlement, or social arrangement was restored exactly. Sources: https://www.gamespark.jp/article/2026/04/12/165084.html and https://www.theverge.com/games/885431/pokemon-pokopia-review

Pokémon film/location material repeatedly depicts research sites or settlements damaged as collateral effects of larger Pokémon incidents. Reusable lesson: the battle/event, physical damage, ownership, repair, and any later compensation need separate provenance. Reference example: Destiny Deoxys summary at https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon%3A_Destiny_Deoxys

These Pokémon sources do not establish insurance as universal canon. They only support long-lived consequences, rebuilding choices, and institutional response to loss.

### PTU public material

PTU character creation explicitly treats money and purchased Items as campaign resources while leaving starting money and item availability substantially to the GM. Source: https://pturpg.wikidot.com/character-creation

PTU consumable documentation gives Items prices and concrete mechanical effects. Source: https://pturpg.wikidot.com/consumables

No public PTU evidence found in this pass establishes a universal insurance, premium, claim, indemnity, property-title, or reimbursement subsystem. Therefore this extension must never create such rules mechanically. A covered PTU Item remains an Item whose battle behavior comes from PTU/Caelo/AutoPTU; its economic coverage record cannot change its combat behavior.

### Claims and risk-sharing process grammar

Insurance Information Institute material demonstrates that loss histories can preserve loss type, date, amount, and claim status independently from current physical condition. Reusable lesson: historical claim records remain useful even after repair, sale, or later events. Source: https://www.iii.org/article/what-is-a-loss-history-report

NAIC reporting material distinguishes date a claim is received, proof-of-loss information, denied claims, and contractual coverage concepts. Reusable lesson: loss occurrence, notice, evidence completeness, decision, and payment are distinct states. Source: https://content.naic.org/sites/default/files/call_materials/Materials_17.pdf

These sources are process references only. Ouros must not import United States law, mandatory time limits, premium formulas, deductibles, underwriting rules, rescission law, consumer regulation, or contemporary corporate institutions.

## Reusable design findings

A useful persistent model must keep the following independent: arrangement existence; covered subject; covered risk definition; effective period; contribution/consideration if any; loss event; loss notice; evidence bundle; claim; review; decision; compensation commitment; actual transfer; repair/replacement; dispute/review; renewal/lapse; historical revision.

A community mutual-aid pool should be representable without pretending it is a modern insurer. A merchant guarantee, guild reserve, civic disaster fund, family risk pool, transport bond, or authored institutional protection plan can use the same continuity grammar while retaining its own canon-specific rules.

A loss can be real while a claim is invalid under the authored arrangement. A claim can be rejected without fraud. A claim can be accepted while payment remains pending. Payment can occur without restoring the original object. Repair can preserve function while changing provenance, appearance, craft tradition, ownership, or sentimental meaning.

## Canon status

Everything introduced by this scan is proposed.
