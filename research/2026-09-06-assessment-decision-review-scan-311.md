# Assessment-dependent decision review scan — Pass 311

Status: RESEARCH / PROVENANCE ONLY / NOT CANON
Date: 2026-09-06

## Scope

This pass searched for structures that support explicit review of prior decisions after new evidence arrives, while preserving the original decision and separating review from downstream repair.

## Sources and reusable lessons

### Office of Justice Programs — Standard Functional Specifications for Law Enforcement Records Management Systems Version II

Source: https://bja.ojp.gov/sites/g/files/xyckuh186/files/media/document/lawenforcementrmsv2.pdf

The specification describes supplemental reports that add information after an initial report and may update an investigation's status. It also keeps supplemental reports linked to the original event/report. Reusable Ouros lesson: later review should be an additional durable event linked to the earlier decision, not an overwrite of the earlier record.

No US legal standard is imported into Ouros.

### Pokémon Tabletop United public reference — Skills

Source: https://pturpg.wikidot.com/skills

The public PTU reference describes Perception as a Skill used both reactively and for active investigation, including searching for evidence and traps. Reusable Ouros lesson: investigation scenes can be mechanically grounded in existing PTU skill families when the project source material confirms the exact use. This does not authorize every forensic or technical action as a Perception check.

### Pokémon Tabletop United public reference — Channeler

Source: https://pturpg.wikidot.com/channeler

The public reference labels Channeler as useful for travel and investigation and describes communication/understanding with Pokémon as a source of reconnaissance. Reusable Ouros lesson: Pokémon perspectives can become independent testimony or search input where source-material mechanics actually support it. A Pokémon perspective is not automatically factual truth.

### Pokémon Living World fan project

Source: https://www.pokeliving.com/

This public fan project emphasizes persistent NPC memory, relationships, world evolution while the player is absent, and ecological consequences. Reusable Ouros lesson: consequences can remain active after the player leaves and later reviews should operate on persistent state rather than resetting an area when a quest closes.

No characters, plot, implementation code or proprietary content were copied.

## Transformation into Ouros

The useful pattern is:

old evidence basis -> world decision -> later evidence revision -> actual receipt by the same decision-maker -> explicit review -> separate consequence repair.

This keeps four different histories queryable: what evidence existed, what the actor knew, what decision was made, and what later review concluded.
