Ouros synthesis

The useful combined pattern is a persistent shared assignment whose participants may separate, act on different information and reconverge later.

The assignment itself should have one durable identity. Each actor retains an independent schedule, location, knowledge state, permissions and observations. The system should not create duplicate quest worlds merely because the player follows one participant while another continues off-screen.

When the participants reunite, knowledge moves only through explicit communication or shared evidence. If one actor reaches a site first, that does not grant the other actor the same observation retroactively.

This pattern supports field surveys, delivery chains, escort preparation, maintenance, archive comparison, search work and ordinary institutional errands without requiring every task to culminate in combat.
