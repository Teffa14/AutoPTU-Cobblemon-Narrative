# Pass 98 Research — First Journeys, Novice Autonomy & Supported Independence

Status: research/provenance only. Not established Ouros canon. No mechanics are authorized by this file.

## Why this pass exists

The repository already has systems for education, credentials, family/kinship, travel, Pokémon partnership, clubs, workplaces, battle institutions, care, housing and public memory. What is still missing is the transition between them: the period when a novice stops being only a student/dependent and begins making unscripted decisions in the wider world.

This pass studies the recurring Pokémon structure of a first journey without assuming a universal starting age, universal legal adulthood, automatic parental permission, or a single League-defined rite of passage.

## Source notes

### Official Pokémon — Scarlet/Violet Treasure Hunt

Source: https://www.pokemon.com/uk/news/news-from-the-august-2022-pokemon-presents

The official August 2022 presentation describes the protagonist as a student at a prestigious academy who then embarks on a Treasure Hunt through Paldea and can pursue three major story paths. Reusable structure:

- an institution can deliberately send learners into a region-wide period of self-directed field experience;
- the same framework can support battle, exploration and other goals rather than one compulsory track;
- autonomy can expand while the academy remains a persistent home/institutional reference point.

Ouros adaptation: a school, club, research program or local institution may sponsor a bounded first expedition without owning the character's entire career.

Do not copy Paldea, Uva/Naranja Academy, its characters, Titans, Team Star, Gyms or Treasure Hunt plot.

### Official Pokémon — Ranger School and recruit progression

Source: https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokemon-video-games/pokemon-ranger-shadows-of-almia/

The official Shadows of Almia page explicitly describes beginning as a young recruit, learning at Ranger School and working upward toward greater responsibility. Reusable structure:

- classroom/instruction and field responsibility are different states;
- early assignments can be scoped to competence and support;
- later autonomy can emerge through demonstrated practice rather than an arbitrary narrative promotion.

Ouros adaptation: supervised field permissions, check-ins and expanding scopes can be stored independently from PTU Skills or Trainer Features.

### Official Pokémon — rookie Ranger identity

Source: https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokemon-video-games/pokemon-ranger/

The official Pokémon Ranger page presents the player as a rookie/cadet who performs real work while still being early in a professional trajectory. Reusable structure:

- novice status does not require fake or meaningless tasks;
- a first journey can matter to the world while still having safety boundaries;
- partner relationships and help from local Pokémon can be part of learning outside formal classes.

### PTU official GM advice — first session

Source: https://pokemontabletop.com/gm-advice-your-first-ptu-session/

This is especially important for AutoPTU. The official PTU blog recommends establishing Pokémon as characters from the first session and notes that extremely early combat differs because Trainers often have only one Pokémon and therefore lack the safety net of a full team.

Reusable design lessons:

- early encounters should teach relationships and decisions, not only battle syntax;
- difficulty should account for the actual party state rather than assuming a mature roster;
- searching, retrieving, helping, negotiating and observing can introduce field play before larger combat complexity;
- the Starter/first partner should receive narrative space outside combat.

This is a planning principle, not permission to invent combat scaling formulas.

### PTU official campaign seeds — selecting new Trainers

Source: https://pokemontabletop.com/campaign-seeds-the-road-to-tomorrow/

The Road to Tomorrow includes a mid-game possibility where established PCs select prospective new Trainers and give them their first Pokémon before those new Trainers contribute to an expedition.

Reusable structure:

- experienced PCs can become sponsors/mentors/gatekeepers for new characters;
- first-partner handoff can be an institutional and relationship event, not merely inventory initialization;
- later campaign generations can start from institutions the older PCs helped create.

This aligns with the existing Family/Generational and Mentorship layers without making inheritance automatic.

### Fangame — Pokémon Alabaster

Source: https://pokemonalabaster.wordpress.com/

The developer describes a protagonist sent to school to learn how to be a Trainer, with the school occupying a meaningful early portion before the broader journey. Reusable structure:

- training can occupy a finite opening arc rather than either being skipped or consuming the whole campaign;
- the first departure feels more meaningful if it follows concrete relationships, routines and local competencies.

Do not copy the region, academy, teams, mysteries, characters or Gym progression.

### Fangame — Pokémon Frozen Spirit

Source: https://frozen-spirit.com/en/

The public project page frames the first partner encounter as the event that propels a character from home into a larger journey. Reusable structure:

- the first partner can be the transition event between local life and broader exploration;
- home remains meaningful because departure follows an established starting place rather than an abstract spawn point.

Do not copy region, species, story or characters.

### Community discussion — journey fiction longevity

Source: https://www.reddit.com/r/pokemonfanfiction/comments/1dznclh

A community discussion describes trainer-journey fiction as an evergreen Pokémon form while also noting its reputation for becoming too long and being abandoned. This is anecdotal community evidence, not authority.

Useful design lesson for Ouros:

- a first journey needs bounded milestones and local conclusions;
- progression should not require visiting every region or resolving every possible hook;
- a journey can remain open-ended while individual legs have satisfying endpoints.

## Cross-check against project sources

The repository's Education layer already separates what was taught, practiced and assessed from actual PTU mechanical state. Pass 98 must consume that distinction rather than creating another curriculum system.

The Family/Kinship layer forbids automatic parent, guardian, family-trauma and inheritance assumptions for PCs. Pass 98 therefore cannot fabricate permission from family members or define a PC's age-dependent obligations.

The Credentials layer owns formal permissions and eligibility. Pass 98 may reference a permission scope but must not invent it.

The Travel layer owns route/service feasibility. A novice journey record does not make a dangerous or closed route legal.

The Pokémon Agency layer owns partnership history and observed cooperation. Receiving a first Pokémon does not automatically set trust, Loyalty or obedience.

The primary Caelo corpus was not reliably retrievable in this run for any universal age, guardianship, starter, supervision or first-journey rule. No Caelo-specific age threshold or permission rule is asserted.

## Design conclusions

1. Treat a first journey as a persistent transition object, not a one-time tutorial flag.
2. Keep chronological age, institutional novice status, mechanical Trainer Level and travel permission separate.
3. Let autonomy expand by explicit scope: route, distance, overnight stay, solo fieldwork, battle responsibility, custody responsibility, emergency authority, or unsupervised access.
4. Preserve a support graph: mentor, institution, clinic, transport contact, emergency return point and known safe locations.
5. First journeys should contain real consequences, but consequences should be proportionate to the actual capabilities and support state.
6. Failure should often change the plan rather than end the character's career.
7. A first partner is a persistent character relationship, not starter equipment.
8. Older or experienced people may also undertake a first formal Trainer journey. Never equate novice with child.
9. Departure and return are both story events. Home, school or sponsor institutions should react to what actually happened.
10. Multiplayer parties may contain characters with different autonomy scopes. One player's permission must not silently transfer to another.
