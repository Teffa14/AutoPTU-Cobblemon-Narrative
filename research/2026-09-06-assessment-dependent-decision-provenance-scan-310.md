# Assessment-dependent decision provenance scan — Pass 310

Status: RESEARCH / NOT CANON
Date: 2026-09-06

## Research question

After Ouros can preserve a custody assessment, revise it, and propagate the revision selectively, what must persist so the world can later explain which concrete decisions were made using the older conclusion?

This pass deliberately studies decision lineage rather than automatic consequence repair. A later correction does not prove that an earlier decision was unreasonable under the information available at the time, and it does not silently reverse that decision.

## Existing Ouros seams checked before writing

Pass 308 gives custody assessments explicit supersession lineage. Pass 309 materializes assessment conclusions as provenance-backed claims and propagates them only to explicit recipients through the ordinary information runtime. `CURRENT_FOCUS.md` requires private knowledge, explicit receipt and preservation of publication/belief history. The source-authority policy keeps PTU mechanical truth outside narrative research and keeps Kairos/Caelo variants non-authoritative unless explicitly adopted.

No existing runtime in the repository records a durable world decision against the exact assessment claim that the deciding actor actually knew. That is the narrow gap addressed by Pass 310.

## New public research

### Singh, Cobbe and Norval — Decision Provenance: Harnessing data flow for accountable systems

Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1804.05741

The paper proposes exposing decision pipelines, including inputs, decisions/actions and downstream effects across interconnected systems. Reusable structure for Ouros: preserve a causal edge from a consequential decision to the information state that informed it, so later review can distinguish what was known then from what became known later.

Transformation boundary: Ouros does not import legal accountability requirements, governance policy, automated-decision standards or compliance claims. The reusable element is only explicit decision/input lineage.

### Decision Provenance reference description

Source: https://decisionprovenance.org/

The public reference description emphasizes preserving decision context, judgement and outcome as they existed when a decision was made, rather than reconstructing them retrospectively. Reusable Ouros pattern: record the exact assessment claim available to an NPC at decision time instead of inferring later that a decision 'must have' relied on whichever conclusion is currently newest.

Transformation boundary: this is conceptual research, not authority for Ouros law or institutional procedure.

### Pokémon Ranger: Shadows of Almia — official Nintendo manual

Source: https://www.nintendo.com/eu/media/downloads/games_8/emanuals/nintendo_ds_21/Manual_NintendoDS_PokemonRangerShadowsOfAlmia_EN.pdf

The manual separates story Missions from citizen Quests and keeps ongoing quest information available through a dedicated quest view. Reusable Ouros structure: requests and institutional/civic obligations can remain durable world commitments rather than disappearing when a scene ends. This supports consequences such as route restrictions, inspections, rescue assignments or service decisions that remain active until explicitly revisited.

Transformation boundary: no Ranger organization, quest text, reward, location, character, capture rule or progression mechanic is imported.

### Not Presently Deceased — Pokémon Tabletop United one-shot

Source: https://music.amazon.in/podcasts/8a3fe022-7965-4fd6-8a04-357612c5fdfc/episodes/ff0b6ddc-cf0c-495d-84c7-b8d315121a2c/not-presently-deceased-an-actual-play-5e-d-d-adventure-the-power-of-friendship-and-unrestrained-violence-p1-pok%C3%A9mon-tabletop-united-oneshot

Reusable structure: a bounded location can support an investigation whose findings create later action rather than requiring the location itself to be a combat dungeon.

Transformation boundary: Professor Sycamore, Reflection Cave, participant characters, encounters, dialogue and the scenario solution are not imported. The source is evidence that investigation-first PTU play is viable, not mechanical authority.

## Ouros design lessons

A world decision should preserve actor, semantic time, affected subject, decision kind, basis assessment and the exact basis claim the actor possessed. This allows later queries such as 'which closure orders relied on the old report?' without rewriting history.

A superseding assessment can make an old decision reviewable only after the deciding actor actually receives the newer conclusion. Global existence of a correction is insufficient because Ouros NPCs are non-omniscient.

Review eligibility is separate from reversal. A closure may remain reasonable for another independent safety reason. A suspension may require an authorized reviewer. A commercial decision may remain lawful or socially accepted despite its original factual basis changing. Pass 310 therefore stops at causal review eligibility.

## PTU / Caelo / Kairos boundary

The project source index points mechanics questions back to the PTU/Kairos material, including Skills/Features, movement, hazards, encounter construction and recurring NPC guidance. None of this pass requires a new PTU rule. No Caelo/Kairos rule, class, Feature, species, form or campaign procedure is adopted.

If a later authored consequence invokes inspection Skills, Trainer Features, Pokémon senses, movement, battle, hazards or interruption mechanics, that element must be validated separately against the active Ouros rules profile and live AutoPTU evidence.
