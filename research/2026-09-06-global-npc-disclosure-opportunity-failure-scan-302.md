# Global NPC disclosure opportunity / communication failure research — Pass 302

Status: RESEARCH / PROVENANCE ONLY — NOT CANON
Date: 2026-09-06

## Research question

How should Ouros distinguish deliberate non-disclosure from inability to communicate, delivery failure, delayed processing and local projection failure without creating an omniscient blame system?

## New public sources reviewed

### NIST — Mission Critical Voice Quality of Experience: Probability of Successful Delivery Measurement Methods

Source: https://www.nist.gov/publications/mission-critical-voice-quality-experience-probability-successful-delivery-measurement-methods

Reusable lesson: in mission-critical communication, successful delivery is a property worth measuring separately from message creation. A sender producing a message does not itself prove that critical content reached the receiver.

Ouros transformation: dispatch attempt and receipt remain separate durable facts. The narrative layer must not turn a failed delivery into deliberate silence.

Not imported: NIST measurement equations, codecs, public-safety standards or real-world reliability values.

### NIST — Modeling and Development of Resilient Communication

Source: https://www.nist.gov/ctl/pscr/modeling-and-development-resilient-communication

Reusable lesson: disaster communication may be disrupted by infrastructure damage, congestion, changing groups, interoperability limits and network partitions. Multiple causes can explain why a message did not arrive.

Ouros transformation: responsibility findings require evidence about communication opportunity and attempt state. Missing receipt alone does not identify the responsible actor.

Not imported: networking architecture, routing algorithms, security design or technical deployment assumptions.

### FEMA — Broadcasters and Wireless Providers / IPAWS resilience

Source: https://www.fema.gov/emergency-managers/practitioners/integrated-public-alert-warning-system/broadcasters-wireless

Reusable lesson: warning systems gain resilience through multiple communication pathways because one pathway may be unavailable while another remains operational.

Ouros transformation: a failed scoped channel proves failure of that route only. It does not establish that no other route existed, nor does it let the runtime invent an alternate route that was never represented in world state.

Not imported: government roles, alert authority, EAS/IPAWS rules or legal obligations.

### Pokémon Ranger: Guardian Signs — Wireless Tower structure

Source: https://pokemon.fandom.com/wiki/Wireless_Tower

Reusable lesson: a communication hub can also be a physical explorable place whose infrastructure condition matters to the wider region. Communication failure can therefore produce exploration, repair and investigation play rather than only dialogue.

Ouros transformation: a regional relay site may become a non-canon worldbuilding candidate where message logs, damaged infrastructure and route conditions provide separate evidence streams.

Not imported: Oblivia, the Wireless Tower, named characters, encounter sequence, antenna design or plot.

### Pokémon Tabletop community — academy/Ranger rescue scenario discussion

Source: https://www.reddit.com/r/PokemonTabletop/comments/9nrfy4/help_need_campaign_ideas_for_a_pokemon_academy/

A 2026 contribution describes Ranger training built around simulated natural-disaster rescue and a dangerous Pokémon functioning as a navigational hazard rather than a mandatory defeat target.

Reusable lesson: PTU-adjacent play can frame hazards and rescue objectives around navigation and protection rather than eliminating an opponent.

Ouros transformation: the full relay-failure concept may use a rescue/repair objective where combat is optional and environmental access is the central problem.

Not imported: academy setting, exact scenario, Pokémon choice, class structure or encounter resolution.

### Pokémon Tabletop community — noncombat campaign advice

Source: https://www.reddit.com/r/PokemonTabletop/comments/1qo26uy/new_to_ptu/

Reusable lesson: investigation, rescue, exploration, survival and social scenes can carry campaign weight between battles, while towns may retain consequences from player action.

Ouros transformation: the reduced version of this pass is intentionally playable as evidence reconstruction and world-state consequence without requiring AutoPTU.

Not imported: any proposed XP handling, campaign premise, NPC or location.

## Original synthesis for Ouros

A missing warning should produce a causal question rather than an automatic betrayal flag. Investigation can reconstruct several distinct histories: the speaker chose silence despite direct access; no route to the receiver was known; a message was queued but delayed; infrastructure rejected the delivery; local Minecraft projection never acknowledged it; or the message arrived and the receiver later acted differently for another reason.

These histories can support rival testimony, infrastructure maintenance, institutional accountability, rescue preparation and long-term trust consequences while remaining grounded in explicit state.
