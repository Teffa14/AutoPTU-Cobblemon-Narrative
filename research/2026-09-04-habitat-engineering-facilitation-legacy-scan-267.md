# Habitat engineering, facilitation and legacy scan — Pass 267

Status: RESEARCH / PROVENANCE. Nothing in this note promotes a new Marea/Sendero species, structure, nest, territory, interaction edge or PTU rule to canon.

## Gap checked against the repository

The repository already has `FACILITATES` in the global species-interaction vocabulary, resource-pulse handling, cue/quality separation, site-use/identity separation, provisional ecological memory and semantic horizons. The missing seam is the physical environmental modification itself: a structure or substrate change can persist after the modifying organism leaves, can be used by other organisms, and can change future observations without proving ecological benefit or demographic growth.

Pass 267 therefore focuses on habitat modification and legacy effects rather than adding another attraction, alarm, migration or identity mechanism.

## Public sources and transformed lessons

### Albertson et al., “The ghosts of ecosystem engineers: Legacy effects of biogenic modifications” — Functional Ecology
Source: https://besjournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/1365-2435.14222

Reusable lesson: biologically produced habitat changes can persist after the engineer is absent. Legacy duration depends on properties of the modification, the modifier and the disturbance regime. A persistent change can influence later individuals, communities and ecosystem processes. Ouros should therefore persist a habitat modification through despawn/unload/restart when its own semantic horizon remains active; the creator’s continued presence is not a validity condition.

### Jones, Lawton and Shachak, “Positive and negative effects of organisms as physical ecosystem engineers” — Ecology
Source: https://esajournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1890/0012-9658%281997%29078%5B1946%3APANEOO%5D2.0.CO%3B2

Reusable lesson: physical ecosystem engineering concerns creation, maintenance or modification of habitat and abiotic resources. Its effects can be positive, negative or trivial. Trophic interaction alone is a different relationship. Ouros should therefore store the physical modification separately from any interpretation that it benefits a recipient. `FACILITATES` must remain an evidentiary/canon decision, not an automatic consequence of co-use.

### Hardin et al., “Secondhand homes: The multilayered influence of woodpeckers as ecosystem engineers” — Ecology and Evolution
Source: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34429930/

Reusable lesson: structures made by one organism can later be used by other organisms, including after abandonment, and the quality of the resulting opportunity depends on context. This supports an Ouros loop in which the player discovers a durable site feature before, after or without ever resolving its maker. The resulting recipient relationship can remain a hypothesis until adequate evidence exists.

### Westbrook, Cooper and Baker, “Beaver assisted river valley formation” — USGS
Source: https://www.usgs.gov/publications/beaver-assisted-river-valley-formation

Reusable lesson: a modification can alter hydrology, sediment, vegetation and later succession; later habitat states may outlive the original structure. Ouros should allow one physical change to create downstream world-state evidence without treating every consequence as a direct action by the original organism.

### Nintendo, Pokémon Ranger
Source: https://www.nintendo.com/fr-fr/Jeux/Nintendo-DS/Pokemon-Ranger-272442.html

Reusable game-design lesson: Pokémon abilities interacting with environmental obstacles can make exploration itself a gameplay layer. Ouros can reuse the high-level pattern of reading the environment, finding a cause or compatible capability, and revisiting the changed route. It must not import Ranger plots, characters, exact obstacle scripts or its capture/field-move rules.

### PTU community example: combat problem converted into ecological investigation and diplomacy
Source: https://www.reddit.com/r/AskReddit/comments/hhl1c5

Reusable GM lesson: a prepared combat can become more memorable when players investigate the environmental cause and address a persistent ecological problem rather than simply defeating the visible Pokémon. Ouros should let habitat changes produce non-combat investigation, mitigation and later consequences while preserving the option for battle when behavior actually escalates.

### PTU skill reference: Survival and Perception
Source: https://pturpg.wikidot.com/skills

PTU cross-check: Survival explicitly supports wilderness scouting, foraging, geography and tracking; Perception supports noticing environmental evidence while specialized knowledge may still be needed to interpret it. These skills can support discovery of a modification where the adopted rules permit. They do not reveal private source identity, prove creator attribution, prove a `FACILITATES` edge, or authorize tactical terrain semantics by themselves.

## Canon/proposal separation

CANON-ALIGNED: Ouros owns ecological truth and persistent world facts; Minecraft/Cobblemon presents them. Existing Marea/Sendero Fletchling population remains 12. Existing `FACILITATES` remains a relationship vocabulary term. PTU remains the mechanical baseline.

PROPOSED: `ECOLOGICAL_HABITAT_MODIFICATION_V1`, with physical-state lifecycle, creator-attribution state, legacy horizon and recipient-evidence links kept separate.

FIXTURE-ONLY: the Pass 267 microstructure, physical change, two anonymous counted-source aliases, creator attribution and recipient-use evidence.

UNCERTAIN: which real Marea/Sendero species create durable structures; which structures occur there; whether any resulting interaction deserves a canon `FACILITATES` edge; whether an engineered world feature should ever acquire tactical cover, blocking, hazard or zone semantics.

## Design consequences

A modification may remain present when its maker is absent. Creator attribution may remain unresolved. A later organism may use the feature without becoming its owner. Repeated use can support a recipient-use observation, while Pass 266 still governs claims about habitat quality. Population counts stay unchanged unless an independent demographic transaction occurs.

World geometry and tactical geometry are separate authorities. A Minecraft block arrangement may present a structure. It cannot silently become PTU cover, difficult terrain, a hazard, a blocker or forced-movement rule. Those effects require explicit semantic mapping and the exact engine capability families.

The reduced narrative version needs only Ouros world-state persistence, observation/provenance and adapter presentation. The mechanically rich version can later add active construction, destruction, pathing and battle effects after their exact capability paths are verified.
