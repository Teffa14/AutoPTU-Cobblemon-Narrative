Pass 90 targets that gap without duplicating the general Science or Calendar layers.

## Reusable structures extracted

### Observation-window loop

1. A predicted or historically recurring window exists.
2. Weather/visibility and instrument readiness are checked independently.
3. An observation session starts only if the site is accessible.
4. Observers record detections and non-detections with timestamps.
5. Analysis creates claims or revises predictions.
6. Public communication may summarize only part of that work.
7. The next window can confirm, contradict or fail to resolve the question.

### Sky-to-ground investigation loop

1. A transient sky event is observed.
2. Multiple reports are reconciled by time/location/instrument.
3. A provisional ground search region may be produced.
4. Cartography/Travel determine access.
5. Science/Geology investigate the site.
6. Any physical object receives exact provenance and custody.
7. Failure to recover material remains a valid result.

### Public observing night

1. Institution publishes an observing window.
2. Event Operations handles visitors and access.
3. Weather/visibility may reduce or cancel observation.
4. The event can succeed socially even if the target is not seen.
5. Photography, oral history and Public Memory may preserve the evening.
6. Scientific data remains separate from public experience.

### Competing sky interpretations

The same observed lights can produce:
- a scientific hypothesis;
- a local mythic interpretation;
- a public rumor;
- a technical explanation;
- an unresolved anomaly.

The generator must preserve all five as separate claim families until evidence justifies stronger conclusions.

## Important anti-inference rules

- A streak of light does not prove a meteorite reached the ground.
- A recovered rock does not prove it came from the most recent observed event without provenance.
- A predicted shower does not guarantee visible meteors.
- A non-detection does not prove the event did not occur.
- A telescope image does not automatically identify a Pokémon, Legendary, spacecraft or anomaly.
- A local constellation tradition does not establish cosmological truth.
- Different cultures may group or name the same visible stars differently.
- Cloud, fog, moonlight or artificial lighting may affect observational quality as world state, but they do not create PTU Accuracy penalties without an authoritative mechanic.
- A meteor shower is not automatically PTU Weather or a tactical Hazard.
- A celestial event does not automatically alter evolution, spawning, Moves, Abilities, forms, statuses or encounter eligibility.
- An observatory instrument does not grant a Skill bonus unless PTU/Caelo and the implementation support the exact effect.

## PTU/Caelo boundary

Current Ouros source architecture already provides Science, Observation, General world-state and Skill-backed actor state. Pass 90 does not create an `Astronomy` Skill, telescope bonus, constellation Feature, meteor hazard table, night Accuracy rule, cosmic damage type or celestial encounter permission.

Any future check for operating an instrument, interpreting data, navigating by stars or studying a celestial object must first be mapped to the governing PTU/Caelo source set and then to AutoPTU if it affects authoritative mechanics.

## Cobblemon reuse boundary

Strong candidates for reuse:
- day/night and sky presentation available through Minecraft;
- weather visuals and clouds as presentation/world observations;
- particles and sounds for authored celestial events;
- entities, models, forms, cries and animations for overworld Pokémon;
- blocks, glass, redstone-like props, screens and decorative equipment;
- UI/networking/synchronization hooks;
- world coordinates and timestamps;
- cameras/maps/books/signs where the project already uses them.

Adapter review required:
- event hooks that turn a world-time window into an Ouros observation session;
- server-side detection records;
- persistence of exact instrument/session state;
- projection of public notices and observing schedules;
- any sky event that creates a later field-search region.

Forbidden battle authority:
- Cobblemon nearby Pokémon do not become participants because they are visible during an observing event;
- Cobblemon BattleState/controller/participant code never decides the encounter;
- AutoPTU remains authoritative for tactical state and result.

## Research outcome

Pass 90 supports a dedicated astronomy/observatory continuity extension because the existing repository has the scientific method, calendar, myth, weather, technology and public-event pieces but no owner for the persistent observing workflow connecting them.

All Ouros-specific observatory names, locations, instruments, constellations, celestial calendars, meteor sites, traditions and institutions remain proposed until canon review.
