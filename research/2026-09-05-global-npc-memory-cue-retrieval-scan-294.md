## Design extraction

Reusable structures:

- return-to-scene cue: revisiting a location can make an old detail accessible;
- object cue: an item tied to an earlier event can recover content without necessarily restoring source attribution;
- person cue: seeing or speaking with an involved actor can restore access to a prior report;
- record-reference cue: a signature, timestamp or dispatch identifier can restore attribution already present in provenance;
- rehearsal cue: deliberate retelling can temporarily strengthen accessibility without altering historical confidence;
- archive corroboration: documentary evidence remains queryable even when personal recall is weak;
- convergent investigation: player conclusions can emerge from multiple evidence channels rather than one omniscient quest flag.

## Rejected shortcuts

- no automatic memory restoration from entering a region;
- no cue can invent a source;
- no documentary lookup silently becomes personal memory;
- no psychological rates or real-world quantitative forgetting curves are asserted;
- no PTU mechanic is inferred from cognitive-science literature;
- no Pokémon quest is copied into Ouros.
