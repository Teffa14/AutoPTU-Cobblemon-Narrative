# Infrastructure failure causal-attribution scan — Pass 303

Status: RESEARCH / PROVENANCE ONLY
Date: 2026-09-06
Canon effect: NONE

## Why this scan exists

Pass 302 can establish that a warning failed because a communication path was unavailable or a delivery attempt failed. That fact must not silently answer why the infrastructure failed. The next reusable world-simulation seam is causal investigation: accident, wear, environmental damage, tampering, responsible actor and deliberate sabotage must remain separable conclusions supported by evidence available to the investigating character.

Repository review before writing found no existing `Three Clue Rule` reference and no executable infrastructure-failure attribution layer. Existing deception/source-attribution work already establishes the stronger pattern that provenance roots, confidence and actor knowledge must survive contradictory investigations without an omniscient truth flag.

## Public sources reviewed

### NIST — Failure Analysis of the WWVB Tower

Source: National Institute of Standards and Technology, `Failure Analysis of the WWVB Tower`, Joseph D. McColskey, Christopher N. McCowan and Raymond L. Santoyo, published 2002-08-01.
https://www.nist.gov/publications/failure-analysis-wwvb-tower

Reusable lesson: a visible infrastructure failure does not identify its cause. The cited investigation used multiple kinds of material examination and concluded that fatigue associated with engineering/specification problems explained the collapse. Ouros can use the general investigation structure: incident first, causal evidence later, and attribution only at the evidentiary level actually reached.

Transformation boundary: no NIST measurements, tower design, real failure details or engineering conclusions become Ouros canon or simulation rules. The source only motivates evidence-driven causal escalation.

### NIST — radio propagation around collapsed structures

Source: NIST, `Propagation and Detection of Radio Signals Before, During, and After the Implosion of a Large Convention Center`, published 2006-10-01.
https://www.nist.gov/publications/propagation-and-detection-radio-signals-during-and-after-implosion-large-convention

Reusable lesson: communication loss can arise from physical attenuation and structural conditions. A missing message therefore cannot itself be evidence of malicious interference.

Transformation boundary: Ouros does not import RF equations, measurements, equipment specifications or emergency-response procedures.

### The Alexandrian — Node-Based Scenario Design / Three Clue Rule

Source: Justin Alexander, `Node-Based Scenario Design – Part 2: Choose Your Own Adventure`.
https://thealexandrian.net/creations/misc/node-design/node-design2.html

Reusable lesson: mysteries are more robust when important conclusions are reachable through multiple clue routes rather than one mandatory clue. For Ouros this becomes a provenance-aware investigation graph: physical trace, maintenance history, witness knowledge and institutional records may independently advance a conclusion.

Transformation boundary: Ouros does not copy an adventure, prose, node map or mandatory clue count. The useful abstraction is redundancy and alternative evidence paths.

## Original Ouros deductions

A failed relay, weather station, lift, beacon, gate, irrigation control, clinic repeater or transport signal can create an investigation without predetermining a villain. The visible failure is one historical fact. Later evidence can support fatigue or environmental damage, prove physical tampering, connect a person to the site, or establish intent. Each step needs its own provenance.

Repeated rumors must not become independent corroboration. Access to an installation proves opportunity at most. Tampering proves deliberate alteration of the object, not who did it. Actor linkage still does not establish motive or intent. Conflicting independent evidence should remain contested rather than being resolved by source count alone.

This structure creates faction and NPC arcs naturally. Maintenance workers can be wrongly blamed before material evidence clears them. A rival can have legitimate access and still be innocent. A real saboteur can exploit pre-existing wear. Repair crews, witnesses, dispatchers and institutions can hold different parts of the history without any character gaining omniscient access.
