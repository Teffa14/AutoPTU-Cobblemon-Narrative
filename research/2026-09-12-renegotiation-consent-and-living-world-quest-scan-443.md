Public source A — Pathologic / Pathologic 2 time and world-state structure

Sources:
- https://www.pathologic-game.com/ru/kickstarter/
- https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/pathologic-2-mindmap-a-questlog-people-actually-read

Reusable structure: the setting continues under a fixed temporal pressure. Opportunities can disappear, districts and access conditions can change, and small events need not all become permanent quest-log obligations. The world continues even when the player does not resolve every opportunity.

Ouros transformation: a renegotiation request may arrive while the original obligation is already deteriorating or expired. Receiving the request should not silently rewrite the prior promise. A separate consent step preserves causality and allows later consequences to distinguish old agreement, new information and later choices.

Excluded protected material: named characters, plague premise, locations, dialogue, authored mysteries, quest text and plot sequence.

Reusable pattern

OLD AGREEMENT -> ADVERSE WORLD STATE -> RESPONDER REQUESTS NEW TERMS -> REQUEST ACTUALLY ARRIVES -> REQUESTER EXPLICITLY ACCEPTS OR REJECTS REOPENING -> RESPONSE MUST TRAVEL -> ONLY THEN MAY NEW TERMS BE AUTHORED.

The two decisions are evidence. They are not replacement terms and not relationship consequences.

Reduced Ouros use

A field specialist asks to renegotiate a blocked survey. The requester receives the message after a short delay. They may accept reopening because another window is useful, or reject because the original deadline mattered. Pass 443 records that decision without changing the original appointment. A later communication seam must inform the specialist.

Mechanically rich extension

If later replacement terms lead to hazardous travel, survey, protection, retrieval, escort, containment or extraction, the same negotiation history remains valid. The encounter must declare all battle dependencies instead of allowing the Minecraft adapter to emulate missing PTU rules.

Open questions

Pass 443 does not send the requester's answer back to the responder. Acceptance still needs a delivery path before the responder can legitimately know negotiation reopened. After that, a replacement-proposal owner must preserve lineage to the old commitment without rewriting it. Rejection still needs communication and later policy for waiting, abandonment, expiry and accountability. Social consequences remain downstream evidence-driven systems rather than automatic effects of either decision.
