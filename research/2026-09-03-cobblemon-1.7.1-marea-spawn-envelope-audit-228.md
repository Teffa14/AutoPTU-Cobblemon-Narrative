## Design corrections produced by this audit

1. Native biome/tag compatibility must be recorded per spawn-detail ID, not per species as one flattened habitat label.
2. Cobblemon `weight` remains a spawn-selection input, not population truth.
3. Ouros ecology may suppress or modulate an already eligible detail but must not expand the native envelope without a reviewed datapack decision.
4. Persistent authored individuals can exist independently of anonymous generic-spawn eligibility, but reconciliation must prevent duplication.
5. Settlement prose must not be converted into `#minecraft:village`, and ecological-cell names must not be converted into biome IDs.
6. The runtime must record which detail ID justified every generic projected spawn so later ecological telemetry can distinguish native eligibility from Ouros modulation.
