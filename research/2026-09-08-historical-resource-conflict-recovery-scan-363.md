## Derived Ouros lesson

Conflict evidence should be captured at the moment the admission read occurs. Recomputing later against a modified reservation ledger can answer whether the window is clear now, but it cannot prove what the allocator or NPC saw earlier.

This distinction supports original consequences: an old conflict can explain a delayed expedition, a temporary reroute, a request for another instrument, procurement pressure or disagreement over whether a prior decision was reasonable. Those consequences still require their own authority and provenance.
