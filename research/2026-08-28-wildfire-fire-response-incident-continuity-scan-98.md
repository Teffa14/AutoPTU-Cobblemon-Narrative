## Why this pass exists

The complete recursive repository tree was inspected before selecting the topic. Existing work already covers generic crisis lifecycle, weather information, forestry/restoration, conservation, worksite safety, public notices, route closures, infrastructure outages and recovery. The remaining gap is a persistent incident-specific model for fire reports, fire perimeters/sectors, evacuation and access areas, response assignments, suppression attempts, verification, residual hot spots/reignition reports, re-entry review and post-fire handoff.

This pass therefore specializes fire operational continuity while leaving generic crisis authority in `crisis-rescue-recovery-layer.md`, woodland state in `forestry-managed-woodland-harvest-restoration-extension.md`, ecological interpretation in Conservation, weather truth in Weather/Seasonality, worker readiness in Worksite Safety/Pokémon Work, and route/facility reopening in their owner systems.

## Source 1 — Pokémon animated series: The Pokémon Water War

Source: https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/EP106

Reusable structure:

- firefighting is represented as organized work rather than merely using a Water-type attack;
- teams have differentiated functions, including large-scale response, tight-space rescue and general response;
- responders coordinate rather than every nearby Pokémon independently acting on the fire;
- rescue of a trapped person and extinguishment are related but distinct objectives;
- a first fire does not make later response trivial.

Transformation for Ouros:

- an emergency institution may maintain individually assigned Pokémon response teams when canon and Pokémon Work evidence support them;
- an actor can be qualified for one response role and unsuitable for another;
- evacuation/extraction, fire suppression, perimeter control and later verification should be separate operational tasks;
- a Water Move, Water typing or firefighting-themed species identity never proves universal suppression capability.

Not imported:

- Captain Aidan, his teams, episode dialogue or sequence;
- automatic role assignment by species/type;
- any assumption that Water attacks extinguish a world fire through ordinary battle resolution.

## Source 2 — Pokémon animated series: The Pokémon Harbor Patrol

Source: https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/BW115

Reusable structure:

- drills and real incidents use overlapping personnel but are different records;
- lookout, firefighting and evacuation/rescue roles can be distinct;
- aptitude for one task does not imply aptitude for every response function;
- access control matters: an apparent emergency can be a drill whose perimeter should not be casually entered;
- a real incident can interrupt ordinary training and expose different operational requirements.

Transformation for Ouros:

- preparedness drills should write durable readiness/training observations without pretending a drill is an actual crisis;
- response assignments should reference exact roles and individual capability evidence;
- training history may inform later assignment, but does not grant hidden stat bonuses;
- perimeter and responder authority must come from canonized institutions/mandates, never from costume, species or proximity.

Not imported:

- named characters, exact rescue sequence, aptitude-test mechanics or any species-specific rule.

## Source 3 — Pokémon Ranger: Shadows of Almia

Source: https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Ranger%3A_Shadows_of_Almia

Reusable structure:

- a forest fire changes access to a route and becomes an assigned operational mission;
- response can require finding a suitable resource before suppression proceeds;
- extinguishment restores traversal only after the scenario explicitly resolves the incident.

Transformation for Ouros:

- incident response may generate resource needs and assignments before an operation can be attempted;
- resource acquisition, assignment, suppression attempt, observed effect and route reopening remain separate transitions;
- a full battle encounter is optional; a response mission can be primarily logistical, investigative and operational.

Not imported:

- Ranger capture mechanics;
- the specific Blastoise/Rain Dance requirement;
- any rule saying Rain Dance, Water-type Moves or one Pokémon automatically extinguish a wildfire.

## Source 4 — Pokémon Horizons: Arboliva's Forest

Source: https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/EP1243

Reusable structure:

- a wildfire remains narratively important after the active flames are gone;
- burned landscape, injured wild Pokémon and changed behavior become a later exploration state;
- encountering defensive or hostile wildlife in a burned area does not prove that the Pokémon caused the fire or wants combat;
- restoration/support can become the premise after immediate response ends.

Transformation for Ouros:

- active incident closure should hand off to Forestry, Conservation, Care, Travel and Recovery rather than deleting the incident;
- post-fire observations preserve burned-area history and can generate later ecological questions;
- wild Pokémon near a burn scar receive ordinary agency/evidence treatment. They are never silently labelled victims, aggressors, arsonists or helpers.

Not imported:

- Arboliva, Ancient Poké Balls, plot beats or character relationships.

## Source 9 — NWCG wildfire terminology and lifecycle

Sources:

- https://www.nwcg.gov/publications/pms205/nwcg-glossary-of-wildland-fire-pms-205
- https://www.nwcg.gov/node/1803224

Reusable operational distinction:

- containment, mop-up/patrol and extinguishment are separate concepts;
- residual burning material and hot spots can remain relevant after forward spread is stopped;
- an incident can require monitoring after the dramatic phase has ended.

Transformation for Ouros:

- use broad authored operational states without importing United States command structures, law, percentages, qualifications or exact terminology as setting canon;
- preserve the concept that `spread stopped`, `perimeter holding`, `residual heat addressed`, `incident out`, `area reopened` and `ecological recovery` are different facts;
- do not require numerical containment percentages.

## Source 10 — 2026 Santa Rosa Island Fire recovery chronology

Source: https://home.nps.gov/chis/learn/management/santa-rosa-island-fire.htm

Reusable structure:

- forward progress can stop before full containment;
- mop-up continues after that point;
- post-fire assessment can begin while the fire's operational history is still recent;
- recovery review can involve hydrology, geology, cultural resources, vegetation, infrastructure, wildlife and public information at the same time.

Transformation for Ouros:

- close the active incident through verified operational state, then create owner-system handoffs rather than one universal `RECOVERED` flag;
- Forestry, Conservation, Geology, Public Works, Travel, Material Culture and Public Memory can all receive different follow-up objects from one incident;
- do not import acreage, agencies, procedures, legal standards or real incident facts into Ouros canon.

## PTU / AutoPTU cross-check

Current read-only AutoPTU evidence contains implemented and tested PTU effects involving Burn, Fire-type attacks, Weather interactions and individual Abilities. This demonstrates that those named PTU mechanics exist in the battle ruleset. It does not establish any generic rule converting overworld flame, wildfire heat, smoke, ash, embers, structural fire or radiant heat into Burn, HP loss, Accuracy penalties, Weather, Zones or forced movement.

The AutoPTU implementation status also shows representative environmental/Ability behavior but does not justify family-wide environmental-fire support.

Therefore Pass 98 does not invent:

- wildfire damage per round;
- smoke Accuracy/Evasion penalties;
- automatic Burn from entering a fire block;
- ember or ash statuses;
- heat exhaustion rules;
- fire-spread arithmetic;
- wind-driven tactical displacement;
- structural ignition HP;
- water quantity/suppression math;
- universal Move-to-firefighting mappings;
- universal Fire-type immunity to environmental fire;
- universal Water-type firefighting competence;
- firefighting Skill DCs;
- emergency carrying/extraction actions.

Caelo-specific environmental rules remain usable only when the exact project source explicitly establishes the relevant effect. No Caelo evidence found in this pass establishes a universal wildfire subsystem.

## High-level design lessons retained

1. Fire response has a lifecycle after visible flames stop.
2. Drill readiness and real-incident performance are distinct records.
3. Different response roles can require different evidence and assignments.
4. Evacuation, suppression, rescue, access control and reopening are separate authorities.
5. A smoke report is evidence, not incident truth.
6. A suppression attempt is an action, not proof of success.
7. Reignition requires new verified evidence; it should not be scripted merely to create drama.
8. Post-fire wildlife behavior should create observations and questions before causal conclusions.
9. Temporary control lines, closures, camps and access routes can leave durable world history.
10. Rich tactical fire mechanics remain reduced until the exact engine families are verified.
