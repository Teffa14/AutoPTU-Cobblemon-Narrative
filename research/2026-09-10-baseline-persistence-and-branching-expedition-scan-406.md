## Ouros synthesis

A durable expedition arc can begin with several unrelated field assignments issued by different offices. Each trip records a local condition, resource handoff, measurement or witness account. Two institutions later reach different recommendations because they possess different subsets of that evidence and value different risks. The player may have completed some assignments, NPC teams may have completed others, and untouched work remains absent rather than retroactively resolved.

A later archive review can reveal that the disagreement depends on chronology. One document may predate a route repair, another may reflect conditions after it, and an instrument may have changed hands between the two surveys. Persisted holder-history baselines and later transitions are useful here because the narrative can distinguish what is demonstrable after a known cut from what remains uncertain before it.
