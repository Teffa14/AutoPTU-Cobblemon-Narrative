# Global NPC deception discovery / trust scan — Pass 298

Status: RESEARCH / PROVENANCE ONLY. Nothing in this file is Ouros canon or a PTU rules adoption.

Repository audit before writing: the recursive tree and current focus were inspected. Pass 283 already supports provenance-preserving ordinary relays, so sincere retransmission was not reimplemented. Passes 295–297 already separate deception, subjective attribution, delivery and durable checkpoint recovery. The uncovered seam is evidence-gated social consequence after deception becomes attributable.

New public sources used this pass:

1. Dobbs, Butler & Swire-Thompson, Scientific Reports (2026), “The differential consequences of correcting misinformation for high and low credibility sources.” https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-026-42329-x
Reusable lesson: correction and source-credibility consequences are separable; perceived intentional inaccuracy can carry stronger reputational consequences. Ouros transforms this into separate falsehood-corroboration and intent-attribution states rather than importing experimental magnitudes.

2. Druckman (2019), Journal of Applied Social Psychology, “Repairing violations of trustworthiness in negotiation.” https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/jasp.12571
Reusable lesson: trust violation and trust repair are different processes, and actions can matter separately from verbal responses. Ouros therefore does not make apology an automatic inverse of a trust penalty.

3. Rapp et al./Cognitive Research review page (2024), “Don’t believe them! Reducing misinformation influence through source discreditation.” https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s41235-024-00581-7
Reusable lesson: source credibility and content correction interact, but evidence is mixed enough that Ouros should avoid a universal credibility formula.

4. Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Explorers material, including the guild's changing assessment of a trusted public figure after contradictory evidence is presented. https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon%3A_Explorers_of_Darkness
Reusable structure only: prior reputation can delay acceptance of a new accusation; corroborating observations can shift a group's willingness to act. No character, plot, dialogue, location or revelation is imported.

5. Pokémon Tabletop United Oracle reference. https://pturpg.wikidot.com/oracle
Reusable GM lesson: incomplete information can be useful when it remains actionable and later gains meaning. It reinforces keeping evidence availability separate from objective world truth. No Oracle mechanics are adopted by this pass.

Authority boundary: external stories and research are inspiration only. The narrative repository's README requires PTU/Caelo plus live AutoPTU evidence for mechanics. Pass 298 adds no combat rule, Feature, Edge, Skill effect, social progression reward or Caelo rule.

Original synthesis: a lie may be contradicted before intent is established. A world that immediately labels the speaker a liar would collapse investigation into omniscience. Ouros instead keeps the contradiction, proof of intent and relationship consequence as separately queryable events.
