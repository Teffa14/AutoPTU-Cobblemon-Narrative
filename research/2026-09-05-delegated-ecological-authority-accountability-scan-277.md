# Delegated ecological authority and accountability scan — Pass 277

Status: RESEARCH / PROVENANCE. No canon effect.
Date: 2026-09-05

Question

How should Ouros represent a legitimate authority delegating a narrow ecological-management function to another actor without converting temporary performance, repeated renewal or visible world presence into permanent jurisdiction?

Repository fit

Pass 276 already records `delegation_refs` inside joint-jurisdiction records but deliberately leaves delegation semantics undefined. `design/agreements-mediation-repair-layer.md` already owns negotiated commitments and compliance evidence. `canon/ecosystem-conflict-managed-development-foundation.md` authorizes future institutional responses while leaving concrete jurisdiction open. This pass therefore adds only the missing authority-transfer/accountability seam.

Public-source findings

1. Delegation can transfer operational responsibility while the delegating body retains oversight responsibility.

EPA's delegated-environmental-program policy describes delegation as another government assuming operational responsibilities while EPA retains continuing oversight and accountability for progress toward environmental goals. This supports keeping `delegated_function` separate from `retained_oversight_authority`.

Source: US EPA, “Policy on Oversight of Delegated Environmental Programs” and companion delegation policy, public EPA materials. Accessed 2026-09-05.

2. A valid delegation can have an explicit term and renewal gate.

30 CFR 1227.110 uses a fixed term for delegation agreements, requires an affirmative renewal request, and conditions renewal on meeting standards. This is useful as a structural pattern: expiry does not silently renew, and repeated past performance does not create permanent authority.

Source: Electronic Code of Federal Regulations / Cornell LII, 30 CFR §1227.110. Accessed 2026-09-05.

3. Delegated functions should identify procedures, records, resources, monitoring and reporting.

40 CFR §35.3010 requires delegation agreements to identify procedures, records, staffing/funding and the form and frequency of oversight, monitoring, evaluation and reporting. Ouros can transform this into explicit evidence obligations rather than a generic `delegate_is_trusted` flag.

Source: Electronic Code of Federal Regulations / Cornell LII, 40 CFR §35.3010. Accessed 2026-09-05.

4. Deficient performance can trigger correction, partial termination or revocation rather than retroactively erasing valid prior acts.

Federal delegation examples include notice of deficiencies, a correction opportunity and possible termination of all or part of a delegation. The reusable design lesson is progressive accountability with scoped remedies. Ouros should preserve historical acts while changing future authority state.

Sources: 43 CFR §3191.3-1; 30 CFR §1227.802. Accessed 2026-09-05.

5. Adaptive management requires monitoring, evaluation and adjustment.

USGS describes adaptive management as structured learning in which monitoring and evaluation inform later adjustment. Adaptive monitoring likewise evolves when management questions and conditions change. For Ouros, reporting obligations can matter because they support review and learning, not because paperwork itself creates ecological truth.

Sources: Allen et al., “Adaptive management of rangeland systems” (USGS publication record, 2017); McCord & Pilliod, “Adaptive monitoring in support of adaptive management in rangelands” (USGS, 2022).

6. Shared or delegated governance still needs explicit allocation of authority, responsibility and accountability.

FAO's protected-area governance overview distinguishes government-delegated management from shared, private and community governance, framing governance around who holds authority, responsibility and accountability for key decisions. This supports an explicit delegation record rather than collapsing all cooperation into one organization.

Source: FAO Sustainable Forest Management Toolbox, Protected Areas module. Accessed 2026-09-05.

Pokémon / game-structure findings

Pokémon Mystery Dungeon uses discrete jobs obtained through boards or clients. Jobs have bounded objectives, eligibility constraints, completion conditions and rewards; accepting or repeatedly completing jobs does not itself make the team the permanent governing authority of every destination. Ouros can reuse the high-level structure of a bounded assignment with an issuer, objective and completion evidence while discarding proprietary characters, dialogue, locations and story beats.

Source: public Pokémon Mystery Dungeon job documentation summarized by Bulbapedia. Accessed 2026-09-05.

This source is used only as an abstract mission-structure reference. No Mystery Dungeon guild, character, rank system, reward formula or plot element is proposed for Ouros canon.

Reusable Ouros design lessons

A delegation should specify the delegator, delegate, exact function, affected scope, effective semantic horizon, retained powers, reporting/evidence duties, review route, subdelegation permission and end conditions.

Authority and execution must stay separate. An actor may be authorized but unable to perform. Another actor may be capable of performing but lack authority.

Successful ecological outcome does not prove compliant process. Conversely, a reporting defect does not prove ecological failure.

Expiry, suspension or revocation changes future authorization. It does not delete the historical fact that earlier actions were validly authorized at the time.

Renewal must be explicit. Repeated renewals do not silently create ownership, permanent office or a broader mandate.

Subdelegation needs its own authority. A delegate cannot create another delegate merely by assigning work informally.

Oversight evidence should be source-backed and auditable. Minecraft proximity, NPC uniforms, signs, repeated quest completion or player investment are presentation/history, not authority sources.

UNCERTAIN: which real Marea/Sendero institutions can delegate; which functions are delegable; whether player organizations can ever receive stewardship functions; appeal/review structure; compensation; liability; exact emergency interaction.

PTU / Caelo / Kairos cross-check

No public governance source creates a PTU rule. PTU remains the mechanical baseline where actual checks, movement or combat are invoked. Caelo and Kairos remain comparative sources under `design/ouros-source-authority-and-species-policy.md`; no source-specific delegation rule is adopted here.

Mechanics boundary

The reduced delegation loop requires only Ouros persistence, provenance, semantic time/review horizons, governance state and Minecraft/Cobblemon presentation.

Any mechanically enforced boundary, escort, detention, blocking, forced displacement, reaction, environmental hazard, Move, Ability, Item or Trainer Feature effect must pass the existing engine capability gates. A delegation instrument cannot synthesize a missing PTU mechanic.

Research limitations

The legal and conservation sources are used as structural analogies, not as a legal code for Ouros. Their jurisdiction-specific requirements are not imported. The Mystery Dungeon material contributes only a generic bounded-assignment pattern.
