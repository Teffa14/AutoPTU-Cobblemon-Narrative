# Rumor, Testimony & Local-Knowledge Research Scan — Pass 61

Status: research/provenance only. Nothing here is Ouros canon.
Date: 2026-08-26

## Scope

The repository already models information packets, publication, witness reports, actor knowledge, public memory, field reports and case evidence. The remaining gap is the playable middle between a first-person claim and a formal report: informal claims that circulate through places, change through retelling, remain locally useful and can be tested without converting belief into world truth.

Repository-wide file inventory was checked before writing. Existing boundaries remain: Media/Communications owns channels and delivery; Public Memory owns community-scale remembered interpretations; Observation owns direct observations and knowledge records; Case/Authority owns formal evidentiary consequences; world truth remains separate from every claim source.

## Sources and reusable patterns

### Pokémon Legends: Arceus — A Peculiar Ponyta
Source: https://www.serebii.net/legendsarceus/requests/apeculiarponyta.shtml
Supporting reference: https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Appendix%3ALegends%3A_Arceus_walkthrough/Requests_1-30

A local report describes an unusual Pokémon. The player receives the report as a lead, visits the location, observes the subject directly and returns with findings. Reusable lesson: a report can be actionable before it is verified. Preserve claimant, place, time and wording, then let investigation determine what survives.

### Pokémon Legends: Arceus — The Mysterious Will-o’-the-Wisp
Source: https://pokemondb.net/legends-arceus/missions-requests

A witness makes a claim another resident doubts. The structure asks the player to test the claim rather than choose who to believe. Reusable lesson: contradictory confidence can coexist, and confirming a sighting does not automatically validate every inference attached to the original story.

### Pokémon Legends: Arceus — Strange Happenings at Midnight
Source: https://www.serebii.net/legendsarceus/requests/strangehappeningsatmidnight.shtml

A resident reports repeated symptoms in a familiar domestic space. Investigation checks several mundane surfaces before the cause is found. Reusable lesson: symptom claims and causal claims must be separate. A resident can accurately report a recurring sound while being wrong about what causes it.

### Pokémon Legends: Arceus — The Sea’s Legend
Source: https://www.serebii.net/legendsarceus/requests/thesea%27slegend.shtml

A current observation is connected to an older text. The player combines testimony, place knowledge and archival lore. Reusable lesson: old stories can narrow a search without becoming canonical cosmology. Historical story, modern sighting and encounter result need separate provenance.

### Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Explorers — Fogbound Lake and information control
Source: https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Wigglytuff%27s_Guild
Supporting reference: https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Appendix%3AMystery_Dungeon_2_walkthrough/Chapter_6

An expedition begins partly from a treasure rumor. After the discovery, the group withholds important information and outsiders retain an incomplete interpretation. ### Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Explorers — trusted account later reinterpreted
Source: https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Mystery_Dungeon%3A_Explorers_of_Time_and_Explorers_of_Darkness

A respected source gives an account that causes a community-wide response; later evidence changes the interpretation. Reusable lesson: credibility affects propagation, not truth. Corrections should not erase the historical consequences of an earlier claim.

### PTU community campaign — Harlona rebuilding pitch
Source: https://rpol.net/display.cgi?date=1454079513&gi=4&ti=34579

The region is introduced through competing rumors about league expansion, investment and other future pressures. Reusable lesson: multiple rumor families can coexist around one place and pull characters toward different stakeholders before any one claim is confirmed.

Source: https://forums.giantitp.com/archive/index.php/t-527075.html

A character follows a professional goal toward a known specialist, but the expected situation has changed dramatically. ## Structures extracted

Claim ladder: direct observation → first-person account → retelling → local rumor → repeated belief → formal inquiry/publication → confirmation, correction or unresolved dispute. This is a transmission ladder, never a truth ladder.

Corroboration triangle: direct observation; testimony from actors with plausible access; records/objects/environmental traces/timestamps. Strong investigations often combine the three without making any one category omnipotent.

Useful distortion tags: memory compression, second-hand retelling, ambiguous visibility, mistaken identity, local metaphor read literally, protective omission, status pressure, fear, faction framing, deliberate bait, outdated information and two true events merged into one story. These are narrative metadata, not numeric modifiers.

Local expertise: a resident may know a route, seasonal habit, maintenance sound, neighborhood routine, old nickname or institutional custom through repeated exposure. Expertise stays narrow and provenance-bound; it does not imply species mechanics or hidden world truth.

A false claim may still expose true social state: who repeats it, who benefits, which place residents avoid, which institution is distrusted or which event people are trying to explain.

## Originality and mechanical boundary

Do not import distinctive characters, dialogue, exact plots or unique quest solutions from these sources. Reuse only high-level structures.

Rumor state cannot create Skill bonuses, Accuracy/Evasion changes, initiative changes, automatic social success, species behavior rules, Legendary permissions, supernatural communication, tracking DCs, battle preparation bonuses or Trainer Feature effects. Any PTU/Caelo mechanic must be validated against project sources and current AutoPTU evidence.

## Recommendation

Add an informal-knowledge continuity extension with explicit claim provenance, transmission events, local-knowledge scopes, contradiction links, deliberate-planting metadata and resolution outcomes. Keep most scenes noncombat. If a rumor-driven investigation culminates in combat, declare the permanent capability dependencies and preserve a static reduced version while richer tactical families remain unverified.
