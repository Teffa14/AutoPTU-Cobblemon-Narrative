# Downtime, Hobbies & Personal Projects Research — Pass 49

Status: research and provenance only. Not Ouros canon. Not a PTU/Caelo rules source.

Date: 2026-08-20

## Scope

This pass investigates low-stakes life between major adventures: downtime, hobbies, routines, personal projects, recurring leisure, casual Pokémon interaction, collecting, decorating, games, neighborhood habits and small self-directed goals.

Existing project boundaries reviewed before research:

- `design/social-bonds-mentorship-clubs-layer.md` owns persistent relationships, clubs, conversation topics and mentorship.
- `design/homes-housing-neighborhoods-layer.md` owns residences, households and neighborhood state.
- `design/food-agriculture-hospitality-layer.md` owns food production, dishes, restaurants and meal-service state.
- `design/care-recovery-welfare-layer.md` owns medical/recovery state.
- `design/workplaces-professions-staffing-layer.md` owns employment schedules and institutional staffing.
- `design/education-academies-field-practice-layer.md` owns formal learning and supervised practice.
- `design/sports-racing-athletic-culture-layer.md` owns formal or record-bearing athletic competition.
- `design/public-memory-event-legacy-layer.md` owns public events and institutional recurrence.
- `design/pokemon-agency-partnership-release-layer.md` owns persistent Pokémon identity and partnership history.

The repository did not have a dedicated layer for voluntary personal-time activity and small projects that matter to a character without being a job, school requirement, public event, formal competition or crisis response.

## Source findings

### Official Pokémon: Pokémon Camp makes ordinary interaction visible

Official Pokémon Sword/Shield material presents Pokémon Camp as something Trainers can establish while exploring. The player can watch party Pokémon interact, call them over, play with toys, cook and visit other players' camps.

Sources:
- https://swordshield.pokemon.com/en-ca/gameplay/pokemon-camps/
- https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokemon-video-games/pokemon-sword-and-pokemon-shield/

Reusable structures:

- travel can contain voluntary pauses that reveal Pokémon behavior without becoming a quest;
- temporary social spaces can exist almost anywhere when terrain/world state permits;
- repeated casual activities can build memory and characterization even when nothing dramatic occurs;
- different Pokémon can display different observable preferences or behavior during the same rest period;
- multiplayer downtime can be shared without requiring a formal club/event object.

Not imported:

- Camp EXP;
- Friendship increases;
- curry healing/effects;
- toy mechanics;
- exact cooking minigame rules.

Those remain governed by PTU/Caelo plus the existing Food and Pokémon Agency layers.

### Official Pokémon: Pokopia planner separates daily goals from long-term projects and memories

The official 2026 Pokopia planner distinguishes daily checklists, home/storage organization, recipes, long-term projects and a journal for favorite moments.

Source:
- https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokemon-news/tidy-up-your-pokemon-life-with-the-pokemon-pokopia-daily-planner

## Cross-source conclusions

### Downtime is a pacing mode, not a rules shortcut

Ouros should track when a character is intentionally spending discretionary time, but narrative downtime cannot automatically heal, train, craft, teach Moves, increase Friendship/Loyalty, hatch Eggs, generate income or improve stats.

Those effects belong to their authoritative subsystem and only occur when its rules validate them.

### Routine and event are different

A routine is a repeatable pattern such as:

- evening walk;
- morning coffee;
- weekly market visit;
- sketching at a lookout;
- checking a personal garden;
- casual training game;
- tending an aquarium;
- visiting a favorite bench;
- playing cards with coworkers;
- reading in a library;
- photographing a neighborhood Pokémon.

A routine becomes a playable scene only when something meaningfully differs, a player explicitly chooses to focus on it, or it intersects another active world state.

### Hobbies need no productivity justification

A hobby can exist because the character enjoys doing it. The generator should not force every hobby to become:

- a profession;
- a business;
- a competition;
- a club;
- a mastery track;
- a source of combat bonuses.

If a hobby later becomes any of those, the transition must be caused by explicit choices and state.

### Personal projects should have bounded state

Long-running projects benefit from visible steps and provenance. They should not require repetitive click-work.

Examples:

- decorating a room;
- restoring an old bicycle;
- completing a regional sketchbook;
- building a model of a famous ruin;
- cataloguing family photographs;
- maintaining a small noncommercial garden;
- learning local recipes;
- creating a personal trail map;
- assembling a collection;
- writing a field journal.

Completion can produce memory, objects, presentation state or social opportunities without automatically granting PTU progression.

### Absence from a routine is information only after a baseline exists

If an NPC always fishes at dawn and does not appear one morning, that difference can be observed.

The system must not jump directly to danger, illness, death or criminal activity. Work schedule, weather, travel, private plans or changed preference may explain it.

### Pokémon participation must remain individual

A Pokémon joining a picnic, game, walk or hobby session does not automatically mean it likes the activity, gains Friendship or becomes more obedient. Observable participation and behavior can be recorded. Private/internal interpretation remains separate.

### Quiet history should feed later callbacks

Small activities can generate low-weight Chronicle facts such as:

- first time two characters cooked together;
- a Pokémon repeatedly choosing the same resting spot;
- a room gradually filling with collected postcards;
- a hobby project completed after several months;
- a resident teaching a non-mechanical craft technique;
- a regular route becoming personally significant.

These facts can support callbacks without forcing every future quest to reference them.
