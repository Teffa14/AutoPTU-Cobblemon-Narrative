# Interregional Mobility, Recognition & Exchange Research — Pass 32

Status: research/provenance only. Nothing in this file is established Ouros canon.

## Why this pass

The repository already models routes, transport, expeditions, academies, cases, governance, conservation, public memory, battle institutions, media, workplaces and housing. It does not yet have a dedicated model for what happens when people, Pokémon, institutions, records and obligations cross from one region or jurisdictional area into another.

This pass focuses on:

- visiting Trainers and temporary residents;
- interregional travel progression;
- institutional exchange and reciprocal recognition;
- competitions that attract outsiders;
- cross-region research and field programs;
- recognition of prior records without assuming universal equivalence;
- service/jurisdiction handoffs;
- cultural orientation and host-region knowledge;
- temporary surges of visitors;
- interregional continuity without assuming nation-states, passports or modern customs law.

## Source scan

### 1. Pokémon Scarlet/Violet — exchange and field-trip structure

Official Pokémon material for The Hidden Treasure of Area Zero explicitly separates two kinds of away-from-home learning: a field trip to Kitakami and study abroad at Blueberry Academy in Unova. Later official coverage again describes the player as studying abroad at Blueberry Academy.

Sources:
- https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokemon-news/updates-from-the-pokemon-day-2023-pokemon-presents
- https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokemon-news/revisit-paldea-kitakami-and-blueberry-academy-on-nintendo-switch-2

Reusable structural lessons:
- temporary presence does not require permanent transfer;
- a host institution can grant limited access without replacing the home institution;
- travel can expose a character to different local Pokémon, institutions, customs and opportunities;
- home and host records can remain distinct;
- an exchange arc can connect education, housing, travel, clubs, research and public events without creating a new universal progression system.

Copyright boundary:
Do not copy Kitakami, Blueberry Academy, characters, dialogue, quests or Terastal story content. Use only the abstract exchange/host-region structure.

### 2. Competitive events as temporary interregional convergence

Official Play! Pokémon coverage describes International Championships and World Championships as multinational/global events that bring Trainers from many countries/regions together at one host location. The 2026 North America International Championships specifically describes a multinational stage, and the World Championships repeatedly describe Trainers from across the globe converging in one place.

Sources:
- https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokemon-news/watch-the-2026-north-america-international-championships-live
- https://www.pokemon.com/us/features/watch-the-very-best-compete-to-become-champion
- https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokemon-news/2024-pokemon-world-championships-pikachu-travelogue

Reusable structural lessons:
- large events can temporarily change a host settlement's population and service demand;
- visitors may arrive with different competitive histories and institutional recognition;
- event participation and local residency are separate states;
- temporary visitor influx can affect transport, housing, media, markets, clinics, security and public memory;
- formal results can become portable records while local reputation may not transfer cleanly.

Do not import real Play! Pokémon tournament rules, country codes, ranking math or event logistics as Ouros rules.

### 3. Alola as a host region for visitors

Official Pokémon material frames Alola as a place characters visit and explore, while animation episodes explicitly show visitors from another region being shown around local places and activities.

Sources:
- https://www.pokemon.com/us/strategy/the-first-timers-guide-to-the-alola-region
- https://www.pokemon.com/us/animation/seasons/22/episode-10-alola-alola

Reusable structural lessons:
- a visitor may know Pokémon battling but still lack local route, etiquette or ecological knowledge;
- hosting another character can generate social/exploration content without requiring a crisis;
- orientation can be player-driven: locals can become guides, interpreters, hosts or connectors;
- being an outsider should create information asymmetry, not automatic incompetence.

### 4. Public Pokémon RP — interregional progression gates

The Pokémon Reddit Roleplay rules historically separated early-game regional play from later interregional travel. The same framework expected characters seeking a complete team to eventually travel across regions.

Source:
- https://pokemon-reddit-roleplay.fandom.com/wiki/The_Essentials_for_the_Pok%C3%A9mon_Reddit_Roleplay

Reusable structural lessons:
- interregional access can be a distinct progression dimension;
- a campaign can preserve strong regional identity even in a shared world;
- travel eligibility should be explicit rather than assumed;
- access restrictions should be visible to players instead of discovered only after content generation.

Do not import its admin rules, rarity rules or progression requirements.

### 5. Public RP — visitors arriving for different institutional reasons

A public PokéFarm RP for the Rorie Islands distinguishes experienced League visitors from Trainers arriving through a research program, with different assumptions about why they came and what they bring.

Source:
- https://pokefarm.com/forum/thread/430178/Welcome-to-the-Rorie-Islands-setup-open

Reusable structural lessons:
- visitor purpose can matter more than a generic tourist/resident flag;
- the same destination can support competitor, researcher, student, worker, family guest or expedition visitor arcs;
- host institutions may set different scopes of activity for different visitors;
- prior regional history can remain relevant without automatically granting local authority.

Do not copy the Rorie setting, exact restrictions, characters or Pokémon availability rules.

### 6. PokeMMO — explicit multi-region transition design

PokeMMO's support documentation uses explicit port-based region travel and progression gating. It also separates progression state by region instead of treating all prior accomplishments as universally portable.

Source:
- https://support.pokemmo.com/knowledgebase/article/traveling-system

Reusable structural lessons:
- regional progression can remain separate after interregional travel;
- arrival points matter and can become travel hubs;
- returning to a known region and entering a new region are different transitions;
- not every credential or progression artifact should automatically apply everywhere.

Do not import the four-badge requirement, level caps, party boxing or teleport behavior.

### 7. Multi-region fan projects

Pokémon Crossroads connects several canonical regions into one adventure, while Pokéngine projects are presented as many community-built regions inside one larger world. Pokémon Itinerant begins with a vacation that strands the player in a foreign region.

Sources:
- https://github.com/eonlynx/pokecrossroads
- https://www.eeveeexpo.com/expo-news/
- https://eeveeexpo.com/threads/5480/

Reusable structural lessons:
- multiple regions need explicit continuity rules or they collapse into one undifferentiated map;
- arrival context can define an arc before any villain appears;
- ports, ferries, exchange programs and shared institutions are natural connective tissue;
- a player can retain history from another region while still encountering unfamiliar local systems.

Do not copy plots, regions, characters or progression mechanics.

### 8. Pokémon Adventures — regional arcs and continuity

Pokémon Adventures generally organizes major stories around distinct regions and protagonists while allowing time and continuity to persist across arcs. This is useful at a structural level for Ouros: regional stories can remain self-contained while old actors, institutions or consequences later cross boundaries.

Sources:
- https://www.viz.com/blog/posts/pokemon-digital-manga-release-guide
- https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Pok%C3%A9mon_Adventures

Reusable structural lesson:
Regional identity does not require narrative isolation. Cross-region callbacks become more meaningful when each place first develops its own institutions, history and conflicts.

## PTU/Caelo grounding

The supplied Caelo material already treats access as explicit world state. The Player's Guide states that some locations require the correct items/levels and describes travel between islands through available transport/Pokémon movement options. Caelo also distinguishes activity types such as Social, Encounter, Job, Raid, Contest, Gym and Dojo.

For Ouros this implies:
- interregional arrival must not bypass location-specific access rules;
- being admitted to one institution does not grant unrelated regional authority;
- travel capability must be checked against the authoritative PTU/Caelo and engine state rather than narrative flavor;
- host-region activity can reuse existing activity containers instead of inventing a separate quest system.

No passport, visa, customs, citizenship, immigration, quarantine, extradition or border-policing rule is established by the supplied PTU/Caelo corpus. Those concepts must remain absent or explicitly authored if Ouros later needs them.

## Design conclusions

### Region is not automatically a sovereign state

Ouros should model a `region` as a geographic/cultural/gameplay container first. Governance, League jurisdiction, conservation jurisdiction, transport networks and institutional service areas may overlap without being identical.

Never infer:
- national borders;
- citizenship;
- passports;
- immigration law;
- customs duties;
- criminal extradition;
- universal border guards.

### Visitor purpose should be explicit

Possible visitor purposes include:
- tourism;
- competition;
- education/exchange;
- research;
- employment/contract work;
- family/social visit;
- expedition;
- medical referral;
- performance;
- crisis response;
- institutional delegation.

These are narrative purposes, not legal statuses unless canon says otherwise.

### Recognition must be scoped

A record can be:
- recognized fully;
- recognized for limited purposes;
- recognized provisionally;
- not recognized;
- pending verification;
- irrelevant to the host institution.

A respected battler elsewhere may still need local route knowledge. A medical referral may be accepted without recognizing an unrelated academic credential. A Badge should not automatically grant travel rights unless Ouros canon explicitly establishes that rule.

### Regional reputation should not become universal omniscience

Portable facts:
- published competition results;
- public institutional records;
- player-authored identity information;
- widely distributed media reports.

Nonportable by default:
- private NPC trust;
- local rumors that never left the region;
- hidden case evidence;
- private academic records;
- clinic records;
- secret faction knowledge.

### Interregional events are world-state multipliers

A major tournament, festival, research congress or exchange week can temporarily increase:
- transport load;
- lodging demand;
- clinic demand;
- media coverage;
- vendor activity;
- security workload;
- translation needs;
- visitor guidance;
- opportunities for local NPCs.

That creates cross-layer content without requiring a villain.

## Candidate mechanics/implementation boundaries

Most interregional state is overworld/database state and does not need battle-engine support.

Mechanically rich arrival or transfer encounters may depend on:
- complete movement/interception/forced movement if escort/crowd control enters the grid;
- terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions if a port or crossing has active hazards;
- Trainer Features/perks if formal authority or Feature-based actions matter;
- AI tactical policy for objective-aware opponents;
- Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback for crowds, transport, checkpoints and persistent access state.

Do not implement passport checks, contraband rules, tariffs or border-search mechanics unless Ouros canon deliberately establishes them.
