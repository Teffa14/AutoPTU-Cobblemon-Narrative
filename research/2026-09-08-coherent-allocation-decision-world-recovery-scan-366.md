## Reusable Ouros structures

A scarce instrument can create a historical conflict without remaining scarce forever. A scheduler's authority can matter as evidence rather than exposition. A decision may exist before its downstream application. Different NPCs can rationally know different portions of the chain. A restart should preserve conflict, authority and decision without retroactively applying Pass 349 or granting knowledge to uninformed actors.

This creates useful mysteries at small scale: why one team changed route, why a booking still appears active after someone chose another window, whether the decision was authorized when made, and whether a later operational failure came from the decision itself or from failure to apply or communicate it.
