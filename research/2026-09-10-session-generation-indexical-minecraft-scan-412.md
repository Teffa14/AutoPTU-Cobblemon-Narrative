## Source 1 — Clara Fernández-Vara, “Game Spaces Speak Volumes: Indexical Storytelling”

Source: https://dl.digra.org/index.php/dl/article/view/512
Published: 2011.

The paper describes indexical storytelling as environmental information whose interpretation matters to successful traversal and play, rather than environment used only as background exposition.

Reusable Ouros structures:

- physical traces can be evidence of prior actions without being an omniscient explanation;
- reading a place can change a route, hypothesis or next investigation step;
- multiple traces can establish sequence without establishing motive;
- player interpretation should remain distinguishable from world fact and NPC knowledge.

No example scene, prose, layout or specific game event from the paper is imported.
