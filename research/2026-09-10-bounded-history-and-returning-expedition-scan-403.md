## Source 1 — Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: The Bright Future

Source:
https://hacks.skytemple.org/h/pmd_the_bright_future

Retrieved: 2026-09-10

Publicly described structure:

The finished fan story is set many years after the events of Explorers of Sky. Its premise explicitly revisits a world whose present condition exists because an earlier adventure changed the timeline. The public FAQ also distinguishes narrative implications from implemented mechanics: a concept can matter to the story even when the corresponding gameplay transformation is not available to the player.

Reusable Ouros lessons:

A location can remain narratively legible across long intervals while its social, ecological or infrastructural context changes.

A successful earlier intervention can become the cause of later conditions rather than closing a place permanently.

Returning characters do not need perfect knowledge of the changed world. Familiarity with the old state can create useful but incomplete expectations.

A story concept may exist above the tactical layer without authorizing an unimplemented battle mechanic.

Not imported:

Characters, dialogue, guild identity, dungeon layouts, bosses, time-travel plot, species roles, custom assets or the hack's specific story.

## Source 2 — Pokémon Mystery Dungeon: Explorers of the Spirit

Sources:
https://hacks.skytemple.org/h/spirit
https://hacks.skytemple.org/jam/hoty22

Retrieved: 2026-09-10

Publicly described structure:

The project advertises redesigned dungeons and a large character cast. Public feature material also describes occasional guest companions and states a design rule that those guests should create new dungeon-design possibilities rather than function as a hindrance.

Reusable Ouros lessons:

A temporary expedition specialist can alter the available interpretation, route or objective set without becoming an escort burden by default.

A return expedition can use the same broad location with different objectives, access assumptions or environmental reading.

Companion-specific content should expose additional choices and information. It should not silently become a punitive protection mechanic unless that is an explicit encounter premise.

Not imported:

Characters, dialogue, dungeon maps, enemy placement, guest identities, awards, plot, mechanics or bespoke assets.

## Transformation into Ouros material

The combined pattern supports a return-expedition structure:

An old route was previously stabilized, surveyed or made usable. Enough time passes for residents, infrastructure and Pokémon activity to adapt around that successful intervention. A new expedition returns for a different reason. A specialist who knows the older condition can accompany the team and point out differences, but the specialist is not an automatic escort liability.

The strongest mystery comes from divergence between two valid states of the same place. Old evidence can be accurate for its own semantic cut while current evidence is also accurate now.

This structure fits the existing persistent-world contracts because it rewards provenance, dated observations, non-omniscient memory and world revision without requiring retroactive rewriting.
