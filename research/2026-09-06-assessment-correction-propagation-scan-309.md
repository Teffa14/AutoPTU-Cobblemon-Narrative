# Assessment correction propagation research scan — pass 309

Status: RESEARCH / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-06

## Question

How should Ouros handle a later correction when some NPCs acted on an older conclusion and others never receive the revision?

## New public sources

1. Westbrook, Wegener & Susmann, “Mechanisms in continued influence: The impact of misinformation corrections on source perceptions” (Memory & Cognition, 2023). https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36988856/
   - Reusable structure: a correction can change both belief in the disputed information and perception of the original source.
   - Ouros transformation: a later assessment is a new information event. It may alter belief or relationship state only for actors who actually receive and process it. No human-effect size becomes a rule.

2. Buczel et al., “How do forewarnings and post-warnings affect misinformation reliance?” (Memory & Cognition, 2024). https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38261249/
   - Reusable structure: remembering a correction does not guarantee that the earlier information stops influencing later reasoning.
   - Ouros transformation: receiving a superseding assessment does not erase the old claim or its historical consequences. Replanning must consume current evidence without deleting provenance.

3. Ecker et al., “Correcting false information in memory” (2011). https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21359617/
   - Reusable structure: correction strength and prior encoding matter, while corrections can still leave residual influence.
   - Ouros transformation: correction delivery is explicit and source/provenance-aware. No universal automatic belief reset.

4. Pokémon Tabletop community discussion, “PTU, but as Pokémon?” (2023). https://www.reddit.com/r/PokemonTabletop/comments/16p1d59
   - Reusable structure: a campaign mystery can emerge from an unusual persistent capability or behavior of a Pokémon and be investigated over time.
   - Ouros transformation: recurring anomalies may seed inquiry threads that persist across ordinary play. Community homebrew is not mechanical authority.

5. Pokémon Gaia overview. https://pokehackdb.com/hacks/pokemon-gaia
   - Reusable structure: archaeological mystery progresses alongside ordinary regional travel, with climbing, underwater exploration and revisiting sites as the player gains context and access.
   - Ouros transformation: evidence correction can motivate a return to an old site with a different interpretive frame. No Gaia locations, factions, plot, dialogue or puzzles are copied.

## Design lessons adopted

- A correction is an event with its own sender, recipients, channel, time and provenance.
- A later conclusion must not mutate the historical conclusion it supersedes.
- The audience of the correction is not inferred from the audience of the earlier claim.
- A failed or deferred delivery leaves the receiver legitimately outdated.
- Receiving the correction does not itself reverse every action, accusation or relationship effect caused by the earlier belief.
- Reopened investigations benefit from revisiting old locations after the meaning of evidence changes.

## Authority boundary

This scan changes no PTU, Caelo, Kairos, Cobblemon or AutoPTU rule. It defines information-flow and narrative patterns only. Any Skill check, Move, Ability, Item, Trainer Feature, hazard, travel or combat behavior must still be validated against project-authoritative material.
