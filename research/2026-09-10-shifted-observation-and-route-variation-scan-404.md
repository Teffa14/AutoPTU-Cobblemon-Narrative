## Combined transformation

The combined reusable structure is a two-watch investigation.

A route or site is surveyed during one time window and appears ordinary or only partly explains a reported problem. A second watch at another semantic time reveals a different traffic pattern, Pokémon presence, environmental cue, maintenance behavior or social routine. The two observations are both valid for their own cuts.

This supports an Ouros assignment where the central challenge is deciding what must be observed again, what information should be handed between shifts and which discrepancy actually deserves escalation.

The world-agent system can execute either watch off-screen. Evidence remains with the actors who observed it until communicated. Missing observation remains missing rather than being reconstructed from the later state.
