# Residential, Household & Relocation Continuity Research — Pass 101

Status: RESEARCH / PROVENANCE ONLY. Nothing in this file is established Ouros canon.
Date: 2026-08-28
Narrative baseline inspected before writing: `545dc15b075b1ea2b018aa86355d2e25dff7d934`.

## Gap selected after repository inspection

The repository already contains settlement capability, hospitality/lodging, crisis/recovery, accessibility, finance, commercial services, public works, care, shelter/sanctuary and maintenance layers. `observation-settlement-time-layer.md` has a coarse settlement `housing` capability, while Pass 100 handles temporary guest stays. No dedicated layer was found for persistent residences, household membership, permanent or temporary relocation, home repair displacement, return-to-home, vacancy, abandoned housing, or reuse of former residences.

The proposed Pass 101 scope is therefore narrow: preserve the continuity of a home and the people/Pokémon explicitly associated with it without inventing property law, tenancy law, family relationships, ownership, wealth or rest mechanics.

## New external sources

### Pokémon Sun / Moon — player household relocation from Kanto to Alola

Sources:
- Bulbapedia, Mom (Alola): https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Mom_%28Alola%29
- Bulbapedia, Pokémon Sun and Moon: https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Sun_and_Moon
- Bulbapedia, Alola Route 1: https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Alola_Route_1

Reusable structure:
- relocation is a process with a previous home, a new address, registration/administrative consequences, a household that may include Pokémon, and a later settled routine;
- moving region does not erase connections to the previous region;
- a home becomes a persistent callback location rather than a one-scene arrival prop.

Transformation for Ouros:
- preserve origin residence, destination residence, relocation event, effective move date, explicit household-member links and external-contact continuity separately;
- do not infer ownership from occupancy;
- do not infer family relation merely because actors share a residence;
- do not create a universal address-registration system unless Ouros canon establishes one.

### “So Long, Sophocles!” — temporary relocation during home repair

Source:
- Bulbapedia, SM026: https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/SM026

Reusable structure:
- residents may believe a move is permanent before understanding its actual scope;
- a household can relocate temporarily while its ordinary home is repaired;
- social consequences begin before the move occurs because people react to the information they currently possess;
- repair completion and return are separate events.

Transformation for Ouros:
- distinguish announced move, understood move, temporary accommodation, repair work, verification and actual return;
- preserve the incorrect earlier understanding as actor knowledge/history without making it world truth.

### “A Corsola Caper!” — residence depends on environmental context

Source:
- Bulbapedia, EP213: https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/EP213

Reusable structure:
- a home can become unsafe because its physical viability depends on local ecology;
- rescue, structural safety, ecological interpretation and later housing decisions are separate problems;
- rebuilding the same structure without understanding the dependency can reproduce the failure.

Transformation for Ouros:
- residential habitability may depend on authoritative facility, geology, ecology, water or public-safety state;
- Pokémon presence near or under a structure is evidence to investigate, not automatic proof of causation;
- no species is converted into a structural component or construction rule by narrative inference.

### Pokémon Rejuvenation — abandoned town and partial residential return

Source:
- Rejuvenation Wiki, Oblitus Town: https://rejuvenation.wiki.gg/wiki/Oblitus_Town

Reusable structure:
- a settlement may retain abandoned homes after population loss;
- restoration can be partial rather than resetting the location to a full previous state;
- former homes, repaired homes and newly built homes can coexist and visibly encode history.

Transformation for Ouros:
- preserve vacancy, abandonment, repair, reuse and new construction as separate residence histories;
- a restored settlement need not recover its old population, layout or social structure;
- abandoned buildings do not become free player property or loot by default.

### PTU community / tabletop campaign practice — home base value comes from people and continuity

Source:
- r/PokemonTabletop campaign anniversary example: https://www.reddit.com/r/PokemonTabletop/comments/y4nuaf
- broader tabletop home-base discussion used only as general design comparison: https://www.reddit.com/r/rpg/comments/ylxedc/have_you_ever_gotten_players_invested_in_a_home/

Reusable lesson:
- persistent places become emotionally legible through recurring people, routines and consequences, not through an abstract upgrade score alone;
- a base should be optional and emerge from player/world continuity rather than being forced as a mandatory ownership reward.

Ouros transformation:
- player residences may exist when explicitly established, but ordinary NPC homes are equally important;
- avoid turning every residence into a purchasable base, upgrade tree or defense encounter;
- preserve recurring neighbors, service routes, household routines and visible repairs as callbacks.

## PTU/Caelo cross-check

The project-supplied source inventory includes PTU Core, Pokédex material, Caelo Player's Guide, Caelo rulebook/errata, character-creation material and the Caelo Region Location & Encounter List. Existing repository rules already require exact mechanical validation against that source set.

No governing evidence inspected for Pass 101 establishes universal rules for:
- property ownership;
- rent or leases;
- mortgages;
- household membership;
- residential capacity;
- eviction;
- inheritance of real property;
- zoning;
- building-code checks;
- domestic labor bonuses;
- a home granting PTU recovery by itself.

PTU Rest/Extended Rest remains the relevant recovery boundary already documented in Pass 100. Residence state must not execute recovery merely because a character is at home or used a Minecraft bed.

## Design lessons extracted

1. Residence identity should persist through repair, vacancy and resident turnover.
2. Occupancy, ownership, custody, permission and household membership are different facts.
3. Temporary relocation must not silently rewrite permanent residence.
4. Repair completion must be followed by habitability/access verification before return.
5. Household knowledge can be wrong or outdated without changing world truth.
6. Home disruption should propagate into travel, work, school, care and neighborhood routines.
7. Returning home can be a meaningful state transition without being a reward screen.
8. Abandoned residences retain provenance and prior-use history.
9. Pokémon sharing a home do not automatically become owned, dependents, workers or combatants.
10. A residence should become tactically active only after Ouros explicitly composes an encounter.

## Explicitly rejected imports

Do not copy:
- the Alola protagonist family as Ouros characters;
- Sophocles's exact misunderstanding or farewell sequence;
- the Corsola episode's specific family, rescue sequence or biological premise;
- Oblitus Town's exact history, residents, Gym state or restoration rewards;
- any tabletop campaign's NPCs, setting, property reward or base-upgrade mechanics.

## Candidate Ouros uses

- a household temporarily relocated during repairs returns in stages;
- an abandoned lane regains only some residents after infrastructure restoration;
- a recurring neighbor notices a route or wildlife change before institutions do;
- a former residence becomes a workshop, clinic annex, community room or habitat buffer with provenance intact;
- a disputed address record creates a mystery without inventing fraud;
- a household splits between two locations during a crisis and later reunifies;
- a residence remains habitable while one access route is blocked;
- a home is physically repaired but cannot yet be reoccupied because another dependency remains unresolved.

All of these remain proposed until canon review.
