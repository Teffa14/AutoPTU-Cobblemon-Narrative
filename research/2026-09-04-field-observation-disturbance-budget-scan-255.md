# Field observation disturbance budget scan — Pass 255

Status: RESEARCH / PROVENANCE
Canon effect: NONE
Date: 2026-09-04

## Question

How should Ouros let players and NPCs study recurring wild Pokemon without making observation a free, consequence-less information action or silently turning research activity into demographic truth?

## New public evidence

USGS, Reintsma et al. (2018), "Preliminary evaluation of behavioral response of nesting waterbirds to small unmanned aircraft flight": remote aerial survey produced much shorter exposure and less flushing than physically entering colonies in the studied cases. Reusable lesson: observation methods can differ in disturbance cost even when both produce useful evidence.
https://www.usgs.gov/publications/preliminary-evaluation-behavioral-response-nesting-waterbirds-small-unmanned-aircraft

USGS, Meixell & Flint (2017), "Effects of industrial and investigator disturbance on Arctic-nesting geese": direct investigator nest visits produced longer incubation breaks and measurable effects, while indirect vehicle/aircraft disturbance was less severe in the studied context. Reusable lesson: the observer can become a causal ecological input and must be modeled separately from the thing being measured.
https://www.usgs.gov/publications/effects-industrial-and-investigator-disturbance-arctic-nesting-geese

USGS Northern Prairie Wildlife Research Center, "Improving monitoring techniques for nests of interior least terns and piping plovers": more frequent visits can improve detection while potentially altering attendance/behavior. Reusable lesson: evidence quality and ecological impact are separate axes; better data can cost more disturbance.
https://www.usgs.gov/centers/northern-prairie-wildlife-research-center/science/improving-monitoring-techniques-nests

USGS Bird Banding Laboratory, Bander's Code of Ethics and permit guidance: handling/marking should minimize time and stress, serve a valid scientific purpose, use appropriate methods, and require authorization. Reusable lesson: a physical-marking workflow needs an explicit authority/welfare gate and cannot be an ordinary player interaction.
https://www.usgs.gov/labs/bird-banding-laboratory/banders-code-ethics
https://www.usgs.gov/labs/bird-banding-laboratory/science/general-permit-information

Pokemon Tabletop community, "How do you plan your wild encounters?" (2020): GMs recommend environmental evidence, territorial markings and Skill use before direct encounters, with terrain prepared around wild behavior. Reusable lesson: investigation can produce meaningful play before combat and does not require spawning a target immediately.
https://www.reddit.com/r/PokemonTabletop/comments/jivcud/

Pokemon Tabletop community, "Any tips for a future GM?" (2022): advice emphasizes narrative reasons for wild encounters rather than making every route a sequence of random fights. Reusable lesson: consequences and context should determine when observation becomes encounter pressure.
https://www.reddit.com/r/PokemonTabletop/comments/xzkco3/

PTU Skills reference: Survival explicitly supports scouting, ecology information and tracking. This supports gathering evidence but does not itself authorize Ouros to expose hidden population identity or make demographic writes.
https://pturpg.wikidot.com/skills

## Transformed Ouros lessons

1. Observation has two outputs that must not be conflated: evidence gained and disturbance caused.
2. Passive or remote evidence may have low disturbance but lower individual-resolution quality.
3. Close approach can improve marker/photo/behavior evidence while increasing local disturbance risk.
4. Repeated visits can accumulate pressure even if no single visit causes a demographic event.
5. Disturbance pressure is behavioral/ecological state. It cannot directly mean mortality, emigration, population loss or combat initiation.
6. Researcher-caused disturbance must have provenance. A later projection change should be explainable by specific field actions rather than an unexplained pressure delta.
7. Physical handling/marking requires a separate authorization and welfare contract. Pass 254's fixture-only marker remains non-canon.
8. Species, nesting state, temperament, habitat and current stress can change sensitivity. Fixture thresholds cannot become universal species constants.

## PTU/Caelo boundary

PTU Survival can support scouting/tracking and Perception-like observation quality where existing project rules allow it. It does not grant omniscience, persistent actor IDs, population totals or permission to manipulate wildlife.

No new Caelo rule is asserted by this research note. Any setting institution, field permit, marker programme, ethics board, researcher NPC or protected-zone rule remains PROPOSED until approved in canon.

## Proposed implementation consequence

Add an Ouros observation-impact ledger. Each deliberate field observation can declare a method class, evidence-quality result, disturbance impact and provenance root. Only an explicit accepted impact event may write disturbance pressure. Projection can then consume that pressure through the existing Pass 248 envelope.

A recovery event may lower pressure after a quiet interval or other canon-approved condition. Recovery is not population recruitment and should use hysteresis where world-event logic needs it.
