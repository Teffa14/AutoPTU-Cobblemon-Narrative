## Public ecology sources

Romero et al. 2015, Biological Reviews, meta-analysis of 122 studies: animal ecosystem engineers showed an overall positive effect on species richness, with habitat-creating engineers producing stronger effects than simple habitat modification or bioturbation. Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/brv.12138

Fairfax & Westbrook 2024, Annual Review of Ecology, Evolution, and Systematics: beavers rapidly transform landscapes by dams, burrows, canals and tree cutting; effects include altered hydrology, sediment/nutrient retention, habitat mosaics, climate buffering and persistence of engineered wetlands. Source: https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-ecolsys-102722-122317

Stringer & Gaywood 2016, Mammal Review: beaver-created ponds, dead wood, woody debris, graded edges and successional stages increase habitat heterogeneity, while some habitats can also experience negative effects. Source: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/mam.12068

USGS, Tape et al. 2018: beaver colonization can create new pond complexes and alter permafrost, stream temperature and freshwater/riparian habitat. Source: https://www.usgs.gov/publications/tundra-be-dammed-beaver-colonization-arctic

Reusable ecological lessons:

- an engineer population changes more than its own abundance;
- physical structures can persist after the builders leave;
- abandonment can begin a new successional stage rather than reset the patch;
- engineering can benefit some populations and disadvantage others at the same time;
- engineering effects operate at different time scales: immediate obstruction, seasonal water/resource change, multi-year vegetation succession;
- habitat heterogeneity is a useful world output, not a universal biodiversity bonus.

## Pokémon species evidence

Bulbapedia's Bibarel biology and Pokédex aggregation records official entries describing Bibarel building nests by damming streams using bark and mud, with additional biology summary describing bark, roots and branches. Source: https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Bibarel_(Pok%C3%A9mon)

Bulbapedia's Diglett biology aggregates official entries describing shallow tunneling that leaves soil tilled; farmers may value Diglett because its activity tills fields and enriches soil. Source: https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Diglett

These sources support species-level candidate tendencies. They do not establish that Bibarel or Diglett exist in Marea, that every individual performs the behavior, or that Minecraft block edits should occur automatically.

## Fan-game / campaign structure scan

A public PTU campaign log on r/PokemonTabletop shows a useful recurring pattern: ordinary wild encounters, player treatment of wild Pokémon, local actors and later consequences become connected rather than isolated combat rooms. In one session, player aggression toward wild Pokémon became information explaining hostility later in the same area. Source: https://www.reddit.com/r/PokemonTabletop/comments/noo9kz

Pokemon Burning Scales publicly describes a small open world focused on exploration, recurring characters and numerous side quests rather than a linear badge corridor. Source: https://github.com/Benitex/Pokemon-Burning-Scales

Pokémon Odyssey publicly describes an exploration-first structure using gathering/mining points, naval exploration, sidequests, shop improvement and enemy encounters rather than the standard Gym/Evil-Team/League spine. Source: https://whackahack.com/juegos/pokemon-odyssey/

Reusable narrative lessons:

- ecological structures should recur across quests instead of being consumed after one objective;
- the same altered patch can support research, traversal, local dispute, rescue, resource gathering and later battle content;
- player or faction treatment of the habitat can become later evidence and social memory;
- a small number of persistent locations can carry more narrative weight than many disposable routes.

## Design conclusion

Ouros should represent ecological engineering as a persistent world relation with three separate layers:

1. builder behavior and population pressure;
2. engineered structure/state with lifecycle and provenance;
3. downstream habitat/resource/population consequences.

The structure must survive ordinary chunk unload and can survive builder departure when the structure physically persists. Minecraft projects the approved semantic state; it does not infer canonical ecology from arbitrary block changes.
