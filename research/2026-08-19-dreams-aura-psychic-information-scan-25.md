# Research Scan — Dreams, Aura, Psychic Information & Subjective Reality — Pass 25

Status: research/provenance only. Not Ouros canon.

Date: 2026-08-19

## Why this pass

The existing repository already has strong layers for public information, actor knowledge, cases/evidence, myth, archaeology, media, memory, NPC agency and science. A gap remains around information that is perceived through supernatural or subjective channels: dreams, Aura, telepathy, psychic residue, hypnosis, visions and similar experiences.

This needs its own boundary because subjective access to information can otherwise accidentally become omniscience. A dream can be meaningful without being literally true. An Aura reading can reveal only what its governing PTU capability actually permits. A telepathic message can be genuine while still being mistaken, deceptive or incomplete. A psychic observation must not silently bypass privacy, Mindlock, evidence standards or player consent.

## Source 1 — Pokémon Tabletop campaign seed: Midnight City

Source: Pokémon Tabletop RPG, “Campaign Seeds: Mysterious Ruins”
https://pokemontabletop.com/campaign-seeds-mysterious-ruins/

The Midnight City seed uses a Dream World that contains warped versions of real places and memories. Its strongest reusable lesson is epistemic rather than aesthetic: dream exploration can expose information about a community while forcing players to distinguish fact from interpretation.

Reusable structures:

- a parallel subjective space can reflect real history without being a reliable historical record;
- dream locations can combine multiple people’s memories and therefore contain contradictions;
- information discovered in dreams can generate real-world investigation rather than directly resolve it;
- dream access can create a secret community of specialists without making them universally authoritative;
- local crises can be solved through dream-derived leads without escalating every dream story into a world-ending plot;
- normal daily life and dream exploration can alternate, giving the campaign two linked activity loops.

Copyright boundary: do not reuse Midnight City, its specific premise, its named location or its example plots. Only the abstract separation between subjective dream evidence and external verification is retained.

## Source 2 — Official Pokémon: Munna and Musharna

Sources:
https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokedex/munna
https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokedex/musharna

Official Pokédex material associates Munna and Musharna with dreams and dream mist. This supports a Pokémon world where dreams can leave observable external traces without implying that every dream is prophetic or mechanically actionable.

Reusable structures:

- dream-related Pokémon may create physical evidence adjacent to an otherwise private experience;
- color, form or behavior of a residue can be observable while interpretation remains uncertain;
- a researcher can study a dream phenomenon without reading the dreamer’s entire mind;
- dream ecology can exist around places where many people or Pokémon sleep.

Ouros rule: dream residue must be stored as an observation or artifact record. It cannot automatically reveal the underlying dream content unless a validated PTU/Caelo rule explicitly permits that access.

## Source 4 — Official Pokémon: Darkrai/Cresselia and nightmare incidents

Sources:

- shared symptoms do not prove a shared cause;

Ouros rule: a nightmare cluster must begin as an incident pattern, not as `Darkrai_present=true`.

## Source 6 — Official Pokémon: Aura as sensing/protection/navigation

Sources:
https://www.pokemon.com/us/animation/movies/pokemon-lucario-and-the-mystery-of-mew
https://www.pokemon.com/us/animation/seasons/12/episode-6-steeling-peace-of-mind

Official material uses Aura as more than a projectile. It can connect to sensing, locating others, resisting an external influence and cultural traditions around Aura users.

Reusable structures:

- a supernatural sense can be useful for navigation or anomaly detection without revealing every hidden fact;
- historical institutions may develop traditions around rare capabilities;
- two actors with similar supernatural access may interpret what they perceive differently;
- environmental interference can selectively affect some beings while others detect or resist it.

Ouros rule: narrative Aura use must never exceed the exact PTU/Caelo capability text available to the actor. “Aura user” is not permission for omniscience.

## Source 7 — Fan-game implementation: persistent dream space

Source: Pokémon Plastic Pyrite discussion, Eevee Expo
https://eeveeexpo.com/threads/9579/page-8
https://eeveeexpo.com/threads/9579/page-9

Public developer/player discussion describes a Dream World that connects multiple areas and remains explorable rather than functioning only as a one-time set piece. The developer explicitly discusses connecting dream sections and avoiding one-use maps.

Reusable structures:

- dream-space geography can persist and become increasingly understood;
- access can unlock from multiple real-world locations;
- returning to the same subjective location can reveal changed context rather than duplicate content;
- sequence breaking is a real design risk when dream access crosses ordinary progression boundaries.

Ouros implication: persistent dream spaces need explicit access contracts and world-state dependencies. They must not become a backdoor around route, dungeon, badge, custody or story gates unless that bypass is intentionally authored.

Copyright boundary: no names, maps, creatures, plot events or bespoke mechanics are reused.

Source: Pokémon Onirics, Eevee Expo

The public project description centers on a condition where people remain asleep and experience prolonged nightmares. The reusable lesson is that dream phenomena can become a regional service/institution problem rather than only a surreal dungeon theme.

Reusable structures:

- investigation can span medical, ecological, psychic and historical explanations;
- the player can help without immediately knowing whether the cause is supernatural, technological or biological.

Copyright boundary: do not reuse the game’s illness, region, characters or story. Retain only the multi-system consequence pattern.

## Source 9 — Public roleplay patterns

Examples:
https://forums.serebii.net/threads/pok%C3%A9mon-dreamrunners-enigma-labyrinth.535021/
https://forums.spacebattles.com/threads/pok%C3%A9mon-mystery-dungeon-heroes-of-courage-and-kindness-ic.895303/

Public Pokémon roleplay repeatedly uses shared dream spaces, group interpretation and dream-specific exploration. The reusable design lesson is that multiplayer dream content benefits from separating what each participant perceives from what the group later agrees happened.

Ouros implication:

- each participant can receive a private perception packet;
- the shared scene state should contain only facts that are actually common;
- players can voluntarily disclose their perceptions;
- the engine should never merge contradictory private experiences into a single canonical narrative automatically.

## PTU/Caelo rules grounding

The supplied PTU Core material includes relevant capabilities such as Aura Reader, Aura Pulse and Dream Reader, with Mindlock limiting some forms of mental access. The key design requirement is narrow authority: each capability grants only its written information/action scope.

The current Python AutoPTU oracle also contains concrete Trainer Feature actions for Telepath, Thought Detection, Suggestion and Psionic Analysis, including Mindlock blocking and psychic-residue state. This demonstrates that the Python rules engine already distinguishes mental sensing, suggestion binding and residue analysis rather than treating “psychic” as one universal permission.

Current Java evidence does not show this Trainer Feature family. Therefore any encounter depending on Telepath/Thought Detection/Suggestion/Psionic Analysis remains dependent on `Trainer Features/perks` and cannot be considered implemented in Java merely because lifecycle and temporary-effect infrastructure exist.

## High-value Ouros structures from this pass

### Subjective evidence packet

Store dream/psychic/aura information as typed observations with source and confidence rather than as canon facts.

Suggested categories:

- DREAM_REPORT
- DREAM_OBSERVATION
- DREAM_RESIDUE
- AURA_READING
- TELEPATHIC_MESSAGE
- THOUGHT_DETECTION_RESULT
- PSYCHIC_RESIDUE_ANALYSIS
- HYPNOTIC_EFFECT_REPORT
- VISION_REPORT
- SHARED_DREAM_EVENT

### Interpretation boundary

Every subjective packet should distinguish:

- what was perceived;
- what the observer believes it means;
- what governing capability produced it;
- whether the target consented or was aware;
- whether a blocker such as Mindlock applied;
- whether independent evidence supports the interpretation.

### Mental privacy

Psychic access is not ordinary conversation. It can create serious multiplayer trust and privacy problems. Ouros needs explicit handling for:

- PC-to-PC private thoughts;
- NPC mental access;
- involuntary sensing;
- consent-sensitive roleplay;
- information revealed to one player but not others;
- permanent storage of intimate information.

Default rule: narrative generation must not invent private thoughts for a player character. It may store player-authored thoughts only when the player explicitly supplies them for that purpose.

### Dream-world persistence

A dream space can be persistent as a world-state object while remaining subjective.

Potential state:

- connected dream anchors;
- known entrances;
- observed geometry;
- stable landmarks;
- unstable regions;
- associated dreamers;
- residue/artifacts;
- access requirements;
- historical interpretations;
- unresolved contradictions.

The dream map is not automatically a map of objective reality.
