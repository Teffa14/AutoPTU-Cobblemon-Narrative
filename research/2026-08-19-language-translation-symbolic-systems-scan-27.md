# Pass 27 — Language, translation & symbolic systems research

Status: research/provenance only. Nothing here is canon.

## Why this pass

The repository already models archaeology, myth, science, media, communications, cases, player knowledge, dreams, infiltration and dungeon structure. What it does not yet model directly is interpretation: a message may physically exist and be correctly preserved while different actors still disagree about what it means.

This pass focuses on language, scripts, ciphers, inscriptions, incomplete translations, symbolic conventions, oral transmission and puzzle readability.

## Sources and reusable lessons

### Official Pokémon — Unown and ancient writing

Pokémon's official Unown Pokédex entry explicitly connects the species to ancient writing while preserving uncertainty over whether the symbols or the Pokémon came first.

Source: https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokedex/unown

Reusable lesson: symbolic resemblance is evidence, not automatic proof of historical causation. Ouros should be able to record a resemblance between a Pokémon form and a script without promoting a speculative relationship into canon.

### Official Pokémon — Ruins of Alph

The official Johto retrospective describes the Ruins of Alph as a scientific site with chambers, puzzles and ancient drawings whose completion reveals access to deeper spaces associated with Unown.

Source: https://www.pokemon.com/us/pokemon-news/celebrate-25-years-of-pokemon-with-memorable-moments-from-the-johto-region

Reusable lesson: decipherment and spatial progression can reinforce each other. A language puzzle is strongest when solving it changes access, context or interpretation rather than merely outputting a password.

### Pokémon Black/White — Abyssal Ruins cipher

Public reference material documents the Abyssal Ruins' invented symbols, unusual reading direction, changing cipher relationship between floors and messages assembled across multiple inscriptions.

Source: https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Undersea_Ruins

Reusable lesson: a symbolic system can have several independent dimensions: glyph identity, reading order, substitution rule, numbers/logograms and context. Ouros should represent those separately so a player can understand one dimension while remaining wrong about another.

### PTU campaign seed — Mysterious Ruins

Pokémon Tabletop's public Mysterious Ruins seed explicitly treats ruin exploration as enough material to support an entire campaign rather than a single dungeon interruption.

Source: https://pokemontabletop.com/campaign-seeds-mysterious-ruins/

Reusable lesson: translation and inscription work can create recurring research loops, competing interpretations, expedition planning and long-term discoveries instead of being solved in one Skill Check.

### Pokémon fan game — Unown Ruins

Pokémon Unown Ruins is a short puzzle-focused fangame where rooms contain self-contained puzzles and can reset if the player leaves and re-enters.

Source: https://eeveeexpo.com/threads/2407/

Reusable lesson: puzzle recovery matters. A failed interaction should not permanently destroy the player's ability to reason unless the fiction explicitly makes destruction part of the stakes. Resettable or recoverable puzzle states can keep experimentation safe.

### Pokémon fan game — Ghosts of Knowledge

Ghosts of Knowledge centers exploration of a labyrinthine ruin for knowledge and treasure, with the region becoming more difficult to understand as the expedition advances.

Source: https://eeveeexpo.com/ghosts-of-knowledge/

Reusable lesson: deeper exploration can increase interpretive complexity rather than only enemy level. New discoveries can invalidate or refine the party's earlier translation model.

### Public Pokémon RP — imperfect translation as a quest engine

A Pokécharms roleplay premise begins with an imperfect translation of ancient writing, then uses that uncertainty to motivate an expedition to compare sites and test the interpretation.

Source: https://forums.pokecharms.com/threads/myth-of-the-legendary-king-prp.12100/post-234026

Reusable lesson: translation uncertainty can generate travel and comparative research. The useful pattern is not the source's plot; it is the idea that a provisional reading creates hypotheses that can be checked against another location.

### Public Pokémon RP — ruins, tourism and incomplete cultural memory

Another public RP at the Ruins of Alph and Oblivia distinguishes tourist explanations, unsafe unexplored areas, old symbols, festivals and local traditions whose original meanings are partly lost.

Sources:
- https://forums.pokecharms.com/threads/pokemon-role-play-thread.28798/
- https://forums.pokecharms.com/threads/pokemon-role-play-thread.28798/page-74

Reusable lesson: the same symbolic site can have academic, local, tourist and ritual interpretations. None needs to be automatically privileged as world truth.

### Fangame usability lesson — clue discoverability

A 2026 Eevee Expo support exchange for Pokémon Supernova shows a player unable to identify the intended clue for a multi-lever puzzle and the creator acknowledging that the clue may need to be made more obvious.

Source: https://www.eeveeexpo.com/threads/8804/page-10

Reusable lesson: difficulty should come from reasoning, not from failing to notice that a clue exists. Ouros puzzle contracts should distinguish discovery difficulty from interpretation difficulty and manipulation difficulty.

## Design conclusions

1. Preserve source text separately from translations.
2. Every translation should carry author, method, confidence and evidence.
3. Multiple translations may coexist.
4. A translated statement does not become canonical truth merely because the translation is accurate; the original author may have been wrong, deceptive or metaphorical.
5. Reading order, segmentation and symbolic category should be modeled independently.
6. Player-created notes and interpretations should remain attributable to the player who authored them.
7. Translation puzzles should support partial progress.
8. Puzzle failure should normally be recoverable unless irreversible change is the explicit narrative stake.
9. Optional expert assistance should change cost, speed or confidence rather than erase all player reasoning.
10. Language barriers should not become mandatory grind or lock basic multiplayer communication without an explicit canon decision.

## PTU/Caelo boundary

This pass does not invent language mechanics, General Education or Occult Education DCs, automatic translation Features, Unown powers, psychic translation, literacy rules, deciphering bonuses or Skill substitutions.

Any mechanical resolution must be checked against the supplied PTU Core Rulebook, Pokédex, Caelo Player's Guide, Caelo errata/editation and actual AutoPTU support.

Capabilities or supernatural tools such as Aura, telepathy, Dream-related effects or Unown-specific mechanics must use their authoritative rules if they become relevant. Narrative interpretation state cannot manufacture those capabilities.
