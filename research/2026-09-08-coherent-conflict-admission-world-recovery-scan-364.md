## Public source: The Reign of Fuhen — ongoing PTU campaign recruitment

Source: https://www.reddit.com/r/lfg/comments/1tisg6v/online_other_pokemon_tabletop_united_the_reign_of/

Public 2026 campaign material describes a long-running Pokémon Tabletop United game in a fully homebrewed region, already several sessions into play, with both combat-heavy and roleplay-heavy stretches.

Reusable structure for Ouros:

Long-running PTU play benefits when intermediate world facts survive between sessions. A schedule conflict, failed rendezvous, observed resource shortage or unresolved obligation can remain relevant several sessions later without becoming the main plot. Persistence should preserve the historical reason actors changed plans rather than forcing the GM or players to reconstruct it from memory.

Excluded: the Fuhen region, campaign characters, badge progression, encounter content and house rules.

## Public source: Pokémon Prisme

Sources:
https://www.pokemonprisme.fr/
https://pokemonprisme.com/

The public project describes a continuously expanding multi-region fangame with a quest book/story journal and a large body of main and secondary quests. Its Orange Archipelago material also emphasizes travel among many islands and support for combat, exploration and completion-oriented play.

Reusable structure for Ouros:

A large living world needs durable secondary obligations that can be left, resumed and contextualized later. The useful abstraction is a journal-quality causal chain: what was requested, what changed, what evidence existed when a decision was made, and what remains unresolved now. The current state alone is insufficient when the world contains many concurrent activities.

Excluded: Prisme characters, organizations, plots, locations, rewards, regional forms and cross-media continuity.

## Public source: Pokémon Mineral

Source: https://www.pokeharbor.com/2024/08/pokemon-mineral/

The public fangame description frames the player around a concrete occupation: cave mining, fossil recovery, quests and delivery of recovered specimens for scientific study.

Reusable structure for Ouros:

Field professions create natural reasons for shared scarce resources, bounded observation windows, sample custody and delivery obligations. These loops can create story pressure without requiring an antagonist. A conflict over one survey instrument can redirect a crew toward another site, change a collection window, or leave historical evidence that later explains why a specimen was missed.

Excluded: Roark, Professor Rowan, specific fossils, Noble Pokémon, characters, mining implementation and plot consequences.

## Transformed Ouros direction

The combined reusable pattern is a field team whose plan changes because a scarce instrument was genuinely contested at one historical moment. Later calendar cleanup may remove the visible conflict. The world should still be able to explain why the team rerouted, which actor knew about the conflict, and which evidence supported the decision at the time.

That supports a proposed expedition structure with two implementations:

Reduced implementation: semantic travel, reservations, rescheduling, communication, knowledge and admission history resolve the episode without tactical combat.

Full implementation: the rerouted team reaches an alternate observation site during a constrained environmental window. Ordinary positioning can use verified base movement. Currents, collapsing ground, forced displacement, reactive zones, weather phases or complex status pressure remain separately gated by their exact engine capability families.
