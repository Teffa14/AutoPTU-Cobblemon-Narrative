# The Conflict That Was Gone by Morning — Pass 363

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-08

A field team asks to move a pickup window for a shared survey meter. When the new window is screened, another active reservation overlaps it. The conflict is recorded and the team temporarily changes its plan.

Later, the competing reservation is cancelled for an unrelated reason. By the time the world is saved and restored, the proposed window looks clear if anyone checks only current reservations.

The durable admission record preserves the earlier fact: at the moment the team replanned, the meter really was contested. That evidence can explain why somebody delayed departure, requested another unit or chose a different survey route without inventing blame after the fact.

The current clear calendar does not erase the historical conflict. The old conflict does not automatically prove that either party had priority, behaved badly or caused the later cancellation.

Reduced implementation: semantic time, reservations, reschedule proposal, Pass 347 admission assessment, durable admission history, private knowledge and replanning. No AutoPTU dependency is required.

Rich implementation: while waiting for the equipment question to settle, the field team can take an alternate observation route. A wild interruption may make the objective reach a safe observation point, collect a non-combat reading and withdraw before a field window closes. Defeating every opponent is optional rather than the universal success condition.

Rich-version capability gates remain explicit. Ordinary targeting and base traversal may use verified audited contracts. Tactical interception, push/pull, knockback or forced displacement depend on complete movement and remain PARTIAL. Round deadlines depend on the full turn/round lifecycle and remain PARTIAL. Persistent conditions depend on status lifecycle and remain PARTIAL. Any weather, hazard, zone or reaction must be gated by its exact implemented family. Move, Ability, Item and Trainer Feature behavior is individually gated. Objective-aware retreat/rerouting depends on AI tactical policy and remains BLOCKING. Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics objective and playback support remains PARTIAL/BLOCKING end-to-end.

Canon questions deliberately left open: the actual institution that owns the meter, its allocation policy, whether Marea uses this exact equipment workflow, who can authorize priority, and whether repeated contention creates procurement or political consequences. None of those are established by this proposal.
