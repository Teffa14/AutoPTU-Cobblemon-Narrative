# The Attempt That Survived With the World — Pass 358

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-08

Premise

A field team has an accepted request and a valid authorization to collect a shared instrument. The receiver arrives at the authorized location, finds the provider absent and records the failed pickup. Before a second attempt occurs, the session ends.

When the world is restored, the missed rendezvous returns with the same semantic moment as the NPCs, their knowledge, communication queues, reservation, request and authorization history.

The player can later encounter a disagreement over what happened. The provider may remember expecting the pickup later. The receiver may possess an observation showing the counter was closed. A third party may know only that the instrument eventually changed hands. The world can expose all three positions without rewriting the earlier failure.

State structure

The historical chain can contain:

request -> acceptance -> authorization -> failed attempt -> restart -> retry -> later transfer

The failed attempt remains true after success. Success does not retroactively convert the first rendezvous into a successful pickup.

Player-facing uses

A repeated failure can motivate the player to verify the appointment, contact both parties, arrange an alternate pickup, choose another instrument, reroute an expedition or document that an environmental observation window was missed.

The first failure alone does not automatically create blame, hostility or disciplinary consequences. Those require an institution, relationship or policy owner with supporting evidence.

Reduced implementation

This version needs no AutoPTU battle. It uses semantic time, reservations, resource requests, handoff authorization, failed-attempt provenance, world checkpoint restore, communication, travel and replanning.

Rich encounter

A retry can occur while access to the pickup point is disrupted by wild Pokémon or another environmental event. The objective is to complete or safely abandon the pickup before the authorization window closes.

The narrative premise survives if this is resolved without tactical combat. A semantic travel/access interruption is the fallback.

Capability dependencies for the rich version

Targeting/footprints/range/LoS: required for exact perception and target selection around the access point.

Base movement legality: required for ordinary approach and repositioning.

Complete movement: PARTIAL/BLOCKING if interception, forced displacement, push/pull, knockback, rescue or carrier movement restrictions are used.

Core calculations: required for ordinary PTU arithmetic during battle.

Action economy/initiative: required if pickup, drop, guard or handoff become explicit tactical actions.

Full turn/round lifecycle: PARTIAL for round-bound access deadlines or delayed objective transitions.

Full stateful damage pipeline: PARTIAL if actors or the carried instrument can take battle damage.

Status lifecycle: PARTIAL for persistent conditions.

Terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions: gated by the exact mechanic selected.

Move-specific behavior, abilities, items and Trainer Features/perks: individually gated.

AI legal-action infrastructure: required for any new objective interaction action.

AI tactical policy: BLOCKING for delivery-first, protect-carrier, retry, retreat, reroute and disengage-after-objective choices.

Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback: PARTIAL/BLOCKING for end-to-end cargo/objective presentation and acknowledgement.

None. No named person, institution, place, Pokémon population or historical event from this proposal becomes Ouros canon without explicit promotion.
