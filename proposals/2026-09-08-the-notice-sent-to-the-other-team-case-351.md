# The Notice Sent to the Other Team — Pass 351

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-08

## Premise

Two field teams use the same shared instrument pool. An authorized allocation decision displaces one team's booking. The office creates the correct notice obligation but addresses the actual message to the lead of the other team.

The message is real. The delivery is real. The recipient is wrong.

## Why it fits Ouros

This scene grows from systems already present in the world instead of requiring a scripted villain. Resource scarcity, reservations, allocation authority, private communication, non-omniscient knowledge and travel can produce the conflict causally.

The displaced holder can continue toward the original pickup because no valid notice reached them. The unintended recipient can reasonably change their own schedule because they received an official update that appears relevant to shared equipment.

Neither behavior requires stupidity or deception.

## Player-facing investigation

The player can establish:

- which exact resource unit was involved;
- which reservation was displaced;
- who held that reservation;
- which notice obligation was created;
- which communication event was linked or attempted;
- who the envelope was actually addressed to;
- whether it was delivered;
- which actor changed plans after receiving it;
- whether the real holder had already departed before a corrected message could arrive.

A corrected notice should be a new communication event. The wrong-recipient message remains part of history.

## Consequence branches

If discovered early, the office can send a corrected message and both teams simply replan.

If the displaced holder has already left, the player may need to intercept them through an ordinary world route or contact someone they are likely to encounter.

If the unintended recipient already surrendered another valid opportunity because of the mistaken message, the incident can produce a later service-recovery or relationship consequence without assuming misconduct.

Repeated addressing errors can eventually justify an institutional process change. One isolated error should not automatically create a broad reputation penalty.

A wrong-recipient event remains unlinked to the actual notice obligation. The affected holder's notice state remains outstanding.

## Rich encounter version

If the correction reaches the player after the holder enters a field route, the player may choose to catch up before a narrow observation window closes. A wild encounter can obstruct the route while the narrative objective remains reaching the courier or preserving the instrument rather than defeating every opponent.

The full version may require complete movement for interception or forced displacement, full lifecycle for round-based deadlines, status lifecycle for persistent conditions, terrain/weather/hazard families if the route uses them, and AI tactical policy for non-elimination objectives.

A reduced encounter keeps the same premise but resolves travel interruption semantically or uses only ordinary verified targeting, movement legality, arithmetic and legal-action support.
