# Marea / Sendero spatiotemporal partition investigation loop — Pass 270

Status: PROPOSED. No new species, schedule, resource preference, avoidance relation, NPC or quest becomes canon here.

The player revisits one established microhabitat at several observation windows. The visible pattern changes without changing the authoritative population: two already-counted sources may use the same place at different times, later overlap in time while using different portions of a resource, or appear in different nearby spaces under a disturbance context.

The dramatic question is interpretive. A resident may conclude that one group fears another because they are rarely seen together. A field observer may instead notice that the schedules track resource availability, weather, disturbance or another unresolved driver. The knowledge pipeline can preserve both claims while the ecology ledger keeps the causal state uncertain.

A useful sequence is: establish a baseline; observe temporal separation; collect a second axis such as resource fraction or micro-location; introduce a contextual change; revisit; compare whether the pattern shifts; reject premature claims of competition, avoidance or population change unless direct evidence supports them.

Reduced implementation uses persistent ecology, semantic time, existing observation/provenance and Minecraft/Cobblemon presentation. It needs no AutoPTU handoff and can run entirely with already-counted sources.

Mechanically rich implementation can add an actual access contest only when the required families are verified for that exact path. Detection uses targeting/LoS. Ordinary approach uses base movement. Interception, push, pull, knockback and forced displacement require complete movement. Structured contest sequencing requires action economy and lifecycle. Terrain/reaction semantics require that family. Moves, Abilities, Items and Trainer Features enter only when they produce the observed shift. Autonomous choice of when or where to forage, avoid, pursue, wait or yield requires AI tactical policy. Persistent damage/status aftermath requires the corresponding stateful pipelines and semantic-result admission.

Design payoff: Ouros gains a world where different observation times reveal different ecological slices without treating spawn visibility as population truth. A place can feel different at dawn, midday, dusk or after disturbance while the ledger retains exactly what changed: space use, time use, resource fraction, evidence and unresolved mechanism.
