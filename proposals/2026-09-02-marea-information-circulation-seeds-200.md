# Marea Information Circulation Seeds — Pass 200

Status: PROPOSED CONTENT. None of the events below are canon-approved by this file.
Date: 2026-09-02

These seeds use only established Marea people, places and institutional roles unless a future implementation explicitly adds reviewed supporting actors.

## 1. The Ferry Delay Everyone Already Explained

Lia can verify that a ferry is delayed and can record the revised arrival window. Several residents independently offer explanations for why. Mina may have direct operational information, while market talk may only repeat an earlier guess.

Playable question: which claims concern the verified delay, and which concern an unverified cause?

No disaster, sabotage or weather cause is canonized.

## 2. Mirador Heard It Twice

Mara receives two similar reports about unusual Pokémon activity near Sendero del Vidrio. They initially look corroborative. Pia or Nerea traces both accounts back to one visitor conversation.

Outcome: the two reports still matter as evidence of information spread, but count as one origin chain for the underlying sighting.

## 3. The Old Yard Notice

A Battle Yard schedule changes. One old physical notice remains visible while Sela and Jace are using the revised schedule.

Two groups can arrive with legitimate but different expectations. The resolution is a continuity/publication problem before it is a competitive problem.

No relationship penalty is automatic.

## 4. Teo's Missing Part

A repair waits on one component. A market conversation simplifies this into “Teo cannot get parts anymore.” Another resident repeats it as evidence of general shortage.

Ivo or Teo can narrow the statement to the actual work order and part.

The correction does not explain Thin Delivery Season.

## 5. One Sighting Becomes a Migration

Ema records one bounded field observation. By the time the claim reaches Puerto Bruma, someone describes it as a migration.

Nerea issues a qualified correction explaining what the observation actually supports. Some residents have already heard the stronger version.

This directly connects pass 194 population evidence to pass 200 circulation.

## 6. Pia's Correction Slip

Pia discovers that one circulated copy of a Tideglass notice omitted a qualifier. The source deposit remains unchanged. She prepares a correction referencing the affected copy version.

The player can later encounter both the corrected notice and an NPC who only saw the old copy.

## 7. The Visitor's Route Warning

A temporary visitor warns that another route or coastal destination had trouble recently. Because that external geography/event is not yet canonized, Narrative stores the statement strictly as attributed testimony.

Mara may still decide that the claim warrants a bounded local preparation or question. Operational caution does not canonize the outside event.

## 8. Thin Delivery: The Phrase “Every Week”

A vendor says deliveries have been smaller “every week.” Brin's records show several smaller lots but also at least one ordinary dispatch in the relevant period.

The goal is not to catch the vendor lying. Their phrase may be conversational generalization. Tideglass or cooperative records preserve the narrower timeline.

## 9. The Board Was Right Yesterday

A market availability board still reflects a quote or lot from the previous day. Ivo has already received a revision.

A customer repeating the board is not fabricating information. Pass 199 transaction versioning supplies the underlying offer history; pass 200 records who encountered which publication.

## 10. Jace's Win Travels Faster Than the Conditions

Jace wins an audited Battle Yard match under specific roster and format conditions. Local talk reduces the story to “Jace beat X easily” or “Jace is unbeatable now.”

Battle history remains exact. Social retellings can exist without changing Jace's level profile, rival state, official rank or future battle legality.

## 11. Mara's Deliberately Narrow Notice

Mara publishes a route advisory using narrow language because evidence is incomplete. A later paraphrase drops the qualifier and sounds like a closure order.

The field office can correct the public version without retroactively making earlier actors irrational for responding to what they heard.

## 12. Taro Keeps the Wrong Version Too

Tideglass receives a corrected statement. Taro keeps the prior version with supersession metadata instead of deleting it.

A later investigation can reconstruct how an outdated claim spread precisely because the old artifact survives.

## 13. A Correction Arrives after the Choice

The player or an NPC makes a reasonable low-stakes decision using the best available version. A correction arrives afterward.

The story records the timing. Later dialogue should distinguish a decision made under incomplete information from knowingly ignoring the correction.

## Long arc: What Bruma Thinks It Knows

This arc spans multiple ordinary events rather than one conspiracy.

Phase 1: Small claims circulate through existing work routines: ferry timing, market lots, route observations, Battle Yard scheduling and visitor accounts.

Phase 2: Tideglass and Mirador make source chains visible. The player encounters cases where several repeaters share one upstream source, and cases where two genuinely independent observations converge.

Phase 3: Corrections propagate unevenly. Some NPCs update quickly because their work channels connect them directly. Others retain an older version until a plausible contact reaches them.

Phase 4: The district develops better information habits through visible practices: version dates, attribution, qualified notices and source references. This is institutional growth, not a global information-quality meter.

The arc can strengthen Thin Delivery Season without resolving it. A future conclusion should emerge from independent evidence across production, dispatch, transport, market and field observations rather than from whichever explanation became most popular.

## Mechanically rich encounter: Field Check after the Glass Bend Warning

Premise: a circulating warning says wild Pokémon have taken over the seasonal crossing. Mara or Mirador asks for a bounded field check. On arrival, the team discovers only what the current scene actually supports.

Full intended form may include observers, protected withdrawal, route geometry, an immediate wild actor, Interception, displacement, environmental constraints and objective-aware behavior.

Required capability families:
- targeting/footprints/range/LoS;
- base movement legality;
- complete movement including push/pull/knockback/interception/forced movement when those interactions are authored;
- core calculations;
- action economy/initiative;
- full turn/round lifecycle;
- full stateful damage pipeline;
- status lifecycle where selected content uses statuses;
- terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions when route conditions become tactical;
- move-specific behavior;
- abilities;
- items;
- Trainer Features/perks;
- AI legal-action infrastructure;
- AI tactical policy;
- Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback support.

Full disposition: BLOCKED while required partial/blocking families remain incomplete.

Reduced form:

Narrative keeps the circulating claim, source lineage, field purpose, observers and ecology records outside BattleSpec. Observers withdraw to safety first. If one concrete actor still blocks immediate passage, compile one ordinary audited battle on stable geometry using supported content.

Allowed world handoffs:
- `IMMEDIATE_ROUTE_THREAT_CONFIRMED_AT_TIMESTAMP`
- `IMMEDIATE_ROUTE_THREAT_WITHDREW`
- `IMMEDIATE_PASSAGE_CLEAR_AT_TIMESTAMP`

The encounter can narrow the public claim. It cannot determine rumor origin, malicious intent, population size, ecological cause, permanent route safety, future recurrence, correction reach or broad reputation.

## Recommended first implementation slice

`Mirador Heard It Twice` is the recommended first slice.

It requires no battle, new NPC, new species, external geography or PTU Skill adjudication. It validates the central pass-200 behavior: two reports can exist as separate records while provenance reveals a shared root source. It also plugs directly into Mara, Nerea, Pia, Mirador and Tideglass, all already canonical.
