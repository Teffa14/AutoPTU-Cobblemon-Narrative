# Second Survey Season — Pass 403

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Canon effect: NONE

## Premise

A route that was previously surveyed and made reliably usable is visited again after enough time has passed for the surrounding world to change.

The original intervention was successful. That success is part of the setup rather than a hidden failure. Traffic patterns changed. Maintenance practices changed. Pokémon learned new paths around human activity. Vegetation, water flow or erosion may have altered the physical approach. None of those specific causes are canon until separately approved.

A new survey team is assigned to compare the current route with the older record.

One temporary specialist joins because they participated in, archived or studied the earlier work. Their value is context. They can identify what used to be normal, point out old markers and recognize which differences deserve attention. They are not automatically an escort burden.

## Narrative loop

The expedition begins with an older route record and a present operational question.

At each meaningful site, the team can compare dated evidence with the current state. Differences can be classified provisionally as expected change, maintenance change, uncertain change or anomaly requiring another source.

Old testimony can be correct for the old state while current testimony is correct now. A contradiction between observations at different semantic cuts does not establish deception.

The team may return to archives, contact earlier workers, request another instrument, inspect another branch of the route or schedule a third visit under different conditions.

If the player ignores the assignment, persistent NPCs can perform some or all of it. Their observations remain private until communicated through existing information systems.

## Longer arc potential

Several seasonal surveys can accumulate into a regional history of how routes, settlements and Pokémon activity respond to successful human interventions.

Some changes should remain mundane. Others may generate new maintenance work, ecological concerns, access disputes, research opportunities or rival interpretations.

The arc should resist converting every discrepancy into one hidden conspiracy. A living region gains credibility when many changes have ordinary causes and only a minority justify deeper investigation.

## Reduced implementation version

The full narrative premise can run without AutoPTU.

Use semantic time, persistent site revisions, travel, observations with provenance, private NPC knowledge, explicit communication, archive lookup, schedules and world-resource logistics.

Environmental change occurs between world revisions. The temporary specialist contributes information and optional investigation branches through world-agent state. No tactical buff is implied.

A holder-history baseline can support equipment provenance when the survey uses an older shared instrument. The baseline proves holder continuity only from its own authoritative cut forward. It does not complete earlier custody history.

## Mechanically rich version

A later survey may occur while the route is physically hazardous or while wild Pokémon activity complicates access. The narrative objective remains survey, comparison, safe withdrawal, rescue or preservation of evidence as appropriate. KO is not an automatic completion condition.
