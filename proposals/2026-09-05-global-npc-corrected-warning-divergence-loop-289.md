# Proposal: Corrected warning divergence loop

Status: PROPOSED. Region-neutral. Not canon-approved.

A public service releases an urgent route warning based on incomplete information. Different NPCs receive it at different times and change plans accordingly. Later, the same publisher issues a correction after new evidence arrives.

The narrative value comes from history that cannot be rewound. An NPC who already cancelled a delivery, missed a meeting, or diverted through a slower route did so for a reason that was valid from that character's information state at the time. Another NPC may receive the correction before acting. A late receiver may only encounter the corrected version. Someone outside coverage may remain unaware of both.

The player can reconstruct the chain through receipts, witnesses, schedules, travel records, and the publication lineage. This supports mysteries where the question is not only what is true now, but what each actor reasonably knew when a consequential choice was made.

A retraction can create a different hook. The publisher withdraws an earlier claim but has no verified replacement yet. NPCs who receive the withdrawal know the old bulletin is no longer endorsed, but the system does not invent an opposite fact. That uncertainty can motivate investigation rather than a forced belief flip.

Reduced implementation version: publication lineage, individual receipt, semantic-time delivery, private knowledge, agenda replanning, and travel consequences only. No structured encounter is required.

Full encounter version: a stale warning can put a traveler into a dangerous scene before the correction arrives. The exact dependency families depend on the scene. Ordinary range/LoS uses targeting/footprints/range/LoS. Pursuit with interception or forced displacement requires complete movement. Mechanical weather, hazards, zones, or reactions require terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions. Temporary or delayed effects require the relevant lifecycle/status owner plus move/ability/item/Feature behavior. Autonomous combat choices require AI tactical policy. Visible end-to-end execution requires Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback support.

This proposal does not canonize a broadcaster, route, city, communications technology, faction, or incident.
