# Pass 161 — Interregional Crossing Processing & Clearance Seeds

Status: NON-CANON CANDIDATES / PROPOSALS.
Date: 2026-08-25

These concepts extend `interregional-crossing-processing-clearance-protocol.md`. None establishes national borders, citizenship, visas, customs law, tariffs, detention powers or universal search authority.

## 30 worldbuilding candidates

### 1. The Badge Works, the Road Does Not

A Trainer clears a League eligibility check normally, then learns that the onward mountain route closed an hour earlier. The crossing record and Travel state disagree only because they answer different questions.

### 2. Valid Credential, Wrong Scope

A field certificate is genuine and recognized, but it authorizes ordinary survey sectors rather than the protected nesting area the visitor intended to enter.

### 3. The Secondary Review Clears It

A routine record mismatch sends a museum loan to specialist review. The discrepancy is an old catalogue number, not misconduct. The uneventful resolution becomes a useful institutional baseline.

### 4. One Consignment, Three Authorities

A refrigerated research shipment simultaneously needs Supply Chains receiving state, Research Ethics/sample authority and Biosecurity review. No single desk can truthfully answer all three questions.

### 5. The Ferry Arrives Before the Records

Weather lets a ferry arrive early while the digital transfer packet is still delayed elsewhere. Passengers can continue through one process while selected cargo waits.

### 6. The Gate That Outlived the Boundary

A League route was redesigned decades ago, but its old reception building survives as a transit hall and local landmark. Historical checkpoint signage still causes visitor confusion.

### 7. Migration Week Crossing Hold

A normal wildlife migration intersects an approach road. The facility pauses one lane for several evenings each year without declaring the route permanently closed.

### 8. The Pokémon Does Not Cross

A working Pokémon accompanying a research team stops before an unfamiliar transfer ramp. The team reroutes the operation rather than interpreting refusal as disobedience.

### 9. The Emergency Lane

A temporary responder lane created during a flood works so well that later planning debates whether to preserve it as ordinary infrastructure.

### 10. The Old Inspection Shed

A disused specialist shed contains dated forms, signs and photographs that reveal how regional institutions once coordinated transfers. The paperwork documents procedure, not proof that every old rule was good.

### 11. The Missing Declaration Was Never Required

A later auditor notices no declaration record for a historical transfer. The rule in force at the time never required one.

### 12. Two Correct Purpose Records

A visiting Trainer originally arrives for a tournament, then remains under a separate research invitation. Both purpose records are historically correct for different windows.

### 13. The Cold Chain Waits

A perishable clinical consignment receives protected storage while one specialist decision is pending. Its contents are physically safe even though the process is incomplete.

### 14. The Fossil Loan at the Gate

A museum fossil has valid loan paperwork, but a recent collection revision changed the receiving institution’s storage location. The transfer pauses for a custody handoff correction, not a provenance dispute.

### 15. Three Crossings, One Region

A mountain road, ferry and rail terminal all serve the same interregional corridor. Each processes different scopes and remains operational under different disruptions.

### 16. The Route Changed, the Gate Stayed

A new bridge redirects most traffic away from an old crossing facility. The site becomes quieter but remains important for one protected-area route and emergency access.

### 17. The Signage Changed First

Public signs begin using a new institutional name before all databases and credentials finish updating. The mismatch is expected during transition.

### 18. Records Are Down, Permission Is Not

A local lookup outage prevents instant verification. Existing underlying permissions remain valid; the institution follows its authored fallback rather than silently revoking access.

### 19. The Random Review

A completely ordinary consignment receives a routine/random additional check and clears normally. The Chronicle stores it without manufacturing suspicion.

### 20. The Former Partner at a Crossing

A released former partner may be observed near a crossing used by seasonal wildlife. Identity remains an unresolved or evidence-backed observation; no ownership, recapture or destination is inferred.

### 21. The Accessible Route Takes Longer

Two visitors with the same permission receive different onward instructions because only one route currently satisfies accessibility needs. Clearance does not imply identical movement path.

### 22. The Festival Queue

A yearly event triples the crossing’s process load. By the third edition, organizers have learned enough to stagger arrivals and the once-famous queue becomes routine history.

### 23. The Shared Desk

Two institutions create a joint reception desk after years of duplicated referrals. They still retain separate authority and record systems.

### 24. The Parcel Is Cleared, the Recipient Is Gone

A postal item passes the transfer point correctly, but last-mile delivery later fails because the addressee moved. Clearance and delivery remain separate histories.

### 25. The Conservation Transfer Returns

A planned relocation reaches the transfer point, but the receiving habitat closes after new field evidence. The Pokémon and carers return without the attempt being labelled a failed capture, contraband event or institutional scandal.

### 26. The Wrong Queue

A visitor waits forty minutes at a freight/documentation desk because old signage still points there. No permission issue ever existed.

### 27. The Replacement Operator

A veteran staff member retires and a successor inherits the same facility role but initially refers more edge cases. Over several years, training and better documentation reduce those referrals.

### 28. The Crossing Goes Offline

A radio outage disconnects a remote checkpoint from a regional system. Some scopes continue under local fallback; others pause. Nothing about the outage proves sabotage.

### 29. The Clearance Was Correct Then

A historical research transfer would not satisfy today’s tighter rules. Its original clearance remains part of history rather than being retroactively rewritten as unauthorized.

### 30. Nothing Happened at Meridian Crossing

A complete season passes with ordinary arrivals, releases and handoffs. Years later, those records become the baseline showing how unusual a later disruption really was.

## Longer-term arc 1 — Five Revisions at Meridian Crossing

Year 1: Meridian Crossing uses several separate desks inherited from older institutions.

Year 2: an event surge exposes a queue bottleneck, but no underlying security or authorization failure.

Year 3: a wildlife corridor changes one approach lane seasonally.

Year 4: institutions introduce a shared intake desk while preserving specialist authority downstream.

Year 5: a communications outage tests the fallback process. The facility operates more slowly but avoids the cascading confusion that would have occurred earlier.

Chronicle payoff: operational maturity is visible because fewer routine issues become player quests.

## Longer-term arc 2 — The Crossing That Became a Station

An old League gate gradually accumulates a ferry connection, transit stop, public information desk and research transfer function. Architecture and service change while the original building remains a recognizable historic layer.

Possible consequences:

- old badge-check booths become exhibit space;
- one former lane becomes an accessible route;
- a retired gatekeeper becomes an oral-history source without retaining authority;
- legacy signage creates a later records puzzle;
- the institution’s meaning changes without needing demolition or retcon.

## Longer-term arc 3 — One Consignment Across Three Systems

A persistent scientific collection moves through several institutions over years. One batch may pass through a ferry terminal, research station, museum loan, temporary cold storage and later return.

The story follows custody and process provenance rather than smuggling drama by default. If a genuine diversion eventually occurs, Illicit Networks receives that case only after evidence supports it.

## Encounter contract — Checkpoint Evacuation During Wildlife Movement

Narrative premise:

A recurring wildlife movement intersects an active crossing facility while visitors are already processing. The objective is to stop normal operations safely, give wildlife a route through and prevent crowd compression or conflict.

FULL version dependencies:

- targeting/footprints/range/LoS — VERIFIED baseline for any actual combat;
- base movement legality — VERIFIED baseline;
- complete movement including interception/forced movement — BLOCKING if civilians or wildlife must dynamically `CROSS`, `WITHDRAW` or be intercepted;
- core calculations — VERIFIED baseline;
- action economy/initiative — VERIFIED baseline;
- full turn/round lifecycle — PARTIAL if objective state depends on deeper multi-round timing;
- full stateful damage pipeline — PARTIAL for ordinary combat fidelity;
- status lifecycle — PARTIAL only if an exact Status is invoked;
- terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions — BLOCKING if protected lanes, moving crowd boundaries or environmental effects have tactical meaning;
- move-specific behavior — PARTIAL for exact Moves;
- abilities — PARTIAL for exact Abilities;
- items — PARTIAL for exact tactical Items;
- Trainer Features/perks — PARTIAL for exact Features;
- AI legal-action infrastructure — VERIFIED baseline;
- AI tactical policy — BLOCKING for `EVACUATE`, `CROSS`, `WITHDRAW`, `CLEAR_ROUTE`, `PROTECT_SITE`;
- Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback — BLOCKING for crowds, wildlife objectives, lanes and world-state handoff.

REDUCED version:

World state suspends processing, moves civilians away and resolves the wildlife crossing outside battle. If a separate hostile confrontation remains, AutoPTU receives a static arena away from the active lane. After battle, the processing session resumes from its prior state.

## Encounter contract — Secondary Inspection Bay Interruption

Narrative premise:

A consignment is already on a legitimate scoped hold when an unrelated disturbance reaches the inspection/staging area. Protecting staff or the object does not answer the pending specialist question.

FULL version dependencies:

- verified baseline families for ordinary static combat;
- complete movement — BLOCKING for moving staff, custodians or a protected object through contested space;
- AI tactical policy — BLOCKING for `PROTECT_CUSTODIAN`, `REACH_EXIT`, `CLEAR_ROUTE`, `WITHDRAW`;
- adapter/playback — BLOCKING for staff, cargo/custody semantics and handoff;
- terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions — BLOCKING only if machinery, spill, structural damage or another validated environmental effect changes tactics;
- items — PARTIAL if the protected object is also an exact battle-semantic Item.

REDUCED version:

The held consignment remains outside the grid. Staff move to safety first. AutoPTU resolves a conventional static fight in the cleared bay. The specialist review resumes afterward and is unaffected except where the world incident produced separately recorded physical damage or delay.

## Encounter contract — Emergency Crossing Lane

Narrative premise:

A crisis requires one facility to route evacuees or responders through a lane normally used for another purpose while a separate combat threat exists nearby.

FULL version dependencies:

- complete movement — BLOCKING for moving evacuees/responders inside battle;
- AI tactical policy — BLOCKING for `EVACUATE`, `REACH_EXIT`, `PROTECT_RESPONDER`, `CLEAR_ROUTE`;
- adapter/playback — BLOCKING for noncombatant actors and lane semantics;
- terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions — CONDITIONAL BLOCKING if the crisis itself changes tactical environment;
- ordinary exact Move/Ability/Status/Item/Feature families remain PARTIAL when invoked.

REDUCED version:

Emergency Services resolves evacuee/responders movement first. The crossing records the temporary lane state. Any remaining confrontation uses a static safe perimeter. Battle outcome does not itself grant or revoke clearance.

## Non-combat contract — Declaration Reconciliation Review

Inputs can include:

- declaration revisions;
- Supply Chain/Postal records;
- Identity and credential records;
- Travel route history;
- custody/provenance state;
- timestamped crossing events.

Valid outcomes include:

- clerical mismatch resolved;
- two records refer to different process stages;
- emergency reroute explains the discrepancy;
- later record superseded an earlier one;
- specialist case required;
- evidence remains insufficient;
- separate Case/Illicit Network referral is justified by independent evidence.

No battle-engine dependency. A Guile/Charm/Command/Perception check must not be invented as a truth detector.
