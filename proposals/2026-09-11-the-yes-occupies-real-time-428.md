# The Yes Occupies Real Time — Pass 428

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON

Premise

A named NPC receives a request for help and chooses ACCEPT. The answer returns to the requester. The responder now has a real obligation window in their own agenda.

The useful consequence is temporal rather than instantaneous. Saying yes can make a later period busier, compete with another goal, become late, or be missed. The acceptance remains historically true even if later events prevent the work from happening as first imagined.

Existing canon surfaces

Mara Veyra and Teo Lark remain possible regression actors because their current Marea roles can plausibly generate coordination needs. This proposal does not establish a new request, repair, schedule, duty, standing agreement, communication channel or relationship fact.

Reduced version

Mara requests assistance. Teo receives the request and accepts. His acceptance is delivered back. A bounded `ScheduledCommitment` is then attached to Teo's ordinary agenda with provenance from his ACCEPT action.

Before the start minute, the commitment is UPCOMING. During its window it becomes DUE. A grace period may exist if explicitly authored. If the effective deadline passes, the existing agenda system produces missed-commitment follow-up instead of pretending Teo completed the work.

This form needs no AutoPTU battle. It uses private knowledge, semantic time, world-action provenance, explicit communication and the existing global schedule model.

Important boundaries

The commitment does not move Teo.

It does not reserve a route, destination, vehicle, tool or Pokémon.

It does not make Mara omniscient about Teo's later movements.

It does not complete the assistance request.

It does not guarantee that a higher-priority emergency, critical need or hard obligation cannot interrupt the plan.

It does not create PTU permission from a job title or narrative role.

If the committed work later needs travel, the travel owner must plan it. If it needs a scarce resource, the resource/reservation owners must handle it. If structured mechanics become necessary, the selected agenda action must enter the normal explicit AutoPTU handoff.

Mechanically rich version

A committed helper may eventually join an escort, retrieval, rescue, route-protection or extraction scene. The appointment remains a world-level cause for being available; it does not supply the tactical mechanics.

Targeting/footprints/range/LoS remains VERIFIED only in audited scopes.

Base movement legality remains VERIFIED only in audited scopes.

Complete movement including push/pull/knockback/interception/forced movement remains PARTIAL and is required when the scene depends on those movement families.

Core calculations remain VERIFIED only in audited scopes.

Action economy/initiative remains PARTIAL. A helper joining initiative, switching into an active encounter or receiving an exact action window cannot be inferred from the existence of a commitment.

Full turn/round lifecycle, full stateful damage pipeline and status lifecycle remain PARTIAL.

Terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions remains MIXED/PARTIAL/BLOCKING by exact behavior.

Move-specific behavior, Items and Trainer Features/perks remain individually gated. Abilities remain PARTIAL and individually gated.

AI legal-action infrastructure remains VERIFIED only for audited ordinary actions. Rescue, escort, protect, retrieve, carry, interact, brace and extract still need explicit admission.

AI tactical policy remains BLOCKING for specialized objective-aware behavior.

Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback remains PARTIAL/BLOCKING for authoritative specialized objective state, specialized non-KO completion and in-flight recovery.

Reduced fallback

If the eventual tactical scene needs unsupported mechanics, the premise survives. Teo can reach the commitment window, perform a basic world-level inspection or support step, and the richer encounter can be deferred or replaced by a verified simpler resolution. The acceptance and time cost remain meaningful.

Longer-term story use

Repeated accepted commitments can reveal workload pressure without scripting personality labels. A reliable actor may still miss a window because another hard obligation appeared. Another may accept only soft windows. A community may later learn to request assistance earlier because travel and existing schedules matter.

Those patterns require actual event history. They do not become reputation or relationship canon automatically.

Canon questions left open

No default duration for accepted help is established. No Marea response-time expectation is established. No actor is guaranteed to accept a request. No automatic trust reward or penalty follows from completion or lateness. No route, meeting place or resource bundle is implied by the time window.
