# The Job Everyone Needs Done — Pass 407

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Canon effect: NONE

A practical problem appears on a shared community board. Several local institutions agree that the work must be done, but they disagree about priority, acceptable risk, ownership of the result or what should happen afterward.

The request can be ordinary: inspect a damaged crossing, recover a shared instrument, verify a contaminated water point, escort a repair specialist, document repeated Pokémon interference or reopen a minor service route. Exact site, actors and cause remain open for canon review.

## Persistent-world structure

The request has an issuer, publication time, current status and evidence requirements. Multiple actors may know about it because the board is public, but later changes still require actual communication.

The player may accept the work. A persistent NPC team may accept it first. Nobody may accept it before conditions worsen. An institution may withdraw or replace the request if new evidence changes the problem.

Completion records what actually happened. It does not award a universal faction score. Consequences follow from concrete state: who supplied equipment, who performed the work, who now controls access, who received the report, which institution must maintain the result, and which actors learned what.

## Institutional tension

Three broad positions can coexist without assigning fixed morality.

One actor wants the fastest practical repair because residents depend on the route. Another wants observation before intervention because changing the site may destroy useful evidence or disturb Pokémon behavior. A third cares about long-term maintenance and refuses a temporary fix that creates an obligation nobody can sustain.

A successful intervention can therefore create a later disagreement even when the original job was completed correctly. The world preserves the operational reason behind each position instead of converting disagreement into hidden alignment points.

## Follow-up paths

If the player completed the work, later actors can reference only records or testimony they actually received. If an NPC team completed it off-screen, the player may first encounter the consequence instead of the original job. If nobody acted, the missing intervention remains part of the world's history.

A later quest can ask who should maintain the repaired site, whether the original evidence was sufficient, whether access should remain restricted, or whether another institution should reimburse the actor who used its own resources.

## Reduced implementation

The reduced form needs persistent obligations, public request state, semantic time, schedules, travel, communications, relationships, permissions, resource custody and provenance-bearing evidence. Environmental change can occur between world revisions. No battle is required.

## Rich encounter version

A dangerous inspection or repair can occur while wild Pokémon activity, unstable ground or another pressure complicates the objective. Success can mean completing the inspection, protecting a specialist, retrieving the required evidence and leaving safely. Defeating every opponent is not required by the narrative premise.
