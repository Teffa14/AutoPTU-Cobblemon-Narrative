# Marea Institutional Continuity Seeds — Pass 179

Status: PROPOSED / NOT CANON
Date: 2026-09-01
Depends on: `design/institutional-role-delegation-succession-layer.md`

## Scope

These proposals use only already-established Marea residents, workplaces and relationship edges as starting conditions. They do not create promotions, new family ties, PTU classes or permanent offices.

The aim is to make ordinary responsibility visible: who can do a task, who reviews it, what continues during an absence, what must wait, and how residents learn to carry more of a shared institution over time.

## 1. Pia's First Independent Circulation Run

Primary actors: Pia Min, Taro Min
Sites: Tideglass Archive, Market Hall, ferry landing

Premise:

Taro gives Pia a bounded circulation assignment covering copies already cleared for movement. Pia can retrieve, deliver, record receipt and flag a missing item. She cannot alter archival interpretation, declare provenance resolved or release restricted material.

Player surface:

The player may carry one packet or accompany part of the route, but Pia remains the responsible worker. The interesting state is whether each custody handoff is documented correctly.

Possible outcomes:

- all items return with clean custody history;
- one recipient is unavailable and Pia correctly retains custody rather than improvising a release;
- a resident asks for material outside the delegation and Pia escalates to Taro;
- a stale public notice causes someone to treat Pia as the archive's general decision-maker.

World consequence:

Pia can acquire a persistent history of independently completed circulation duties without becoming Taro's successor.

Battle dependency: none.

Implementation value: HIGH. This can exercise physical quest objects, information packets, schedules, custody provenance and bounded authority with current non-tactical RPG infrastructure.

## 2. Ema Signs the Preparation Line

Primary actors: Ema Rey, Dr. Nerea Sol
Sites: Estacion Mirador, transect trailhead, Tideglass Archive

Premise:

Ema prepares a routine observation packet from a completed protocol. For the first time, the packet records Ema as preparer while Nerea remains reviewer.

The distinction is visible on the document rather than represented by a hidden competence number.

Player surface:

The player can help recover field notes, compare timestamps or carry the reviewed copy to Tideglass. The player does not certify Ema.

Possible complication:

A minor discrepancy appears between an instrument note and the packet summary. Ema can either flag it before review, overlook it, or overstate its importance. Correctly escalating uncertainty is a positive competence signal even if the packet needs correction.

Battle dependency: none.

Long arc potential:

Later packets can move from `REVIEW_REQUIRED` to `SAMPLE_AUDIT` only through explicit canon-approved development, not automatic repetition counting.

## 3. The Field Office Without Mara at the Desk

Primary actors: Mara Veyra; possible supporting residents chosen later by canon
Sites: Marea Field Office, Sendero del Vidrio

Premise:

Mara is physically off-site on a route check. The Field Office remains open for a limited subset of functions.

This seed is intentionally incomplete until canon identifies who can cover which verbs. The design question is the content: some reports can be received and queued, some urgent notices can be relayed, and some decisions must wait for Mara or another explicitly authorized actor.

Player surface:

The player encounters a real service boundary instead of a frozen NPC marker. A resident may be frustrated that the desk can receive their report but cannot approve the requested action.

Narrative value:

Demonstrates that an institution persists during ordinary absence while still respecting mandate limits.

Battle dependency: none unless Mara's separate route assignment creates an independently audited encounter.

Canon blocker:

No acting Field Office authority is currently established. Do not nominate one silently.

## 4. Ferry Intake Surge

Primary actors: Lia Morn, Mina Cors; possible temporary helper TBD
Site: ferry landing

Premise:

Several arrivals cluster within a short operational window. Lia retains berth and unloading-priority authority but delegates one bounded record lane to a temporary helper.

The helper can RECORD arrival identifiers and RECEIVE routine paperwork. They cannot redirect vessels, change berth order or certify an irregular cargo discrepancy.

Player surface:

The player may help move ordinary non-sensitive documents between landing and Market Hall. A request outside scope tests whether the helper escalates correctly.

World consequence:

The event can expose whether the ferry operation has a trained backup for basic intake without inventing a new permanent employee.

Battle dependency: none.

Canon blocker:

Temporary helper identity must be selected or created through normal canon approval.

## 5. Brin's Storehouse Handover Window

Primary actors: Brin Havel, future cooperative delegate TBD
Sites: Loma Clara cooperative storehouse, producer lane

Premise:

Brin must leave before one dispatch cycle closes. A bounded custody handover transfers responsibility for sealed outgoing lots without changing the underlying intake records.

Player surface:

The player can witness or transport the physical handover packet. The key state is chain of custody, not a fetch-quest completion flag.

Complication options:

- a lot is physically present but missing from the handover list;
- the list contains a lot already dispatched;
- the incoming delegate correctly refuses to sign until the discrepancy is reconciled;
- Ivo expects a delivery based on an older dispatch estimate.

Battle dependency: none.

Canon blocker:

No second storehouse authority is currently canonized.

## 6. Jo's Supervised Teaching Block

Primary actors: Jo Venn; supervising institution/actor TBD by canon
Site: Loma Clara field school

Premise:

A practical observation lesson is run by Jo with a bounded curriculum and explicit responsibility for a group activity.

This seed is useful primarily as a semantic guard. Jo's canon Mentor / Researcher class concepts do not automatically grant any specific PTU Mentor Features, educational license or institutional hierarchy.

Player surface:

The player may demonstrate observation practice, provide a safe field sample, or answer questions from learners. No mechanical Training bonus is invented.

Battle dependency: none in the default version.

PTU dependency:

If the lesson ever uses actual Mentor-class Features such as Guidance, Lessons or Move Tutor, Trainer Features/perks becomes an exact required family and the specific Feature must be parity-audited before use.

## 7. Jace Runs One Yard Session

Primary actors: Jace Orrin, Sela Orrin, Teo Lark
Site: Bruma Battle Yard

Status inside proposal: MECHANICALLY RICH / FULL VERSION BLOCKED

Premise:

Sela delegates one bounded local training session to Jace. Jace may manage check-in, routine fixture inspection and the scheduled match. Sela retains authority over exceptional safety decisions, official policy and any function not explicitly delegated.

Teo may already have completed ordinary fixture maintenance before the session. Jace checking the fixture does not make him a repair authority.

Institutional evaluation:

The review should consider:

- whether Jace stayed within assigned scope;
- whether participant records were correct;
- whether an exception was escalated;
- whether the match record accurately reflects the battle result;
- whether the yard was left in the expected operational state.

Winning his own battle, if he participates, is not the evaluation criterion.

### Intended full encounter

One audited Trainer battle occurs inside the session with the ordinary full tactical presentation expected by Ouros.

Potential permanent capability dependencies:

- targeting/footprints/range/LoS — required;
- base movement legality — required;
- complete movement including push/pull/knockback/interception/forced movement — required if any selected content uses those effects;
- core calculations — required;
- action economy/initiative — required;
- full turn/round lifecycle — required;
- full stateful damage pipeline — required;
- status lifecycle — required if selected content applies statuses;
- terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions — required if battlefield or selected content uses them;
- move-specific behavior — required for every selected Move;
- abilities — required for every selected Ability;
- items — required for every selected held/used Item;
- Trainer Features/perks — required for participating Trainer Features;
- AI legal-action infrastructure — required for autonomous combatants;
- AI tactical policy — required for meaningful autonomous tactical choice;
- Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback support — required for the intended visible in-world battle.

Current status: BLOCKED for the intended full version because several required families remain partial or blocking.

### Reduced executable version

Preserve the exact institutional premise but select an audited matchup that avoids:

- forced-movement-dependent Moves;
- battlefield hazards, zones and weather interactions;
- complex statuses not verified for the chosen content;
- unsupported held Items;
- unsupported Trainer Feature interrupts;
- tactics whose intended behavior depends on missing AI policy.

The canonical role/delegation state remains outside BattleSpec.

Allowed tactical output:

`YARD_SESSION_MATCH_COMPLETE`

plus the normal authoritative match result and Trainer record update.

Disallowed direct outputs:

- `JACE_PROMOTED`
- `JACE_SUCCESSOR`
- `JACE_FULL_YARD_AUTHORITY`
- `SELA_RETIRED`

Those require a separate institutional decision if they ever become canon.

## 8. The Acting Contact Misunderstanding

Primary actors: any future approved role pair
Site: Puerto Bruma service hub

Premise:

A public notice names one resident as the contact for a narrow period. Some residents interpret the notice as a permanent promotion.

The canonical role state remains unchanged. The misinformation propagates through the existing claim/local-knowledge system.

Player surface:

The player can hear both versions and inspect the actual notice. No dialogue choice magically changes the office holder.

Narrative use:

Provides a low-stakes demonstration of how public understanding of institutions can diverge from actual mandate.

Battle dependency: none.

## 9. One Process, Two Specialists

Primary actors: Nerea Sol, Teo Lark, Ema Rey
Sites: Estacion Mirador, repair row

Premise:

A field instrument requires maintenance before a dataset can be reviewed. Teo owns ordinary maintenance work; Ema owns observation preparation under protocol; Nerea owns project interpretation/review. No single person owns the entire chain.

Player surface:

The player can move the instrument or record between sites and see how custody and responsibility cross institutional boundaries.

Design lesson:

Competence is compositional. The world becomes more resilient through clear interfaces rather than a single omnipotent quest giver.

Battle dependency: none.

## 10. Long Arc — Second Hands of Marea

Status: LONG-TERM PROPOSAL

Premise:

Across many unrelated episodes, Marea residents gradually expose whether their institutions rely too heavily on individual people.

This is not a succession storyline requiring everyone to replace their mentor. Instead it tracks institutional resilience:

- Pia can carry more archive circulation work while Taro remains custodian;
- Ema can independently prepare more observation records while Nerea remains project lead;
- Jace can run selected Yard sessions while Sela remains manager;
- ferry operations can document bounded backup procedures;
- Loma custody workflows can survive one person's absence;
- the Field Office can define what happens when Mara is off-site.

Possible thematic payoff:

The district becomes more capable because knowledge and duty are shared clearly, not because the older generation disappears.

A later genuine vacancy or succession would then matter because the player has seen the work behind the title.

Canon requirements:

Every permanent role change remains individually approved. This long arc does not pre-authorize any succession.

## Recommended first implementation candidates

### Pia's First Independent Circulation Run

Best first test because it connects a canon mentorship to bounded real work, uses existing physical sites and can operate entirely outside tactical combat.

### Ema Signs the Preparation Line

Best provenance test because preparer and reviewer can become visible server-owned facts without changing Nerea's role.

### Jace Runs One Yard Session — reduced version only

Best later bridge into tactical engine integration once a deliberately simple audited matchup is chosen.

- Does Tideglass have formal named offices beyond Taro's current description?
- Who, if anyone, can cover specific Field Office duties when Mara is off-site?
- Does the ferry landing already have unnamed operational staff, or should surge coverage come from another institution?
- Who can accept cooperative custody from Brin?
- Is Jo formally part of a larger educational institution or simply the field-school instructor?
- Does Bruma Battle Yard have written delegation policy?
- Are any Marea roles appointed, elected, contracted, inherited, licensed or informal?
- What counts as permanent promotion in local public memory?
- Does Caelo source material define any relevant organizational custom?

Until answered, these remain proposals rather than lore facts.
