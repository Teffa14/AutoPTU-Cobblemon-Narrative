# The Notice Behind the Booking — Case 350

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-08

## Premise

Two field teams have legitimate claims on the same specialized instrument. An authorized allocation decision gives the later window to one team and Pass 349 makes the earlier reservation non-operative for planning. The displaced team's original booking remains in the historical ledger.

A notice obligation now exists for that displaced holder. The actual message has not arrived yet.

The displaced team leaves for the collection point using information that was correct when they last received it.

## Play structure

The player can reconstruct the sequence from persistent evidence: the original booking, the later conflict, the authority-backed decision, the allocation application, the notice obligation, the communication event and its delivery state.

A useful resolution does not require assigning fault. The allocator may have followed policy. The sender may have authored the update promptly. The receiver may have entered poor coverage before delivery. The old booking can remain authentic evidence even though it no longer controls current access.

The player can redirect the team, deliver the update through another valid channel, recover a different technically ready unit, reorder field work, or document the lost window and replan.

Repeated incidents can later become source evidence for an existing Procurement or institutional-policy process. This proposal does not automatically create a purchase order, trust penalty or faction conflict.

## Reduced implementation

The reduced version uses semantic time, Pass 339 reservations, Pass 348 allocation resolution, Pass 349 application, Pass 350 notice obligations, existing communications, memory/belief, travel and replanning. AutoPTU is not required.

## Rich encounter variant

After a correctly informed team collects the selected unit, a field interruption can threaten arrival before the observation window closes. The narrative objective is preserving the instrument and reaching the site, rather than necessarily defeating every opponent.

This variant requires ordinary targeting and base movement support already audited. Interception, carrying constraints, push/pull/knockback or forced displacement depend on complete movement and remain PARTIAL. Round deadlines depend on the full turn/round lifecycle, which remains PARTIAL. Stateful injury to actors or equipment requires the full stateful damage pipeline. Persistent conditions require status lifecycle. Weather, hazards, zones and reactions remain gated by their exact families. Every Move, Ability, Item and Trainer Feature remains individually gated. Escort and delivery-first behavior remains blocked by missing AI tactical-policy coverage. Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics playback remains partial/blocking end-to-end.

## Canon boundary

No named NPC, faction, location, institutional policy or historical event is added to canon by this proposal. It is a reusable scenario pattern awaiting placement through normal canon review.
