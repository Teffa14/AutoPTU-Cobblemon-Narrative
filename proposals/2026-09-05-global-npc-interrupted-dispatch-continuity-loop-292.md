# Interrupted Dispatch continuity loop — Pass 292

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-05

A named NPC observes that a route, facility or environmental condition has changed and sends a warning to another actor already committed to travel or work. The world service restarts while the warning is in transit.

After recovery, the sender still has the observation. The warning keeps its semantic ETA. The receiver cannot learn it early. If delivery already completed, it cannot happen again. Uninvolved actors remain uninformed. Technical uptime never becomes a story reset.

The pattern can support couriers, field researchers, tournament logistics, ranger-style warnings, rival travel, supply chains or institutional corrections. All places, organizations and actors remain content bindings; none are canonized here.

The player can later reconstruct observation time, dispatch time, actual delivery, receiver belief, replanning and travel consequence. A genuinely late warning can therefore be distinguished from deliberate silence, deception or simple ignorance.

Reduced version: observation → private claim → queued dispatch → checkpoint/recovery → delivery → selective wake-up → agenda/travel consequence. This stays entirely in Ouros semantic world state and requires no AutoPTU tactical capability.

Full version: if the warning arrives after the receiver enters structured danger, AutoPTU owns resolution. Ranged/AOE positioning depends on targeting/footprints/range/LoS. Ordinary traversal uses base movement legality. Interception, push, pull, knockback or forced movement depend on complete movement. Attack/type/stat arithmetic uses core calculations. Structured order uses action economy/initiative. Delayed phase consequences use full turn/round lifecycle. Persistent HP/injury effects require the stateful damage pipeline. Afflictions require status lifecycle. Mechanical terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions require that family. Bespoke Moves, Abilities, Items and Trainer Features each require their respective capability. Autonomous combat selection requires AI tactical policy. Visible authoritative execution requires Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback.

A content author can ship the reduced premise first and add richer mechanics only as their exact capability families become verified.

Valid narrative failures include a genuinely unavailable channel, a message that arrives too late, a route changing while the message is in flight, or a local conversation waiting for Minecraft ACK. Invalid technical failures include a crash erasing memory, duplicating delivery or creating knowledge for a bystander.

Open canon questions remain intentionally unresolved: which Ouros institutions operate reliable dispatch services, which areas have fast/slow/no remote communication, and which incidents justify structured danger after a missed warning.
