# The Meter Two Teams Needed — Pass 348

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-08

## Premise

Two legitimate jobs need the same individually tracked field meter during overlapping windows.

One team needs it for a narrow observation window. Another has a preventative-maintenance reservation tied to an upcoming service obligation. Pass 347 proves that the windows conflict. Neither reservation is fraudulent and neither team is automatically at fault.

A scoped institutional actor has current authority to allocate that instrument window.

## Scene structure

The player or nearby NPCs can establish:
- which exact meter is contested;
- both active reservation IDs;
- the purpose and timing evidence behind each commitment;
- whether an alternate meter is actually available and operationally ready;
- which actor has current authority over the contested resource window;
- which policy/evidence that actor is allowed to use;
- what decision was recorded;
- which downstream changes still have not happened.

The allocator can preserve the proposed field window, preserve one existing reservation, or defer while more evidence is gathered.

The decision record does not cancel the losing reservation. That separate action must still occur through its owner. This allows a later communication failure, stale schedule, voluntary release, replacement unit or further negotiation to remain possible.

## Consequences

A one-off conflict can end with a mundane schedule adjustment.

Repeated conflicts involving the same equipment family can accumulate evidence for:
- procurement pressure;
- maintenance-capacity expansion;
- longer booking lead times;
- revised local scheduling policy;
- cross-team frustration;
- new substitution practices;
- requests for a second validated unit.

None of these consequences are automatic reputation or morality changes.

## Reduced tactical variant

If an immediate wild actor blocks ordinary passage, Narrative can compile an audited battle using stable geometry and individually verified content. The meter remains Narrative-owned semantic cargo outside tactical item semantics.

Allowed narrow outcomes can include:
- `IMMEDIATE_ROUTE_CLEAR`;
- `IMMEDIATE_ROUTE_THREAT_WITHDREW`;
- `FIELD_TEAM_CAN_CONTINUE`.

The battle cannot decide:
- which team deserved the meter;
- whether the allocation decision was procedurally valid;
- whether a reservation is cancelled;
- who owns the meter;
- whether the institution should buy another unit;
- later relationship consequences.
