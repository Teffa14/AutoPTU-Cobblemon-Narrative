# The Window Someone Else Needed — Pass 347

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON

This proposal extends the existing Marea equipment thread without changing established canon.

Existing facts reused:
- Teo Lark maintains field instruments at Puerto Bruma repair row;
- Dr. Nerea Sol works from Estación Mirador;
- Ema Rey performs equipment checks and field work under Nerea's protocols;
- Puerto Bruma and Mirador are already connected through the persistent Marea network.

Everything about the specific conflict below remains proposed.

## Premise

Ema's original pickup window for a serviced field meter no longer works. She sends a reschedule request to Teo. The message arrives. Teo can accommodate the later handoff from his own schedule.

The later window, however, overlaps a separate active reservation for that exact meter.

The second reservation belongs to another legitimate piece of work. Nobody double-booked the instrument maliciously. Teo's personal availability and the instrument's allocation availability are different facts.

## Playable question

The interesting problem begins after the social answer appears easy.

Teo can say that the later meeting time works for him while the resource ledger says the meter is already committed elsewhere.

The player can help establish:
- whether the other reservation is still active;
- whether it refers to the same stable meter or merely the same equipment type;
- whether the reservation supports the same request or an unrelated obligation;
- whether another verified-ready unit exists;
- whether either job can move without inventing authority to reprioritize it;
- which participants have actually received the updated schedule.

## Possible continuations

A clean resolution can use another currently available instrument, another finite window, a voluntary release by the other reservation holder, or a different sequence of field tasks.

A messier but still ordinary branch occurs when each team has a reason its work cannot move. The scene then becomes a negotiation or escalation to the institution that actually owns scheduling authority.

The conflict itself must not assign blame, rivalry or deception. Those consequences require later evidence.

## Persistent consequences

If Ema waits, her field itinerary changes.

If another unit is offered, the handoff must reference that unit explicitly rather than rewriting the original request.

If the other reservation is released, the release remains in the reservation history.

If the two jobs repeatedly compete for the same scarce instruments, that pattern can later justify procurement, maintenance-capacity or scheduling-policy storylines without retroactively claiming either team caused the shortage.
