# Marea/Sendero delegated stewardship investigation loop — Pass 277

Status: PROPOSED. Fixture-backed narrative pattern. No canon effect.

Premise

An ecological concern in Sendero requires recurring observation and maintenance of temporary field markers. The authority that owns the relevant function cannot perform every field visit, so it issues a narrow, time-limited delegation to another actor. The narrative tension comes from scope, evidence and accountability rather than from inventing a permanent institution.

Canon guard

The authority, delegate, assignment, markers, reporting system and exact ecological concern remain fixture-only. The existing authoritative Fletchling population remains 12. Nothing in this proposal establishes a player guild, ranger service, stewardship office, permit regime, ownership right or route law.

Reduced loop

1. The player receives evidence that a local ecological concern needs repeated field observation.
2. The competent authority cannot cover every visit and offers a bounded assignment: observe a named microhabitat and maintain temporary non-mechanical markers.
3. The assignment UI makes the limits visible. Observation and marker maintenance are allowed; relocation, closure, capture authorization and tactical enforcement are reserved.
4. During fieldwork, another helpful actor offers to take over one visit. If the delegation forbids subdelegation, handing over the authority-bearing part of the job is invalid even if the helper is competent. The player can still collaborate on ordinary non-authority tasks.
5. Completing the visible field action is not the whole assignment. Provenance and a change log are required so later observers can distinguish ecological change from maintenance history.
6. A missing report creates a correctable process deficiency. It does not announce that the ecology failed or that the player acted maliciously.
7. A later review can renew the assignment explicitly, narrow it, change its obligations, let it expire or revoke one function while preserving others.
8. When the mandate ends, the world keeps historical traces that genuinely persist under their owning contracts. Authority to perform future delegated actions ends separately.

Player-facing value

This creates an MMO role beyond combat without turning jobs into disposable fetch quests. A player or group can build a history of reliable ecological fieldwork while still needing explicit authority for each standing mandate.

Trust becomes evidence-backed. NPCs can cite completed reports, missed obligations, corrected deficiencies and prior reviews instead of using a generic reputation score as omniscient truth.

Repeated assignments can create relationships and future opportunities without automatically creating property rights, government office or permanent control of the habitat.

Decision variants

A conscientious delegate completes the field task and reporting. Review evidence supports a clean renewal.

A competent delegate performs the ecological work but misses a documentation obligation. The next beat is corrective evidence, not immediate villainization.

A delegate repeatedly pushes outside scope. The authority can narrow or partially revoke the mandate while leaving valid historical work intact.

An external disturbance makes the assignment temporarily impossible. Capacity/feasibility is recorded separately from compliance intent.

A helper is capable and trusted but lacks subdelegated authority. The player can ask the delegator for an amended instrument instead of treating competence as permission.

Longer-term arc potential

If Ouros later canonizes institutions or player organizations, this contract can support stewardship careers with escalating function classes. Advancement would mean access to explicitly broader mandates after evidence and review, not a magical global rank.

A mature arc could involve several mandates from different authorities, conflicting reporting standards, a mandate renewal during ecological uncertainty, or an agreement that allocates costs among parties. Those additions should link Pass 276 and the agreements/mediation layer instead of widening this fixture into canon.

Environmental storytelling

Minecraft/Cobblemon can show dated marker states, replaced signs, observation posts, NPC schedules and changed access messaging. These are projections of authorized Ouros state.

A rope, sign, vest, badge, camp or guard model has no PTU collision, interception, zone or reaction semantics unless a verified mechanical path supplies them.

Mechanically rich version

If a later quest turns a stewardship dispute into a tactical encounter, dependencies become explicit:

- targeting/footprints/range/LoS for tactical selection or detection;
- base movement legality for ordinary traversal;
- complete movement for push/pull/knockback/interception/forced movement;
- core calculations for adopted deterministic PTU arithmetic;
- action economy/initiative and full turn/round lifecycle for structured sequencing;
- full stateful damage pipeline and status lifecycle for persistent combat consequences;
- terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions for mechanical protected areas or environmental effects;
- exact move-specific behavior, Abilities, Items and Trainer Features/perks when invoked;
- AI legal-action infrastructure for legal choices;
- AI tactical policy for autonomous enforcement, escort, withdrawal or confrontation decisions;
- Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback for live projection.

The reduced investigation loop uses none of those tactical families beyond ordinary world presentation.

Unresolved canon questions

What appeal or correction route exists after suspension or revocation?
