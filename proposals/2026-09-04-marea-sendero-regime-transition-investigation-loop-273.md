# Marea/Sendero regime-transition investigation loop — Pass 273

Status: PROPOSED NARRATIVE/QUEST STRUCTURE. No canon promotion.

Pass 272 allows a field team to discover that a response has not returned after a pressure ends. Pass 273 turns that unresolved state into a longer investigation without deciding in advance that the ecosystem has crossed a threshold.

The player first revisits a known Marea/Sendero microhabitat after a documented disturbance has ceased. A response dimension remains shifted across several comparable semantic windows. An NPC or field note may propose that the place has “changed permanently”, but Ouros records only a persistent-shift or alternate-state hypothesis.

A second stage asks what could maintain the new configuration. Evidence may support a local feedback candidate, while other observations can contradict it or reveal a secondary pressure. The useful story tension comes from causal uncertainty: removing the original problem was necessary, yet may not have been sufficient.

The third stage tests reversibility. Simply restoring the initiating driver to its previous range may fail to return the response. That single failure still does not establish hysteresis. A better investigation repeats matched assessments, then performs a targeted intervention against the supported feedback. If the return path differs materially from the departure path across adequate comparisons, the fixture can support a narrow hysteresis interpretation.

This structure produces several outcomes without changing the narrative premise. The evidence can remain inconclusive; a secondary pressure can explain the apparent non-recovery; ordinary delayed recovery can resume; a local regime transition can become narrowly supported; or a feedback-targeted intervention can reveal return-path asymmetry. None of these requires a population change.

The reduced implementation uses already-authorized ecological sources, semantic time, Pass 271 comparison records, Pass 272 recovery trajectories, evidence/provenance and Cobblemon/Minecraft presentation. It can be played as repeated field visits, environmental clues, NPC disagreement and intervention follow-up without AutoPTU.

The full version may allow wildlife to react tactically while the player changes the site. Targeting/footprints/range/LoS is required for tactical detection. Ordinary approach uses base movement legality. Push, pull, knockback, interception, blocking or forced movement require complete movement. Structured intervention timing uses action economy/initiative and, where state crosses phases, full turn/round lifecycle. Persistent damage or status effects require their exact pipelines. Tactical environmental semantics require terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions. Moves, Abilities, Items and Trainer Features/perks enter only when an exact verified mechanism is used. Autonomous wildlife choices require AI tactical policy in addition to legal-action infrastructure. Live enactment requires the Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback path.

The narrative presentation should never display a scientific-strength label such as “hysteresis confirmed” to the player merely because an internal fixture reaches a support disposition. Player-facing language can remain evidence-based: the site persists in a different condition; one feedback is supported; reversing the original pressure did not retrace the earlier path; further follow-up remains possible.

Until sourced and approved, every concrete mechanism remains fixture-only.
