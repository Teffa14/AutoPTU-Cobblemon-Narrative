# What You Bring Back Changes What Comes Next — Pass 418

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON

Research provenance: research/2026-09-10-partial-return-consequence-scan-418.md

Premise

A field assignment asks for several distinct observations, samples, confirmations or deliveries. The party returns before collecting everything. The outing still changed the world because somebody now possesses new evidence, spent time and resources, learned that one approach is unsafe, or established that another lead deserves attention.

Persistent-world structure

Each requested component has its own world identity and provenance. Returning one component does not imply that the others were completed. Reporting an unsuccessful search is also information when the search actually occurred and its limits are recorded.

The receiving NPC reacts from knowledge they truly receive. A partial report may create a revised obligation, a safer route, a request for specialist help, a later expedition or a decision to stop pursuing one lead. Another named NPC may act off-screen only when their knowledge, schedule, permissions, travel and motivations support that action.

No global QUEST_COMPLETE flag rewrites the history of the outing. Completion can be derived when the relevant owner system has enough durable facts to satisfy its own requirement.

Possible canon surfaces

Marea Field Office can receive field observations and practical assistance reports. Estación Mirador can provide route observations. Tideglass Archive can preserve attributed records. Loma Clara Producers Cooperative can provide ordinary logistical context. These are existing surfaces only. This proposal adds no canon incident, assignment, item, species event or NPC duty.

Reduced playable version

The assignment can run entirely through semantic time, travel, schedules, obligations, permissions, private knowledge, durable memory, explicit communications, provenance and persistent observations. An outing may end early and still produce meaningful follow-up content.

Full encounter version

A later implementation may place one component behind a tactical retrieval, escort, protection or extraction objective. The narrative premise remains unchanged when that tactical layer is unavailable: the reduced version represents the same field problem through authored world state and explicit reports.

Capability dependencies

Targeting/footprints/range/LoS: VERIFIED only in audited scopes if the rich encounter uses attacks or targeted actions.

Base movement legality: VERIFIED only in audited scopes.

Complete movement including push/pull/knockback/interception/forced movement: PARTIAL. Any rescue reposition, dragging, interception or forced displacement depends on this family.

Core calculations: VERIFIED only in audited producing paths.

Action economy/initiative: PARTIAL. AutoPTU-Java now has a frozen Python oracle for replacement initiative insertion, but that is evidence for a narrow switching seam rather than family completeness.

Full turn/round lifecycle: PARTIAL. Timed multi-round protection or extraction remains gated.

Full stateful damage pipeline: PARTIAL. Persistent damage consequences remain gated by exact producing path.

Status lifecycle: PARTIAL.

Terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions: MIXED/PARTIAL/BLOCKING by exact behavior. Environmental pressure cannot be assumed from a representative implementation.

Move-specific behavior: individually gated.

Abilities: PARTIAL and individually gated.

Items: individually gated.

Trainer Features/perks: individually gated.

AI legal-action infrastructure: VERIFIED only for audited ordinary actions. Specialized retrieve, escort, protect, carry, interact or extract actions need explicit admission.

AI tactical policy: BLOCKING for objective-aware protection, retrieval priority, evidence-preserving withdrawal and disengage-after-objective policies without dedicated contracts.

Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback: PARTIAL/BLOCKING for authoritative tactical objective state, specialized interactables and in-flight battle recovery. Minecraft may present authored clues and outcomes but does not infer PTU truth.

Longer arc use

Repeated partial returns can reveal competence, caution, overconfidence or changing priorities through actual decisions instead of static reputation tags. A relationship may change because a character reliably reports limitations, because they conceal missing evidence, or because they repeatedly accept unsafe follow-up work. Any such change still needs an explicit social/provenance transaction rather than automatic sentiment from quest percentage.

Canon questions left open

Which institution owns the first suitable assignment, if any; whether the components are observations, physical samples or documents; what threshold counts as enough evidence for the owner; and whether a future rich encounter uses any structured PTU objective at all.
