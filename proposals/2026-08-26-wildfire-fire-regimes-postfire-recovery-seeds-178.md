## Long arc: Five Reviews of Meridian Burn Unit

A management unit accumulates five reviews over fifteen years. The first uses coarse field plots. The second adds aerial imagery. The third follows a prescribed treatment. The fourth must reconcile a reburn and a relocated monitoring station. The fifth revises confidence in the regional fire-regime interpretation without changing any historic event record.

## Long arc: The Prescribed Fire Ledger

A conservation institution keeps a decade of planned burns, cancellations, partial implementations and follow-up monitoring. One project is operationally perfect but ecologically mixed. Another is visually irregular yet supports its objective. Later staff improve decisions because the old reasons and methods remain visible.

## Long arc: Three Maps of the Same Fire

An emergency perimeter map, a final fire footprint and a later severity product become confused in public discussion. Over several years researchers, archivists and residents learn that all three can be correct for different purposes.
