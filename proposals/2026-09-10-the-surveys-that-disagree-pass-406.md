# The Surveys That Disagree — Pass 406

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Canon effect: NONE

Several small field assignments were completed across different semantic windows. Some were handled by the player. Others were completed by persistent NPC teams. Some may never have been accepted. Months later, two offices use the surviving reports to recommend incompatible responses to the same regional problem.

Neither office needs to be corrupt or foolish. One may possess a survey from before a route repair while the other holds a later observation. A shared instrument may have changed hands between those visits. One team may know why a measurement was taken while another has only the archived result. The dispute becomes a provenance problem: establish when each observation was made, who actually had the relevant equipment, which site revision it describes, who received the result and what evidence never existed because a job was left undone.

The player can investigate the disagreement without a predetermined faction winner. A new expedition may confirm one recommendation, show that both were locally correct at different times, or reveal that both lacked an important observation. Previous optional work changes available evidence rather than assigning an omniscient reputation score.

The reduced version needs no AutoPTU battle. It uses semantic time, persistent observations, private knowledge, communication, archive lookup, site revisions, travel, reservations, custody and bounded holder-history evidence. If a survey was never performed, the missing measurement remains missing.

The rich version sends a mixed team back to one disputed site while conditions are changing. The intended goal is to obtain a defensible new observation and leave safely. Combat, if it occurs, is pressure around that objective rather than the automatic victory condition.

Targeting/footprints/range/LoS is required for tactical protection and measurement positions and may use only verified scopes. Base movement legality is required for ordinary traversal. Complete movement is required for interception, dragging, push/pull, knockback, forced movement or rescue reposition. Core calculations are required for ordinary audited resolution. Action economy/initiative remains partial and is required for contested sequencing. Full turn/round lifecycle is required for timed observation windows, delayed collapse or staged evacuation. Full stateful damage pipeline is required if injuries persist beyond the encounter. Status lifecycle is required for complex continuing conditions. Terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions is required for any active wind, flooding, unstable ground, visibility changes, triggered zones or reaction hazards. Move-specific behavior, abilities, items and Trainer Features/perks remain individually gated.

AI legal-action infrastructure may use only its verified ordinary scopes. AI tactical policy is blocking for observe-first, preserve-evidence, protect-surveyor, rescue-first, avoid-damaging-site, objective-aware withdrawal and disengage-after-objective. Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback support is partial/blocking for persistent measurement objectives, environmental revisions, evidence interaction and authoritative non-KO completion.

Open canon questions include the institutions involved, the disputed location, the original purpose of each survey, the environmental or infrastructural change between observations, the specific resource used, species present, whether combat occurs, and which PTU Skills/Edges/Features or Items are relevant.
