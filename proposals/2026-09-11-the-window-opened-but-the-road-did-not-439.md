# The Window Opened but the Road Did Not — Pass 439

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Canon effect: NONE
Location assignment: UNRESOLVED

## Premise

A specialist has already accepted a future assistance window. Before that window begins, the specialist receives credible evidence that the intended access route is unavailable. The closure remains unresolved when the promised start minute arrives.

The narrative tension comes from two durable truths existing together: the promise is real, and normal fulfillment is currently blocked.

## Reduced version

This version requires no battle resolution.

Before the appointment, the responder records a route/access blocker with provenance. A start watch survives ordinary world progression and wakes only that responder at the accepted start minute. The latest viability evidence is still `BLOCKED`, so the world records `BLOCKED_AT_START`.

The agreement remains unchanged.

The responder must then make a new world-level choice using current knowledge and circumstances. Possible future choices can include contacting the requester, waiting, looking for another legitimate route, asking another actor for information, proposing new terms or eventually reporting a missed obligation. Pass 439 does not preselect any of those actions.

The requester does not learn the problem automatically. Knowledge still requires ordinary communication or direct observation.

## Rich version

The same premise can expand into a field scene if the actors choose to attempt fulfillment despite the obstruction.

Possible structures include a detour survey, escorting qualified personnel through a safe alternative, retrieving required equipment from another access point, protecting an inspection team, or extracting someone if conditions deteriorate.

The objective should remain explicit. Defeating every nearby Pokémon is not automatically the goal.

## Environmental storytelling

The obstruction should leave evidence that exists whether or not combat occurs. Examples include a marked closure, damaged crossing, changed waterline, abandoned maintenance equipment, disturbed Pokémon movement or contradictory local reports.

Those clues can explain why the promise became difficult while still leaving room for uncertainty about cause, duration and responsibility.

## Consequence structure

The story should preserve later distinctions between unavoidable obstruction, preventable lateness, poor communication, intentional abandonment and successful recovery.

A later faction, requester or rival can react differently depending on the evidence chain. That reaction should be based on what they actually know, not on omniscient quest state.

The reduced form needs semantic-time commitments, viability evidence, start-time wake-up, private knowledge, communication, ordinary world replanning and persistence.
