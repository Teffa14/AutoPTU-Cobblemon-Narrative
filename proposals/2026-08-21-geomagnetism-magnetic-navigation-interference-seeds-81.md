# Geomagnetism, Magnetic Navigation & Interference Seeds

Status: NON-CANON PROPOSALS.

Pass: 81

These are original Ouros candidates derived from abstract research patterns. They do not copy external plots, characters or dialogue. Every PTU effect remains subject to exact rules validation.

## 01 — The Compass That Aged

A route guide used by generations of hikers has become steadily less reliable even though the trail itself has not moved. The explanation may be a changed magnetic correction, a moved landmark, instrument drift or a local anomaly.

Useful systems: Cartography, Travel, Science, Geomagnetism.

## 02 — Observatory Fence

A magnetic observatory begins producing noisy readings after a neighboring workshop expands. The workshop may be operating correctly. The conflict is about measurement quality, land use and mitigation rather than villainy.

Useful systems: Geomagnetism, Technology, Public Works, Agreements.

## 03 — The Probopass Shift

A Probopass begins appearing near a relay station at the same time that some instruments behave erratically. The species is a plausible hypothesis, but several failures began before the first confirmed sighting.

Useful systems: Pokémon Agency, Technology, Science, Geomagnetism.

## 04 — Clefairy Field Night

A recurring Clefairy gathering produces a measurable but short-lived local magnetic anomaly. Researchers, photographers and residents disagree about whether the event should be publicized heavily.

Useful systems: Astronomy, Photography, Tourism, Conservation, Geomagnetism.

No battle effect is implied.

## 05 — The Wrong North

Two expedition teams return with incompatible bearings from the same pass. Both records are internally consistent. Their maps were printed in different decades.

Useful systems: Cartography, Archives, Science, Geomagnetism.

## 06 — Aurora Ferry Delay

A visible aurora coincides with degraded navigation and communications. A ferry operator delays departures while tourists gather at the waterfront to watch the sky.

Useful systems: Maritime, Astronomy, Communications, Tourism, Geomagnetism.

## 07 — The Silent Magnetometer

One instrument stops reporting during an otherwise ordinary period. The redundant station still works. Recovering the instrument becomes a field job; interpreting the gap remains a science task.

Useful systems: Science, Workplaces, Travel, Geomagnetism.

## 08 — The Mineral Ridge

A mapping survey discovers a persistent local magnetic deviation above a metal-rich formation. The geological finding becomes useful for navigation history and mineral research without creating a battle hazard.

Useful systems: Geology, Cartography, Science, Geomagnetism.

## 09 — Mini-Nose Survey

A known Probopass is repeatedly observed sending its Mini-Noses through a narrow valley. Researchers want to understand the pattern. The observations must not be converted into a generic species mechanic.

Useful systems: Pokémon Agency, Photography, Science.

## 10 — The Broken Calibration Year

A year's magnetic dataset appears to show a major regional shift. Later inspection reveals that part of the station foundation moved after nearby ground work. The corrupted dataset stays preserved with a corrected interpretation.

Useful systems: Archives, Science, Infrastructure, Geomagnetism.

## 11 — Compass Market

A town famous for route guides sells compasses calibrated to its region. Visitors assume they are defective when they use them far away without correction.

Useful systems: Material Culture, Tourism, Cartography, Geomagnetism.

## 12 — Night Train Static

A railway's communication system develops intermittent noise on specific nights. A magnetic event, worn equipment, nearby industrial loads and Pokémon activity are all initially plausible.

Useful systems: Technology, Communications, Travel, Cases, Geomagnetism.

## 13 — Magnezone Signal Archive

An observatory has decades of unexplained signal observations that sometimes coincide with Magnezone presence. The records are real; the interpretation remains unresolved.

Useful systems: Archives, Science, Pokémon Agency, Digital Systems.

## 14 — The Compassless Guide

A veteran guide deliberately navigates by terrain, stars and route memory after local compass readings become unreliable. Their expertise becomes valuable without creating a numeric navigation bonus by narrative decree.

Useful systems: Travel, Astronomy, Cartography, Social Bonds.

## 15 — Aurora Market Week

A forecasted period of strong aurora draws visitors and temporary vendors. The event succeeds culturally even if no unusual Pokémon appear.

Useful systems: Tourism, Food/Hospitality, Public Events, Astronomy, Geomagnetism.

## 16 — The Steel Storage Complaint

A warehouse reports small metal objects shifting from their marked positions. Staff blame a nearby magnetic Pokémon. Inventory records show that some discrepancies are ordinary handling errors.

Useful systems: Material Culture, Workplaces, Cases, Geomagnetism.

## 17 — Route Marker Reversal

Old magnetic route markers installed by an expedition are discovered pointing differently from modern survey expectations. The original crew may have used a now-obsolete correction rather than installing them incorrectly.

Useful systems: Archives, Cartography, Public Memory.

## 18 — Observatory Blackout Buffer

A settlement wants to build housing closer to an old magnetic observatory. The observatory argues that local interference would reduce data quality. The question becomes a planning dispute with evidence and compromise options.

Useful systems: Civic Governance, Housing, Science, Agreements.

## 19 — The Clefairy False Alarm

A sudden magnetic reading occurs on a full-moon night and residents assume Clefairy are gathering. Field teams find no confirmed group. The anomaly still requires explanation.

Useful systems: Astronomy, Rumors, Science, Geomagnetism.

## 20 — Magneton Without Evolution

A long-monitored Magneton spends months in a known anomaly zone and does not evolve. The null result becomes important evidence against a simplistic local theory.

Useful systems: Evolution, Science, Pokémon Agency.

No evolution eligibility is invented.

## 21 — The Old Air Route Correction

Historic pilots used a magnetic correction printed on route cards. The route is now being reopened, and the archived correction cannot simply be reused.

Useful systems: Aerial, Archives, Cartography, Credentials.

## 22 — Aurora Clinic Night

A geomagnetic event disrupts parts of a settlement's communications while the clinic remains operational. The story tests redundancy and staffing rather than creating an electromagnetic medical condition.

Useful systems: Care, Communications, Technology, Crisis.

## 23 — The Magnetic Survey Club

Students maintain a small network of observation points and compare readings over seasons. Their work can eventually reveal an instrument problem, a local anomaly or ordinary long-term change.

Useful systems: Education, Clubs, Science, Geomagnetism.

## 24 — Mining Compass Drift

Workers in an underground complex notice compasses behaving differently in separate galleries. The effect may be local geology, equipment, wiring or mapping assumptions.

Useful systems: Geology, Subterranean, Workplaces, Geomagnetism.

## 25 — The Public Aurora Map

A media outlet publishes a simplified aurora-visibility map. The map is useful but gets mistaken for a guaranteed schedule. Corrections become part of public memory.

Useful systems: Media, Astronomy, Cartography, Geomagnetism.

## 26 — The Relay That Worked

During a strong regional geomagnetic event, one old relay continues functioning because of an earlier upgrade that players helped fund. A small past infrastructure choice becomes a meaningful callback.

Useful systems: Technology, Finance, Chronicle, Geomagnetism.

## 27 — The Wandering Calibration Point

A reference marker used by surveyors appears physically intact but no longer reproduces expected measurements. The investigation eventually touches geology, construction history and instrument records.

Useful systems: Science, Geology, Architecture, Archives.

## 28 — Released Magnemite Observation

A previously released Magnemite is photographed near a monitoring site years later. Its identity must be confirmed before connecting it to earlier partnership history or current interference.

Useful systems: Pokémon Agency, Photography, Science.

## 29 — The Magnetic Quiet

A region known for small daily variation becomes unusually stable for several weeks. Nothing breaks. The absence of expected variation becomes a scientific question rather than an emergency.

Useful systems: Science, Downtime, Geomagnetism.

## 30 — The Three Norths Exhibit

A museum builds an exhibit showing geographic north, magnetic north and the changing correction used by explorers across different eras. A disagreement about an old expedition route becomes understandable once visitors see the historical field context.

Useful systems: Archives/Museums, Cartography, Public Memory, Geomagnetism.

# Long arc A — The Meridian Survey

Year 1: A new survey reveals that several old route maps use corrections that no longer match current observations.

Year 2: The region installs a small magnetic-observatory network. One site suffers local interference from new construction.

Year 3: A geomagnetic event produces an unusual aurora and exposes weaknesses in communications/navigation infrastructure.

Year 4: Archived expedition records are reinterpreted using historical field revisions. A supposed navigation mystery becomes partly explainable.

Year 5: The region publishes a new atlas with both current corrections and historical provenance. Some local anomalies remain unresolved.

The arc can end with better knowledge rather than a solved cosmic mystery.

# Long arc B — The Signal Valley

A valley accumulates overlapping evidence: Magnezone observations, relay noise, local mineral anomalies and irregular instrument readings.

Different institutions build competing hypotheses. Players can support surveys, equipment recovery, Pokémon observation and archival work.

The final outcome may show multiple causes. No single Pokémon or faction needs to be responsible for everything.

# Long arc C — Nights of the Low Aurora

A sequence of rare aurora events changes tourism, public culture and infrastructure planning over several years.

The first event is treated as spectacle. Later events expose communication vulnerabilities, create new scientific programs and inspire traditions. Eventually the region can prepare without treating every aurora as a crisis.
