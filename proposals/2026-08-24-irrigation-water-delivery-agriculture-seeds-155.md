# Pass 155 Proposals — Irrigation & Agricultural Water Delivery

Status: NON-CANON / PROPOSED. Requires explicit canon approval before becoming established Ouros lore.

These candidates extend the research and systems layer without defining universal water law, crop mechanics or PTU environmental rules.

## Worldbuilding and quest candidates

1. The Field That Stayed Dry — the source and main canal are normal; a forgotten lateral was never reopened after maintenance.
2. Two Gauges, One Delivery — upstream and field measurements disagree because one instrument was recalibrated recently.
3. The Turnout With Three Names — three generations use different names for the same persistent control point.
4. The Wet Canal, Dry Orchard — water passes beside the farm but its scheduled delivery never begins.
5. The Orchard That Receives Water Last — a recurring rotation is locally normal, not evidence of neglect.
6. The Delivery Window Moved — drought response shifts timing and collides with a festival or work schedule.
7. Lotad on the Maintenance Day — a local Pokémon group carries water during a short outage, but staff still need to repair the network.
8. The Headgate Nobody Touched — a shortfall initially blamed on an operator turns out to originate farther upstream.
9. Tailwater at Dusk — water observed downstream hours later becomes a Freshwater investigation rather than proof of waste.
10. The Seepage That Became Habitat — a long-lived wet strip beside an old lateral develops ecological value before the leak is repaired.
11. The Repair That Changed the Spring — reduced canal seepage is followed months later by a groundwater observation; causation remains under study.
12. One Breach, Three Consequences — delivery interruption, road damage and a temporary wildlife refuge all arise from the same physical event.
13. The Turnout Still Works — the obsolete control remains functional after the destination field changes use.
14. The Farmer Who Asked for Less — an actor voluntarily changes a delivery request after new crop or soil observations.
15. The Schedule on the Wall Is Old — a public board shows the previous revision while operators use the current one.
16. The New Field Has No Lateral — agricultural expansion creates demand before conveyance capacity exists.
17. The Pump Is Fine — a delivery fails because the intake condition changed, not because the pump broke.
18. The Water Arrived After Harvest — an administrative or routing delay becomes institutional memory rather than a villain plot.
19. The Wildlife Crossing at the Ditch — repeated observations show a lateral functioning as a movement corridor at particular times.
20. The Emergency Bypass — a temporary route solves one season and later becomes a debate over whether to make it permanent.
21. The Buried Turnout — redevelopment uncovers a historical irrigation asset whose records belong to another boundary revision.
22. The Return Flow Nobody Counted — a downstream wetland receives seasonal water linked only later to agricultural return flow.
23. The Clear Water, Different Chemistry — visual appearance looks normal; any quality question is handed to the proper environmental layer.
24. The Canal Crew Remembers — oral history preserves an old operating sequence that surviving records do not explain fully.
25. The Replacement Operator — succession/handoff exposes undocumented knowledge without proving wrongdoing.
26. The Pokémon Helper Opts Out — a working Pokémon pauses participation and the institution uses relief coverage instead of forcing the task.
27. The Same Delivery, New Crop — delivery volume looks similar but Food/Agriculture interprets it differently after crop rotation.
28. Three Shortfalls, Three Causes — one caused by source state, one by conveyance loss, one by bad measurement.
29. The Lateral Atlas — decades of maps retain branches that were real in their own periods.
30. Nothing Happened at Turnout Four — a completely routine season becomes the baseline used years later to diagnose a subtle change.

## Longer arcs

### Five Summers on Cedar Lateral

Year one establishes normal requests, measurements and maintenance. Year two brings a drought-driven schedule revision. Year three a repair reduces seepage and a nearby wet patch changes. Year four a wildlife movement pattern shifts around the lateral. Year five a new agricultural block requests service and forces the institution to compare expansion with existing delivery capacity.

The arc can remain mostly procedural and ecological. No mastermind is required.

### The Water That Returns

A single diversion is followed through headgate, canal, field, tailwater and later groundwater/surface-water observations. Different institutions initially hold different pieces of the story. The arc teaches the player that water leaving a field has not necessarily disappeared and that delayed effects require provenance rather than instant causation.

### Three Generations at Meridian Headgate

The same control asset survives multiple operators, renamed fields, revised maps, changing crops and one major repair. Older operating notes remain useful but not universally correct. The story can connect Workplaces, Succession, Archives, Metrology, Agriculture and Freshwater without turning the gate into a magical landmark.

## Encounter contracts

### Headgate Failure During Delivery Window

FULL: technicians must reach two control points while changing water access and withdrawing wildlife modify safe routes. Requires complete movement, environmental family if water/mud is tactical, AI tactical policy and Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics playback.

REDUCED: shut the intake in world state, evacuate noncombatants, freeze a dry adjacent arena, resolve a conventional battle if needed, then resume repairs and delivery accounting.

Narrative invariant: the conflict delays or complicates operation. Victory does not repair the headgate or prove the cause.

### Canal Breach at Orchard Reach

FULL: changing breach geometry, moving water/debris, workers retreating and possible route protection. Requires complete movement; terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions if the breach affects combat; tactical AI; adapter/playback.

REDUCED: isolate the breach, stop delivery, move workers clear and freeze the map. AutoPTU receives only real combatants on stable ground.

Narrative invariant: orchard water service remains a world-state problem after battle.

### Wildlife at the Lateral During Drought Rotation

FULL: a wild group uses the lateral during a constrained delivery period. Actors need CROSS/WITHDRAW/PROTECT_ROUTE behavior rather than default KO logic. Complete movement, tactical AI and playback are blocking; environment only if water depth/mud is mechanically relevant.

REDUCED: resolve wildlife movement and access outside combat. If a confrontation remains, use a static encounter. Capture, KO or despawn never writes `irrigation_problem_solved`.

### Delivery Record Reconciliation

Primarily non-combat. Metrology, Archives, Irrigation and Freshwater compare source readings, turnout notes and field observations. The valid result may be `UNRESOLVED` or “two records describe different points in the chain.”
