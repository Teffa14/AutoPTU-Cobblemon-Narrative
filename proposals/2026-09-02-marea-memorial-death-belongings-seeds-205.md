# Marea Memorial, Death & Belongings Seeds — Pass 205

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-02

These candidates extend current Marea canon without establishing any current resident death, regional funeral law, inheritance system, afterlife model or universal memorial tradition.

## Continuity anchors already canon

Useful existing anchors:
- Tideglass preserves route surveys, oral-history deposits and old market records.
- Taro preserves contradictory testimony and editions.
- Pia performs document circulation and source retrieval.
- Mara coordinates field reports without owning truth.
- Oren handles actual care cases rather than generic healing.
- Teo repairs ordinary equipment.
- Sendero del Vidrio is an old survey road with maintenance history.
- Minecraft actor state cannot author death.

## Recommended first slice: The Label on the Old Field Case

Premise:
Tideglass has an old field-equipment case whose historical user is recorded as deceased in an archived source. The case has two physical labels from different periods. One identifies a user; the later one identifies an institutional storage location. A current catalog summary has compressed those two facts into one sentence that incorrectly implies continuous ownership.

Playable actions:
- inspect the two labels;
- compare the catalog entry with the older accession note;
- ask Taro about what the archive actually knows;
- have Pia retrieve the relevant copy if it is not already at the desk;
- correct the catalog statement while leaving physical custody unchanged.

Persistent outputs:
- provenance correction;
- two label observations;
- historical-user/death claim linkage;
- unresolved current ownership field;
- archive-custody state unchanged.

No battle.
No supernatural claim.
No inheritance decision.
No item reward.

Canon gate:
The historical actor cannot be added as canon automatically. Implementation requires a human-approved historical identity or an already-approved deceased subject from later canon.

## Seed: Marker Before the Repair

A small route-side remembrance marker occupies a place where Teo or a maintenance crew needs temporary access for ordinary repairs.

Narrative tension:
The practical question is how to document, protect and temporarily move the marker without erasing its relation to the site.

Outputs can include before/after documentation, temporary custody and restoration to place.

Do not infer who legally authorizes relocation until Ouros canon establishes that authority.

## Seed: The Name in Two Registers

Tideglass has two memorial or historical registers containing the same name but different dates or role descriptions.

Potential explanations remain open:
- transcription error;
- two different people with the same name;
- later correction;
- one date records death while the other records memorial dedication;
- reused public wording.

The quest ends by narrowing provenance, not by forcing a dramatic hidden truth.

## Seed: Margin Watches the Empty Chair

Taro's Noctowl, Margin, repeatedly occupies a chair during a recurring archive interview block after an older interview participant stops attending because that person is confirmed deceased in an authored future arc.

This behavior can be observed and remembered.

It cannot prove grief, spirit perception, hidden memory transfer or supernatural awareness.

Use only after the relevant death exists in canon.

## Seed: A Companion Without a New Trainer

A persistent Pokémon survives the confirmed death of a Trainer in a future authored arc.

Immediate story questions:
- who is providing ordinary care now;
- where the Pokémon stays;
- which past relationship facts are documented;
- whether the Pokémon participates in public remembrance;
- what claims different actors make about future partnership.

Hard boundary:
The Pokémon is not automatically inherited, captured, reassigned or battle-ready for another Trainer.

## Seed: Flowers, No Explanation

A small maintained planting or object appears regularly near an established remembrance site.

The world can record:
- who tends it when observed;
- what people say it commemorates;
- interruptions in maintenance;
- changes in form across seasons.

The system should not invent a universal mourning custom or assume every visitor shares the same belief.

## Seed: The Old Locker

After a confirmed historical death, an old work locker remains in institutional custody.

Contents are inventoried as individual item instances with former-user provenance. Some may belong to the institution, some may have only been used by the deceased, and some may have unresolved ownership.

This seed is useful for testing `former holder != current owner`.

## Seed: The Notice Was Published First

A public notice reports a death before Tideglass receives the underlying record.

Pass 200 information-circulation rules preserve:
- publication time;
- source lineage;
- correction path if necessary;
- who saw which version.

Pass 205 prevents publication alone from creating final death truth unless the source is authoritative.

## Seed: Ghost at the Marker

A Ghost-type Pokémon is observed near a memorial marker.

Public reactions may include supernatural claims, ecological explanations or uncertainty.

Mirador records only what was observed. Tideglass records what people said. AutoPTU resolves any actual encounter if one occurs.

The Ghost is never identified as the deceased merely from species, location or timing.

## Seed: The Memorial Object Is Missing

A dedicated object used in remembrance is absent from its expected place.

Potential mundane causes:
- repair;
- temporary storage;
- authorized relocation;
- weather displacement;
- catalog error;
- theft only if evidence supports it.

Do not jump immediately to desecration or criminal intent.

## Seed: A Different Inscription

A replacement plaque reproduces an older inscription but changes one phrase.

Taro preserves both versions. Community-tradition and language-continuity layers can record why different people prefer different wording.

The newer inscription does not overwrite the earlier historical artifact.

## Seed: The Route Named After Someone

A local working name for part of Sendero references a deceased historical person or Pokémon.

Cartography rules preserve that local name as a naming claim rather than moving canonical coordinates or proving the story behind the name.

## Seed: One Bell, Several Meanings

A remembrance object has a repeated action associated with it, such as sounding a small bell or leaving a written tag.

Different participants may describe the act differently: remembrance, routine, gratitude, habit or family custom.

Narrative stores the observed action and attributed meaning separately.

No supernatural output.

## Seed: Return the Tool, Keep the Story

A tool once used by a deceased worker is later confirmed to belong to an institution and returns to ordinary service after documentation.

The item's history persists even though it is no longer displayed as a memorial object.

This tests that heritage meaning and practical function can coexist without freezing every old object in a museum.

## Longer arc: What Remains in Marea

This arc should emerge slowly from approved historical deaths and normal institutional continuity.

Possible long-term surfaces:
- changing memorial practices;
- restored route markers;
- archive corrections;
- objects moving between service and display;
- surviving companions with durable identity;
- old addresses or quarters entering history;
- conflicting public recollections;
- Ghost ecology around places people already associate with death.

The arc has no requirement that death be frequent. Its purpose is continuity when death is already part of authored world history.
