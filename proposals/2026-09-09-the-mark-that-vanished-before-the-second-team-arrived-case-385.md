# The Mark That Vanished Before the Second Team Arrived — case 385

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Canon effect: NONE

## Premise

A field researcher visits a persistent site and records a narrow physical mark crossing one exposed surface.

The observation is durable and becomes private knowledge of that researcher because they directly saw it.

Before a second team reaches the same place, the site changes. Water, dust, repair work, shifting material, deliberate covering or another still-unresolved cause obscures the mark.

The second team sees no mark and has no direct basis for believing it was ever there.

The resulting disagreement does not require anyone to lie. Each actor has a valid knowledge state tied to a different physical revision of the same site.

## Evidence and knowledge structure

Revision one exposes the surface.

Observation A records the mark under revision one and names researcher A as observer.

Pass 385 materializes that exact observation into researcher A's private knowledge ledger. No other actor receives it.

Revision two changes the surface and obscures the mark.

Researcher B visits after revision two and records an independent observation that the current exposed surface contains no visible mark.

Both observations remain valid historical evidence. Neither one silently overwrites the other.

An interpretation such as “researcher A fabricated the mark” or “someone deliberately removed it” remains a theory until supported by additional evidence.

## Reduced playable form

The reduced version runs entirely in world-agent systems.

A named NPC travels to the site, records one site observation and receives the corresponding private claim. A later site revision changes access or material condition. Another named NPC visits later and records a different observation.

Progress then uses ordinary private communication, archive access, travel and replanning. The player can interview one or both researchers, compare records, revisit the site or seek another source.

No AutoPTU battle is required.

## Rich encounter form

A richer version can make documentation time-sensitive while the site continues changing. Objectives may include reaching a viewpoint, recording enough evidence before visibility closes, protecting a fragile reference marker, escorting a specialist, recovering equipment or withdrawing once adequate evidence is secured.

Wild Pokémon or hostile actors can create pressure, but success remains tied to observation/documentation rather than defeating every opponent.
