# Marea Sendero divergent Fletchling response loop

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Pass: 256
Canon effect: NONE

## Premise

Repeated human activity on the lower Sendero can make two members of the same wild population behave differently even when both experience the same local disturbance pressure.

One already-known Fletchling may remain visible at a quiet edge after repeated benign observation. Another already-counted but unresolved member may begin withdrawing earlier from similar approaches. The player can discover the divergence through repeated field observation rather than through a quest marker or mandatory battle.

## Reduced playable loop

The player first observes normal lower-shelf activity.

After several close approaches, Pass 255 raises shared disturbance pressure.

Later visits show inconsistent visibility: one familiar individual can still be observed during a quieter edge window while another presentation source withdraws earlier.

Independent observations allow the player to form a hypothesis that individual history matters. The interface may report behavioral evidence such as earlier withdrawal or continued feeding, but never hidden wariness coefficients, response slopes, population totals or persistent actor IDs.

A passive observation can then test whether the pattern persists without adding another disturbance impact.

No battle, capture, demographic change or canon marker is required.

## Consequences

A player who repeatedly pushes closer for better evidence may gradually make some individuals harder to observe while other members become more tolerant of benign presence.

A careful observer can learn that absence from one viewing point is not absence from the population.

NPC researchers can disagree legitimately because they may have observed different individuals or different stimulus histories.

A later conservation or access-policy storyline can use these accumulated histories as evidence without retroactively claiming that early fixture thresholds were canon biology.

## Canon questions
