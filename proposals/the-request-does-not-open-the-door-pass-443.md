# The Request Does Not Open the Door — Pass 443

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Canon effect: NONE
Location assignment: UNRESOLVED
Named NPC assignment: UNRESOLVED
Institution assignment: UNRESOLVED
Pokemon population assignment: UNRESOLVED

Premise

A field specialist has already accepted a local assistance window. Conditions deteriorate and the specialist explicitly asks to renegotiate. The message reaches the requester. The arrival of that message creates a decision point rather than silently reopening the agreement.

Core tension

The requester may still need the original deadline, may be willing to accept a later window, may prefer the previously named alternative, or may decide that reopening terms is acceptable even after the old window expires. The specialist cannot know which position the requester took until a later reply actually arrives.

Reduced version

The full scene works with world-level systems only.

1. An accepted negotiated assistance commitment exists.
2. Viability becomes adverse and the responder chooses `REQUEST_RENEGOTIATION`.
3. The renegotiation request travels through an ordinary information channel.
4. Terminal delivery creates requester knowledge.
5. The requester records either `ACCEPT_RENEGOTIATION_REQUEST` or `REJECT_RENEGOTIATION_REQUEST`.
6. The original commitment remains unchanged.
7. A later communication step must carry the answer back.

Possible narrative consequences after later systems exist

An acceptance can lead to a new time window, different access point, narrower scope, remote alternative, substitute specialist or a fresh independent request. A rejection can preserve the original deadline, lead to documented non-fulfillment, trigger another attempt, or close the local opportunity. None of those outcomes is selected by Pass 443.

Environmental storytelling hook

The place itself can change while the negotiation unfolds. Water level, access barriers, Pokemon behavior, human repair work, weather evidence, temporary signage or traces of an earlier incident can show that the world continued during communication latency. These observations should be authoritative world evidence, not decorations that rewrite the prior agreement.

Character archetype use

The requester can be deadline-sensitive, flexible, risk-averse, institutionally constrained or personally sympathetic without making any one response universally correct. The responder can be reliable but constrained, opportunistic, overcommitted or genuinely surprised by changing conditions. Personality may influence policy later; Pass 443 only records the chosen answer.

Faction dynamic use

An institution may care about the original service window while an individual requester is personally willing to renegotiate. Those are separate pressures. Shared faction membership does not transmit the requester's decision to supervisors or the responder.

Full mechanically rich version

Replacement terms can eventually lead to a hazardous survey, escort, protection task, retrieval, containment or extraction. The narrative premise remains the same: the actors first had to agree that terms could be reopened.
