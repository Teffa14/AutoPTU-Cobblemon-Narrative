# Marea Community Tradition & Oral-History Seeds — Pass 197

Status: PROPOSALS ONLY. NONE OF THESE ARE CANON.
Date: 2026-09-01
Design dependency: `design/community-tradition-oral-history-continuity-layer.md`

## Proposal policy

These seeds use only established Marea places, residents, responsibilities and existing continuity systems where possible.

They do not establish religion, sacred geography, official holidays, festival dates, priesthood, spiritual efficacy, region-wide belief or a universal custom.

A repeated practice becomes stronger evidence for local culture only through authored/observed instances. A resident participating once does not make the practice universal.

## 1. Two Tellings at Tideglass

Status: PROPOSED. Recommended first implementation slice.

Canon anchors:

- Tideglass exists as Marea's archive/history surface;
- Taro and Pia already have provenance/record responsibilities.

Premise:

Two separately sourced accounts describe an older Marea work practice or storm-season habit. Most operational details overlap. One origin claim differs and one step appears in only one telling.

Playable work:

Pia creates a comparison without merging the sources. Taro reviews provenance. A later mundane document can confirm that the disputed step existed by a particular period while leaving its origin unresolved.

Persistent state:

- two oral accounts remain distinct;
- overlapping claims link to one working practice identity;
- one sequence detail becomes better supported;
- the origin remains attributed/unresolved;
- future handouts or public-memory records can cite the disagreement.

Mechanical dependency: none required.

Canon questions deliberately left open:

- exact age of the practice;
- who originated it;
- whether it still occurs;
- whether it had ceremonial meaning.

## 2. The Route Walk That Means Different Things

Status: PROPOSED.

Premise:

Several residents have, on more than one occasion, walked the same portion of Sendero del Vidrio together. One account frames it as an old practical check of the route. Another describes it as something people simply do together at a certain time. Neither source proves a formal ceremony.

Persistent state:

- same observed route sequence;
- separate meaning claims;
- participant histories;
- no universal label.

Reduced implementation: noncombat route walk and conversation only.

Rich implementation: `Community Route Walk at Glass Bend`, dependency-gated in the design/readiness snapshot.

## 3. Visitor Calls It a Shrine

Status: PROPOSED.

Canon anchors:

- visitors can pass through Puerto Bruma under the visitor continuity layer;
- Marea can contain old markers/objects only where separately authored.

Premise:

A visitor sees an old route-side marker and calls it a shrine. A local record only establishes that the object marks a historical stopping/check point. Another resident uses a different everyday label.

The quest is not to prove the visitor wrong. It is to keep their interpretation attributed.

Persistent state:

- visitor label recorded as a claim;
- local documentary label preserved;
- object provenance unchanged;
- no religion or sacred-site canon created.

Mechanical dependency: none.

## 4. The Custom Nobody Scheduled

Status: PROPOSED.

Premise:

Residents repeatedly arrange a small shared activity around an existing work rhythm, but no institution formally schedules or owns it.

One occurrence may happen without the player. Another may be skipped because work runs late.

Design purpose:

Show that social repetition can emerge from actors and schedules rather than a quest-giver or festival authority.

Persistent state:

- observed instances;
- changing participants;
- no organizer invented;
- a skipped instance remains part of history.

Mechanical dependency: none.

## 5. Ivo's Shared Table

Status: PROPOSED.

Canon anchor:

Ivo Solan is already reserved as a future hospitality/festival bridge.

Premise:

On several busy arrival periods, Ivo has helped assemble a practical shared meal or refreshment table using ordinary provisioning. A later visitor remembers it as a distinctive Bruma welcome. Locals may regard it simply as sensible hospitality.

Guardrails:

- do not declare it a festival;
- do not invent a ritual food;
- no food buff;
- no universal hospitality rule;
- stock/provisioning remains authoritative for actual supplies.

Long-term potential:

Repeated evidence could eventually support an authored local custom, but only after canon review.

## 6. Old Route Phrase, New Safety Meaning

Status: PROPOSED.

Premise:

A short phrase historically associated with a familiar route remains in use, but current residents use it primarily as a practical safety cue. Tideglass holds an older occurrence whose surrounding context suggests a different emphasis.

Integration:

- language/translation layer owns wording and interpretation;
- tradition layer owns repeated use;
- safety/procedure systems own current operational meaning.

Do not infer that the phrase is a prayer, oath or magical formula.

## 9. Battle Yard Courtesy

Status: PROPOSED.

Canon anchors:

- Sela operates Bruma Battle Yard;
- Jace works there in a junior role;
- challenge contracts and audited battle formats already exist.

Premise:

Jace learns an informal courtesy commonly used before or after ordinary Yard sessions. Its social value is visible through repeated behavior, but it is not part of the challenge contract and has no PTU effect.

Guardrails:

- no accuracy/initiative/morale bonus;
- no Trainer Feature;
- refusing the courtesy is not automatically misconduct;
- one observed courtesy does not prove ancient tradition.

This gives the Battle Yard culture beyond combat mechanics without changing combat authority.

## 10. Practice Paused, Not Lost

Status: PROPOSED.

Premise:

An expected recurring activity does not happen because of an already legitimate world-state constraint: weather, ferry delay, work pressure, mourning, ecological advisory or unavailable participants.

The server records preparation and cancellation separately.

Persistent consequence:

A later instance can reference the interruption and perhaps adopt a changed procedure.

Design lesson:

Continuity becomes richer when the system can remember that something did not happen.

## 11. Private Version, Public Version

Status: PROPOSED.

Premise:

A household or small-group telling contains details that Tideglass does not have permission or reason to publish. A local-public summary omits them.

The two records are both legitimate for their scopes.

Integration:

- oral-history layer owns teller/account identity;
- privacy/public-memory workflows own publication;
- archive owns custody.

Do not treat the public version as evidence that private details were false or forgotten.

## 12. Ferry Token With Two Stories

Status: PROPOSED.

Premise:

An old ordinary object connected to ferry work has accumulated two explanations for why people kept or displayed examples of it. One explanation is practical; another is social/commemorative.

The object has provenance independent of either story.

Possible player work:

- inspect records;
- compare oral accounts;
- identify when the object actually entered use;
- leave meaning unresolved.

Guardrail:

Do not turn the object into a magical item or universal Marea symbol without explicit canon approval.

## 13. The Empty Place at the Table

Status: PROPOSED.

Premise:

A recurring shared gathering leaves one ordinary place unused after a participant becomes temporarily absent, departs, or dies only if an independently authoritative state establishes that fact.

Different residents may explain the empty place differently: courtesy, habit, expectation of return, remembrance or simply no need to rearrange.

Integration:

- visitor continuity if the actor departed temporarily;
- mourning/private memory if a validated death exists;
- tradition layer for repeated arrangement;
- no inferred grief or spiritual meaning.

Mechanical dependency: none.

## Long arc — What Marea Repeats

Status: PROPOSED LONG-TERM ARC.

The arc follows small practices across many ordinary world changes rather than centering one founding myth.

Possible progression:

Early Marea:

- Tideglass records incompatible tellings;
- a work habit repeats twice;
- a visitor mislabels an object;
- Ivo's hospitality creates a recognizable pattern.

Middle continuity:

- a practice is skipped once;
- another changes its route for safety;
- Jo uses a reviewed account in field-school material;
- Pia finds an older wording;
- Ema documents an ecological coincidence without endorsing the causal story;
- Jace adopts an informal Yard courtesy.

Later continuity:

- a repeat visitor recognizes the practice from an earlier stay;
- a public description is corrected;
- old and current variants coexist;
- a resident who used to observe now hosts one instance if canon/role authority supports it;
- a future festival can borrow from accumulated local practices only after explicit canon review.

The arc's payoff is not a single revelation. It is that Marea acquires memory, habits, disagreements and recognizable rhythms that survive the player moving around the world.

No global `culture_score`, `tradition_level` or belief meter is required.

## Rich encounter seed — Community Route Walk at Glass Bend

Status: PROPOSED / DEPENDENCY-GATED.

Narrative premise:

An already-supported repeated walk uses part of Sendero del Vidrio. Participants may be present for different reasons. Local wild activity threatens safe withdrawal.

Full intended form can include:

- narrow route geometry;
- noncombatant participants;
- one or more wild actors;
- withdrawal/protection objective;
- Interception;
- forced movement;
- exact content interactions;
- tactical route conditions;
- objective-aware AI;
- Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics playback.

Full form is BLOCKED under current engine evidence.

Reduced form:

- Narrative owns the practice, participants, objects, route meaning and continuity;
- noncombatants move to a safe world-state position before battle compilation;
- use stable geometry and audited content;
- omit unverified weather/hazard/reaction/forced-movement objective complexity;
- run a conventional battle only if one immediate wild threat still prevents passage;
- consume `IMMEDIATE_ROUTE_THREAT_WITHDREW` or `IMMEDIATE_PASSAGE_CLEAR` only.

The battle cannot decide culture, belief, historical truth, supernatural claims, public adoption or future recurrence.
