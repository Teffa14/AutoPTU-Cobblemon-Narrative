# The Duty That Survived the Shift — Pass 369

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-08

## Premise

A field office controls one calibrated survey instrument needed by two ecological teams. A real scheduling conflict is reviewed, an authorized allocator chooses the time-sensitive project, and Pass 349 applies that decision by displacing the other team's valid reservation.

The application creates a Pass 350 duty to inform the displaced holder. The office shift changes before anyone authors the message. The world is saved.

After restart, the instrument calendar correctly shows the new allocation. The notice obligation also survives. The outgoing scheduler can truthfully remember that the booking was displaced. The incoming clerk can truthfully see the unresolved duty. The field team can truthfully continue according to the last schedule it actually received.

No actor needs omniscience, deception or artificial amnesia.

## Play structure

The player can discover the unresolved duty at the office, through an audit, through a request from the newly favored team, or because the displaced group fails to appear where somebody expected them. The task can remain small: identify the exact affected holder, author a valid update through the existing communication system, and verify whether it was delivered before the old travel plan creates a larger problem.

If the player ignores the duty, the field group can spend time or supplies travelling toward an instrument it no longer controls. That consequence can affect trust in the institution without treating the displaced holder as irresponsible.

If several reservations were displaced, each holder carries a separate obligation. Informing one person does not close the others.

## Canon questions

No institution, survey site, team name, instrument identity or regional location is canonized here. Before promotion, a later lore pass must place the work inside an approved Ouros/Caelo institution and location without contradicting existing source material.

The scenario also leaves open who is institutionally responsible for authoring unresolved notices after a shift change. Pass 350 records the affected holder and the eventual sender on a link, but it does not invent an organizational staffing policy.
