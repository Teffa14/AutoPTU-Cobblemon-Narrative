# The Cancellation Beyond Coverage — Pass 345

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-07

Purpose

Exercise the Pass 345 distinction between an authored cancellation and a cancellation actually received before a time-bound handoff.

## Premise

A field team has an accepted request and a valid authorization to collect a shared instrument at a trailhead counter before continuing into a low-connectivity route.

The provider discovers a legitimate reason the exchange cannot proceed. The unit may need another inspection, the provider may have been reassigned, or the handoff site may become unavailable. The provider authors a cancellation early enough that, under ideal communication, the courier could avoid the trip.

The message does not reach the courier before departure.

The courier therefore travels on the last information they genuinely possess and reaches the handoff site in good faith.

No resource changes hands.

## Information state

Provider knowledge can include:

- the handoff was previously authorized;
- a cancellation was authored;
- the communication event was submitted;
- delivery may still be unknown.

Courier knowledge can include:

- the original authorization;
- the original place and window;
- no delivered cancellation before departure.

The accountable field lead may occupy a third state. They might receive the cancellation after the courier leaves, never receive it, or learn of the failed pickup only when the courier reports back.

These are valid divergent histories. Shared institutional membership cannot collapse them into one state.

## Player-facing investigation and intervention

The scene can be understood through ordinary evidence: the original authorization, message timestamps, communication delivery state, departure time, travel route and the later handoff attempt record.

Useful interventions include getting a later message to the courier through a reachable intermediate contact, redirecting the courier toward another authorized unit, changing the field task order, or accepting the lost travel time and rescheduling the instrument-dependent work.

The intended emotional consequence is grounded inconvenience rather than melodrama. Repeated failures may eventually motivate better process, but one delayed message does not automatically create distrust or disciplinary blame.

## Environmental storytelling

The handoff site can show evidence that the world continued while the courier travelled: a changed service notice, a closed counter, a written redirect, another worker using the area, or a later communication received after arrival.

Those observations may create new evidence. They cannot retroactively make the courier aware of the original cancellation before departure.

## Reduced implementation

The reduced version requires no structured battle.

It uses:

- semantic time and schedule commitments;
- Pass 343 handoff authorization;
- Pass 345 appointment notice ledger;
- Pass 283–287 communication delivery truth;
- Pass 281 travel state;
- Pass 344 failed-attempt provenance if the courier reaches the site;
- memory/belief and selective replanning.

## Mechanically rich version

A richer version may place an ordinary encounter on the courier's route after communication coverage is lost. The narrative objective remains reaching, redirecting or safely abandoning the delivery plan rather than forcing total defeat of opponents.

Capability dependencies remain explicit:

Targeting / footprints / range / LoS: required for exact tactical target and perception geometry.

Base movement legality: required for ordinary tactical route movement.

Complete movement: required for interception, carrying constraints, rescue, push/pull, knockback or forced movement.

Core calculations: required for deterministic PTU arithmetic.

Action economy / initiative: required for authored tactical pickup/drop/guard/communication actions.

Full turn / round lifecycle: required for a deadline that advances during structured combat.

Full stateful damage pipeline: required for authoritative actor or equipment damage.

Status lifecycle: required for persistent combat conditions.

Terrain / weather / hazards / zones / reactions: required if route conditions become active mechanics rather than world-level travel facts.

Move-specific behavior, Abilities, Items and Trainer Features/perks: each exact mechanic remains individually gated.

AI legal-action infrastructure: required for any new objective action exposed to tactical actors.

AI tactical policy: required for choosing between route progress, protection, retreat, warning another actor or defeat-oriented behavior.

Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback: presentation only. Displaying a cancellation cannot create authoritative receipt.

## Canon questions

No specific cause for the cancellation, named NPC, instrument, route consequence or institutional policy becomes canon through this proposal.

Canon review would be required before attaching the case to a specific Marea resident, assigning blame policy, creating a permanent communication dead zone or establishing a new institutional procedure.
