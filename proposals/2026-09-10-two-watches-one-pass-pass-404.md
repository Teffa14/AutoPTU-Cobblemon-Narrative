# Two Watches, One Pass — Pass 404

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Canon effect: NONE

## Premise

A recurring report claims that something unusual happens along a route, but the first field visit finds only an ordinary working landscape.

The important constraint is time. The first team observes the site during one watch. A later team returns during a different semantic window and sees a different pattern of movement, sound, Pokémon activity, maintenance work, lighting, water level or human traffic. The exact cause remains open until canon approval.

Both observations can be correct.

The assignment becomes an investigation into what changes between the two windows and whether that change is routine, hazardous or evidence of a deeper problem.

## World-agent loop

The first watch creates dated observations with explicit provenance.

A second watch can be assigned to the player or to persistent NPCs. The second team receives only the information actually communicated to it. If the first team's notes remain private, the second team may repeat work or interpret the site differently.

The investigation can then branch into archive lookup, interviews, another timed visit, equipment requests, route comparison, maintenance records or an AutoPTU encounter if a structured conflict actually occurs.

If nobody performs the second watch, no second-watch evidence exists. Later systems must not infer it from the current world state.

## Resource continuity hook

A shared field instrument can connect the watches.

When the second watch begins from a restored world generation, a Pass 404 holder-history baseline can be issued directly from the exact validated WorldResource catalog checkpoint. Its provenance binds the baseline to the catalog SHA-256 and semantic minute.

The baseline only proves holder continuity forward from that cut. It does not manufacture earlier custody history.

## Reduced implementation version

Run the complete premise outside AutoPTU.

Use semantic time, schedules, travel, persistent site observations, private NPC knowledge, explicit communication, archive lookup and WorldResource logistics.

Represent the two watches as separate world revisions. Any environmental or activity difference exists in world-level state between those revisions.

Success can mean identifying a repeatable time-dependent pattern, ruling out a false alarm, locating the correct next observer, preserving evidence or deciding that another visit is required.

## Mechanically rich version

A later watch may become dangerous because access changes while the team is present, wild Pokémon behavior complicates observation, or someone must be extracted from the site.

The narrative objective remains investigation, measurement, rescue, safe withdrawal or evidence preservation. KO is not an automatic completion condition.
