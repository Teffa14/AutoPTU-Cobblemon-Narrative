# Targeted False Warning — Pass 296

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON

A person with real evidence about a route, facility, event or threat decides to send a deliberately altered warning to a small set of people. The false statement travels through ordinary channels. Some intended recipients receive it quickly, another receives it late because of backlog, a colleague in the same organization is never selected, and a local-only contact may require successful world projection before the report enters personal knowledge.

The scenario becomes useful later because the world preserves several different facts: what the speaker actually knew, what they authored, whom they selected, what source they claimed, which deliveries succeeded and what each receiver subsequently believed. An investigation can therefore distinguish deliberate targeting from rumor drift, channel failure, forgotten attribution and honest retransmission.

Possible motives remain authored narrative inputs for now: divert a rival, protect a location, conceal an accident, provoke attendance somewhere else, shield another person, manipulate a market or competition, or create time for a separate action. Pass 296 does not give NPCs a general policy for selecting a lie based on these motives.

Reduced implementation: evidence basis → authored deceptive statement → selective audience → channel/backlog → receiver claim and subjective attribution → later world replanning. This version requires no structured battle.

Full implementation can add a chase, hazardous expedition, confrontation or battle after the false warning changes someone's route. A chase using interception, push/pull, knockback or forced movement depends on complete movement. Mechanical weather, hazards, zones or reactions depend on terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions. Delayed or temporary effects depend on full turn/round lifecycle, status lifecycle and the exact Move/Ability/Item/Trainer Feature owner involved. Autonomous tactical response depends on AI tactical policy. In-world battle playback depends on Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter support.

The premise must remain playable through the reduced world-simulation version even while richer engine families are incomplete.
