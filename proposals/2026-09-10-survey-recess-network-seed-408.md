# Survey Recess Network — Exploration Seed 408

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-10
Canon effect: NONE unless separately promoted through canon review.

## Existing canon anchors reused

This proposal attaches to established Ouros facts rather than creating a separate setting island.

Sendero del Vidrio is already the old survey road between Puerto Bruma and Loma Clara. Estación Mirador already maintains route reports and observations. Tideglass Archive already preserves route surveys and historical records.

The proposal does not establish that any hidden recess, service alcove, sealed room or forgotten facility actually exists. Site count, purpose, builder, age, location and condition remain unresolved until canon review.

## Premise

A routine archive or field comparison reveals that several old survey records use a reference convention that current staff no longer understand completely. The records are individually plausible. When compared, they suggest that measurements may have been taken from small protected survey positions distributed along an older route alignment.

The first playable question is practical: can one of those reference positions still be identified?

Finding one site does not solve a regional mystery. It supplies a new observation and may teach a convention that makes another record interpretable. A second site can be found first. A third may never be located. Order changes what each investigator understands at the time.

## Exploration structure

Each candidate recess is a compact environmental question with its own evidence set. Examples of neutral evidence families include alignment marks, drainage direction, sight-line geometry, repeated measurement notation, repair traces, material joins and later modifications.

These are narrative observation categories. They do not grant a Skill check, automatic truth, hidden-object detector or PTU Feature.

A site can produce several outcomes:

- investigators identify the old reference position with strong provenance;
- they record a plausible but uncertain interpretation;
- they learn that the current site has been altered too much to answer the question;
- they recover evidence that points toward another record or location;
- they leave without enough information and can revisit after acquiring new knowledge.

Different actors can reach different defensible interpretations from the evidence they actually observed.

## Persistent-world behavior

The player does not own the investigation by default.

A persistent NPC who learns about one candidate location and has a compatible obligation may visit it off-screen. That actor gains only the observations actually produced by the visit. Other NPCs learn those findings only through explicit communication, publication or later record access.

If no actor visits a candidate site, no observation is fabricated.

A later site revision can alter what remains observable without rewriting an older observation. This allows the same recess to become more or less informative after maintenance, weather, traffic or another approved world change.

## Cross-site knowledge loop

The intended long-form loop is:

archive clue -> candidate site -> local observation -> interpretation -> another record becomes meaningful -> optional revisit or second site -> revised hypothesis

Knowledge can therefore unlock a destination or question without a literal key item. Access still depends on real travel, permissions and route state.

The network can support EXPLORATION, REGION, CLASS, FACTION, CHARACTER or SECONDARY questline tags depending on the eventual canon owner. It should reuse existing location, NPC and institution IDs whenever promoted.

## Reduced implementation

The reduced version requires no AutoPTU encounter.

It uses existing persistent-world capabilities for semantic time, travel, schedules, obligations, private knowledge, explicit communication, archive/evidence provenance and site revision state. A recess is represented as an investigation site with authored observation opportunities. Environmental changes occur between semantic revisions rather than as tactical hazards.

Success means producing a provenance-backed observation or defensible uncertainty. No combat completion is required.

## Full encounter version

A mechanically richer site can create pressure while investigators inspect or leave the recess. The tactical objective can be to complete an observation, protect an observer, recover a field record, reach an exit or withdraw after enough evidence has been gathered.

Total KO is not the default narrative objective.

Possible pressure sources remain unresolved. No species, hostile actor, hazard, weather pattern or supernatural cause is proposed as canon here.

## Mechanical fallback rule

If the full version asks for a capability that remains unverified, keep the same narrative premise and run the reduced investigation version. Do not emulate missing PTU mechanics in Minecraft.

For example, an authored note can say that a site became unsafe between visits. The reduced version may close that site or change its revision state semantically. It must not simulate a collapsing-floor forced-movement sequence unless complete movement, lifecycle, hazard behavior and adapter playback are all admitted for that exact encounter.
