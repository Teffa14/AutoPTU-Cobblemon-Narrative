# Electric Grid Generation & Distribution Seeds — Pass 106

Status: NON-CANON PROPOSALS. These are reusable situation structures only.
Date: 2026-08-28

All seeds depend on the proposed continuity model in `design/electric-grid-generation-distribution-continuity-extension.md`. None grants electrical PTU mechanics.

## The Generator Is Running, the District Is Dark

A source visibly returns to operation, but the affected district remains unsupplied because the downstream path has not passed verification. Residents disagree over whether the repair "worked" because they are observing different scopes.

Useful outputs: operator follow-up, public-information correction, downstream inspection, recurring resident testimony.

## The Repair Finished Before the Power Returned

A damaged link is physically repaired. Energization remains unauthorized pending inspection. The repair crew is correct that its work is complete, while the service operator is also correct that restoration is incomplete.

## The Clinic Returned First

A limited alternate source supports one critical service while nearby ordinary loads remain unavailable. The narrative pressure comes from visibility and expectations, not from a hidden morality score.

Canon question: who has authority to define priorities in this settlement?

## The Old Connection Is Still on the Map

A restoration plan references a legacy corridor that has not been used in years. Field inspection shows that the land, ecology and surrounding neighborhood have changed. The old record is evidence, not proof that the connection remains usable.

## The Temporary Supply Became a Landmark

A temporary generation point or service yard remains socially important after normal supply returns. Vendors, residents or travelers still use its location as a meeting point. Later planning debates whether to preserve, remove or repurpose the site.

## The Lights Came Back on One Street

Multiple residents report restoration at different times. Investigation shows a phased return across distinct service sectors rather than misinformation.

## The Backup Exists, but It Was Never Verified for This Use

A facility has backup equipment. Its mere presence does not prove readiness for the current dependency. The story becomes an inspection and institutional-memory problem rather than an automatic emergency solution.

## The Decommissioned Site Became Habitat

An old generation or distribution site has acquired ecological value. A proposal to reuse part of the infrastructure now intersects Conservation, Public Works, Technology and local memory. No Pokémon presence automatically blocks or authorizes reuse.

## The New Demand Was Real, the Cause Was Not Settled

A district has experienced sustained growth in electrical demand. Several explanations compete: a new venue, industrial expansion, residential growth, weather, new services or measurement changes. Infrastructure planning proceeds only from supported evidence.

## The Operator Knows a Route the Archive Does Not

A veteran operator remembers a temporary configuration used years ago. The archive contains incomplete documentation. The claim becomes a lead that must be inspected and reconciled rather than instant topology truth.

## The Pokémon Is Always Near the Substation

A recurring individual Pokémon appears near the same technical site. Possible explanations include habitat, food, warmth, noise, social familiarity, work assignment or coincidence. Observation alone proves none of them.

## The Test Passed at the Node, Failed at the Service

A network verification succeeds, but one downstream facility still cannot resume normal operations. Its own dependency or internal equipment remains unresolved.

This seed reinforces cross-system authority: Power Grid can say the sector is available while Care, Manufacturing, Communications or another owner still says its service is not ready.

## Mystery: Four Restoration Times, One Incident

Four records give different restoration times:

- physical repair complete;
- switching executed;
- sector verification passed;
- downstream public service resumed.

All four may be accurate. The mystery is reconstructing scope and sequence, not exposing a hidden liar.

## Mystery: Three Maps, Two Actual Paths

An old plan, a maintenance revision and a current operator schematic disagree. Two describe different revisions of the same physical route. The third includes a proposed connection that was never commissioned.

Resolution depends on provenance, effective dates and field verification.

## Long arc: A District Learns Its Grid

Phase 1 establishes ordinary service, named operators, several downstream dependencies and one old asset whose history is known locally.

Phase 2 introduces a limited interruption. The first observations disagree because they come from different sectors.

Phase 3 uses an alternate path or temporary source. One public service returns before the rest of the district. Businesses, residents, transport or communications adapt.

Phase 4 completes physical repair, but restoration occurs in verified stages. One downstream service finds a separate internal problem.

Phase 5 returns normal supply or adopts a revised normal topology. The temporary workaround leaves social and physical traces.

Phase 6 revisits the district later. A legacy map, old temporary site, recurring operator or ecological change makes the earlier incident relevant to a new story.

The arc never uses a single `power_level` or blackout drama meter.

## Encounter seed: Substation Access Withdrawal

Intended full form: operators need a safe access corridor while a territorial or hostile subgroup contests the external approach. Multiple withdrawal paths, Intercept/forced movement, generalized reactions and objective-aware policy matter. Any live electrical or machinery hazard requires exact PTU/Caelo mechanics plus `terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions`.

Reduced form now: isolate the site first. Keep workers, controls and technical equipment outside BattleSpec. Use explicit combatants in a reviewed static yard/access road. Victory secures access only.

Capability dependency profile:

- VERIFIED: targeting/footprints/range/LoS; base movement legality; core calculations; action economy/initiative; AI legal-action infrastructure.
- PARTIAL: complete movement including push/pull/knockback/interception/forced movement; full turn/round lifecycle; full stateful damage pipeline; status lifecycle; move-specific behavior; abilities; items; Trainer Features/perks.
- BLOCKING: terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions; AI tactical policy; Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback support.

## Encounter seed: Alternate Supply Perimeter

Intended full form: a temporary supply asset supports a limited service while an encounter develops nearby. Protection, route control, reactions, tactical policy and playback matter.

Reduced form now: the asset and operators remain outside the grid; its state is frozen during battle. Combat occurs in a static neighboring area. Battle outcome cannot change capacity, fuel, priority, switching or verification.

## Encounter seed: Legacy Corridor Inspection

Intended full form: an old corridor has become habitat. Investigation may require approach/withdrawal objectives, reviewed terrain, reactions and tactical policy.

Reduced form now: electrical state remains UNKNOWN until post-encounter inspection. Any battle uses a static reviewed area and explicit participants. Old pylons/cables remain scenery. Winning never proves the connection is live or reusable.

## Promotion gates

A full electrical encounter requires all of the following before promotion:

1. exact PTU/Caelo effects for every electrical/environmental interaction;
2. current Java evidence for each required movement/reaction family rather than one representative mechanic;
3. objective-aware tactical policy where protection/withdrawal matters;
4. authoritative technical-object semantics if an object enters BattleSpec;
5. adapter/playback support that reflects Ouros/AutoPTU state without delegating battle or grid authority to Minecraft;
6. recovery tests preventing facility softlocks after unload, interruption or failed verification.
