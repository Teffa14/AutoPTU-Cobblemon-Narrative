# The Message That Arrived Before the Plan Changed — Pass 371

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-09
Canon effect: NONE

## Premise

A field team is already moving toward an observation site when its booking is displaced and the office sends a valid reroute instruction. The message reaches the team leader. The leader is occupied with a hazardous crossing and never performs a check-back or planning cycle before the group continues along the old route.

The world therefore contains a delivered message and durable evidence of that delivery, but no confirmed acknowledgement and no changed route.

## Why this matters

The encounter creates disagreement without requiring deceit or an omniscient quest flag. The scheduler can truthfully say the notice was delivered. The team leader can truthfully say the message arrived. A junior teammate can truthfully say nobody told the group to turn around. The current route can still be the old one because delivery, evidence, acknowledgement and replanning are different events.

## Full version

The player encounters the team after it has entered a dangerous field corridor. The useful observation window is closing elsewhere. The player may communicate the reroute, escort the team, recover equipment from the obsolete site, or split responsibilities so one party preserves the new window while another prevents loss of samples or instruments.

Wild Pokémon, terrain or weather can complicate movement, but the narrative objective is information recovery and route correction rather than defeating every opponent.

Potential outcomes include a successful reroute with the scientific window preserved, a safe withdrawal that sacrifices the observation, an equipment recovery that leaves the team late, or a communication failure that produces a later institutional review. None of these outcomes imply misconduct automatically.

## Reduced version

Run entirely in the world-agent layer. The team remains off-screen or locally projected without structured battle. The player or another NPC reaches the group, presents the reroute evidence, and a new explicit replan trigger is created. Travel resumes using the ordinary semantic-time travel system.

This preserves the same premise and causal history without depending on missing tactical mechanics.

## Required world-agent facts

The scenario needs a source allocation/application history, a notice obligation, a real communication envelope, delivery status, receiver identity, durable receiver evidence, acknowledgement state if introduced, replan-trigger state, and the active travel plan.

No state may be inferred backward from the final route. A team that later changes course does not prove it had acknowledged the earlier message.

## Canon questions

The field office, team, observation site, corridor, communication technology and target species are placeholders. Binding requires approved regional geography, institution ownership and species ecology.

The scenario adds no rule that electronic or remote messaging exists everywhere in Ouros. A canon binding may use radio, courier, station terminal, local messenger or another approved medium while preserving the same causal structure.
