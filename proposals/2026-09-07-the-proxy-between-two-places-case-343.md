# The Proxy Between Two Places — Pass 343

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-07

Premise

Nerea needs a serviced field instrument for a scheduled Estación Mirador observation window. Teo has completed the ordinary maintenance work at repair row and accepts the request to make the instrument available.

Nerea cannot reach Puerto Bruma in time without abandoning another obligation. Ema is already scheduled to pass through Puerto Bruma before returning to the station and is explicitly authorized to collect the unit.

The instrument changes hands from Teo to Ema.

At that moment Ema possesses it. Nerea does not.

Canon anchors reused

The proposal derives only from already approved facts:

- Teo Lark maintains field instruments at Puerto Bruma repair row;
- Nerea Sol works from Estación Mirador;
- Ema Rey performs equipment checks and field work under Nerea's project protocols and connects to Teo;
- Puerto Bruma, Sendero del Vidrio and Estación Mirador have fixed canonical locations;
- named NPCs and their work relationships persist independently of Minecraft entities.

No new relationship, institution, mechanical class effect or permanent schedule is canonized by this proposal.

Case structure

First state:

Teo still holds the serviced instrument at repair row. Nerea has a valid operational need. Teo accepts the resource request. The acceptance alone moves nothing.

Second state:

Nerea explicitly names Ema as the physical receiver for a pickup at repair row during a narrow window. The authorization preserves Nerea as the accountable requester while Ema becomes the intended recipient for this handoff.

Third state:

Ema arrives while Teo still holds the exact unit at the authored location. The handoff records who gave it, who received it, where, when and which request/authorization justified the exchange.

Fourth state:

Ema now carries the instrument toward Estación Mirador through ordinary world travel. The resource does not appear in Nerea's possession merely because Nerea requested it.

Possible complications

Ema can be delayed by an ordinary route obligation.

Teo can discover before pickup that the exact unit is no longer technically ready, requiring a withdrawal or a different offered unit rather than a fake handoff.

Nerea can receive a message saying that pickup succeeded while still not having physical custody.

A second worker can see Ema carrying the instrument and infer that Ema is using it personally. That inference can be sincere and still wrong.

Ema can reach the station after the observation window closes. The handoff remains historical fact even if the intended task fails.

The player may encounter Ema while she is in transit and help with a separate world problem. Helping Ema does not automatically complete the delivery unless the later custody/location state actually supports completion.

Investigation and dialogue value

The case creates distinct truthful statements:

Teo can truthfully know that he handed the unit to Ema.

Ema can truthfully know that she has it.

Nerea can truthfully know that Teo accepted the request and that Ema was authorized, while still lacking evidence that pickup actually happened.

Another NPC can truthfully report seeing Ema on Sendero del Vidrio without knowing whether the instrument reached Mirador.

These facts exercise the existing memory, source-attribution, communication, grounding and partial-audience systems without requiring deception.

Reduced implementation

A missed observation window can become a world consequence without combat.

A richer version can place a structured encounter on Sendero del Vidrio while Ema is carrying the instrument.

Narrative objective: preserve Ema's ability to reach Mirador with the instrument, not necessarily defeat every opponent.

Reduced fallback for the same premise: resolve the interruption as a semantic travel delay or blocked-route event, preserving the custody chain and arrival consequence without tactical carrying rules.
