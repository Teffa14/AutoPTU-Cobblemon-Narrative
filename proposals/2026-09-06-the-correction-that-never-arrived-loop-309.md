# The Correction That Never Arrived — pass 309

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-06

## Premise

A relay investigation once concluded that a physical sample had a custody gap. That conclusion spread through a small service community and contributed to suspicion toward a technician. Later, the original investigator finds documentation that supports continuity and records a superseding assessment.

The correction does not reach everyone.

One foreman receives it immediately. A ranger is outside service range. A merchant who heard the first conclusion was never selected as a correction recipient. A local official receives the notice only after making a staffing decision. Every actor can therefore hold a different evidence history without any of them becoming omniscient or irrational.

## Adventure loop

The player first encounters consequences rather than a quest marker: altered work assignments, guarded conversations, a technician avoiding a familiar route, and contradictory statements about whether the case was “cleared.” Investigation reconstructs two separate histories: the custody-assessment lineage and the communication lineage.

The useful question changes from “which conclusion is true?” to “who knew which conclusion, when did they know it, and what did they do before or after learning it?”

Possible hooks include carrying the revision to an unreachable recipient, discovering that an organization assumed the old audience would automatically receive the correction, revisiting the original relay after the corrected assessment changes the interpretation of physical traces, or confronting a person who received the revision but continues to act on the earlier claim for unrelated motives.

## Consequences

A corrected assessment may repair one factual premise while leaving separate conflicts intact. A technician can be cleared of a custody suspicion while still having poor relationships, unrelated duty failures or rivalry. An institution can acknowledge that its first conclusion became outdated while disputing whether earlier decisions were reasonable at the time.

NPCs update only after actual receipt. Public or private repetition of the earlier conclusion remains a historically real event and is never deleted.

## Reduced implementation version

The complete narrative premise can run without AutoPTU. Required pieces are durable NPC ledgers, custody assessment lineage, explicit assessment conclusion claims, ordinary information delivery, selective replanning and authored world consequences. Travel can use existing world-route planning and static access gates.

## Full encounter version

A late correction motivates a return to the relay and an adjacent records annex after seasonal weather exposed a previously inaccessible service cavity. The party may need to cross damaged access routes while other actors race to recover or secure additional records. A confrontation can occur, but combat is optional.

Full mechanical dependencies:

- targeting/footprints/range/LoS for any structured confrontation;
- base movement legality for ordinary tactical traversal;
- complete movement for forced displacement, interception, rescue pulls or wind-driven movement;
- core calculations for ordinary deterministic combat arithmetic;
- action economy/initiative for structured turns;
- full turn/round lifecycle for timed collapses or delayed environmental phases;
- full stateful damage pipeline for actual structural/environmental injury;
- status lifecycle for persistent shock, restraint or other authored conditions;
- terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions for unstable flooring, wind, electrical danger or rescue reactions;
- move-specific behavior, Abilities, Items and Trainer Features/perks only when explicitly authored into the solution space;
- AI legal-action infrastructure for legal tactical choices;
- AI tactical policy for autonomous tactical rescue or opposition;
- Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback support for reliable visible end-to-end execution.

## Tactical fallback

Keep the annex and investigation unchanged, but model access as safe, blocked or costly nodes. Weather remains descriptive. Remove forced movement, dynamic hazard zones, reaction rescues, delayed collapses and persistent statuses. Use only individually verified actions if a confrontation occurs.

## Canon questions

No relay, technician, institution, regional site, Pokémon species behavior or legal procedure in this proposal is canon-approved. Before promotion, names and local bindings must be reconciled with the intended region, and any mechanical method for discovering or authenticating evidence must be validated against PTU/Caelo authority.
