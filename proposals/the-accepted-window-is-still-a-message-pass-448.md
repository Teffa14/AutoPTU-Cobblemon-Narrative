Premise

A requester receives replacement terms for a previously disrupted obligation and accepts them. The responder does not know yet. During that interval the place connected to the future work keeps changing.

The central tension comes from ordinary causal separation. One person has made a decision. Another is still waiting. The road, worksite, habitat or settlement does not pause for either of them.

Reduced version

The replacement offer arrives. The requester accepts it. Their reply enters a communication channel with real latency. Before delivery, new local evidence appears: access may reopen, another duty may consume the responder, Pokémon activity may change the expected route, or the original need may become less urgent.

No battle is required. The scene can resolve through communication, observation, schedule changes and later planning. The responder cannot act as though the acceptance exists until actual receipt.

This reduced version needs semantic time, private knowledge, durable proposal/decision state and information delivery only. It does not need AutoPTU.

Full version

After actual receipt, a future replacement commitment could lead to an inspection, survey, escort, retrieval, containment or extraction objective. The encounter is authored from the current world state at execution time.

One possible structure uses a short-lived access window through an environmentally active route. The objective remains reaching or protecting a work task rather than defeating every creature present. Pokémon may be territorial, displaced, curious or simply moving through the same space.

If the full route requires forced displacement, interception, weather phases, hazard zones, reactions, delayed effects, ability-created terrain, Trainer Feature interrupts or non-KO objective completion, those exact capability families remain explicit dependencies. The Minecraft adapter must not reproduce missing PTU rules locally.

Reduced structured alternative

If rich dependencies are unavailable, preserve the premise with a static route state, ordinary legal movement, a simple verified battle only when necessary, and narrative objective accounting outside the tactical engine. Do not simulate knockback, reactions, dynamic weather, delayed hazards or specialized objective verbs unless their capability path is admitted.

Consequences and longer arc use

This pattern can feed recurring NPC arcs without pre-authoring a moral verdict. A requester who repeatedly answers promptly, an expert who repeatedly communicates changes, or a locality where access repeatedly shifts may later affect trust, planning habits or institutional procedure. Those consequences require their own explicit evidence and owner.

A delayed acceptance can also generate mystery without deception. The responder may reasonably believe no answer exists while the requester reasonably believes the matter is settled. Later disagreement can be reconstructed from message provenance instead of omniscient quest state.
