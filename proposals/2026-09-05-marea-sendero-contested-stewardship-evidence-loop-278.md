# Marea/Sendero contested stewardship evidence loop — Pass 278

Status: PROPOSED. Non-canon.

Premise

A bounded ecological stewardship assignment has ended, but its accountability record is disputed. The disagreement concerns what the evidence can prove, not whether a hidden quest flag says the delegate succeeded.

Loop

The player first encounters a concrete downstream consequence of the review: a temporary assignment remains under scrutiny, a report is marked incomplete, or an authority refuses to close the accountability record because two logs cannot be compared safely.

The player can inspect the evidence packet and discover that apparently simple contradictions have structure. One observation occurred in good visibility during the authored inspection window. Another used a later method under poor visibility. Two photographs that look like corroboration came from the same capture session. A required change log is absent from the reviewer packet, but absence from the packet does not establish that the delegate failed to create it.

The investigation can branch into several useful actions:

- locate the original source record and restore its provenance;
- reproduce the earlier method in a comparable semantic window;
- obtain a genuinely independent observation;
- identify a confounder that makes the comparison invalid;
- demonstrate that the surviving evidence cannot resolve the question;
- establish a narrow process conclusion while leaving the ecological outcome uncertain.

Possible outcomes

A stronger record can support the exact reporting or inspection obligation. That may allow Pass 277 to clear a corrective-action state if the governing instrument permits it.

A method mismatch can leave the matter unresolved. The player still learns something useful: the world does not manufacture certainty because a quest needs closure.

A later independent comparison can supersede the current interpretation without deleting the earlier one. NPCs can therefore remember that the case was once disputed even after the working interpretation changes.

A formal review, stay, remedy or appeal only exists if a canon institution later defines one. This proposal does not create that institution.

Character and faction opportunities

The pattern supports competent disagreement. A field observer may trust direct familiarity with the site. A reviewer may insist on method comparability. A delegate may correctly argue that a missing file is not proof of failure. An administrator may still need a usable decision by a review horizon.

None of those positions requires corruption, stupidity or secret villainy.

Environmental storytelling

Minecraft/Cobblemon can expose the dispute through physical report stations, dated marker states, observation points, posted requests for repeat sampling, notebooks, NPC schedules or a visible difference between the conditions in which two observations were collected.

Those surfaces remain presentation. Block state, item possession or entity presence does not establish evidentiary validity.

Reduced implementation

The reduced loop requires:

- Pass 277 delegated-authority/accountability state;
- provenance and observation references;
- semantic windows;
- the Pass 278 evidence-contest packet;
- normal Minecraft/Cobblemon presentation.

It needs no AutoPTU battle.

Mechanically rich extension

A later canon story could put the player in danger while collecting replacement evidence. That extension must declare exact dependencies rather than letting the governance premise synthesize mechanics.

Targeting/footprints/range/LoS is required for tactical detection or selection. Base movement legality is required for ordinary PTU traversal. Interception, push, pull, knockback or forced displacement require complete movement. Deterministic PTU arithmetic uses core calculations. Structured sequencing uses action economy/initiative and phase-spanning behavior additionally needs full turn/round lifecycle. Damage requires the full stateful damage pipeline; persistent conditions require status lifecycle. Tactical weather, hazards, zones, terrain or reactions require that capability family. Exact Moves, Abilities, Items and Trainer Features/perks require their verified paths. Autonomous adversarial behavior requires AI tactical policy. Live battle manifestation still requires Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback.

Canon boundary

No actual reviewer, authority, steward, grievance right, punishment, Fletchling behavior, population change or institutional procedure becomes canon through this proposal.
