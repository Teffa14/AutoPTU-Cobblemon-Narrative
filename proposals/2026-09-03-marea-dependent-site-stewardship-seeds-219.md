# Marea dependent-site stewardship seeds — pass 219

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-03

## Boundary

This proposal adds no canonical nest, egg, juvenile, breeding population or new species. It reuses established Marea institutional roles and the existing approach/tolerance/access architecture. The first canon implementation site remains deliberately unresolved so the proposal cannot silently turn the persistent Sendero Fletchling into a breeding adult.

The intended use is a route, ledge, structure edge or service area that temporarily overlaps an active dependent site. The ecological fact should be discovered through evidence rather than exposed as a quest label.

## Seed: Sign Before Species

During an ordinary route or maintenance check, the player notices repeated material placement, localized droppings, shell-like fragments, calls, tracks or another bounded sign near an area people use. None of those cues alone authorizes species identity or an active nest.

The player can document the site, observe remotely, return at another time, ask Nerea to review evidence, or report an operational concern to Mara. A rushed approach can change the behavior of nearby Pokémon and degrade the quality of the observation.

Possible outcomes include a confirmed dependent site, an inactive structure, a feeding/resting site mistaken for one, insufficient evidence, or a different ecological explanation. The proposal does not preselect the answer.

## Seed: The Adult Was Gone Because You Were There

A first visit finds apparent dependent evidence but no attending adult. The easy conclusion is abandonment.

A second observation from farther away or after the Trainer withdraws may record an adult returning. Alternatively, repeated well-documented effort may continue to find no adult. The important mechanic is evidence quality: the player's presence can be part of the disturbance record.

This creates a useful narrative consequence without hidden dice deciding truth. A well-intentioned Trainer can produce bad data by lingering in the wrong place.

## Seed: A Safe Distance Is Contextual

Mara needs an access decision before ordinary traffic or maintenance continues. Nerea can recommend a provisional buffer based on observed alert/withdrawal behavior, site geometry and confidence. Lia can explain which routes or deliveries the restriction affects. Teo may need access to a fixture inside the proposed area.

The decision can be narrower, broader, temporary, revised after new evidence, or unnecessary. No universal number is embedded in canon. A quiet pedestrian, a group of Trainers, a cart, construction work and a stationary observer can be different disturbance classes.

The world consequence is operational: signs, reroutes, task delays, altered observation plans and later review. It is not a hidden combat aura.

## Seed: Do Not Rescue What Is Being Attended

A juvenile, egg or vulnerable-looking actor appears unattended for a bounded observation period. The player can wait, watch from farther away, report, or intervene only when there is actual evidence of danger and an authorized method.

The encounter explicitly resists the collection-game reflex that discovery creates ownership. Removing a dependent from the site can become a serious world-state action requiring provenance, custody and welfare follow-up. Good intentions do not erase the consequence.

The exact species and life stage remain future canon decisions.

## Seed: The Wrong Approach Vector

An adult is present and initially tolerates ordinary route traffic. The Trainer takes a shortcut that places them between the adult and the dependent site, or between the adult and a viable withdrawal/return route.

The same individual may now become alert, warn, reposition, obstruct, distract, withdraw or engage according to species tendencies, real capabilities and legal options. If an aerial or alternate route remains available, the narrative layer cannot declare it trapped merely because the Minecraft camera makes the geometry look enclosed.

This seed directly reuses the existing behavior policy rather than creating a special parental-combat AI.

## Seed: Temporary Closure, Permanent Record

The immediate dependent-site episode ends. A route restriction is removed or revised. The observation record remains.

Next season, staff can compare timing, location, human pressure, prior disturbance and whether the site is reused. Reuse does not have to be guaranteed. A one-year event can remain meaningful without becoming a permanent spawn point.

This supplies long-term institutional memory for Mara and Nerea and prevents temporary conservation decisions from becoming eternal quest flags.

## Mechanically rich encounter: Occupied Shelf Under Shared Use

Working title only. The exact site and species remain unapproved.

A dependent site overlaps a route that people need to use. The player must obtain enough evidence for an access decision while preserving the option to pass, withdraw, reroute or respond to a defensive Pokémon. A battle is possible but never the default objective.

### Intended full version

The full form can use authoritative footprints/range/LoS to determine what each actor can perceive and whether the Trainer is approaching the dependent, the adult or a route between them. Base movement handles ordinary positioning. Complete movement becomes relevant if interception, forced movement, blocking or displacement is actually attempted.

The adult behavior policy consumes species/population evidence, dependent-site context, individual state, Trainer approach, available escape/return routes and any verified Skill/Feature/Edge/Move/Ability/Item effects. Legal-action generation then exposes only actions the actor can actually perform. Tactical policy chooses among warning, withdrawal, repositioning, guarding, obstruction, distraction, engagement or disengagement according to current goals and capabilities.

If the Trainer uses capture, hindrance, trapping, Status, damage, forced movement or a protective reaction, AutoPTU resolves those exact mechanics. Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics receives semantic results and animates them. It may not infer that a swoop hit, a status landed, a juvenile was protected or a route became blocked from animation alone.

If the site itself contains a real hazard, that hazard receives its own explicit contract. `Near nest` never automatically means hazardous terrain or a reaction zone.

## Reduced version: Mark, Yield, Recheck

The same premise can run before tactical completeness.

Ouros records a provisional dependent site, observation provenance, human-activity context and any temporary access decision. Cobblemon continues to handle generic natural spawning under its native conditions. The player can observe from an approved position, withdraw, take an already-valid alternate route, record evidence, return later or report the site.

A visible wild Pokémon can show only world-behavior cues already supported by the authoritative behavior layer. The reduced form does not simulate protective combat AI, invent an aggro radius, assume parentage from proximity, create fake Stealth/Charm bonuses, apply a movement penalty, invent a reaction attack or force a species spawn.

If a legitimate battle begins, the actor enters through the normal authoritative BattleSpec path with its real blueprint. The dependent-site record remains separate from battle state.

## Institutional consequences

A temporary buffer can inconvenience deliveries, inspection and maintenance. That cost is part of the story. Lia may need a route that remains usable. Mara may prefer a conservative closure while evidence is weak. Nerea may prioritize observation quality and repeated review. Teo may argue that delaying repair creates another hazard. Their mandates can conflict without requiring corruption or villainy.

A later review can show that the first buffer was too large, too small or correctly scoped. The institution should be able to learn rather than preserve the player's first decision forever.

## Capture and custody boundary

Discovery never grants ownership. An egg, juvenile or temporarily incapacitated adult remains part of world-state custody/provenance until an approved rule or institutional process changes that fact.

Capture attempts against an attending adult can alter the dependent-site situation even if the capture itself is mechanically legal. Narrative consequences such as an unattended dependent, interrupted care or emergency stewardship must be represented explicitly rather than inferred from a generic battle victory.

No such consequence is canonized in this proposal; it defines the data and encounter requirement for future authored cases.

## Open questions

The first canon species and location remain unresolved. The project also needs exact PTU/Caelo/Kairos verification for Pokémon Education and identification, Intuition/Survival observation, Charm or other calming interactions, Trainer Features/Edges affecting wild handling, capture and custody, restraint, interception and any mechanics that could protect another actor.

The world layer needs a clear rule for when an apparently unattended dependent can transition from `unknown attendance` to an emergency/welfare case. That threshold should be evidence-driven and species-aware rather than a universal timer.
