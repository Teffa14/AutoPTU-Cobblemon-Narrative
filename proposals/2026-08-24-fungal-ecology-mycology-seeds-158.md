## Long arcs

### Five Fruiting Seasons at Cloudroot Grove

A five-year monitoring series records ordinary fruiting, two weak years, a major flush and a disputed harvest closure. Weather, deadwood, visitors and survey methods all change. The arc's payoff is improved evidence and institutional memory rather than revealing a mastermind.

### The Organism Nobody Saw Whole

Separate fruiting patches and root samples accumulate over years. Scientists repeatedly revise whether the observations belong to one persistent system or several similar occurrences. The question can remain legitimately unresolved.

### From Rot Log to Research Reserve

One deadwood object accumulates fungal, seedling, wildlife and public-use observations until it influences later forestry planning. The log can eventually decay away physically while its Chronicle remains useful.

Primarily non-combat. Pass 72 fungal records, Flora, Soil, Irrigation, IPM, Science and Metrology compare competing explanations. A battle cannot establish causation.
