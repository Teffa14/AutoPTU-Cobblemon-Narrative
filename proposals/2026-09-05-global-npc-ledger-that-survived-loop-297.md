# Candidate loop — The Ledger That Survived — Pass 297

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-05

A recurring NPC deliberately sends a false safety notice to a small set of recipients. The notice is still in transit when the world process restarts. After recovery, the same recipients remain selected, the same semantic delivery times remain in force, and the false assertion still carries the real speaker plus its declared source attribution. The restart does not erase, duplicate or sanitize the deception.

Later, a player investigation can encounter three independent traces: the receiver's remembered report, an archival dispatch record, and environmental evidence showing the route condition at the time. These traces can disagree without any one of them rewriting the others.

The narrative question becomes causal: who knew the route state, who authored the warning, which recipients actually received it, and which later decisions followed from that information?

Reduced implementation: observation -> deceptive statement -> explicit audience -> delayed delivery -> checkpoint/restart -> delivery -> receiver belief/replanning -> archival reconstruction. This version requires no AutoPTU battle.

Full implementation option: the false warning causes a party or NPC expedition to enter a dangerous site. The site may later contain a tactical encounter, but the encounter must declare only the mechanics actually used. Interception, push/pull, knockback or forced movement require complete movement. Mechanical weather, hazards, zones or reactions require that capability family. Delayed/status effects require lifecycle/status plus the exact owning Move/Ability/Item/Trainer Feature family. Autonomous tactical choice requires AI tactical policy. Visible authoritative resolution requires Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback support.

Possible consequences include damaged trust, institutional review, a rival gaining an opportunity, a missed rescue window, a delayed shipment, a route investigation, or a later sincere retransmission of the false report. None of these outcomes are canon until separately reviewed and bound to established Ouros places, actors and institutions.
