# The Obligation After the Handover — Pass 353

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Canon authority: NONE

## Premise

A field team arranges access to one specific instrument before a long survey. The booking and the provider's acceptance occur during one period of play. The actual pickup is expected later, after other world activity and possibly after a session or runtime restart.

When the story resumes, nobody should need to recreate the agreement from dialogue. The booking and request history already exist as world facts.

## Reduced version

The case runs without AutoPTU.

A requester has an accepted request for one exact resource. A separate reservation protects the intended window. The world crosses a restart boundary. Both records restore with their original actors, times and provenance. Later events can still invalidate, displace or fulfill the commitment through their existing owners.

The player may encounter a disagreement about whether the instrument was promised, whether the window still applies or whether another team can use it. The answer comes from the persistent records plus later events, not from a reset quest flag.

This version uses semantic time, reservation/request state, communication, memory/belief and later handoff/allocation layers. No tactical engine capability is required.

## Full encounter version

After the resource is eventually collected, the team must cross a route before a narrow observation window closes. A wild encounter can interrupt the trip. Victory means preserving the instrument and reaching the observation site in time; defeating every opponent is optional.

The narrative premise remains identical if the tactical layer cannot yet support the full objective. A reduced travel interruption can substitute until the exact engine families are verified.

Every Move, Ability, Item and Trainer Feature/perk used by the encounter keeps its own evidence gate.

## Story value

The case makes persistence visible through consequence. A promise made earlier matters later even when no character repeats exposition. It also creates a clean foundation for investigations where different actors remember the same commitment differently while the historical record remains inspectable.
