# Fading Witness investigation loop

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-05

## Premise

An old incident becomes relevant again after enough semantic time has passed that the people who experienced it no longer remember every part equally well.

The player is not solving a binary dialogue puzzle. Different witnesses can retain different fragments, lose source attribution, contradict archival records or remember only a later retelling.

## Reusable structure

A world event creates several independent provenance roots: a direct sighting, an institutional record, a second witness and a later report.

Time passes.

When the investigation reopens, current recall is projected from each NPC's durable ledger. One witness may remember both detail and source. Another may remember the detail but no longer remember who told them. A third may fail to retrieve the detail at all despite having historical exposure.

The player can improve the investigation by locating records, revisiting locations, finding independent witnesses or presenting a legitimate cue. Future cue mechanics should affect retrieval access, not rewrite the historical claim.

## Consequences

A weak investigation can still produce action, but the responsible NPC or institution should know whether the evidence is supported, contested or merely recalled without attribution.

A later discovery can show that an apparently independent rumor chain originated from one old witness. Conversely, an archival record can reveal that a witness really did encounter information they can no longer retrieve.

The loop supports rival investigations, old disappearances, environmental incidents, disputed sightings, tournament controversies, faction accusations, missing cargo, abandoned facilities and reopened rescue cases.

## Reduced version

Runs entirely in Ouros world-agent state:

historical claims → semantic time → recall projection → interview/record search → belief reassessment → travel or agenda consequence.

No AutoPTU dependency is required.

## Mechanically rich version

If investigation escalates into a structured confrontation or hazardous expedition, dependencies must be declared from the actual scene.

Target selection or visibility uses targeting/footprints/range/LoS.
Ordinary legal traversal uses base movement legality.
Push, pull, knockback, interception or forced movement uses complete movement.
Mechanical weather, hazards, zones or reactions use terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions.
Delayed or phase-bound effects require full turn/round lifecycle plus the owning Move/Ability/Item/Trainer Feature family.
Autonomous combat choices require AI tactical policy.
Minecraft presentation and acknowledgement require Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback support.

The reduced investigation remains valid if any richer family is partial or blocking.

## Canon boundary

No incident, witness, institution, region or historical event in this proposal is canon. Authored content must bind the pattern to approved setting material separately.
