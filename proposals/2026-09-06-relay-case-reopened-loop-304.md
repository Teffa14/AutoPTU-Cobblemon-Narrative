# The Relay Case Reopened — investigation arc candidate — Pass 304

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-06

A remote relay failed during a warning window. The first inquiry found physical tampering and linked a maintenance contractor through access records, but it never established intent. Weeks later, after unrelated travel and world events, a second body of evidence appears: a recovered work order, environmental traces at a nearby service path and testimony that was unavailable during the original inquiry.

The important structure is persistence of interpretation. The first finding survives as a historical assessment. New evidence produces a later finding rather than rewriting the earlier conclusion. NPCs who only learned the first result may continue acting on it. NPCs who receive the later evidence may revise their trust, obligations or investigation priorities. Public correction, private relay and institutional response remain separate delivery problems.

The site is designed as a revisitable local node. On the first visit it is a communications failure scene. On a later visit it can expose repairs, changed access controls, altered wild-Pokémon behavior, missing maintenance material or newly accessible service records. Environmental clues must be authored and provenance-backed; no object or behavior is treated as an omniscient truth marker.

Full encounter version: the relay sits on a weather-exposed ridge. A later inspection coincides with an unstable storm front, damaged walkways and a stranded technician. Wind can force displacement, debris can create temporary zones, a rescue may require interception, and a hostile actor or territorial Pokémon can complicate access. A timed equipment surge can alter safe routes while the investigation continues.

Reduced encounter version: the same ridge, outage history, stranded technician and evidence graph remain. The storm is presentation-only. Traversal uses fixed safe/blocked nodes and verified base movement. There is no mechanical wind knockback, no dynamic hazard zone, no delayed surge, no persistent status and no reaction-based rescue. Any confrontation uses only already verified tactical primitives and can be omitted without changing the mystery premise.
