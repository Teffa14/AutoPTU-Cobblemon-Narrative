# Proposed world pattern — The Restarted Investigation

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-05
Pass: 291

## Premise

A persistent investigation spans several in-world days. Different named NPCs have seen different parts of the same incident. A server restart, chunk unload or player absence must not reset the social evidence landscape.

One witness directly observed a blocked passage. A traveler received that report. A bystander never heard it. Later, a second witness can contradict or corroborate the first.

The interesting story comes from evidence divergence, not technical uptime.

## Full version

The player reconstructs an event by comparing direct observations, reports with source lineage, institutional records, later corrections, travel and schedule consequences, and social decisions about who was told.

A restarted server preserves the same individual ledgers. People continue to disagree for narrative reasons rather than persistence accidents.

If the investigation culminates in a mechanically rich encounter, exact dependencies must be declared for the selected scene.

A chase with interception or forced movement depends on complete movement. A hazardous storm scene depends on terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions. Delayed effects depend on full turn/round lifecycle plus the owning Move/Ability/Item/Feature/status family. Autonomous enemy choice depends on AI tactical policy. Visible Minecraft execution depends on Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback.

## Reduced version

The complete premise works without battle: interview witnesses, inspect records, compare provenance roots, discover who never received a warning, replan a route or appointment, expose a contradiction, and update relationships or institutional trust.

This version depends only on Ouros world-agent state and the existing verified semantic systems.

## Consequences

A technical restart cannot erase a witness statement, grant the statement to an uninformed NPC, create independent corroboration from one source, remove a contradiction or change belief merely because process memory was rebuilt.

## Canon boundary

The pattern supplies a reusable adventure structure. The actors, route and incident are placeholders and are not canon-approved world content.
