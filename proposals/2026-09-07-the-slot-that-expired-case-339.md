# The Slot That Expired — Pass 339

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-07
Canon authority: NONE

## Premise

Two legitimate field obligations depend on the same scarce survey kit. The first actor reserved it for the morning. The second actor needs it later in the day.

The morning task is delayed for reasons unrelated to the kit. The reservation window ends while the equipment remains physically in its storage location.

The first actor still intends to perform the delayed work. The second actor now has a later valid booking.

The conflict can emerge entirely from scheduling and coordination.

## Play structure

The player can reconstruct the earlier booking, current physical location, later booking and each actor's obligation.

Possible resolutions include an early voluntary release, rescheduling one task, locating a substitute later, or allowing the first window to expire before the next actor checks the equipment out.

Later consequences depend on which obligation was deferred and what happened in the world, rather than on a generic reservation penalty.

## Information boundaries

Knowing the kit exists does not reveal who currently has a valid temporal claim.

Seeing it in storage does not prove it is available.

Seeing an expired reservation does not prove the previous actor no longer needs it.

Having a valid booking does not put the equipment into an actor's hands.

A checked-out resource can remain away from its expected storage location after a booking window ends. That future case requires explicit checkout and return state rather than automatic relocation.

## Reduced version

The case can run through semantic world state using stable resource IDs, two reservation windows, existing schedules and obligations, explicit release/cancel operations, derived expiry, Pass 338 resource-gated intent selection, checkout/return events, selective communication and replanning.

No combat is required.

## Rich version

A later field assignment could be interrupted after checkout, making safe transport of the kit one objective during travel or a structured encounter.

Potential dependencies are explicit: ordinary base movement is VERIFIED within audited contracts; complete movement is PARTIAL for carrying under forced movement, interception or rescue; ordinary action-economy primitives are VERIFIED but any new tactical transfer/use action remains gated; full turn/round lifecycle is PARTIAL for timed effects; full stateful damage is PARTIAL for damaged equipment; terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions is MIXED/PARTIAL/BLOCKING if field conditions affect recovery; Items are PARTIAL and individually gated if the kit has a PTU effect; AI tactical policy is BLOCKING for protect/transfer/withdraw-with-kit objectives; Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics remains PARTIAL/BLOCKING end-to-end.

The reduced version preserves the narrative premise without requiring those mechanics.

## Canon questions

Before promotion, resolve region, owning institution, participating NPCs, the two field obligations, substitute availability, override authority, local late-return policy and whether the kit is mundane equipment or maps to a mechanically meaningful PTU Item.
