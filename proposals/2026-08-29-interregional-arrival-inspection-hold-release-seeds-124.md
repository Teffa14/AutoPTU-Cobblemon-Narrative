# Ouros Proposals — Interregional Arrival, Inspection, Hold & Release — Pass 124

Status: NON-CANON PROPOSALS. These are original Ouros candidates. They do not establish inspection powers, national borders, customs law, ecological danger, ownership or mechanical rules.
Date: 2026-08-29

## Design premise

These seeds assume only that a future canon decision may establish a specific institution with a narrow inspection or receiving mandate at a particular place. If that authority does not exist in canon, the seed must be reframed as voluntary intake, facility admission, research receiving, conservation review or ordinary logistics.

The useful drama comes from scope, timing, handoffs and institutional memory rather than from automatic criminal suspicion.

## The Ferry Arrived, the Consignment Did Not Clear

A scheduled ferry completes its port call normally. One small consignment remains at a receiving desk because its supporting record has not arrived through the expected channel.

The ferry departure, shipment custody, inspection hold and final delivery all have different timestamps. A merchant insists that “the shipment arrived yesterday,” while the destination workshop insists that “nothing arrived.” Both statements can be accurate within their scopes.

Potential follow-up: the missing record is eventually found in an older institutional alias rather than exposed as fraud.

## The Stamp Was Real, the Mandate Was Narrower

A visiting field team carries an authentic authorization issued by a real institution. The problem is scope: it allows use of one research site but does not establish access to the adjacent protected area.

The conflict becomes an institutional clarification problem rather than a fake-document mystery.

Possible consequences include delayed fieldwork, a temporary alternate site, an explicit recognition request and a later revision to public orientation material.

## The Pokémon Came From Elsewhere, That Proves Nothing Else

A Pokémon accompanying a long-term visitor becomes the subject of local rumor because the species is uncommon in the area.

Conservation records show only individual arrival provenance. There is no evidence of a wild population, breeding population, ecological harm or disease.

The quest asks the player to reconstruct what is actually known and stop several systems from collapsing provenance into accusation. The outcome may improve local recordkeeping without deciding any broader ecological policy.

## The Temporary Desk Became the Fastest Route

A receiving/inspection desk opens temporarily inside an underused transit annex during a short institutional program. Travelers discover that the annex also offers better shelter, clearer wayfinding and easier transfers.

When the program ends, the desk closes but passenger flow keeps using the annex. Transit Hub and Public Space systems must decide whether to support the new behavior.

The inspection layer records only the temporary institutional use; the permanent route change belongs elsewhere.

## The Old Gate Still Has the New Number

A gateway moved after renovation. Its identifier stayed the same, but older maps, photographs and staff recollections still point to the former doorway.

A later archive search appears to show that a subject “never entered the inspection room.” The contradiction resolves when the player reconstructs the gateway relocation timeline.

No forged record is required.

## Three Manifests, One Crate

The same crate appears on three documents with different contents counts.

The first is a planned load manifest. The second records what actually departed. The third records inspection intake after one item was removed at an intermediate handoff.

The mystery is solved by document purpose and timestamps. The crate identity can remain stable throughout.

## The Hold Ended, the Delivery Did Not

An inspection hold is formally released in the morning. The receiving laboratory does not record delivery until late afternoon.

Players are asked to find the “missing” interval. Courier records reveal an ordinary staging transfer and a vehicle substitution caused by an unrelated route problem.

This seed teaches that inspection release and delivery completion are independent states.

## The Lab Accepted the Transfer, the Reserve Did Not

A specialized receiving laboratory completes intake for a transferred Pokémon. The destination reserve has not yet decided whether its authored placement conditions are satisfied.

The Pokémon remains under valid care/custody while the ecological placement decision is pending.

A battle, if one occurs nearby, cannot decide placement. The central tension is coordination among receiving, welfare and conservation systems.

## The Visitor Was Cleared, the Equipment Was Referred

A visiting researcher has the right credentials for entry. One piece of specialized equipment has an unresolved provenance/condition question and is referred to another owner system.

The researcher can enter without the equipment. This creates a practical fieldwork problem rather than converting the whole person into “held” state.

Alternative solutions may include borrowing approved equipment, postponing one method, changing the research objective or waiting for the referral.

## No Inspection Was Required Yesterday

A temporary inspection requirement begins after a narrowly scoped incident or institutional decision. A shipment that arrived the previous day has no inspection record because none was required at that time.

Later investigators initially treat the missing record as a gap. The timeline proves it is a legitimate absence.

This is a reusable provenance lesson: missing records can reflect rules that did not yet exist.

## Two Agencies, One Waiting Room

Two unrelated institutions share the same receiving room on different days. Old public notices refer to the room by the building name rather than the responsible body.

Years later, a citizen remembers “the inspection office” as one institution. The archive shows two distinct mandates and two separate record systems.

The mystery concerns institutional identity rather than deception.

## The Package Was Open, the Contents Were Not Judged

A container arrives with visible integrity damage. The inspection episode records that observation and refers the contents to the relevant owner system.

A rumor later claims that “inspectors proved the material was dangerous.” The actual record never made that conclusion.

This seed can connect Public Notices, Rumor/Testimony, Batch Traceability and Case Authority while preserving each system's scope.

## The Transfer Facility Became a Classroom

A receiving site that once handled a regional research-transfer program later becomes a training classroom after the program ends.

Old fixtures, labels and photographs remain. Students can investigate how the former process worked without reactivating it. The site's institutional memory becomes a low-stakes exploration hook and a future callback location.

## Provenance mystery — Six Arrivals, Two Releases

A single journey generates six legitimate milestones:

1. vehicle reaches the hub;
2. subject enters the receiving facility;
3. inspection intake is logged;
4. an inspection hold begins;
5. inspection releases the subject;
6. destination institution accepts the subject.

Two public summaries use the word “released” for different milestones: release from inspection and release from destination custody later in the story.

The player resolves the discrepancy through subject, actor and timestamp scope.

## Provenance mystery — The Correct Form for the Wrong Door

An authorization is authentic, current and issued to the correct actor. It applies to a different facility entrance and different activity scope.

The holder, desk staff and later historian can all describe it differently without anyone falsifying the document.

The solution is recognition of scope rather than a forgery reveal.

## Exploration — The Receiving Hall Beneath the New Platform

A station rebuild leaves an older receiving hall physically intact beneath a newer passenger platform. The hall is no longer active but can be entered through an authorized archive/maintenance visit.

Players compare wall numbering, former gateway maps, cargo markings, old public notices and photographs to reconstruct how interregional arrivals were once routed. A persistent Pokémon observation may help identify which passage was still used after the formal desk moved, but only if that observation already exists in world state.

Implementation-safe form: all traversed rooms are static, inspected and authorized. No active machinery, moving train, sealed evidence or live inspection subject enters BattleSpec.

## Long arc — A Region Learns Its Gateways

Phase one establishes ordinary movement before any inspection storyline. Visitors, Pokémon partners, freight, research equipment and local products move through ports and stations using the existing Travel, Courier and Interregional systems.

Phase two introduces one tightly scoped receiving requirement tied to a specific institution and reason already established by canon. The system creates new queues, temporary desks and misunderstandings about who actually has authority.

Phase three produces differentiated outcomes. Some subjects clear immediately. One document needs verification. One observation is referred to Conservation or Care. Another subject was never in scope at all.

Phase four shows social adaptation. Couriers change handoff timing. A café near the temporary desk gains customers. A research group learns to send records earlier. Public orientation becomes more precise.

Phase five narrows or ends the original requirement. Temporary arrangements may disappear, move or be repurposed. The history remains in maps, staff memory and archived records.

A later arc can reuse the old gateway without assuming the original restriction still exists.

## Encounter concept — Arrival Hall Perimeter

Narrative premise: an unrelated conflict threatens immediate access outside a receiving hall while staff and waiting subjects need to withdraw.

Full intended version may require:
- targeting/footprints/range/LoS;
- base movement legality;
- complete movement including Intercept, escort movement and possible forced displacement;
- core calculations;
- action economy/initiative;
- full turn/round lifecycle for staged withdrawal;
- full stateful damage pipeline and status lifecycle for selected legal combat effects;
- terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions if a protected corridor or changing boundary is tactical;
- move-specific behavior, abilities, items and Trainer Features/perks only where current contracts support the selected combatants;
- AI legal-action infrastructure;
- AI tactical policy for WITHDRAW/PROTECT/CLEAR_ROUTE;
- Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback for semantic evacuation and boundary presentation.

Reduced version: READY with current baseline. Close the inspection operation first. Move staff, visitors, Pokémon not selected as combatants, documents and controlled subjects to safe world state. Exclude the receiving room from BattleSpec. Resolve a conventional static encounter on the exterior apron. Victory may secure immediate access only. It does not clear an inspection, release a subject or reopen the desk.

## Encounter concept — Sealed Consignment Diversion

Narrative premise: a shipment already in valid custody must be diverted away from a conflict at a transit chokepoint.

Full intended version may require complete movement for escort/interception, lifecycle for a timed handoff, reactions/zones for protected crossing rules, objective-aware AI and semantic playback. If the physical package remains on-grid and can be moved or damaged, exact object-interaction and damage contracts would also be required; they must not be invented here.

Reduced version: READY. Complete the custody handoff and move the consignment off-grid before combat. AutoPTU resolves a static fight controlling the chokepoint. Courier decides whether the next delivery leg can proceed. Battle victory cannot authenticate contents or end an inspection hold.

## Encounter concept — Wildlife Transfer Gate

Narrative premise: a receiving facility used for an authored conservation transfer has an external access problem while a specific Pokémon transfer is underway.

Full intended version may require Intercept/withdrawal, lifecycle, reactions/zones, objective-aware AI and semantic playback. Any health, sedation, restraint, ecological exposure or special status mechanic requires exact PTU/Caelo and engine evidence and is currently excluded unless separately verified.

Reduced version: READY. Finish the Pokémon intake/placement step in world state first, or pause it safely under the responsible owner system. The transferred Pokémon is a noncombatant outside BattleSpec unless Ouros explicitly selects it as a legal combatant for an independent reason. Run the encounter on a static external perimeter. Victory does not grant ownership, ecological release or institutional clearance.

## Canon questions

These seeds require future review of whether Ouros has any scoped arrival-inspection institutions, where they operate, which mandates they possess and which subject classes can fall within those mandates.

Nothing here establishes national borders, customs law, immigration status, universal search powers, automatic ecological danger, automatic quarantine or a universal Pokémon-transfer procedure.
