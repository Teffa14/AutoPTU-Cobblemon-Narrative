# The Confirmation That Never Became the Route — Pass 373

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-09
Canon effect: NONE

## Premise

A field crew is already moving toward an observation site when its route office sends a valid change of destination. The message reaches the crew leader and becomes usable evidence. That delivery wakes the leader's planner.

At the same semantic minute, a separate high-priority obligation becomes due: a nearby crew has reported damaged field equipment and needs immediate assistance. The planner consumes both causes and lawfully selects the rescue obligation instead of the requested reroute.

The route office later sees that its instruction was delivered and assumes the crew changed course toward the observation site. A dispatcher reviewing the planning provenance sees that the message was considered but lost to the emergency. A third actor may know only that the crew failed to appear at the requested destination.

All three perspectives can be sincere.

The investigation is about reconstructing the causal chain rather than discovering a liar.

## Original Ouros use

The scenario can attach to any approved institution whose work legitimately uses field teams, route coordination and time-sensitive observations. No named institution, location, species or communications technology is promoted by this proposal.

The useful persistent facts are:

- which instruction was authored and delivered;
- which receiver actually incorporated it as evidence;
- which replan wake-up it created;
- which other triggers entered the same planning window;
- which plan was selected;
- whether acknowledgement or check-back occurred later;
- what action the crew actually began.

Pass 373 makes the first four steps more durable without claiming the later steps.

## Consequence loop

If the player reaches the crew after the rescue diversion, the original observation window may already be degraded or lost. That outcome should not erase everything learned during the failed attempt.

A later attempt can preserve route knowledge, mapped environmental obstacles, verified contact information, institutional permission or a known safe fallback point while still consuming time-sensitive supplies or losing the original window. This uses the general persistence-after-failure lesson extracted from Pokémon Mystère Exploration without copying its dungeon layouts, resource names, town, characters or progression system.

## Reduced implementation

The reduced form requires no AutoPTU battle.

Use semantic travel, ordinary communication delivery, private knowledge, Pass 373 materialization provenance, replan triggers and ordinary agenda selection. Represent the damaged equipment and observation deadline as world-state obligations. Rescue and reroute outcomes can resolve through deterministic travel and state transitions.

This version preserves the narrative premise even while tactical objective AI remains incomplete.

## Full encounter version

The richer version lets the player intercept the diverted crew in a field corridor while both the rescue deadline and the observation window continue to matter.

Possible objectives include reaching the crew before a junction, escorting damaged equipment to a safe node, preserving samples, choosing which team receives limited transport capacity or completing a short observation before withdrawing.

Wild Pokémon or an environmental obstruction may add pressure. Defeating every opponent is not inherently the success condition.

Forced displacement, reaction zones, weather phases, delayed effects, complex statuses, ability-triggered terrain and Trainer Feature interrupts are optional enrichments only. They must not be silently assumed by the reduced version.
