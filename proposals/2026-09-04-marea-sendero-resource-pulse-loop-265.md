# Marea/Sendero resource-pulse field loop — Pass 265

Status: PROPOSED narrative/worldbuilding candidate. No fixture resource, site variant, response magnitude or new actor is canon-approved by this proposal.

A short-lived resource event can make one part of Sendero unusually active without changing how many Fletchling exist in the authoritative population. The player can encounter the event as a field mystery rather than a combat trigger: one visit establishes ordinary activity, a later visit shows several already-counted members using the same resource window, and subsequent visits reveal decline or a spatial shift in where the resource is available.

The narrative payoff is epistemic as much as ecological. A casual observer can conclude that “there are suddenly more Fletchling.” Better longitudinal evidence can instead show temporary concentration. This creates room for disagreement among unnamed observers, field notes that become more precise over time, and future conservation/management stories where the important question is why the animals are concentrated rather than how many spawned.

The event should have recognizable phases without requiring a quest marker at every step. Environmental cues can establish onset, activity can peak, and the resource can later decline or become available elsewhere. The world ledger owns that resource history. Individual Pokémon retain their own source identity and ecological histories.

Reduced implementation: the event changes resource availability and projection eligibility among already-counted sources. Minecraft/Cobblemon presents a busier field window, but no actor is tactically instructed to path toward, defend or fight over the resource. The event survives restart and closes only through its semantic horizon. This version preserves the narrative premise while avoiding missing PTU/AI behavior.

Full implementation: individual Pokémon can detect, approach, ignore, track or contest the resource according to species/individual/context policy. Ordinary approach requires targeting/LoS where relevant, base movement, action economy/lifecycle for tactical sequencing, AI legal-action infrastructure and AI tactical policy. Physical interception, displacement or forced access adds complete movement. A resource patch that acts as a mechanical zone adds terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions. Any Move, Ability, Item or Trainer Feature interaction activates those exact capability families. Damage or persistent status adds the full stateful damage pipeline and status lifecycle respectively. Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics remains presentation/playback and must not invent any missing rule.

Potential longer arc: several temporary resource events across a region could teach the player to distinguish phenology from abundance, then reveal a larger pattern when resource timing shifts between sites. That larger interpretation remains proposed until Ouros has concrete regional resource canon and authority for the environmental observations.

Open canon decisions: identify actual Marea/Sendero resources; determine which species can respond; establish whether the event is natural, managed or anthropogenic; define evidence that authorizes onset/closure; and decide how much of the phenomenon becomes visible to ordinary NPC knowledge versus specialist observation. No answer is assumed here.
