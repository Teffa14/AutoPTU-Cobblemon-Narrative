# The Second Team Left Before the Warning Arrived — Pass 420

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON

## Premise

A first field team returns from an assignment with a bounded observation: a route, work site, crossing or chamber is only partly usable, temporarily unsafe or no longer consistent with the record they were given.

The observation reaches one explicit custodian. A second team is preparing to depart.

The second team does not automatically know the warning because it works for the same institution. Its behavior depends on whether the warning is actually delivered before departure.

## Canon-safe anchors

This pattern can reuse existing Ouros surfaces such as Marea Field Office, Estación Mirador or Sendero del Vidrio where appropriate. Those names are anchors only. This proposal does not add a new incident, route failure, expedition, obligation or named participant to canon.

## State sequence

The narrative should preserve separate facts:

1. The physical condition exists in world state.
2. A field outcome creates a sourced observation.
3. One explicit custodian receives or owns that observation record.
4. A delivery to one explicit recipient is scheduled through a concrete channel.
5. The recipient remains stale while the message is queued, failed or awaiting acknowledgement.
6. Successful delivery creates a private report claim for that recipient only.
7. Event-driven replanning may then change the recipient's departure, route, equipment or request for help.

Another team member may still hold stale information unless separately told.

## Player-facing versions

The player can be part of the first team and decide how quickly to report.

The player can be part of the second team and depart before the warning arrives.

The player can encounter both teams later and discover that apparently reckless behavior followed from information timing rather than incompetence or deception.

A delayed message can also become evidence in a later institutional dispute without proving that any individual intentionally withheld it.

## Reduced version

The reduced version uses only persistent observations, private knowledge, semantic time, communications, schedules, obligations, travel, permissions and selective replanning.

No tactical battle is required. The meaningful consequence is whether a named actor receives the report before a commitment or departure threshold.

## Full version

The initial observation may come from an evacuation, escort, rescue or extraction scene under tactical pressure.

Possible full-version beats:

- reach an observation point before a route closes;
- protect the person carrying evidence;
- withdraw after collecting enough information instead of defeating every opponent;
- survive a timed environmental phase;
- bring a report back that changes the next team's plan.

## Reduced fallback for blocked mechanics

If the rich scene depends on unsupported forced movement, reaction windows or environmental phases, the tactical portion can stop at a simpler admitted objective such as reaching an ordinary legal position or surviving an ordinary resolved battle.

The same persistent observation and explicit-warning-delivery premise remains intact. Minecraft does not implement the missing PTU rule in the adapter.
