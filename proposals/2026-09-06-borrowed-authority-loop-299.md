# Borrowed Authority — investigation loop — Pass 299

Status: PROPOSED OUROS MATERIAL; NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-06

A route dispatcher tells several travelers that a respected field authority personally cleared an alternate route. The dispatcher is the real immediate speaker, but the message borrows the authority's name. The route instruction may be correct or incorrect; the source question remains independently investigable.

The first discovery can be a direct denial from the named authority. That opens a dispute but does not prove which person is lying. A later dispatch log, signed handoff record or other provenance-independent authorship record can establish that the named authority did not originate the instruction. An explicit admission or equivalent evidence can finally establish that the dispatcher knowingly used the false attribution.

Different NPCs can stop at different phases. One traveler may continue trusting the dispatcher because only the denial reached them. Another may see the archival chain and know the attribution was false without knowing whether the dispatcher made an honest clerical error. A third can obtain evidence of intention and change their relationship state. Later dialogue and planning should consume those different evidence histories rather than a global quest flag.

Reduced implementation: statement, delivery, subjective attribution, source denial, independent record, exposure finding, directional relationship consequence and world-level replanning. No structured battle is required.

Full implementation option: the disputed instruction can lead to an expedition or pursuit. Any interception, push/pull, knockback or forced movement depends on complete movement. Mechanical storm, terrain, hazard, zone or reaction behavior depends on terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions. Delayed/status mechanics depend on lifecycle/status plus the exact Move, Ability, Item or Trainer Feature owner. General autonomous tactical choice depends on AI tactical policy. Minecraft-visible authoritative resolution depends on adapter/playback support.

Canon questions remain open: which Ouros institutions can issue authenticated field orders, what records they actually keep, how signatures/credentials work in-setting, and which places or named NPCs should host the first authored version. None are decided here.
