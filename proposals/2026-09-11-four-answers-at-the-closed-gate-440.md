# Four Answers at the Closed Gate — Pass 440

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Canon effect: NONE
Location assignment: UNRESOLVED

## Premise

A specialist accepted a limited assistance window. Credible evidence later showed that the intended access was unavailable, and the route remained blocked when the promised start minute arrived.

The story now depends on what the specialist chooses to do with that conflict between obligation and present reality.

The proposal deliberately supports several responses rather than treating a blocker as automatic cancellation or automatic heroics.

## Four immediate responses

`ATTEMPT_FULFILLMENT`

The specialist decides to try to honor the obligation. This does not mean the path is legal, safe or passable. The next world-planning step must still check travel, permissions, resources and current evidence.

`WAIT_FOR_CLEARANCE`

The specialist remains committed to the accepted window but does not proceed while the current blocker persists. Waiting can preserve safety while consuming scarce appointment time and potentially causing the window to expire.

`REQUEST_RENEGOTIATION`

The specialist decides the original terms no longer fit reality. A later communication can propose a new time, place, scope or alternative. Until those terms are actually sent, received and accepted, the original agreement remains the only accepted one.

`ABANDON_OBLIGATION`

The specialist decides not to keep pursuing fulfillment under the current agreement. This becomes a durable decision but does not itself decide blame, trust loss, excuse, compensation or whether another actor takes over.

## Reduced version

This version requires no battle resolution.

A survey appointment opens while its access path is closed. The responder has current blocker evidence and a durable `BLOCKED_AT_START` assessment. The planner records one immediate disposition.

If the choice is wait, semantic time continues and later evidence may clear the route.

If the choice is renegotiation, communication remains a separate step and can fail or arrive late.

If the choice is abandonment, the original promise remains queryable and later social/accountability systems can decide consequences using provenance rather than inference.

If the choice is attempt, ordinary travel/access checks may still reject the planned route without creating combat.

## Intended rich version

An attempt to fulfill can become an encounter only when the current world state and admitted mechanics justify one. Examples include reaching a safe inspection marker, escorting authorized personnel, protecting a survey while wild Pokémon react to disturbance, retrieving one required object, or withdrawing when conditions become unsafe.

Success should be tied to the explicit objective rather than automatically to defeating every opponent.

## Reduced structured fallback

If the story needs a tactical presentation before the rich objective stack exists, preserve the same premise with static terrain, ordinary legal movement, audited targeting and basic admitted actions. Keep the objective timer and the obligation/disposition state in Ouros world simulation. Do not reproduce missing PTU rules in the Minecraft adapter.

The scene can end through a simple admitted boundary such as reaching a legal marker or withdrawing, while richer escort/protection/hazard behavior remains disabled.

## Canon questions

No region, NPC, institution, route or Pokémon population is assigned by this proposal.

Canon approval would still need answers for where such appointments occur, which institutions can authorize or close access, what kinds of specialists exist in that place, and which local ecology could react to survey activity.

The four response semantics are global implementation candidates, not assertions about the personality of any existing named NPC.
