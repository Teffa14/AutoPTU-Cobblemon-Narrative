# The Answer Can Arrive After the Problem Changed — Pass 426

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON

Premise

A named NPC asks another person for help. The recipient answers honestly, but the world continues while the reply travels. By the time the requester receives ACCEPT, DEFER, REJECT or COUNTERPROPOSE, the original situation may have changed.

The story value comes from preserving both facts: what the responder decided and what the world looked like when the requester finally learned it.

Possible Marea binding surfaces

Mara Veyra, Teo Lark, Nerea Sol and Oren Vale remain possible regression actors because their existing canon roles already create plausible coordination needs. This proposal does not assign a new incident, obligation, schedule, relationship or communication channel to any of them.

Reduced version

Mara requests help with a field problem. Teo receives the request and records a durable response. The answer travels through an ordinary channel.

Before it arrives, Mara may solve the immediate issue another way, move to a different site, suspend the work, or learn new evidence. Teo's reply remains historically valid. Once it arrives, Mara can replan from the current state instead of pretending the answer arrived earlier.

An ACCEPT received after the immediate problem is over might still create a later repair appointment or follow-up inspection.

A DEFER can arrive after Mara has already sought another person. It may still establish when Teo becomes available.

A REJECT can explain why no help came without retroactively rewriting the earlier request.

A COUNTERPROPOSE can open a new branch only after Mara actually receives the alternative.

No battle is required for this form. It uses private knowledge, semantic time, communication latency, schedules, obligations, travel, durable world-action intents and event-driven replanning.

Reduced fallback for rich scenes

If the tactical capability families are incomplete, the premise remains unchanged. The active team resolves the immediate scene through a basic verified path or exits. The responder's answer still travels at world level and affects only later plans after delivery.

Narrative invariants

A response decision and its receipt are different world facts.

Late information can remain useful without being retroactively timely.

A reply cannot change requester knowledge before delivery.

An ACCEPT does not teleport the helper or create free time.

A REJECT does not automatically damage a relationship.

A COUNTERPROPOSE does not edit the requester's schedule remotely.

Minecraft presentation cannot infer that a visible message, nearby NPC or changed environment means the corresponding private information was received.

Longer-term arc use

Repeated delayed replies can reveal institutional strain, unreliable channels, travel pressure and differing work rhythms without hard-coded personality labels. A character can be consistently willing yet often too late. Another can reply quickly but usually narrow the scope. Those patterns should emerge from persistent state and authored events before canon treats them as reputation.
