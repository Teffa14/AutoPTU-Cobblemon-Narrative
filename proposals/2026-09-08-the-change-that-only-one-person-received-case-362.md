# The Change That Only One Person Received — Pass 362

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-08

A field team has a valid pickup authorization for a shared environmental sensor. The receiver sends a request to move the pickup to another location and later time. The provider actually receives the request, accepts it and issues a successor authorization. Another team member never receives the update and continues toward the original rendezvous.

The session ends after the acceptance but before the parties meet again. On restart, the world must preserve the original authorization, the delivered change request, the responder decision, the successor authorization and each NPC's own knowledge state.

Narrative use

The disagreement comes from unequal information, not deception. The provider can correctly insist that the handoff moved. The uninformed teammate can correctly insist that the only schedule they know is the original one. The player can reconstruct the communication lineage, intercept the teammate, redirect them, protect the new observation window or allow the mistake to create a later allocation conflict.

Reduced implementation

The reduced version uses semantic time, world travel, resource requests, handoff authorization, appointment notices, communication delivery, reschedule lineage, private knowledge and checkpoint recovery. No AutoPTU battle is required.

Rich implementation

The teammate acting on stale information is already crossing a field route when the mismatch becomes visible. Reaching them before the successor window opens can become an interception objective. A wild encounter or environmental obstruction may complicate the route, but success remains informing or rerouting the actor in time rather than defeating every opponent.
