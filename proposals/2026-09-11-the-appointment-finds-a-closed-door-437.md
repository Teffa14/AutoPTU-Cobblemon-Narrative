# The Appointment Finds a Closed Door — Pass 437

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Canon effect: NONE
Location assignment: UNRESOLVED

## Premise

Two people already negotiated a specific assistance appointment. Before the window begins, the responder learns that the original way of carrying it out may no longer be possible.

The cause can be mundane or dramatic: a route closes, access permission is withdrawn, required equipment becomes unavailable, the responder becomes temporarily unable to travel, or new evidence shows that the original premise was wrong.

The agreement remains real history. The blocker is newer history.

The story comes from what the responder does with the conflict between those two facts.

## Narrative use

This pattern supports recurring NPC continuity without making promises infallible or meaningless.

A responder who discovers the problem can:

- try to clear the blocker through an appropriate owner;
- contact the requester and explain the changed situation;
- formulate a new counterproposal;
- seek another lawful route or resource;
- wait for a temporary restriction to clear;
- prioritize an emergency and risk missing the appointment;
- discover that the original task is no longer necessary.

None of those outcomes is implied by the viability observation itself.

The requester does not learn that the plan is blocked merely because the responder knows it. Communication remains explicit.

## Example seed

A specialist agreed to survey a small infrastructure problem during a fixed future window. The accepted scope is inspection and diagnosis only.

Before departure, a credible notice says the normal approach is closed. The reason is not predetermined for canon. It could be structural damage, temporary environmental risk, a permit change, wildlife activity, or another authored cause.

The specialist records that the appointment is currently blocked and replans.

If the closure clears before the appointment, the original promise can still become viable without rewriting the earlier blocker. If it does not clear, the specialist may need to contact the requester, negotiate different terms or miss the obligation.

## Reduced implementation

Keep the closed approach authoritative at world level. Do not instantiate a tactical scene at the obstruction.

The responder can receive evidence, mark the commitment blocked, reconsider the agenda and communicate. The eventual narrative result can be reschedule, alternative advice, a missed appointment, permission escalation or later restoration of access.

Required engine families:

- no battle capability family;
- semantic world time;
- private knowledge and explicit communication;
- negotiated assistance commitment persistence;
- pre-start viability observations;
- event-driven replanning;
- ordinary travel/access owners only if a later decision actually uses them.

This reduced version preserves the entire premise: the appointment existed, the world changed, and the responder had to react.

## Intended mechanically rich version

If later design authorizes an explorable obstruction rather than a simple world-state closure, the same premise can produce a field objective such as inspect the safe boundary, reach a marker, escort a qualified worker, protect an inspection, recover a nonliving tool or withdraw when risk increases.

Combat is optional unless the authored situation independently creates hostile opposition.

Potential capability dependencies must be declared by the final encounter contract rather than inferred from this proposal.

## Design guardrails

Do not convert every inconvenience into a tactical encounter.

Do not mark the commitment failed merely because a blocker was observed before the window.

Do not erase the blocker if access later returns.

Do not let the requester know about the change without an explicit information path.

Do not let Minecraft presentation invent route legality, permissions or objective completion.

Do not let a world-level `BLOCKED` state stand in for missing PTU mechanics.

## Canon questions

This seed does not decide where the appointment occurs, which NPCs participate, what institution controls access, whether a Pokémon caused or responds to the obstruction, or whether the task belongs to a larger questline.

Those bindings require separate canon review.
