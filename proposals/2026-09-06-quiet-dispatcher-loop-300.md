# Proposal — The Quiet Dispatcher — Pass 300

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-06

A route coordinator learns that a temporary crossing is unsafe. A rival expedition asks whether the route is usable. The coordinator has three meaningful choices: disclose the closure, refuse to answer, or deliberately provide false information. The chosen posture depends on current goals, secrecy value, relationship history, duty conflict, expected harm and exposure risk rather than a scripted global quest flag.

The same premise can branch without changing canonical truth. One coordinator may tell a trusted colleague immediately. The same person may remain silent toward an unknown competitor. Under severe goal pressure and weak duties, another actor may choose deception. Later investigators can reconstruct the difference between lack of information, withheld information and an authored falsehood because each path has distinct provenance.

Narrative uses include competitive expeditions, hidden research sites, faction negotiations, witness protection, illicit trade, rescue coordination, rival teams, disputed archaeological claims and private ecological discoveries. Silence can itself create consequences without being retroactively labelled a lie.

Reduced implementation version:

private evidence -> communication opportunity -> truth/silence/deception posture -> existing audience/delivery path when a message is sent -> receiver belief/replanning -> later consequence.

This version needs no tactical battle.

Mechanically rich version:

A deceptive route report may send a party into a hazardous field site, contested ruin or pursuit. The narrative premise remains valid while tactical mechanics are gated by exact capability families.
