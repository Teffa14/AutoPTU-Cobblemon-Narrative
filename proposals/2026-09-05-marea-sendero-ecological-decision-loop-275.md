# Marea/Sendero ecological decision loop — Pass 275

Status: PROPOSED. No canon promotion.

Pass 274 ends with a scoped ecological warning rather than a prediction. Pass 275 turns that uncertainty into a playable management problem without revealing a single objectively correct policy.

The player encounters a temporary Sendero decision point after a fixture-only warning persists through matched observations. Two provisional decision owners use the same evidence but hold different objectives. One gives more weight to plausible irreversible habitat loss and prefers a temporary local buffer. The other gives more weight to continued access and wants one short matched-monitoring cycle before restriction. Their disagreement does not fork ecological truth.

The quest can expose three different kinds of threshold through ordinary dialogue, records and world presentation. The ecological record describes what the habitat appears to be doing. The utility record describes what each responsible actor values. The decision threshold describes when that actor changes management. The player can discover that two people who agree on all measurements may still choose differently because they are solving different decision problems.

The monitoring branch is deliberately bounded. The player does not earn progress by collecting an arbitrary number of observations. A useful survey targets a declared uncertainty that could change the next action before the review horizon closes. A low-value sighting still enters the archive but does not become mandatory quest grind.

A precautionary branch can authorize a temporary local access buffer while uncertainty remains. The action must declare owner, scope, cost, reversibility, review horizon and rollback condition. Minecraft may show signs, rope, a changed NPC route or another non-mechanical boundary. Those visuals do not become PTU blockers, difficult terrain, hazards or reactions.

When the next matched window weakens the original warning, the current policy does not flip automatically. It enters `REASSESSMENT_REQUIRED`. The responsible actor can then remove, extend or replace the temporary measure according to the same declared objectives and evidence. Removing the measure does not assert ecological recovery; recovery remains governed by Pass 272.

This structure supports longer arcs. A later observation can reveal that an apparently cautious intervention imposed a real cost on another activity, that a low-cost monitoring method failed to resolve the decision-critical uncertainty, or that a different stakeholder accepted the same ecological risk for a different public objective. These consequences remain governance and world-state questions until explicit canon adoption.
