# The Survey Camp With No Survey Result — Pass 380

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Canon effect: NONE

## Premise

A field team receives a legitimate assignment to inspect a persistent research site. The world can prove that the team selected the trip, departed and reached the destination. It cannot prove that the survey objective completed.

When another group later reaches the site, the camp itself shows that arrival was real, but the work record is incomplete. This creates an investigation after a proven travel terminal rather than another mystery about whether the team ever arrived.

## What can be true at the same time

- the dispatching office can correctly show that the team was assigned and departed;
- V18 can correctly preserve that the destination was reached;
- a colleague can correctly say that no completed survey was received;
- a later visitor can find signs of occupation at the site;
- nobody yet knows why arrival did not become a recorded objective result.

No actor needs to lie for the discrepancy to exist.

## Candidate explanations

These are hypotheses only and must not be treated as canon without evidence:

- an equipment fault stopped the planned work after camp was established;
- the team discovered a nearby ecological problem and reprioritized;
- communications failed after arrival;
- a later instruction changed the objective;
- conditions forced a withdrawal before the work completed;
- another actor interfered with equipment, records or access;
- the survey happened but its evidence never entered the authoritative world owner that future objective-completion provenance will require.

The last possibility is deliberately useful for systems testing: world state should distinguish an unrecorded assumption from a replayable event.

## Exploration structure

The site should be reusable rather than a disposable dungeon. A first visit can establish the camp and incomplete work. Later visits can reveal changed environmental conditions, repaired or further damaged equipment, new messages, changed access, different wild-Pokémon behavior or consequences from whichever explanation is eventually approved.

Evidence should come from independent owners where possible: travel provenance, communications, private knowledge, physical site state and later objective records. One duplicated rumor should not become multiple confirmations.

## Reduced version

This version requires no AutoPTU encounter.

The team travels semantically to the placeholder site. V18 preserves the replayable arrival terminal. No objective-completion record exists. A later actor receives or discovers that discrepancy and can replan toward investigation, contact or another visit.

The narrative premise remains intact even if the physical site is represented abstractly while geography and Minecraft construction remain pending canon approval.

## Full version

A later site visit may become an objective-based encounter. Possible goals include checking several observation points, locating personnel, protecting instruments, escorting a vulnerable actor, restoring a safe route or withdrawing with evidence.

A Pokémon encounter can create pressure without making defeat of every opponent the win condition. The site should remain investigable after combat or avoidance.

## Canon questions before promotion

- approved geographic placement;
- field site's purpose and ownership;
- institution with authority to issue the survey;
- species and habitat evidence for the chosen location;
- technology and communication coverage available there;
- whether the site already exists in established lore under another function;
- what evidence qualifies as a completed survey under the future objective-completion owner.

Until those questions are resolved, every place, institution, survey purpose and ecological detail in this proposal is a placeholder.
