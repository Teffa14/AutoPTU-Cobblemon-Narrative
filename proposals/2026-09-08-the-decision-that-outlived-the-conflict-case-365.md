# The Decision That Outlived the Conflict — Pass 365

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON.

A field ecology team and a restoration crew both need the same indivisible survey meter during overlapping windows. The rescheduled ecology pickup is assessed while the restoration reservation is still active. A scheduler with explicit authority over that resource reviews the conflict and selects the ecology window because of a documented observation deadline.

Later, the restoration crew independently cancels its reservation. By morning the calendar looks empty around the ecology slot. A restart occurs before the teams meet again.

The durable story is not merely that the ecology team has the meter. Ouros can preserve that a conflict really existed, which actor was authorized to decide, what competing reservation was visible, what course was selected, when the choice happened and what evidence was cited. A technician inspecting only the current calendar may reasonably question why allocation intervention was ever needed. The scheduler can answer from contemporaneous provenance rather than omniscient narration.

The decision does not itself cancel the competing reservation, move the meter, accept a handoff successor or transfer custody. Those effects remain owned by later systems.

## Reduced version

This version requires semantic time, reservations, reschedule lineage, historical conflict admission, authority evidence, allocation resolution, communications, private knowledge and checkpoint recovery. It requires no AutoPTU battle capability.

The playable problem is investigative and social: reconstruct why the choice made sense then, decide whether any follow-up is required now, and prevent current state from rewriting institutional memory.
