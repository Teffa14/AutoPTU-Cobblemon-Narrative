# The Expedition That Was Chosen but Never Left — Pass 376

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-09
Canon effect: NONE

## Premise

A field team receives several legitimate demands during the same planning window. A scheduled survey, a personal need, a routine equipment obligation and a new request all compete. The planner selects a remote expedition route and the decision is durably recorded.

The world is saved before the team actually departs.

After recovery, another condition prevents departure before any travel action starts. Possible causes include access closure, unavailable equipment, a new emergency, a delayed teammate or a later institutional order. None is established canon by this proposal.

The important world fact is narrow: the expedition was selected. The world must not infer that the team left, reached the site, encountered Pokémon or completed the work.

## Narrative use

An administrator can truthfully say that the expedition was approved as the selected plan. A teammate can truthfully say that nobody ever left camp. A later investigator can inspect the decision provenance and discover that both statements are compatible.

This supports mysteries based on missing transitions rather than false testimony. The player asks what happened between decision and execution.

## Reduced implementation

The reduced form needs only global world-agent systems:

- semantic time;
- persistent NPC identity;
- communications and private knowledge when the triggering information came from another actor;
- replan trigger provenance;
- plan-selection provenance;
- coherent V16 recovery;
- later world-level state change that can block or supersede execution.

No AutoPTU battle is required.

## Full field version

The selected expedition can point toward a remote research site, pass, ridge, wetland, ruin approach or survey corridor after canon geography validates the location.

If the player later escorts or intercepts the team, encounter goals may include reaching a safe waypoint, preserving samples, recovering equipment, reopening a route, protecting another actor or withdrawing before conditions worsen.

Battle pressure is optional and must remain subordinate to the field objective.
