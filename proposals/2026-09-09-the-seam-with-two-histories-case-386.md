# The Seam With Two Histories — case candidate 386

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-09
Canon effect: NONE

## Premise

A documented wall seam at a persistent field site supports a cautious first interpretation: the visible lower masonry may represent an earlier construction phase. The researcher records that inference with moderate confidence and leaves the site.

During a separate regional assignment, the same researcher later receives another properly sourced observation about a matching structural relationship elsewhere. On returning to the original evidence record, the researcher authors a stronger interpretation that supersedes the first.

The physical observations did not change. The researcher's understanding did.

## Why it matters for Ouros

This creates investigation progress from changed knowledge rather than from retroactive world edits. Another NPC who knows only the original observations can still hold the earlier explanation. A third actor who never received one of the supporting observations cannot legitimately reproduce the later inference from hidden world state.

No participant needs to lie. Expertise does not make either interpretation canon truth.

## Reduced implementation

The reduced form needs no AutoPTU encounter.

Use a persistent site revision and observations from `OUROS_SITE_EVIDENCE_LEDGER_V1`. Materialize the relevant observations into the researcher's private knowledge through direct observation or an existing communication/report path. Record the first site interpretation, materialize it as `INFERENCE`, later add newly learned evidence, author a superseding interpretation, and materialize the revised inference while preserving the earlier claim.

Travel, archives and NPC conversations can reveal the additional evidence. The story loop remains playable even if no tactical scene occurs.

## Rich implementation

A richer version can place the later survey or return visit under pressure: unstable access, a need to escort another specialist, limited time to document an exposed feature, recovery of field equipment, protection of a fragile evidence marker, or withdrawal after enough documentation has been secured.

The narrative objective remains evidence acquisition and safe fieldwork. Defeating every opponent is not required by the premise.
