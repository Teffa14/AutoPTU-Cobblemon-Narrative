## Long arc A — Five Interviews About Meridian Fire

Year 0: responders, residents and workers give separate debriefs after a major fire. Their accounts overlap but conflict on sequence.

Year 1: photographs and dispatch records clarify timestamps. Some interview claims become better supported; others remain unresolved.

Year 3: a retired maintenance worker provides context about an old access problem. This changes interpretation of response delay without proving negligence.

Year 5: a memorial project records community recollections. Public memory now includes details that were absent from the first official summary.

Year 10: the archive releases a previously restricted interview. It adds motive/context to one decision but does not reveal a hidden mastermind.

Design value: one event can accumulate meaning without retconning the original state.

## Long arc B — The Road Remembered by Three Generations

Generation 1 remembers the road before rail.

Generation 2 remembers the construction years and detours.

Generation 3 knows the road mainly as a heritage route.

Maps, photographs, interviews and infrastructure records disagree in ordinary ways because the physical corridor changed. The player can reconstruct several valid historical versions without needing a single omniscient narrator.

## Long arc C — The Legend That Stayed Useful

A community maintains an oral tradition about a recurring Pokémon movement near a mountain pass.

Early researchers dismiss the story because its supernatural explanation is unsupported.

Years later, migration records show that the tradition preserved a real timing/location pattern even though its causal explanation remains unverified.

The tradition becomes scientifically useful without the system declaring the supernatural claim true or false.

## Encounter contract 3 — Archive Interview Handoff

Non-combat by default.

A narrator permits a recording to enter a restricted archive while withholding public release. Research Ethics, Archives, Languages, Identity and the oral-history layer complete the handoff.

No Charm, Guile, Command or battle result can override the authored permission state.
