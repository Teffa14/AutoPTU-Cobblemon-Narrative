# Marea Market Offer & Transaction Seeds — Pass 199

Status: NON-CANON PROPOSALS.
Date: 2026-09-02

All concepts below are original Ouros candidates built on current Marea canon and pass-199 transactional research. Existing NPC/location facts are canon-approved where stated in `canon/`; events, transaction histories, suppliers, quantities, prices, external actors and outcomes introduced here remain proposals until reviewed.

No concept establishes a Caelo currency, tax rule, bargaining system, ownership law, dynamic price model or canonical cause for Thin Delivery Season.

## 1. Ivo's Quote, Tomorrow's Delivery — recommended first slice

CANON anchors:
- Ivo Serrat is purchasing lead at Bruma Market Hall communal kitchen.
- Loma Clara producers supply Puerto Bruma.
- Thin Delivery Season currently produces smaller and less predictable deliveries with no established cause.

PROPOSED situation:
Ivo has a dated quote for a defined ingredient lot expected on a later delivery. Before the lot arrives, a revision changes the available quantity or proposes a substitute.

The player may:
- inspect the original and revised records;
- ask which version Ivo currently considers actionable;
- see that the substitute solves one kitchen need but does not preserve every local dish identically;
- return later to see what actually arrived;
- compare the received lot against the quote history.

Durable outputs:
- original quote remains in history;
- revised quote points back to it;
- actual lot gets its own provenance;
- Ivo's purchasing decision is separately recorded;
- no causal conclusion about Thin Delivery Season is created.

Battle dependency: none.

Why first:
This uses only canon-approved people/places, exercises the new schemas and immediately enriches the opening arc without needing new mechanics.

## 2. Reserved, Not Collected

A small market lot is legitimately held for a named buyer or institution, but pickup has not happened by the expected time.

Useful distinctions:
- stock exists;
- the public stall may look empty because the lot is held;
- reservation does not mean the buyer currently possesses it;
- delay does not prove abandonment;
- expiry requires the actual reservation rule or authored agreement.

The scene can connect to visitor continuity, duty handoffs or correspondence without inventing consumer law.

## 3. The Old Board Is Still Hanging

A physical board in Bruma Market Hall still displays yesterday's availability or quote after a newer version reached the vendor by another channel.

Two residents can therefore cite different figures in good faith.

The player can help establish:
- which board version they saw;
- when the correction arrived;
- whether the old display was ever removed;
- which version governed an actual completed exchange.

The correction never erases the old observation.

## 4. Two Crates, One Description

Two incoming crates are described with the same ordinary commodity label, but provenance shows different producers, packing dates or grades.

A buyer expected one specific lot while the market received the other first.

Possible outcomes:
- accept the alternate lot;
- wait for the intended one;
- split the requirement;
- route one crate to another legitimate need.

No mechanical item quality difference is invented unless authoritative data supports it.

## 5. Substitution on the Kitchen List

A routine ingredient is unavailable in the expected quantity. A supplier proposes another ingredient or batch.

Ivo can distinguish several questions:
- Can the kitchen use it safely under governing rules?
- Does it serve the same operational purpose?
- Does it preserve the same dish/tradition?
- Was the substitution explicitly accepted?

The story keeps culinary identity and transaction fulfillment separate.

No food buff or recipe rule is created.

## 6. One Item, Two Legitimate Needs

A limited incoming component could satisfy either a repair need associated with Teo or a different institutional need already recorded elsewhere.

The tension comes from actual timing and dependencies, not a hidden morality meter.

The player may gather information or help with an alternate dependency, but no universal priority rule is assumed. The actor with actual authority must make or record the allocation decision.

## 7. Price Changed Before Purchase

A customer remembers a previous quote correctly, but the current offer points to a later version.

The narrative question concerns provenance:
- Was the earlier quote time-limited?
- Did quantity change?
- Is this the same lot?
- Did a message fail to reach the customer?
- Is the current board outdated instead?

The system preserves both observations. It does not automatically accuse either side of dishonesty.

## 8. Visitor With Different Terms

A temporary trader or visitor makes an offer that differs substantially from a regular Bruma Market Hall offer.

The difference becomes evidence about that actor, lot and moment. It does not become a regional price index.

Later, the same visitor may return with different goods or terms while preserving social continuity from the first visit.

External origin, currency compatibility and legal authority remain unresolved until separately authored.

## 9. Found Property Beside the Stall

An item is found after a busy market period near a vendor's area.

Its position does not establish that it was stock, sold, abandoned or owned by the nearest vendor.

The existing custody/provenance systems handle the object while transaction records are checked for a plausible match.

This seed deliberately prevents Minecraft pickup behavior from creating ownership truth.

## 10. The Part Teo Was Waiting For

A repair work order from the service-capacity layer is blocked by one component. A legitimate market transaction later obtains the part.

Continuity chain:
`repair request -> blocked dependency -> quote -> approved purchase -> received lot -> provenance -> work order resumes`

The purchase does not itself complete the repair. The repair remains pass-198/workshop state.

## 11. The Lot That Arrived After Lunch

Ivo commits to a plan based on a delivery expected before the communal kitchen's main preparation period. The lot arrives later but still legitimately.

Consequences can be modest:
- one menu plan already changed;
- the late ingredient can be stored for another use;
- a supplier record gains a delay observation;
- tomorrow's planning changes.

The world avoids treating every delay as a crisis.

## 12. Same Supplier, Smaller Lots

Several dated purchase records from the same supplier show smaller lots across a limited period.

This can strengthen one Thin Delivery Season hypothesis while leaving multiple explanations open:
- production changed;
- the supplier divided stock differently;
- other buyers changed purchasing behavior;
- route capacity changed;
- the compared records are not actually equivalent.

Tideglass or Mirador may help establish context, but no institution receives automatic causal authority.

## 13. The Purchase That Happened Off-Screen

The player leaves Puerto Bruma while Ivo has a routine, already-authorized purchase to make.

On return, the player sees consequences rather than a frozen NPC:
- a received batch exists;
- the quote/agreement record is stored;
- kitchen stock changed through the authoritative path;
- a substitution or exception, if any, is separately documented.

This seed demonstrates autonomous world continuity without simulating every shopping trip.

## 14. Long arc — The Ledger of Small Shortages

Across multiple periods, Bruma accumulates a modest record of quotes, substitutions, late lots, normal deliveries, alternate suppliers and occasional surplus.

The arc should feel investigative rather than macroeconomic. Different records can support different explanations at different times.

Potential callbacks:
- an old quote becomes useful when comparing two seasons;
- a substitute introduced during a shortage becomes a normal optional ingredient later;
- a temporary trader becomes a repeat supplier;
- a supplier once considered unreliable later proves consistent;
- an apparent price change turns out to involve a different quantity/grade;
- a delivery pattern correlates with route observations without proving causality.

The arc can eventually contribute to resolving Thin Delivery Season only when enough independently sourced evidence exists.

No global scarcity meter or inflation simulation is required.

## 15. Mechanically rich encounter — Shortfall Delivery at Glass Bend

### Narrative premise

A shipment tied to a recorded Bruma Market transaction is moving through Sendero del Vidrio. A localized wild confrontation threatens immediate passage.

The transaction already exists before combat:
- lot identity;
- buyer/seller or institution refs;
- custody state;
- expected destination;
- agreed terms or approved procurement context.

The battle cannot rewrite those facts.

### Intended full version

Possible tactical features:
- protect a withdrawal corridor;
- keep a specific carrier or escort from being displaced into danger;
- changing lines of sight around cargo/terrain;
- Interception or forced movement where selected PTU content actually supports it;
- terrain or weather if the current world state and engine can represent them;
- wild actors whose policy values escape/territorial goals rather than mandatory KO behavior.

Permanent capability dependencies:
- targeting/footprints/range/LoS;
- base movement legality;
- complete movement including push/pull/knockback/interception/forced movement;
- core calculations;
- action economy/initiative;
- full turn/round lifecycle;
- full stateful damage pipeline;
- status lifecycle when selected content uses statuses;
- terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions if represented tactically;
- move-specific behavior;
- abilities;
- items;
- Trainer Features/perks;
- AI legal-action infrastructure;
- AI tactical policy;
- Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback support.

Current readiness: BLOCKED as a complete implementation because several required families remain PARTIAL or BLOCKING.

### Reduced executable version

Keep shipment, cargo, transaction terms, custody, noncombatants and delivery schedule entirely in Narrative world state.

Before BattleSpec creation:
- place cargo in a safe semantic state outside the tactical engine;
- withdraw noncombatants;
- identify one immediate wild actor/group that still prevents passage;
- use stable audited battle geometry;
- omit tactical weather/hazards/objective reactions that are not verified.

Allowed battle handoffs:
- `IMMEDIATE_ROUTE_THREAT_WITHDREW`
- `IMMEDIATE_PASSAGE_CLEAR`
- `IMMEDIATE_DELIVERY_TEAM_CAN_WITHDRAW`

After battle, Narrative decides whether the shipment continues, waits, returns or changes custodian based on actual world state.

The result cannot determine:
- price or value;
- payment state;
- ownership;
- whether the lot satisfies the agreement;
- cause of shortage;
- future stock;
- vendor relationship;
- liability;
- Thin Delivery Season truth.

## 16. Minecraft/Cobblemon presentation candidates

Safe early visuals:
- dated market board variants;
- labeled crates tied to lot ids;
- a reserved tag on a displayed space;
- a received-lot area behind Ivo's kitchen workflow;
- old/new paper notices coexisting until an NPC updates the board;
- empty display geometry driven by authoritative stock state.

Presentation guards:

- client-side duplication cannot duplicate a lot;
- destroying a sign cannot cancel the offer record;
- chunk unload does not cancel a reservation or shipment;
- a vendor entity disappearing because of render distance does not close the market.
