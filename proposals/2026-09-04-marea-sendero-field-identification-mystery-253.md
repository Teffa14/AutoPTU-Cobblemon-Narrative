# Marea Sendero field-identification mystery — Pass 253

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Canon effect: NONE until approved

## Premise

The recurring-Fletchling idea from Pass 252 can become a small investigation loop about familiarity rather than ownership. A player sees a Fletchling more than once around lower Sendero and gradually asks a simple question: is this actually the same wild individual?

Ouros knows the hidden answer for the established persistent actor. The character does not.

## Encounter structure

An initial sighting establishes ordinary field evidence: broad place, activity window, perch use and witnessed retreat direction. A later independent sighting can make the notebook suggest that the observations may belong to the same individual.

A repeated report from an NPC who merely heard about the second sighting does not strengthen the hypothesis because it shares the same provenance root.

A later same-species sighting can deliberately complicate the pattern. In the fixture, a visually plausible Fletchling retreats a different way. The notebook preserves ambiguity instead of merging it into the familiar bird. This confounder is fixture-only and does not create a second named or persistent canon actor.

Further independent compatible evidence can raise the original hypothesis to `probably the same individual`. The language remains epistemic. No internal Ouros ID is exposed.

## Narrative value

This makes local wildlife capable of accumulating history before capture or combat. Familiarity emerges from player attention and longitudinal evidence rather than from a quest marker.

The same structure can support future naturalist work, behavioural research, rival observer disagreements, false leads, conservation decisions and mysteries where knowing whether two sightings concern one individual or several changes the interpretation of the world event.

It also permits the game to be wrong at the character-knowledge layer while the simulation remains internally coherent. A player can form a plausible hypothesis that later evidence weakens.

## Reduced implementation

Run entirely in overworld presentation and observer-knowledge state. Present the established Fletchling on multiple occasions, record independent evidence roots, allow an ambiguous same-species observation, and update the notebook hypothesis. No tactical handoff occurs.

Required capability family: Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback support for persistent presentation and observation capture. The epistemic reducer itself remains Ouros-side.

## Rich implementation

A player can follow the suspected individual to obtain stronger contextual evidence. A mechanically meaningful pursuit adds base movement legality, complete movement, full turn/round lifecycle, AI legal-action infrastructure, AI tactical policy and Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback support.

Targeting/footprints/range/LoS becomes relevant when detection or tactical sight lines are mechanically adjudicated. Terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions enters only when route conditions have mechanical effects. Damage, status, Moves, Abilities, Items and Trainer Features/perks enter only if actually invoked.

## Canon safeguards

No band, scar, special plumage, nickname or fixed personality is introduced.

The exact fixture behaviours are examples, not Fletchling canon.

No new persistent population member is created for the confounder.

The lower-Sendero Fletchling population remains unchanged.

## Future extensions

A later research system could let trained observers distinguish evidence quality more effectively. Any Trainer Feature/perk bonus requires PTU-source verification and engine support before implementation.

A canon-approved field-marking program could create stronger identity evidence, but its ethics, ownership, persistence and mechanics require separate worldbuilding approval.
