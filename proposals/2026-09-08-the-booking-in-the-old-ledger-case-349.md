# The Booking in the Old Ledger — Pass 349

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-08

## Canon boundary

This proposal reuses only established Marea facts and the already-proposed equipment thread as a regression surface. It does not promote the conflict, decision, equipment identity or event outcome to canon.

Established facts reused:
- Teo Lark handles ordinary equipment/repair activity around Puerto Bruma;
- Dr. Nerea Sol works from Estación Mirador;
- Ema Rey participates in equipment checks and field work under Nerea's project protocols;
- Puerto Bruma, Sendero del Vidrio and Estación Mirador are connected parts of the established Marea network.

Any allocator, exact policy, field-meter identity, booking window and downstream consequence remain proposed.

## Premise

Two legitimate jobs competed for the same field instrument. Pass 347 detected the overlap. A properly scoped authority holder used Pass 348 to select the proposed field-work window over an older reservation.

Pass 349 then applies the decision operationally.

The older reservation still exists in history.

The derived current schedule no longer lets it block the instrument.

The actor who held the older booking did not personally cancel it.

## Opening situation

The player sees two records that appear contradictory only if history and current operational state are treated as the same thing:

- the original reservation record says the instrument was booked;
- the current allocation view says that reservation was displaced by a later authorized decision.

A worker who only saw the original booking may still believe it is usable. A supervisor who saw the allocation application may believe the current window is clear. Both can be reporting evidence they actually possess.

## Investigation loop

The player can reconstruct:
- when the original reservation was created;
- which resource ID it named;
- what later scheduling conflict was detected;
- who held authority to choose between the competing claims;
- which Pass 348 resolution was recorded;
- when the resolution was applied;
- which reservation IDs were displaced;
- whether and when each affected actor was notified;
- whether another technically ready resource existed;
- whether the displaced job still needed completion afterward.

The useful reveal is procedural rather than conspiratorial. The booking record is genuine. The displacement record is also genuine.

## Branches

### Branch A — notice arrived

The displaced reservation holder receives the decision before travel. They replan, request another slot, use an authorized substitute or postpone the dependent task.

No wasted trip is required.

### Branch B — notice did not arrive

The holder acts from the older valid evidence and reaches the equipment point expecting the original booking to remain operative.

The resulting delay can use the existing communications, belief and replanning stack. Pass 349 itself does not blame the actor.

### Branch C — decision becomes stale before application

One conflicting reservation is independently released before the allocation application executes.

Pass 349 fails closed rather than applying the old conflict set as if nothing changed. The world must reassess the situation.

### Branch D — repeated pattern

Several displacement records accumulate around the same equipment family over time.

Those records can become evidence for the existing Procurement owner, maintenance-capacity review or a local scheduling-policy discussion. Frequency does not automatically approve a purchase or establish misconduct.

The player solves the problem by comparing persistent records and current state, not by winning a battle.

## Mechanically rich version

After the winning allocation is applied and the resource is physically collected, a field team can face a route interruption while trying to complete the time-sensitive work.

Narrative objective:
preserve the instrument and reach the observation site inside the useful window.

Reduced tactical fallback:
represent the interruption as an ordinary travel delay or use only an already-audited basic battle that does not require escort/cargo policy. The allocation premise remains unchanged.

This case supports a low-drama institutional arc where the same world can remember:
- who booked scarce equipment;
- who was displaced;
- who made each decision;
- whether notice reached the affected people;
