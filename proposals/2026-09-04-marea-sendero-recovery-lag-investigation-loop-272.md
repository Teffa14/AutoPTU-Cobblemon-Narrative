# Marea/Sendero recovery-lag investigation loop — Pass 272

Status: PROPOSED NARRATIVE / ENCOUNTER CONCEPT. No canon promotion.

## Premise

A localized ecological pressure has been removed from a known Sendero micro-site. The obvious problem is gone, but familiar Fletchling activity does not immediately return to the previously documented pattern. The investigation asks what “recovered” actually means rather than treating source removal as quest completion.

## Field loop

The player first revisits with a pre-disturbance reference already documented through the Pass 271 comparison protocol. Site use and activity timing are tracked separately. An early revisit can show both still shifted. A later visit can show the perch-use pattern back inside its reference band while timing remains altered.

That split creates the central mystery. The remaining change may represent ordinary recovery lag, an overlooked second pressure, a persistent state shift, or an invalid reference. The story advances by improving evidence rather than by selecting a dialogue answer.

A proposed field observer archetype emphasizes continuity of behavior and cautions against declaring recovery from one familiar sighting. A proposed maintenance/operations archetype can accurately report that the physical pressure was removed while remaining agnostic about ecological recovery. Their disagreement comes from different scopes of evidence, not incompetence or deception.

## Consequences

Prematurely declaring the site recovered can end monitoring before the remaining shift is explained. Keeping observation active can reveal a second fixture-only pressure and produce a new cessation-and-follow-up cycle. If all tested dimensions later return to their reference bands, the record can close as a recovered trajectory without asserting that population size ever changed or that every ecosystem property has been restored.

A future branch may support a persistent-shift or hysteresis investigation, but only after the stronger evidence gate in the Pass 272 contract is satisfied. The narrative should never use the term hysteresis as a dramatic synonym for “it took a while.”

## Environmental storytelling

Minecraft presentation can make the same micro-site feel different across visits through already-authorized projection and non-authoritative visual context. The world can show changed timing, occupancy, or environmental traces, but visuals remain observations. Entity absence does not prove ecological absence; a normal-looking animation does not prove recovery; apparent injury does not create PTU Injury/status.
