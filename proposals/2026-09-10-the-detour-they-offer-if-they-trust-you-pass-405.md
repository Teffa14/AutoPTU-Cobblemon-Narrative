# The Detour They Offer If They Trust You — Pass 405

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Canon effect: NONE

## Premise

A route, work site or controlled crossing is temporarily closed for a legitimate local reason. The restriction is real for everyone. The interesting variation is what the responsible NPC is willing to do next.

A party with no relevant history receives the ordinary closure and must wait, leave or find another lawful plan. A party whose prior reliable conduct is known to the deciding NPC may be told about a provisional detour, invited to help verify a route, granted supervised access to a narrow area or asked to carry out a limited task that reduces the problem. A party associated with contradictory or worrying evidence may face extra verification instead.

The deciding actor never reads a global reputation number. Their response must be derived from information they actually possess: direct memory, relationship history, institutional duty, an explicit record, a message they received or another provenance-backed source.

## Why it belongs in Ouros

This makes old actions matter without converting the world into a morality scoreboard. Earlier work changes later options through people and institutions. The later physical problem still exists, so prior success does not erase content. It changes how the player can approach it.

The same structure works off-screen. A persistent NPC team with a documented history may receive a different response from a gatekeeper than another team. If the gatekeeper never learned the relevant history, no special option appears.

## Reduced implementation

The complete narrative premise can run without AutoPTU.

The world layer needs the closure state, responsible actor, access policy, relationship/evidence state, explicit knowledge available to that actor, communication, travel and obligations. The detour can be represented as an ordinary alternate route whose availability becomes known only after the gatekeeper communicates it.

Possible outcomes include waiting for reopening, returning later, accepting a supervised inspection, taking the disclosed detour, relaying evidence to the responsible institution or leaving without resolving the closure.

No combat is required. No hidden Skill bonus or invented Trainer Feature is required.

## Full encounter version

The provisional route can lead through a location where the main objective remains traversal, verification or assistance rather than defeating every opponent.

Examples of optional tactical pressure include protecting the person checking the route, reaching marked positions, carrying evidence back out, helping an injured traveler, withdrawing once a safe path is established or keeping a conflict away from fragile infrastructure.

The world premise survives if those mechanics are unavailable. The reduced version simply resolves the route check through persistent-world investigation and travel state.
