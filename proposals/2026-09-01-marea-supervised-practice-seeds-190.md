# Marea Supervised-Practice Seeds — Pass 190

Status: PROPOSED / NOT CANON
Date: 2026-09-01

These candidates use existing Marea people and institutions as anchors. They do not promote anyone, create credentials, or change established authority. Exact role authority must be checked before promotion to canon.

## 1. One Routine Opening Without Sela

Anchor: Jace / Bruma Battle Yard / Sela.

Jace is given a narrow, ordinary opening task that he has already assisted with before. Sela remains available but does not perform each step for him. The meaningful outcome is a dated observation of what Jace handled, what he checked, and whether any exception appeared.

No battle is required. Completion does not promote Jace or make him equivalent to Sela.

## 2. The Exception He Must Escalate

Anchor: Jace / Battle Yard.

A routine setup develops an unusual condition outside the scope being practiced. The strongest performance is to stop, preserve the current state, and call the authorized senior rather than improvise past the limit.

This establishes escalation as competence instead of treating every quest as a test of heroic self-sufficiency.

## 3. Procedure Version Changed

Anchor: Battle Yard or another established Marea worksite.

An NPC has prior successful observations under an older procedure. A new revision changes one material step. Earlier evidence remains in the record, while the changed step requires a new supervised demonstration.

World persistence: both old and new reference sheets may physically coexist until the obsolete copy is removed.

## 4. Pia Drafts, Taro Reviews

Anchor: Tideglass / Pia / Taro.

Pia prepares a source-grounded draft or catalog entry using an established workflow. Taro retains whatever review authority canon already assigns him. The player can help locate provenance or identify an overclaim, but cannot convert their own preference into institutional approval.

The quest can end successfully with a revision request.

## 5. Provenance Handoff

Anchor: Tideglass / Pia.

Pia practices receiving a routine historical packet, recording custody and linking it to an existing provenance record before substantive interpretation occurs. A mismatch appears in one identifier.

Correct behavior may be to preserve the mismatch and escalate it rather than choose the most convenient identity.

## 6. Ema Leads the Familiar Segment

Anchor: Mirador / Ema / Nerea.

Ema leads a familiar observation segment while Nerea remains available. The task tests ordinary preparation, record discipline and knowing when an observation exceeds the current procedure.

No invented Researcher roll or generic science bonus is allowed. Any PTU mechanic must be sourced separately.

## 7. The Field Note Correction

Anchor: Mirador.

A trainee or junior contributor notices that yesterday's field note contains a transcription error. Correcting it through the established revision trail becomes positive evidence of professional behavior.

The previous note remains historically visible rather than being silently rewritten.

## 8. Player Could Do It Faster

Anchor: any established Marea institution.

The player can personally finish a routine task quickly, but an NPC currently needs supervised practice in that exact narrow scope. Supporting the attempt produces no special mechanical reward; the payoff is local capability that later exists when the player is elsewhere.

The world should still function if the player chooses to take over. The consequence is lost practice opportunity, not punishment.

## 9. Drill Success, Live Scope Pending

Anchor: preparedness layer.

An NPC performs a task correctly during an existing drill. The observation is recorded with DRILL context. It can support a later review but cannot silently establish unrestricted live independence.

This prevents preparedness exercises from becoming magical certification machines.

## 10. Routine Intake, Disputed Container

Anchor: Brin / provisioning or salvage continuity.

A worker or assistant handles ordinary intake under a defined scope. One container has conflicting provenance or condition evidence. The routine part can be completed; the disputed object is separated and escalated.

The quest proves that partial completion and proper exception handling can coexist.

## 11. Handoff Before Absence

Anchor: any pair whose authority already exists in canon.

A senior resident expects to be unavailable for a short period and conducts a procedure handoff. The handoff records current version, routine steps, open exceptions and explicit boundaries.

The scene creates future gameplay: an ordinary case may proceed without the senior, while a boundary case remains pending rather than forcing the absent NPC to teleport back into the story.

## 12. Another Resident Signs the Observation

Anchor: institution with multiple established workers.

The player witnesses competent work, but only an authorized resident can issue the relevant scope decision. The player's account becomes one evidence source. This preserves NPC institutional agency and prevents protagonist authority creep.

## Long arc — Hands That Learn the Work

Across multiple seasons, small changes become visible:
- an NPC performs one more routine step without prompting;
- an old checklist gains annotations from prior mistakes;
- a senior delegates a narrow task while remaining available;
- a previously difficult exception is recognized earlier;
- local work continues while the player is away;
- procedures survive staff absence because knowledge was handed off rather than stored in one person.

The arc does not require promotions, titles, universal skill meters or a graduation ceremony. If canon later approves a role change, the accumulated evidence can make that change feel earned without forcing it.
