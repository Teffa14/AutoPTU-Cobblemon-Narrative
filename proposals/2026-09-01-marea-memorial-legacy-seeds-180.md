# Marea memorial and legacy stewardship seeds — pass 180

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-01
Depends on: `design/mourning-memorial-legacy-stewardship-layer.md`

These candidates are designed to connect existing Marea residents, sites and records without inventing a current death, cemetery, religion or afterlife doctrine. Any reference to older losses remains deliberately hypothetical until canon review.

## 1. The Name That Appears Three Ways

Surface: Tideglass Archive / Puerto Bruma
Primary residents: Taro Min, Pia Min, Mara Veyra

Premise:

Three legitimate records from different periods appear to refer to the same former route worker or field partner, but the name is spelled or abbreviated differently in each source. A proposed public label for an old survey object cannot be approved until the archive resolves whether the records refer to one subject or several.

Gameplay:

- inspect three physical documents;
- compare dates, workplaces, handwriting/transcription and route references;
- preserve uncertainty when evidence remains incomplete;
- prepare a source note for Taro rather than selecting whichever spelling looks most plausible.

World consequence:

A later public label can include a versioned note or remain deliberately non-specific.

No combat dependency.

Useful systems:

- archives/provenance;
- language/transcription layer;
- local knowledge;
- public memory;
- memorial text versioning.

## 2. Retired, Not Lost

Surface: Bruma Battle Yard
Primary residents: Sela Orrin, Jace Orrin, Teo Lark

Premise:

An old piece of Battle Yard equipment is being removed from ordinary use. Jace assumes it should be thrown away; Sela wants its service history checked first; Teo needs to decide whether components remain useful or safe.

Purpose:

Teach that legacy content can begin with retirement rather than death.

Possible outcomes:

- retained in use after repair;
- archived/displayed with provenance;
- dismantled after records are captured;
- repurposed for instruction;
- returned to a prior owner if records require it.

No combat dependency.

## 3. The Empty Line in the Ferry Ledger

Surface: ferry landing / Tideglass
Primary residents: Lia Morn, Mina Cors, Pia Min

Premise:

A historical ferry ledger includes one service date with an unexplained blank where a crew or cargo notation should be. Later copies fill the blank differently. The task is to reconstruct what can actually be supported, not invent a dramatic disappearance.

Potential sources:

- arrival/departure books;
- market receipt dates;
- Tideglass copies;
- weather observation records if they already exist;
- oral recollections only when an authored living witness exists.

Outcome:

The archive may conclude `UNRESOLVED`. That is a valid result.

No combat dependency.

## 4. A Marker Needs a Custodian

Surface: Sendero del Vidrio
Primary residents: Mara Veyra, Teo Lark, Taro Min

Premise:

An old physical marker beside the route has become unstable. It is historically meaningful enough that Mara will not authorize removal until Tideglass verifies what it is and Teo assesses whether it can be stabilized safely.

Possible consequences:

- marker repaired in place;
- photographed/transcribed and moved;
- replaced by a replica while original enters archive custody;
- left untouched behind a small access restriction;
- determined to be ordinary infrastructure rather than a memorial object.

This candidate exercises site aftermath plus custody without requiring any current death.

Reduced implementation: inspection and repair only.

Full mechanical version, only if route danger is tacticalized, inherits the same dependencies as `Last Light on the Upper Path` and remains blocked by incomplete movement/hazard/tactical-playback families.

## 5. The Companion Care Plan Nobody Wants to Need

Surface: clinic / Marea Field Office
Primary residents: Oren Vale, Mara Veyra, Sela Orrin

Premise:

Mara asks institutions to review contingency records for what happens to a resident's companion Pokémon during hospitalization, disappearance or permanent incapacity. The exercise is administrative preparedness, not a prediction that anyone will die.

Key questions:

- who may provide immediate care;
- where the Pokémon stays temporarily;
- who may make routine non-battle decisions;
- what records are private;
- what cannot be transferred without separate ownership/capture authority;
- what happens if the companion refuses a proposed arrangement, if Ouros canon supports meaningful consent in that context.

Important boundary:

No current resident is removed or harmed. No legal ownership model is canonized by this proposal.

No battle dependency.

## 6. First Partner Record Day

Surface: Tideglass Archive / Battle Yard
Primary residents: Taro Min, Sela Orrin, Pia Min

Premise:

Tideglass pilots a voluntary oral-history/archive day where Trainers can deposit a short record about an early partner Pokémon, retired team member or formative training period. Subjects may be alive, active, retired, missing or deceased; the archive distinguishes those statuses.

Design value:

This provides remembrance content without making every story about bereavement.

Player-facing choices:

- public archive deposit;
- restricted archive deposit;
- private note not accepted into institutional custody;
- no contribution.

No combat dependency.

## 7. The Correction Beside the Plaque

Surface: Loma Clara field school or Tideglass
Primary residents: Jo Venn, Taro Min, Pia Min

Premise:

A public teaching plaque uses an old summary that later records partially contradict. Removing the plaque would erase evidence of how the community previously understood the event; leaving it unchanged would preserve known error.

Possible solution:

Install a small dated correction beside the old text and link both to the underlying archive record.

Purpose:

Exercise public-memory revision without retconning world history.

No combat dependency.

## 8. Quiet Cargo

Surface: ferry landing -> Tideglass
Primary residents: Lia Morn, Mina Cors, Pia Min

Premise:

A sealed archival package containing legacy objects arrives by ferry. The player may help move it, but custody requires explicit handoffs at departure, landing and archive intake.

Failure modes are procedural rather than melodramatic:

- seal mismatch;
- wrong intake window;
- missing receiving signature;
- package temporarily held at the dock;
- object list and container count disagree.

The player cannot open the package merely because they carried it.

No combat dependency in the recommended version.

## 9. The Route Walk Before Dawn

Surface: Sendero del Vidrio / upper route marker
Primary residents: Mara Veyra, Taro Min, Nerea Sol

Premise:

A small group plans a permitted walk to an older survey marker before regular route traffic begins. The purpose is to review the record of a past field season and compare historical observations with the present route.

Nothing in the proposal requires the past field team to be dead. The same structure can later support memorial use if canon establishes it.

Recommended implementation:

- schedule the walk;
- inspect one historical record before departure;
- visit two fixed observation points;
- compare current conditions without claiming causation;
- return a field note to Tideglass.

No combat required.

Mechanically rich variant:

If a wild encounter interrupts access, use `Last Light on the Upper Path` dependency rules. The remembrance/history objective remains outside BattleSpec.

## 10. Legacy Without Ownership

Surface: Bruma Market Hall / repair row / Tideglass
Primary residents: Ivo Serrat, Teo Lark, Pia Min

Premise:

A long-used kitchen or repair tool is associated with a former worker. Residents disagree about whether it belongs in active service, in an archive, with the worker's family/authorized contact, or with the institution that originally purchased it.

The task is to trace provenance and current custody, not decide by sentiment.

Possible evidence:

- purchase record;
- repair marks;
- loan notation;
- personal inscription;
- inventory number;
- testimony;
- later replacement record.

No combat dependency.

## 11. Historical Missing-Person File

Surface: Marea Field Office / Tideglass
Primary residents: Mara Veyra, Taro Min

Premise:

An old unresolved route disappearance exists in records with no verified conclusion. A later community story treats the person as dead, but the official file never reached that status.

Narrative purpose:

Demonstrate `MISSING != DECEASED` and allow a local tradition to coexist with documentary uncertainty.

Possible player action:

- review records;
- map last confirmed observations;
- identify which later claims have no primary source;
- prepare an archive annotation.

Do not resolve the disappearance unless a future authored arc supplies real evidence.

No battle dependency in this seed.

## 12. Last Light on the Upper Path

Status: MECHANICALLY RICH PROPOSAL / NON-CANON
Surface: Sendero del Vidrio
Potential residents: Mara Veyra, Taro Min, Ema Rey

Premise:

A legacy record or permitted remembrance token must be delivered to an older route marker while the district is managing a separate access concern. The emotional/history objective and the route-safety objective remain distinct.

### Intended full encounter

Possible tactical elements:

- bounded escort corridor;
- wild Pokémon acting from ecological motives rather than villainy;
- line-of-sight around route geometry;
- safe withdrawal path;
- displacement or interception if selected Pokémon content uses it;
- unstable/hazard cells only if the engine family becomes verified;
- actor protection without making the legacy object a combatant.

Permanent capability dependencies:

- targeting/footprints/range/LoS — required;
- base movement legality — required;
- complete movement including push/pull/knockback/interception/forced movement — required if displacement/interception is selected; currently PARTIAL;
- core calculations — required;
- action economy/initiative — required;
- full turn/round lifecycle — required for full battle; currently PARTIAL;
- full stateful damage pipeline — required; currently PARTIAL;
- status lifecycle — required only for selected status-producing content; currently PARTIAL;
- terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions — required for tactical route danger/protected corridor; currently BLOCKING as a complete family;
- move-specific behavior — exact selected set must be audited; family PARTIAL;
- abilities — exact selected set must be audited; family PARTIAL;
- items — exact selected set must be audited; family PARTIAL;
- Trainer Features/perks — exact selected set must be audited; family PARTIAL;
- AI legal-action infrastructure — available for covered contracts;
- AI tactical policy — BLOCKING for unscripted tactical objective behavior;
- Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback support — BLOCKING as a complete faithful-battle family.

### Reduced version

- world state decides whether the upper route is open enough to attempt the visit;
- all non-combat actors remain outside BattleSpec;
- the legacy object stays in ordinary custody state;
- route hazards are visual/world restrictions, not tactical cells;
- any battle happens separately on a stable clearing;
- matchup is limited to audited Moves/Abilities/Items/Features;
- no displacement, reaction, hazard, weather-phase or escort-objective rule is required;
- battle may emit only `IMMEDIATE_ROUTE_THREAT_WITHDREW`;
- the visit, memorial/history meaning, custody and route reopening remain server-authored narrative/world facts.

### Why this seed is useful

It proves a mechanically rich story can keep its premise while shipping a simpler version first. The adapter never needs to invent missing PTU rules in order to make the history objective playable.

## Recommended first implementation order

P0 candidate: `The Name That Appears Three Ways`.

Why:

- uses Tideglass, Taro, Pia and Mara already present in canon;
- reinforces provenance and transcription systems;
- requires no new location or combat family;
- can produce physical documents/lecterns through already demonstrated RPG object surfaces;
- establishes memorial/legacy architecture without first canonizing a death.

P1 candidate: `Retired, Not Lost`.

Why:

- connects Sela/Jace/Teo;
- demonstrates legacy without death;
- creates visible environmental change at Battle Yard;
- reuses custody and site-aftermath logic.

P2 candidate: `Quiet Cargo`.

Why:

- connects ferry operations to Tideglass;
- makes chain of custody visible in the overworld;
- supports future archive/memorial content.

## Canon questions before promotion

- Does Marea have any public memorial location at all?
- Are old route/service markers maintained as historical objects?
- What institution can approve a public plaque or correction?
- Does Tideglass accept private deposits, permanent gifts or loans?
- Does Marea have a policy for unresolved disappearances?
- What Caelo material governs permanent death or companion continuity?
- Are religious or spiritual practices relevant to memorials, and if so which are public versus private?
- Are there privacy restrictions on posthumous care/medical information?

Until answered, these remain implementation candidates only.
