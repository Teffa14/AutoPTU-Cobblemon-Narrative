# Ouros Proposals — Fisheries, Aquatic Harvest & Working Waterfront Seeds — Pass 86

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON. Requires continuity, originality, setting and mechanical review before promotion.

These candidates use `design/fisheries-aquatic-harvest-landing-stewardship-extension.md`. Names are working titles, not established Ouros locations, institutions or traditions.

## Design intent

Use fisheries and working waterfronts as persistent social/ecological places rather than infinite encounter nodes.

The strongest stories come from mismatches between:
- what people expected to happen;
- what was actually observed;
- what reached the landing site;
- what downstream systems assumed;
- what ecology or research can genuinely support.

Do not assume wrongdoing, depletion, cruelty or regulation merely because a discrepancy exists.

## The Boat Came Back Light

Premise:
A familiar crew returns with much less than observers expected. Market gossip immediately turns toward ecological decline.

Actual playable problem:
Reconstruct the day's effort before making an ecological claim.

Evidence can include:
- departure/return times;
- route changes;
- weather/service state;
- equipment trouble;
- deliberate release events;
- alternate landing sites;
- crew testimony;
- previous comparable effort windows.

Possible outcomes:
- genuinely unusual contact rate worth scientific follow-up;
- ordinary low effort caused by route interruption;
- several released contacts omitted from market gossip;
- equipment failure shortened the activity;
- evidence remains insufficient.

No hidden scarcity score is created.

## The Catch Everyone Calls the Same

Premise:
A market term that residents have used for years turns out to cover multiple biological/material identities.

Hook:
A research, care or traceability case suddenly needs exact provenance rather than the familiar local label.

Consequences:
- old recipes and oral histories remain valid as cultural records;
- landing records may require a new exact-identity field;
- vendors do not become dishonest merely because the historic term was broad;
- public signage can be updated without erasing earlier language.

The story explores classification and tradition, not a reveal that local people were ignorant.

## The Closed Cove Still Feeds the Market

Premise:
A cove is temporarily restricted by an owning stewardship authority, yet products associated with that area continue appearing at a market.

Investigation possibilities:
- older stored batch;
- another legal source using the same market name;
- mislabeled source record;
- a landing from before the restriction;
- an actual policy breach;
- evidence too incomplete to determine origin.

The system must follow provenance before accusing anyone.

## The Net Is Empty, the Water Isn't

Premise:
Fishers report poor results, while researchers continue recording many aquatic Pokémon in the same broad region.

Conflict:
The two observations appear contradictory because they measure different things.

Potential causes:
- research occurs in different depth/shore zones;
- target practices changed;
- observed individuals are not the targeted population;
- timing differs;
- fishing method/equipment differs;
- behavior changed without population collapse.

Outcome:
Players help define a better observation design rather than choosing which group is “right.”

## Return Before Market

Premise:
A fishing activity produces a wild Pokémon contact that should return to the water before the crew proceeds to normal landing routines.

Possible reasons remain open until canon defines them:
- research protocol;
- stewardship condition;
- welfare decision;
- fisher's own choice;
- unsuitable target for intended activity.

The interesting part is making sure release identity, location and observation data survive the handoff.

No relationship/ownership is inferred from handling the Pokémon.

## The Gear Came Back Without the Catch

Premise:
A rod, lure case or other exact equipment instance is recovered after a failed or interrupted outing, but nobody has a complete account of what happened to the original activity.

Systems intersecting:
- Shared Equipment;
- Found Property;
- Maritime journey history;
- personal testimony;
- Maintenance;
- fishery activity records.

The gear can be recovered without proving a loss, injury or encounter outcome.

## Research Tag in the Contact

Premise:
A wild Pokémon encountered during fishing carries or matches a valid wildlife-monitoring identifier from an earlier study.

Required response:
Connect the exact actor to Wildlife Monitoring and preserve the detection/re-sighting event.

Potential consequences:
- release and observation only;
- care review if condition warrants it;
- discovery of a wider route than expected;
- correction of a previously assumed range;
- no immediate consequence beyond a useful data point.

The monitoring identifier never gives ownership of the Pokémon to the research institution.

## The Old Fishing Spot Became Nursery Ground

Premise:
A traditional spot remains physically usable, but repeated observations suggest it now overlaps a sensitive reproductive/juvenile-use area.

Competing interests can include:
- longstanding local practice;
- recent ecological evidence;
- tourism pressure;
- research uncertainty;
- alternate sites with different costs or access.

Possible resolution:
A seasonal/partial adjustment, new observation window, changed route or no immediate restriction while evidence improves.

Do not invent breeding mechanics, Egg state or a legal closure merely from the narrative label “nursery ground.”

## The Landing Moved, the Tradition Didn't

Premise:
Infrastructure repair or route change relocates normal landings to another part of the settlement.

Persistence consequences:
- vendors may move or split operations;
- an old meeting place loses traffic;
- equipment storage becomes inconvenient;
- a community meal continues at the old site;
- notices and visitor wayfinding lag behind;
- fishers may use two landing points during transition.

This is a settlement story generated from logistics rather than a new villain.

## The Fisher Who Records Releases

Character archetype:
A working fisher keeps unusually careful records of contacts that were returned rather than landed.

Narrative value:
Their notebooks can become useful ecological evidence precisely because commercial records normally emphasize what reached shore.

Boundary:
The character's interpretation can still be wrong. Their observations remain claims with provenance.

## The Market Vendor Who Knows Arrival Patterns

Character archetype:
A vendor has strong local knowledge of which crews normally arrive when, which kinds of batches arrive through which desk and what “late” means for this specific waterfront.

Use:
They can notice an anomaly earlier than a centralized institution.

Boundary:
Routine expertise gives local-pattern knowledge, not omniscient ecological truth or private crew information.

## The Young Crew That Changes One Habit

NPC arc:
A new crew copies the visible routines of established operators but adopts one different practice after an early incident or research collaboration.

Possible persistent changes:
- more complete release logging;
- different equipment handoff;
- changed departure time;
- alternate landing desk;
- voluntary avoidance of one zone;
- new cooperation with a researcher.

No “professional level” stat is required.

## Mystery — Three Boats, Two Landing Records

Observed facts:
Three departure events are independently supported. Only two landing records exist at the expected site.

Investigation graph:
- compare Maritime journey endpoints;
- inspect fishery activity IDs;
- compare landing timestamps;
- inspect equipment return;
- query public notices and route state;
- compare market intake;
- interview actors whose knowledge scope plausibly covers the handoff.

Possible explanations:
- two crews landed together under one batch/handoff entry;
- one vessel diverted to a secondary landing site;
- one activity returned without a formal landing operation;
- record damage or administrative omission;
- unresolved discrepancy.

Fraud or unauthorized activity is only one possible hypothesis and requires evidence.

## Mystery — Seven Crates, Six Sources

Premise:
Seven physically distinct landing/storage units exist, but only six exact source records can currently be reconciled.

Useful systems:
- Batch Traceability;
- Food provenance;
- Courier/Storage;
- Found Property;
- personal records;
- market intake.

Possible outcome:
One crate may be a split batch, transferred packaging, returned empty container, duplicate label or genuinely unresolved provenance.

Do not infer counterfeit or illegal harvest from the mismatch alone.

## Long arc — A Waterfront Learns the Water

Visit 1 — baseline:
The waterfront is established as ordinary working space. Players learn who uses the docks, when landings happen, where gear is stored and which parts are public, commercial or quiet.

Visit 2 — small anomaly:
Several activities produce unusual results. Nobody yet knows whether the cause is environmental, operational or statistical noise.

Visit 3 — better observation:
Fishers, researchers or local stewards compare effort and observation methods. One longstanding assumption turns out to be too broad.

Visit 4 — operational change:
A route, landing routine, equipment practice or voluntary use pattern changes. Minecraft presentation visibly reflects the new normal.

Visit 5 — unintended consequence:
The change reduces one pressure but moves traffic, market demand or wildlife contact elsewhere.

Visit 6 — review:
The community adapts again. The resulting system can remain mixed, imperfect and regionally distinctive.

The arc succeeds when the same location gains history rather than being replaced by a bigger port.

## Encounter — Working-Waterfront Withdrawal

Narrative premise:
Wild Pokémon use or enter the active edge of a landing area while workers are still present.

Full intended version:
- explicit withdrawal/route-clearing objective;
- narrow shore/dock geometry;
- complete Intercept and displacement where exact mechanics call for them;
- tactical water/terrain only if validated;
- territorial/escape-aware AI;
- moving noncombatant presentation;
- AutoPTU semantic playback in Cobblemon.

Capability dependencies:
- targeting/footprints/range/LoS: VERIFIED
- base movement legality: VERIFIED
- complete movement including push/pull/knockback/interception/forced movement: PARTIAL
- core calculations: VERIFIED
- action economy/initiative: VERIFIED
- full turn/round lifecycle: PARTIAL
- full stateful damage pipeline: PARTIAL
- status lifecycle: PARTIAL
- terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions: BLOCKING
- move-specific behavior: PARTIAL
- abilities: PARTIAL
- items: PARTIAL
- Trainer Features/perks: PARTIAL
- AI legal-action infrastructure: VERIFIED
- AI tactical policy: BLOCKING
- Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback support: BLOCKING

Reduced version:
Stop work first. Remove workers, customers, landing batches and equipment from the tactical state. Ouros selects exact combatants. Use a static reviewed shore/dock arena. No current, tide, slick-surface penalty, falling-cargo damage or scripted knockback. AutoPTU resolves the combat and the waterfront system decides reopening afterward.

## Encounter — Cove Release Window

Narrative premise:
A monitored or deliberately released wild individual must be returned to an appropriate location while another wild group currently occupies part of the cove.

Full intended version:
- explicit release/protect/withdraw objective;
- verified exact actor identity;
- water movement and access state;
- full Intercept/reaction handling if relevant;
- objective-aware territorial AI;
- adapter playback separating release target from combatants unless explicitly added.

Capability profile:
Same permanent categories as Working-Waterfront Withdrawal. The largest blockers are tactical objective support, terrain/weather/hazard/reaction family, AI tactical policy and Minecraft playback.

Reduced version:
Keep the release target out of battle. Perform or pause release as world state. If a battle is necessary, select separate explicit combatants in a static nearby arena. The battle result does not itself complete the release.

## Encounter — Fishing Contact Turns Hostile

Narrative premise:
A PTU-authoritative fishing procedure produces a Pokémon contact that genuinely escalates into combat.

Full intended version:
- PTU/Caelo fishing resolution produces the contact;
- Ouros binds that exact actor to an explicit encounter manifest;
- AutoPTU constructs and resolves the battle;
- Cobblemon renders the fished actor, animation and subsequent tactical projection;
- the outcome returns to capture/release/activity world state.

Important dependencies:
This encounter additionally requires a reviewed fishing-to-BattleSpec handoff. Cobblemon's own fished-spawn/battle code cannot substitute for it.

Reduced version:
Until that handoff exists, fishing may remain a noncombat overworld activity or generate a reviewed ordinary encounter only through explicit Ouros authoring. Do not silently let Cobblemon decide the tactical participant because its Poké Rod spawned an entity.
