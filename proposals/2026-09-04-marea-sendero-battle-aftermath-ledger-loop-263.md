# Marea / Sendero battle aftermath ledger loop — Pass 263

Status: PROPOSED. No new NPC, Injury, status, battle, site or second persistent Fletchling is canonized here.

The player can encounter a wild individual whose ecological history already matters, enter a tactical encounter, leave the battle space, and later discover that the world remembers only consequences that AutoPTU actually adjudicated and that Ouros could bind to the same individual.

The full loop is:

1. Ouros selects an eligible persistent ecological subject under the existing population/projection contracts.
2. Ouros creates an opaque battle-session subject binding. Minecraft receives only presentation correlation.
3. AutoPTU runs the tactical encounter as rules authority.
4. Typed semantic results return through the session binding.
5. Ouros admits only result types backed by exact engine capability evidence.
6. Admitted consequences can affect later ecology/presentation; quarantined results remain visible to operators but do not become world truth.
7. A later encounter can show aftermath without relying on the original Minecraft entity UUID or battle instance.

The reduced loop can ship earlier. A recurring wild Fletchling can enter and leave a battle with a valid correlation/completion receipt while HP/Injury/status aftermath remains intentionally absent because those result families are not yet admitted. The narrative premise still works: the player recognizes a recurring individual and the world remembers that an encounter occurred, but Minecraft does not counterfeit missing PTU persistence.

A richer future version can support durable battle scars, recovery behavior or persistent statuses only after the exact Java producing path is admitted. If knockback, interception, weather, delayed reactions, Ability-created terrain, Trainer Feature interrupts or other specialized mechanics participate, their corresponding capability families become explicit dependencies for that result.

A useful mystery hook follows from the same architecture. An observer may report that a familiar wild Pokémon appears injured after a disturbance. The observation can enter the knowledge ledger immediately. The ecological Injury itself remains unconfirmed unless an authoritative battle result or another canon-approved non-battle authority establishes it. This permits disagreement, investigation and environmental storytelling without collapsing observation into rules truth.

Longer-term, the same ledger can support rescue/rehabilitation arcs, recurring rivals, territorial aftermath and changed exploration routes. Each consequence remains source-backed and subject-bound rather than attached to whichever Cobblemon entity happens to render later.
