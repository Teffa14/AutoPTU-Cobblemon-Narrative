# The Sample That Changed Hands — Pass 306

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-06

A damaged relay brace recovered after the outage becomes important because its surface may preserve evidence relevant to the competing accident/tampering theories from Passes 303–305.

The brace is collected at the scene, transferred toward an analysis site and later examined. One handoff is missing from the records available to the first investigator. That creates a documentation gap. It does not establish that the brace was altered.

A second investigator later finds an emergency-transfer receipt. If that record fits the known sequence, the apparent gap can close without changing what the first investigator reasonably believed at the time. A different branch can instead produce independent evidence that the storage seal was broken after collection. That supports compromise of the evidence item, but still does not identify who altered it or why. Actor attribution and intent remain under the infrastructure-attribution contracts.

The loop supports several consequences without requiring a single canonical culprit. A maintenance office may distrust an analysis because its chain is incomplete. A field technician may defend the physical observation while accepting that custody was weak. A rival investigator may possess the missing receipt and reach a different conclusion. Later communication can reconcile those beliefs only if the relevant records actually travel between actors.

## Reduced implementation

The reduced version is fully world-simulation driven. The player travels between the relay, transfer desk, storage room and analysis office using ordinary world travel. Each recovered document or observation enters a specific NPC/player evidence ledger with explicit provenance. Custody assessment uses only known records. No battle is required.

If the site itself must be rendered, traversal uses static passable/blocked nodes and verified base movement. Weather is presentation only. No forced movement, delayed collapse, mechanical shock, persistent status or dynamic hazard is required.

## Full encounter version

The brace can remain inside a storm-damaged service gallery above the relay. Recovery may involve unstable walkways, wind displacement, debris zones, timed electrical surges, a rescue opportunity and a confrontation with an actor who also wants the artifact.

That version requires exact capability families according to authored mechanics:

- targeting/footprints/range/LoS for spatially resolved interactions;
- base movement legality for ordinary traversal;
- complete movement for wind displacement, push/pull, knockback, interception or forced rescue movement;
- core calculations for deterministic battle arithmetic;
- action economy/initiative for structured tactical turns;
- full turn/round lifecycle for timed surges, collapse phases or delayed effects;
- full stateful damage pipeline for environmental or combat damage;
- status lifecycle for persistent shock/burn/other conditions if authored;
- terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions for active storm, debris, electrical zones or reaction rescues;
- move-specific behavior, abilities, items and Trainer Features/perks only when those owners are mechanically active;
- AI legal-action infrastructure for legal tactical choices;
- AI tactical policy for autonomous tactical strategy;
- Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback support for authoritative visible execution.

The full version must not be implemented by re-creating missing PTU mechanics inside Minecraft. Until the relevant families are verified, the reduced version preserves the same evidence, suspects, custody dispute and downstream story.

## Canon questions

The relay, brace, evidence office, handlers and analysis facility are placeholders. They do not establish Ouros canon.

Before promotion, local worldbuilding must define where such evidence is stored, which institution owns the site, who has legitimate access and which PTU/Caelo abilities or Skills can discover or authenticate each clue. A Pokémon sense or Move cannot become a forensic shortcut without explicit mechanical validation.
