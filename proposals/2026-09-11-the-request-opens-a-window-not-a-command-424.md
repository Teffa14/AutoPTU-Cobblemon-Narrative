# The Request Opens a Window, Not a Command — Pass 424

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON

This file proposes a reusable Ouros quest and NPC-agency pattern. It does not establish a new incident, duty, relationship, route condition, battle, faction rule or historical fact.

## Premise

A named actor asks another named actor for help. The request is meaningful because it can alter the recipient's planning once it actually arrives. It does not directly edit that recipient's agenda.

The useful narrative unit is therefore a decision window:

- a requester has a reason to ask;
- the message travels through a real channel;
- the recipient receives the request at a particular semantic time;
- only then does that recipient reconsider its existing commitments;
- the resulting choice can be accept, defer, reject, narrow the requested scope, suggest another person or request more information.

The request remains historically true even when the recipient declines it.

## Why this matters for Ouros

Persistent NPCs become more credible when assistance has opportunity cost. A mechanic who is already maintaining equipment, an observer who is already committed to a route check or a caregiver who cannot leave immediately should not become free merely because a quest system selected them as useful.

The same request can therefore produce different stories at different times without changing its wording. Earlier delivery may permit immediate help. Later delivery may produce a deferral. A channel failure may mean the intended helper never knew they were asked.

This creates consequence from timing, communications and prior commitments instead of from arbitrary quest flags.

## Candidate Marea use

Marea Interior already supplies plausible surfaces without adding canon.

Mara Veyra and the Marea Field Office can serve as one possible request origin. Teo Lark can serve as one possible recipient when ordinary equipment or field instruments are relevant. Other established residents can fill equivalent roles only when their canon responsibilities make sense for the selected case.

These are examples of binding points, not new obligations. No resident is canonically assigned to a new mission by this proposal.

## Reduced version

The reduced form needs no battle resolution.

One NPC receives information and chooses to request assistance. The request is sent. The target receives it later. Delivery creates a replanning opportunity. The target may continue an existing obligation instead of helping.

This form requires semantic time, private knowledge, explicit communication, durable world-action intents, schedules, obligations, permissions, travel constraints and selective replanning.

Minecraft may present the participants and message surfaces. It must not infer receipt or acceptance from proximity, UI state or visible dialogue decoration.

## Full mechanically rich version

A request can originate from an active field problem: a rescue, escort, retrieval, extraction, protected observation or environmental emergency. The recipient may later join, bring equipment, hold another route or provide information rather than physically enter the encounter.

The full version must declare exact battle dependencies instead of treating “help arrives” as one generic mechanic.

Targeting/footprints/range/LoS — needed for any spatially constrained protection or rescue action; VERIFIED only in audited scopes.

Base movement legality — needed for ordinary legal movement; VERIFIED only in audited scopes.

Complete movement — needed for dragging, interception, knockback, forced slides or other push/pull/forced movement; PARTIAL.

Core calculations — VERIFIED only in audited scopes.

Action economy/initiative — needed when a helper enters or gains a legal action window; PARTIAL. Current replacement-insertion evidence does not establish the full category.

Full turn/round lifecycle — needed for multi-round rescue windows, delayed arrivals or phased hazards; PARTIAL.

Full stateful damage pipeline — needed when damage consequences must persist through the encounter; PARTIAL.

Status lifecycle — needed for persistent or timed conditions; PARTIAL.

Terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions — needed for flooding, unstable ground, weather phases, triggered debris, reaction windows or zone effects; MIXED / PARTIAL / BLOCKING by behavior.

Move-specific behavior — individually gated.

Abilities — PARTIAL and individually gated.

Items — individually gated.

Trainer Features/perks — individually gated.

AI legal-action infrastructure — ordinary audited actions only; rescue, escort, protect, retrieve, carry, interact, brace and extract need explicit admission.

AI tactical policy — BLOCKING for specialized objective-aware behavior such as rescue-first movement, protect-carrier, evidence-preserving withdrawal or disengage-after-objective.

Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback — PARTIAL / BLOCKING for authoritative specialized objectives, non-KO completion and recovery of an encounter already in flight.

## Narrative invariants

Receipt is not acceptance.

Acceptance is not instantaneous travel.

Institution membership is not broadcast knowledge.

A request cannot erase the target's prior obligations.

A requester cannot silently create another actor's commitment.

A failed or queued message cannot influence an NPC that has not received it.

The eventual reply should have its own provenance and communication path if the requester must know it.

## Longer-term arc use

Repeated assistance requests can reveal relationships through behavior rather than relationship-score exposition. One person may consistently respond quickly but only for certain categories of work. Another may be reliable when given notice but unavailable for emergencies. A third may often suggest alternatives because their own role is difficult to leave.

Those patterns should emerge from persistent schedules, obligations, knowledge and decisions. They should not be hard-coded personality verdicts unless later canon explicitly approves them.

## Canon decision required later

Canon would need to choose a specific incident, requester, recipient, timing, reason and outcome before this becomes a world fact. Until then the proposal is only a reusable structure.
