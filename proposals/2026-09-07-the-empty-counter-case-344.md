# The Empty Counter — Pass 344

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-07

Premise

Continue the existing Marea equipment thread without changing canon. Teo Lark has accepted a request for a serviced field instrument. Ema Rey is the authorized physical recipient on Nerea Sol's behalf. The pickup window and repair-row location are valid.

Ema arrives.

Teo is not there.

The instrument has not changed custody. The accepted request remains historical fact. The proxy authorization remains historical fact. The absence is a new fact.

Existing canon used

- Teo works at Puerto Bruma repair row and maintains ordinary equipment and field instruments.
- Nerea works from Estación Mirador.
- Ema performs equipment checks and field work under Nerea's project protocols and connects to Teo.
- Puerto Bruma, Sendero del Vidrio and Estación Mirador are persistent authored places.

Nothing about why Teo is absent is canonized by this proposal.

Playable reconstruction

Ema can record the failed pickup, contact Teo or Nerea through an available channel, wait while the authorization remains active, perform another nearby obligation, or leave and replan.

Later evidence can explain the absence in several ordinary ways: Teo was pulled into another repair, misunderstood the pickup time, had to move the instrument, became unavailable because of another valid obligation, or believed a cancellation had been communicated.

The player can investigate which explanation is supported by schedules, message receipts, travel state and direct testimony. Absence alone does not prove negligence or deception.

Branch: resource absent

Teo may be present while the exact instrument is not. That creates `RESOURCE_ABSENT`, not `RESOURCE_LOST`. The unit may have been moved under another valid event, returned to inspection, or transferred before the appointment. Provenance must decide.

Branch: explicit refusal

A participant can refuse at the meeting because circumstances changed. That closes the current authorization semantically but still does not transfer ownership or custody. A later exchange needs new authorization.

Reduced implementation

Run entirely through semantic time, Pass 343 authorization, Pass 344 attempt state, communication, travel and selective replanning. No combat or unsupported equipment mechanics are needed.

Mechanically rich continuation

If a later trip to retrieve the same instrument intersects a real tactical interruption, preserve the logistical premise while gating every mechanic individually. Carrying/interception requires complete movement; tactical handoff requires action economy and legal-action support; changing site conditions require the exact terrain/weather/hazard/reaction contracts; equipment damage requires full stateful damage; any Move, Ability, Item or Trainer Feature remains individually gated; objective-aware protection/retreat remains blocked on AI tactical policy; Minecraft remains playback only.

Narrative value

This case makes a mundane failure persistent. The world can remember that Ema arrived, that no custody transfer happened and that three actors had to alter their plans. Later tension can emerge from accumulated reliability history without inventing villainy from a single missed meeting.
