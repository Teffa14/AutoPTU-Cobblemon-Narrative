# The Dead Relay Inquiry — Pass 302 Ouros candidate

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-06

## Premise

A route-safety dispatcher was expected to warn a traveling group about a dangerous closure. The group never received the warning and later suffered a serious delay or required rescue. Public blame settles quickly on the dispatcher.

The investigation starts with a simpler question: what actually happened between knowledge and receipt?

## Evidence structure

The dispatcher has a private, timestamped observation that the route became unsafe. A disclosure expectation proves that the traveler was within the warning duty. Communication records then determine whether a usable path existed.

Possible histories remain distinct:

- direct contact was available and the dispatcher deliberately stayed silent;
- the traveler had no known reachable channel at the relevant time;
- the dispatcher sent the warning and it remained queued behind overload;
- infrastructure failed when delivery became due;
- the warning reached a local projection boundary but never received an authoritative acknowledgement;
- the warning arrived, and the traveler's later decision had another cause.

No branch is selected for dramatic convenience. The candidate should bind to whichever facts the persistent world actually contains.

## Investigation loop

The player can compare the dispatcher’s evidence, duty record, route state, dispatch log, relay condition, receiver history and later testimony. Each source answers a different causal question. A broken relay can exonerate one actor without proving that every institution behaved correctly. A confirmed dispatch can clear deliberate withholding while opening an infrastructure or backlog investigation.

The consequence may become trust repair, institutional maintenance, route-policy change, a new redundancy project, rivalry escalation or a later hearing. These outcomes remain proposals until the relevant social and institutional systems support them.

## Reduced version

The complete narrative premise runs without tactical combat:

private route observation → disclosure expectation → communication decision → explicit access/attempt evidence → delivery outcome → investigation → responsibility finding → replanning/social consequence.

The reduced version uses current global NPC memory, communication, disclosure and relationship seams. It does not require Minecraft entity presence or AutoPTU.

## Full version: relay-site rescue and repair

The investigation may lead to a damaged relay site overlooking the affected route. The full authored scene can combine infrastructure inspection, reaching stranded actors and restoring a communication path while local environmental danger changes access.

Combat is optional. A hostile or panicked Pokémon can be an obstacle to route choice rather than a mandatory defeat target.
