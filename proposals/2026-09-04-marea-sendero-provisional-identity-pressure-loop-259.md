# Proposal — Marea/Sendero provisional identity-pressure loop (Pass 259)

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON.

The world should not create a fully persistent actor every time wildlife is seen twice. At the same time, Ouros must not lose an individual consequence merely because the animal still belongs to an anonymous counted pool.

The proposed loop lets a counted source carry a bounded provisional episode. A brief observation or one-off site-use trace can expire after it stops mattering. The player keeps the observation in field history, but the simulation does not keep unnecessary private linkage forever.

A different source can accumulate something more consequential, such as an individual disturbance-response history that should remain attached to one biological individual across later encounters. If Ouros also has admissible internal continuity evidence, aggregation has become lossy. The source then enters `PROMOTION_REQUIRED` and Pass 258 converts that already-counted unit into a persistent actor with population delta zero.

This creates a useful worldbuilding gradient:

anonymous wildlife -> repeatedly observed candidate -> consequential individual -> persistent ecological character

The gradient is internal. Character knowledge continues to follow the recognition contracts from Passes 253–254. Internal promotion never tells the player automatically that two sightings were the same Pokémon.

## Reduced playable version

A trainer visits the lower Sendero more than once. One Fletchling-like observation leaves only a short site-use history and later becomes irrelevant. Another already-counted source exhibits a repeatable disturbance response that affects future projection. Ouros preserves the latter history and eventually promotes its source internally when continuity is strong enough. No combat is required, no new Fletchling is created, and no population total changes.

## Rich version

A future field-study scene can require following the animal, maintaining line of sight, choosing a low-impact route, or reacting when it withdraws. That version must call AutoPTU only for mechanics that AutoPTU actually owns and has verified. Minecraft/Cobblemon remains presentation and observation input, not battle-rule authority.

## Capability dependencies

The reduced bookkeeping/projection version has no tactical AutoPTU dependency. Production use still depends on Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback support, currently PARTIAL/BLOCKING end-to-end.

A rich follow scene uses targeting/footprints/range/LoS (VERIFIED within audited contracts), base movement legality (VERIFIED), action economy/initiative (VERIFIED), full turn/round lifecycle (PARTIAL), AI legal-action infrastructure (VERIFIED), AI tactical policy (BLOCKING), and adapter/playback (PARTIAL/BLOCKING). Interception, blocking, push/pull or forced movement adds complete movement (PARTIAL). Terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions remains MIXED/PARTIAL/BLOCKING when those elements mechanically affect detection or approach. Trainer Features/perks remains PARTIAL if a Feature modifies evidence. Other capability families are dependencies only when the scene explicitly uses them.

## Canon gates still open

No universal sighting count should trigger persistence. Species-specific retention policy, allowed provisional state classes, production continuity evidence, and any future marker/injury/relationship trigger require separate canon/mechanical approval. The Marea Fletchling population remains 12 and this proposal does not create another canon actor.
