# The Calendar Remembers Both Promises — Pass 450

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Canon effect: NONE

## Premise

A specialist accepted a local survey appointment. Access failed at the original start, the specialist requested new terms, the requester agreed to reopen the arrangement, and both actors eventually accepted a later window.

The world then passes through a restart or a long off-screen interval.

When activity resumes, the old appointment still exists as historical evidence. The later appointment is the only executable obligation. A ledger entry, old notice, dated field mark or other ordinary record can reveal that the earlier promise existed without making it current again.

No region, institution, named NPC, species population, blame outcome or relationship consequence is assigned by this proposal.

## Narrative use

The distinction creates an investigation-sized story without inventing a villain. Someone examining the site may initially see evidence of the first appointment and wonder why the work did not occur. The later record explains that the plan changed, but does not by itself establish whether the change was reasonable, avoidable or socially acceptable.

Meanwhile the new date remains exposed to the live world. Access can reopen or close again. Supplies can become available. Another obligation can compete for attention. Local Pokémon activity can alter which approach is sensible. A preparation task can remove one constraint or reveal a safer route.

The player can therefore encounter history through the environment while current NPC scheduling continues independently.

## Reduced implementation

The reduced form needs semantic time, checkpoint V6, private knowledge, explicit communication history, ordinary agenda execution, access/resource evidence and standard travel.

A practical sequence is:

1. restore the old and replacement records together;
2. expose evidence that two dated commitments existed;
3. retain only the replacement in the responder's executable agenda;
4. reevaluate current access shortly before the new window;
5. allow a basic preparation objective to improve access or information;
6. complete the survey narratively if no structured mechanics are required.

This form does not need AutoPTU.

## Mechanically rich version

The same premise can support a later inspection or escort across a damaged route during unstable conditions. Optional preparation can secure equipment, identify a safe section or obtain an alternate approach. The new appointment still supplies only the social/scheduling premise. The battle engine receives a structured objective only if current conditions require it.

Possible full objective: reach several survey points, protect the specialist while observations are recorded, then withdraw if conditions become unsafe.

This version must declare exact dependencies:

- targeting/footprints/range/LoS — required if attacks, protection zones or ranged interactions matter; VERIFIED only in audited scopes;
- base movement legality — required; VERIFIED only in audited scopes;
- complete movement including push/pull/knockback/interception/forced movement — PARTIAL and required if wind, current, impact or interception can displace actors;
- core calculations — required for audited combat calculations; VERIFIED only in audited scopes;
- action economy/initiative — PARTIAL if the survey objective consumes structured actions during battle;
- full turn/round lifecycle — PARTIAL if timed objective progress spans rounds;
- full stateful damage pipeline — PARTIAL if combat damage can affect completion;
- status lifecycle — PARTIAL if persistent conditions matter;
- terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions — MIXED / PARTIAL / BLOCKING for evolving weather, unstable ground, hazard zones, delayed collapse or reactions;
- move-specific behavior — individually gated for every required Move;
- abilities — PARTIAL and individually gated, especially if an Ability changes terrain/weather or reacts to movement;
- items — individually gated if equipment has tactical effects;
- Trainer Features/perks — individually gated; no generic interrupt coverage may be inferred from the narrow switch-trigger evidence;
- AI legal-action infrastructure — must explicitly admit survey/protect/brace/withdraw verbs used by the scene;
- AI tactical policy — BLOCKING until the specialized objective policy is verified;
- Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback — PARTIAL / BLOCKING for survey-point state, non-KO completion, hazard presentation and interrupted-scene recovery.

## Reduced equivalent for missing battle capability

If dynamic weather, forced movement, reactions, delayed hazards or specialized objective actions are not admitted, preserve the same story with fixed safe/unsafe waypoints, static access evidence and ordinary movement. Survey progress becomes explicit world-level observations at reachable points. Danger can close a route between semantic-time checks without simulating tactical knockback or weather phases.

The narrative premise remains unchanged: the actors are honoring a replacement appointment whose history survived restart.

## Guardrails

An old record does not prove breach or misconduct.

A replacement record does not prove that access, resources or travel are currently viable.

Preparation completed by another actor does not retroactively change what either party knew when they negotiated.

A restart does not erase the original agreement and does not revive it as an active schedule entry.

Environmental evidence can reveal history only to actors who actually observe or receive that evidence.

## Open questions

A later accountability layer still needs rules for interpreting original and replacement commitments together without assigning automatic fault.

Repeated renegotiation of an already replaced commitment needs a deliberate multi-generation lineage contract.

Terminal outcomes such as completed, cancelled or missed should remain separate from this persistence proposal.
