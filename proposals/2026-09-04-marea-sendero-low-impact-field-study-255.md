# Marea / Sendero low-impact field study — Pass 255

Status: PROPOSED CONTENT
Canon effect: NONE
Date: 2026-09-04

## Premise

Field research in Sendero can become a gameplay loop where the player chooses how much disturbance to accept for better evidence. The environment reacts to the method, not to a hidden quest flag.

No researcher, institution, permit system, observation threshold or marking programme is canonized here.

## Reduced playable loop

A player receives a request to document activity of a familiar local Fletchling population. A passive observation point can collect weaker evidence with little or no modeled disturbance. Moving close to obtain a clearer view can improve individual evidence but may add local disturbance pressure.

Repeated close approaches can make later projections less exposed or shift them toward quieter windows through the existing Pass 248 envelope. The player can then choose to wait, change observation method, use indirect signs, or return after a recovery interval.

The key consequence is longitudinal: careless research can make the same study temporarily harder without deleting Pokemon or fabricating migration.

## Research conduct hook

If a task suggests physical marking, capture for handling or another invasive method, the action remains unavailable until canon defines who is authorized, why the study requires it and what welfare rules apply. A quest giver cannot manufacture that authority.

This keeps Pass 254's marker-registry idea available as future content while making its activation an explicit worldbuilding decision.

## Investigation structures

The same pattern can support nest-watch requests, behavior diaries, disturbance-source mapping, before/after comparisons around construction or crowds, and debates between researchers who value data quality differently.

Independent evidence roots remain important. Multiple observers repeating one report do not become multiple field visits, while genuinely separate observations can reveal a behavioral change.

## Reduced dependencies

Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback is required to present the relevant actor, observation distance/method and later visibility changes. No battle family is required.

## Rich dependencies

A structured shadowing sequence requires targeting/footprints/range/LoS, base movement, full turn/round lifecycle, AI legal-action infrastructure, AI tactical policy and adapter/playback. Complete movement becomes required when interception, blocking or forced repositioning occurs. Terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions applies when terrain or conditions alter access or detection.

Damage, status, Moves, Abilities, Items and Trainer Features/perks are dependencies only when the authored scene actually invokes them. Any Trainer Feature that improves field observation remains blocked until its exact Java contract is verified.

## Canon questions

Before activation, canon must decide whether Sendero has protected observation zones, whether research access differs from ordinary public access, which activities count as unacceptable disturbance for local species and whether any institution is authorized to physically mark Pokemon.
