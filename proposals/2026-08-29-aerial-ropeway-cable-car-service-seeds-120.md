# Ouros Proposal Seeds — Aerial Ropeway / Cable-Car Continuity — Pass 120

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON.
Date: 2026-08-29

These seeds use the Pass 120 continuity model without establishing a particular Ouros ropeway, region, operator, engineering standard or Pokémon role.

## Situation seeds

### The Cabin Arrived, Service Did Not

A carrier reaches the lower station during a limited-service period. Several travelers assume arrival means normal operations have resumed, but the operator has not released the next trip. The useful conflict is information and expectation: who received which notice, which trip actually completed and what the current service record says.

### The Upper Station Is Open, the Line Is Not

The upper building remains staffed and reachable by an older trail while the aerial line is suspended. Visitors repeatedly confuse destination availability with line availability. The station becomes a meeting point for guides, researchers and residents arriving by different routes.

### Power Returned, Inspection Did Not

An upstream outage ends. Lights and station equipment visibly return. Public expectation jumps ahead of the operator's restart sequence. Maintenance and verification remain separate records, creating pressure without inventing technical failure rules.

### The Old Footpath Became the Main Route

A long suspension redirects ordinary foot traffic through an older alignment. Vendors, guides and a small social routine emerge there. When the ropeway returns, the alternate path does not simply become empty again.

### The Temporary Shuttle Outlived the Repair

A canon-approved replacement service begins during a closure. After the line reopens, one neighborhood wants the replacement retained because it connected people who previously had no convenient route. Travel and civic systems decide the consequence; the ropeway layer only preserves why the route appeared.

### The Pokémon Waits by the Retired Station

One individual Pokémon continues a documented routine around a decommissioned terminal. Residents disagree about why. Observed behavior can seed a relationship or investigation, but it does not grant species-wide transport knowledge or supernatural sensing.

### Three Departure Boards, Two Service Days

Screenshots, a printed sign and a traveler memory show different times. Each source was correct at a different revision point. The mystery is solved by effective timestamps and superseded notices rather than by discovering a liar.

### The Station Moved, the Route Name Stayed

A rebuilt line retained the old public route name while one terminal moved. Older maps, business addresses and oral directions therefore point to different physical structures under one familiar label. Stable IDs and effective dates resolve the contradiction.

### Everyone Saw the Same Cabin Stop at Different Times

Passengers report the interruption time, operator logs record when motion ceased, a station worker records when the trip was marked interrupted, and public notices show when the service suspension was announced. The records describe different milestones of one event.

### The Summit Market Stayed Open

The ropeway is unavailable, yet a summit market remains active because workers and supplies have another verified route. Outsiders assume the market must be closed. Local life demonstrates that transport modes and destination state are related but independent.

## NPC and faction candidates

An operator veteran is known for correcting the phrase “it is running” into a more precise service state. Their value is institutional memory, not secret technical omniscience.

A mountain guide built a stronger livelihood during a prolonged closure and now advocates for keeping the older ground route maintained even after aerial service returns.

A station vendor remembers which disruptions changed visitor patterns and can connect present-day behavior to old service episodes without being an authoritative engineering source.

A maintenance technician can explain what work order is actually open but cannot independently decide public access, weather truth or rescue outcomes.

An accessibility advocate tracks whether announced alternatives genuinely work for different travelers. They provide actor-specific evidence instead of accepting “there is a trail” as a complete answer.

An individual Pokémon may have a trained, documented operational or social role around a station. That role belongs to the individual and does not become an automatic species mechanic.

All institutions, professions and roles remain candidate material until placed into canon.

## Mystery: Six Times the Cable Car “Stopped”

Six records seem to assign six different stop times. They actually refer to departure interruption, carrier motion cessation, operator acknowledgement, whole-line suspension, passenger transfer completion and public-notice publication.

Resolution requires matching each timestamp to its subject and event type. The investigation can finish without declaring any record fraudulent.

## Mystery: Two Stations With Three Names

A historical brochure, maintenance archive and current map appear to describe three different terminals. Renovation history reveals two persistent station IDs with changing display names and a relocated entrance. The mystery can connect to old businesses, route memories or an unresolved historical event.

## Exploration: The Station Above the Old Road

Players investigate a retired service road and earlier station footprint beneath a rebuilt terminal. Useful evidence includes dated photographs, old maps, operator notices, trail records, resident memories, structural remains and a recurring Pokémon observation.

The current implementation uses only verified static navigation and provenance. No moving carrier, climbing cable, wind mechanic or fall hazard is required.

A future richer form could place active operations around the investigation, but only after the exact movement, hazard, reaction and playback families are supported.

## Longer arc: A Mountain Learns Two Ways Up

Phase one establishes ordinary use. People commute, visit, hike, deliver goods and meet around the two terminals. The older ground route still exists but is secondary.

A bounded service interruption then shifts traffic. Different institutions react at different speeds. Some travelers use the old path, some delay journeys, some businesses move activity, and some actors require accommodation because the alternate route does not work for them.

Recovery is staged. A repaired component does not equal verified service. Verification does not equal full capacity. Public information catches up through successive notices. The first returning trips do not erase temporary social arrangements.

Months later, the mountain has two meaningful route cultures instead of one dominant transport service. A later historical question, weather episode, festival, rescue or infrastructure problem can reuse those relationships.

## Encounter: Lower Station Withdrawal

Full intent: a conflict unfolds while noncombatants move toward a verified safe exit or alternate route. This depends on complete movement including interception/forced movement, full lifecycle if withdrawal timing matters, terrain/zones/reactions if access cells change, objective-aware AI for protection/withdrawal and adapter/playback for meaningful presentation. Ordinary damage/status/Move/Ability/Item/Trainer Feature dependencies apply whenever those mechanics are present.

Reduced form: civilians leave before BattleSpec creation. The carrier and operational machinery remain outside tactical authority. AutoPTU resolves a static station-apron battle. Success secures the immediate perimeter and nothing more.

## Encounter: Interrupted-Line Rescue Staging

Full intent: a stopped aerial trip creates a rescue operation while another conflict occurs around carriers or suspended infrastructure. This would require moving-platform semantics, complete movement, hazards/zones/reactions, lifecycle, any governed fall/environmental damage, protection AI and semantic playback. Those capabilities are not assumed.

Reduced form: Crisis/Rescue has already moved affected passengers to a stable authored staging location. The interrupted carrier remains background scenery. AutoPTU resolves a conventional encounter at the staging perimeter. Battle success does not complete rescue documentation, repair or reopening.

## Encounter: Summit Diversion Junction

Full intent: travelers are actively rerouting while a conflict threatens the alternate connection. Dynamic protection depends on complete movement, generalized reactions, tactical policy and possibly environmental zones/weather rules.

Reduced form: complete the traveler reroute in world state before combat. Resolve a static junction encounter afterward. Travel and Accessibility determine route viability; victory only clears the immediate obstruction.
