# Not Now Does Not Mean Yes — Pass 430

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-11

## Premise

An NPC receives a legitimate request for help and chooses DEFER. The answer is durable, but it creates neither an acceptance nor an obligation. It records a future point at which that same NPC should reconsider the request against whatever the world looks like then.

The dramatic value comes from change between those two moments. The original problem may worsen, disappear, move, become somebody else's responsibility or require a different kind of help. The responder may become free, acquire a harder obligation, lose access to the route, learn new evidence or simply decide the request is still lower priority.

## Reduced version

The requester asks for help. The responder receives the message, chooses DEFER and names a reconsideration minute. The world continues. At that semantic minute only the responder wakes. The global planner re-evaluates current goals, needs, commitments, permissions, knowledge and situational intents.

No combat is required. The later result may be ACCEPT, another DEFER, REJECT, COUNTERPROPOSE or no assistance-related action if current priorities legitimately dominate it.

## Full version

The original request may concern an escort, retrieval, rescue, containment problem, hazardous crossing or contested repair. By the time the deferral opens, the tactical shape can differ from the original report. A retrieval may now be a negotiation. A rescue route may be blocked. A threat may have moved. Another team may already have resolved the incident.

The world layer should determine whether structured resolution is still needed. If it is, the selected world intent must use the ordinary explicit REQUEST_AUTOPTU boundary.

## Capability dependencies for the mechanically rich form

targeting/footprints/range/LoS: required only when the later scene needs tactical targeting or spatial visibility; VERIFIED only in audited scopes.

base movement legality: required for ordinary tactical movement; VERIFIED only in audited scopes.

complete movement including push/pull/knockback/interception/forced movement: required if the changed incident includes forced displacement, interception or rescue movement; PARTIAL.

core calculations: required for ordinary audited combat arithmetic; VERIFIED only in audited scopes.

action economy/initiative: required for a structured encounter; PARTIAL.

full turn/round lifecycle: required for timed tactical phases, delayed effects or round-bound objectives; PARTIAL.

full stateful damage pipeline: required when damage persists through the encounter state; PARTIAL.

status lifecycle: required for persistent or phase-sensitive status behavior; PARTIAL.

terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions: required if the delayed situation changed because of hazardous terrain, weather, zones or reaction windows; MIXED / PARTIAL / BLOCKING by exact behavior.

move-specific behavior: individually gated.

abilities: PARTIAL and individually gated.

items: individually gated.

Trainer Features/perks: individually gated.

AI legal-action infrastructure: VERIFIED only for ordinary audited actions. Specialized rescue, escort, protect, retrieve, carry, interact, brace or extract actions need explicit admission.

AI tactical policy: BLOCKING for specialized objective-aware rescue, escort, protection, extraction and withdrawal policy.

Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback support: PARTIAL / BLOCKING for specialized authoritative objective state, non-KO completion and in-flight recovery.

## Worldbuilding use

This pattern can be bound to existing Ouros actors and institutions without asserting a new incident. It is especially useful when a specialist, caretaker, coordinator or field worker has legitimate competing duties.

A future authored adventure can use the pattern to make reliability, timing and changing context visible without assigning a permanent personality label such as “helpful” or “unreliable.” The history should show what the NPC knew, what they had already promised and what changed before the reconsideration window opened.

## Canon boundary

No new named incident, obligation, location, faction rule, relationship or outcome is canonized here. This is a reusable world-agent and narrative candidate only.
