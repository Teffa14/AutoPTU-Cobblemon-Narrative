# The Third Window Is the One on the Door — Pass 451

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-12

## Premise

A field specialist agrees to inspect a site. Access fails. Both parties negotiate a second date. Before that date arrives, another legitimate change forces a second renegotiation. The site now has three historically valid appointment records, but only the newest one controls what the specialist should do next.

Nothing here establishes a region, named NPC, institution, species roster or canonical event.

## Narrative value

The older promises remain visible in notices, logs, messages or memory. They explain delay, preparation and frustration without acting like three simultaneous quests.

The latest date can produce different environmental evidence because the world continued between appointments. A migration may have moved on, a damaged path may have reopened, another team may have stabilized part of the site, or a local Pokémon group may now react differently to visitors.

This supports recurring NPC stories where history accumulates without turning every prior state into current work.

## Reduced implementation

Use semantic time, assistance commitment history, the Pass 451 lineage view, communication history, ordinary access evidence, travel and observation.

No structured combat is required. The latest visit can resolve through inspection, conversation, evidence collection or a simple world-state update.

Required battle capability families: none.

## Full encounter variant

If the latest visit discovers an active hazard or protection problem, the same premise can become a structured survey-and-withdrawal scene. The group must inspect marked points, protect the specialist and leave if conditions cross a safety threshold.

Possible mechanics and dependencies:

- targeting/footprints/range/LoS for attacks or protected observation positions;
- base movement legality for route traversal;
- complete movement if the scene uses push, pull, knockback, interception or forced displacement;
- core calculations for admitted combat resolution;
- action economy/initiative and full turn/round lifecycle for structured scene timing;
- full stateful damage pipeline if injury outcomes matter;
- status lifecycle for persistent conditions;
- terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions if the site changes by phase, uses dangerous ground, delayed zones or interrupts;
- move-specific behavior for every move used beyond generic admitted handling;
- abilities, items and Trainer Features/perks only where each exact behavior is admitted;
- AI legal-action infrastructure for specialized verbs such as INSPECT, PROTECT, BRACE, WITHDRAW or EXTRACT;
- AI tactical policy for choosing among those objectives;
- Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback for objective state, non-KO completion and interrupted-scene recovery.

## Reduced fallback for the same premise

If advanced movement, reactions, phased weather or specialized tactical verbs are unavailable, keep the same site and history. Use static route geometry, admitted movement and ordinary battle if needed, while resolving inspection progress and withdrawal thresholds in the narrative/world layer outside AutoPTU.

The adapter must not recreate missing PTU rules.

## Consequence hooks

Later accountability can compare the original promise, first replacement and second replacement without treating delay itself as proof of blame. A requester may remember repeated inconvenience. A specialist may have spent resources preparing twice. Another institution may care only that the final inspection happened. Those are later evidence consumers, not lineage owners.
