# Proof Before Grudge — Pass 298

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON.

A respected route coordinator gives several travelers a warning that later proves false. One recipient reaches the location and personally sees evidence contradicting the warning. That produces a playable suspicion, not automatic knowledge that the coordinator lied.

The reduced loop has three stages. First, the recipient holds the original report and independent contradictory evidence. Second, the player can investigate how the coordinator obtained the information: dispatch records, a witnessed conversation, an admission, a timetable, or another provenance-backed clue may establish that the coordinator already possessed contrary evidence. Third, only characters who actually obtain enough evidence to attribute intent can suffer a directional trust change toward the coordinator.

This produces divergent consequences. A traveler who only sees the route open may think the coordinator was mistaken. An investigator with the dispatch record may conclude the warning was deliberate. A colleague who never sees either piece can continue trusting the coordinator. A later public correction still does not retroactively synchronize those private histories.

The premise can attach to a route closure, missing shipment, tournament scheduling, ecological warning, rescue dispatch, faction negotiation or disputed sighting without naming a canon location or institution.

Reduced version: evidence ledger -> corroborated contradiction -> investigation -> intent evidence -> directional trust consequence -> world-level replanning. No structured battle is required.

Full version: if investigation leads into a tactical confrontation, dependencies must be declared from the actual encounter. A chase with interception or knockback depends on complete movement. Mechanical weather, hazards or reaction zones depend on terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions. Delayed or complex status effects depend on full turn/round lifecycle and status lifecycle plus the owning mechanic family. Autonomous enemy choices depend on AI tactical policy.
