# Ouros Transit Social-Scene Seeds — Pass 54

Status: PROPOSED. Non-canon Ouros candidates.

These seeds test the Pass-54 transit extension without establishing regional geography, transport technology, companies, laws or permanent institutions. Names are deliberately functional placeholders until canon review.

## Candidate A — The Regular on the Third Departure

A recurring transport worker or regular passenger appears on the same service often enough to become familiar. The first two meetings are ordinary. On a later trip, the person is absent and another route worker mentions a concrete change: reassignment, family obligation, research placement, illness already grounded by care state, or service rerouting.

The hook is continuity rather than danger. The player may follow up, ignore it or learn the answer incidentally later.

Persistent outputs can include a journey-contact edge, a new destination/service lead and a callback when the actor returns. No relationship label is inferred from repeated proximity.

Canon questions:
- Which established Ouros services plausibly have regular crews or passengers?
- Which region or route can support a recurring service without conflicting with existing geography?

## Candidate B — Last-Leg Witness Window

A case already has several incomplete public or voluntary witness accounts. Three relevant witnesses happen to be traveling on the same service before they separate at different stops.

The scene has a natural clock: arrival. The player decides whom to approach first and whether to spend time resolving contradictions, requesting future contact or leaving a lead for later. Statements remain claims until corroborated.

The scene can end without a complete solution. A missed interview becomes a future lead instead of a failed quest.

Implementation state:
No battle mechanics are required. Full Minecraft execution still depends on adapter/playback support for persistent journey actors and scene presentation. A text/state-driven version can run earlier.

## Candidate C — Mobile Workshop Day

A specialist who normally rotates between several small settlements is aboard a scheduled service with tools and unfinished commissions. A service disruption strands the specialist at an unscheduled hub for one day.

Local residents suddenly have temporary access to a useful service while customers at the intended destination face delay. The player can help communicate, reroute a critical commission, connect the specialist to local facilities or simply use the opportunity if their own request is valid.

The specialist never gains new recipes or effects from narrative convenience. Material Culture and crafting rules remain authoritative.

Persistent outputs:
- workshop/service availability changes at two locations;
- commission status changes with a causal reason;
- one mobile professional may become a recurring journey contact;
- public information reflects the temporary service shift.

Canon questions:
- Which crafts or repairs are already supported mechanically and culturally?
- Does Ouros canon support a recurring transport schedule appropriate for this pattern?

## Candidate D — Unscheduled Ecological Stop

A routine service pauses or reroutes because an existing ecological state makes normal passage temporarily inappropriate. The stop exposes passengers to a location normally seen only from a distance.

The player can observe, help communicate the delay, assist a steward with noncombat work, or investigate why the closure became necessary. The scene should use current ecological evidence rather than generating a rare Pokémon merely because the player is present.

Possible continuation:
Later journeys show whether the route reopened, adopted a seasonal schedule or changed infrastructure.

Mechanical boundary:
The ecological restriction is world state. It does not create tactical terrain, encounter-rate bonuses, movement penalties or forced displacement unless those exact mechanics become verified.

## Candidate E — Broken-Deck Containment

A damaged transport area and frightened Pokémon create a safety problem. Civilians and cargo complicate the fiction, but the combat engine must not be asked to simulate unsupported crowd protection, sliding objects or collapsing zones.

Full intended version:
A later engine could support changing deck hazards, forced movement, protective interception, reaction windows and AI goals that favor withdrawal or territorial defense.

Current reduced version:
Evacuate passengers and secure loose cargo before combat instantiation. Resolve any fight on a static legal section of the deck or platform. Only authoritative battle participants enter AutoPTU. The world then records whether the service remains limited, enters repair or reopens.

## Candidate F — The Wrong Departure

The party boards or follows the wrong service because of an understandable information error: similar signage, stale route knowledge, a temporary platform change or a rushed assumption. The mistake must be recoverable and should never depend on hiding information the UI should have shown.

The unexpected stop offers valid choices:
- wait for the next connection;
- take a known alternate route;
- contact the destination about delay;
- explore only if the player wants to;
- help with a local issue already present in world state.

This pattern is useful once, not as a recurring punishment. Afterward the player's route knowledge should improve.

## Longer arc — A Route Becomes Familiar

This is a repeat-visit structure rather than a mandatory quest chain.

Visit 1 establishes the route, crew rhythm and major stops.

Visit 2 compresses unless a small callback matters.

Visit 3 intersects a service disruption or unusual passenger cohort and creates one persistent contact.

Later visits show state change: staff turnover, repaired infrastructure, seasonal timing, changed demand, an old contact returning, or a mobile service appearing at a different stop.

The arc demonstrates that transit can accumulate personal and public history without making every trip a bespoke adventure.

## Canon review boundary

No candidate above establishes that Ouros has a particular ferry, railway, aircraft, port, company or legal system. Before promotion, each seed must attach to already approved geography, infrastructure and institution state. Where a concept needs combat, its full and reduced forms must be rechecked against current AutoPTU evidence.
