# The Map That Changed Before the Team Knew — Pass 368

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-08

## Premise

Two field teams share one calibrated environmental survey unit. A conflict is admitted, an authorized scheduler selects the project with the tighter observation window, and Pass 349 applies that decision by displacing the other reservation. The world is saved before Pass 350 creates the notice obligation or before any message reaches the displaced team.

When play resumes, the facility calendar correctly shows the new allocation while the displaced team is already travelling toward the old observation site using the last schedule they actually received.

Nobody needs to be lying. Nobody needs to be irrational. The disagreement exists because operational state and individual knowledge advanced at different times.

## World-state structure

The scheduler can know:

- the historical conflict;
- the authority basis;
- the selected resolution;
- that the operational application already occurred.

The displaced team can know:

- its earlier valid reservation;
- the route and preparation plan based on that reservation;
- nothing yet about the displacement.

A technician at the instrument location can know only that the current calendar now favors the other team.

A later player investigation can therefore reconstruct a causal chain from persistent evidence instead of receiving one omniscient quest-state answer.

## Reduced implementation

This version does not require AutoPTU.

Use semantic time, reservations, rescheduling, historical conflict admission, authority evidence, allocation resolution, allocation application, communications, private knowledge, travel and replanning.

Success can mean reaching the displaced team, rerouting them to a useful alternate task, preserving their samples, or arranging a later replacement window before their travel becomes wasted effort.

## Rich field version

The displaced team is already crossing an exposed observation route when the player catches up. A sudden local Pokémon disturbance or environmental condition blocks the simplest return path. The objective remains coordination: reach the team, preserve the carried samples/instrument cases, and move everyone to a safe or useful destination before the environmental window closes.

Defeating every Pokémon is not required by the narrative premise.
