# The Warning Changes One Assignment — Pass 421

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON

## Premise

A field report reaches one named person shortly before an assignment begins. The new information matters, but it does not issue a universal command to the entire institution.

The recipient reassesses their own plan. Another worker who has not received the report continues from the state they legitimately know.

## Canon-safe anchors

The pattern can reuse Marea Field Office, Estación Mirador, Tideglass Archive, Loma Clara Producers Cooperative or Sendero del Vidrio when an authored story needs them. These are existing surfaces only.

This proposal adds no new incident, schedule, duty, relationship, route closure, field team or institutional rule to canon.

## Narrative state sequence

A useful implementation keeps the following state changes distinct:

1. A world condition already exists.
2. A sourced observation records a bounded fact about that condition.
3. One custodian owns the record.
4. A message is scheduled to one explicit recipient.
5. Successful delivery gives that recipient a private claim with preserved provenance.
6. Delivery schedules a replan for that recipient.
7. Their agenda resolver decides whether the new fact actually outranks current goals, commitments, needs and permissions.
8. Any changed plan becomes a later world action or handoff.

The report creates a reason to reconsider. It does not predetermine the answer.

## Example outcomes

A recipient with flexible work may delay departure and request a safer route review.

A specialist already committed to an urgent search may keep moving but request another person to inspect the reported problem.

A worker without the required permission may know about the warning yet remain unable to perform the desired response.

A second person in the same organization may continue with stale information until they receive a separate report, observe a visible consequence or speak with someone who knows.

These differences can later become roleplay material. Apparent disagreement may be explained by timing, responsibility and information scope rather than deception.

## Assignment composition

The report can change the composition requested for future work without changing past facts. A recipient may seek a person with another role, request equipment, postpone until support is available, or choose an alternate route.

Composition remains world-level planning until a selected action requires structured mechanics. A narrative request for a specialist does not invent a Trainer Feature or grant a mechanical permission.

## Reduced implementation

The reduced version uses persistent observations, private knowledge, semantic time, communication latency, schedules, commitments, permissions and event-driven replanning.

No battle is required. A complete scene can consist of a report arriving, one NPC revising a departure plan, another NPC continuing under older information, and the player later discovering why their choices diverged.

## Full implementation

A richer version can place the source observation inside an escort, rescue, retrieval or extraction encounter. The later warning can then affect a second assignment.

The full scene may include reaching an observation point, protecting a carrier, withdrawing after enough evidence is secured, or deciding whether to continue through a hazardous area.

## Reduced fallback for blocked mechanics

When a rich encounter needs unsupported forced movement, reaction windows, phased hazards or special objective policy, resolve the narrative observation through a simpler verified encounter or non-battle field event.

The report, selective delivery and recipient-specific replan can remain unchanged. The adapter does not duplicate the missing PTU rule.
