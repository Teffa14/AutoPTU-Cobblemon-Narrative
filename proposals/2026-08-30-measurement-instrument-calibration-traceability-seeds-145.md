# Ouros Measurement Instrument, Calibration & Traceability Seeds — Pass 145

Status: NON-CANON PROPOSALS. These are reusable narrative candidates, not established Ouros history.

## Use rule

Each seed depends on existing world state. Names, institutions, technologies, units, Pokémon roles, regions and technical practices must be supplied by canon or later authored material.

A discrepancy should first be explained through chronology, scope, configuration, instrument succession, method, reference provenance or record delay. Fraud, sabotage and conspiracy require independent evidence.

Scientific results remain under Science and the relevant domain owner. Battle outcomes never validate a measurement.

## Seed — The Station That Never Moved

Premise:

An archive describes the same long-running observation location with three different device identifiers. A recent researcher assumes the station itself moved three times.

Actual candidate structure:

- one persistent `measurement_point_id`;
- three successive instrument instances;
- one short overlap period between the second and third devices;
- an old public map that labels the site with the first instrument's nickname;
- a later maintenance file that uses only a fleet/inventory identifier.

Possible player work:

- compare installation dates;
- inspect old mounting points;
- recover a field photograph;
- interview former staff;
- identify the overlap record;
- update a research dataset's provenance without changing the underlying location history.

Possible outcomes:

- continuity confirmed;
- one transition remains temporally uncertain;
- two records are proven to refer to another nearby temporary instrument;
- `ACCEPTED_AMBIGUITY` where the archive cannot resolve one gap.

No combat required.

## Seed — The Reading Before the Cleaning

Premise:

A field instrument produced an unusual indication. During the next service visit, staff cleaned accumulated material from the sensor and the reading returned closer to its prior pattern.

Important separation:

`UNUSUAL_READING != SENSOR_FOULING_PROVED`.

Candidate explanations can coexist:

- a genuine short-lived environmental event;
- fouling contributed bias;
- both occurred;
- the comparison method was unsuitable;
- the record cannot resolve the cause.

The pre-cleaning value must remain preserved.

The story can produce a repeat observation job rather than a binary “fix the sensor” quest.

## Seed — The Reference Arrived Late

Premise:

A remote team completed several days of observations before a reference/comparison instrument reached the site.

The early data may remain useful under a narrower authored purpose. Later collocation cannot retroactively create evidence that did not exist at collection time.

Potential consequences:

- Science retains the early records with a quality limitation;
- one institution declines to use them for a stronger claim;
- another still uses them for broad field screening;
- a later expedition repeats only the most consequential observations;
- local residents remember the original result even after the stronger review is published.

## Seed — The Famous Number Was Corrected

Premise:

A measurement became locally famous after appearing in a newspaper, museum display or civic speech. Months later, a bounded instrument review produces a revised result.

Narrative tension comes from historical authenticity rather than deception.

The original publication remains a real artifact of what people knew at the time. Science can issue a correction without destroying the old source.

Possible world consequences:

- the museum adds an annotation instead of replacing the display;
- a teacher uses both versions to discuss how evidence changes;
- a faction selectively quotes the old number but does not necessarily fabricate it;
- a later historian asks why the correction took so long;
- a downstream decision made under the old result remains historically valid as a decision event even if its evidence later changed.

## Seed — Two Instruments, One Case

Premise:

A case file contains two measurements that appear contradictory. They were made by different instruments at different times and under different configurations.

The investigation should determine whether the measurements are actually comparable before judging either one wrong.

Possible resolution states:

- comparable and genuinely different;
- method-not-comparable;
- one result corrected after review;
- both results valid for different scopes;
- provenance incomplete.

Case/Authority retains evidence custody. Science and the relevant domain owner retain interpretation authority.

## Seed — The Community Sensor and the Institutional Monitor

## NPC archetype — The Community Observer With Excellent Local Continuity

Role:

The archetype prevents expertise from being reduced to institutional title alone.

## Faction/institution dynamic — Speed versus provenance

Two internal groups share the same broad mission but prioritize different risks.

One wants rapid provisional results during changing world conditions. The other prioritizes reference checks and carefully bounded claims.

Neither side needs to be corrupt or foolish.

The system can generate disputes over:

- what can be published now;
- what needs follow-up;
- whether a result is adequate for one use but not another;
- how a correction should be communicated.

## Long arc — A Valley Learns What Its Measurements Mean

Status: NON-CANON ARC CANDIDATE.

Act 1: Routine

A monitoring site becomes familiar background. Players see technicians, seasonal visits, equipment cases and recurring Pokémon around the location.

Act 2: Small discrepancy

A visiting comparison instrument differs from the aging primary device under one bounded condition. Science opens a question. Operations continue under documented limitations.

Act 3: Succession

A replacement arrives. Old and new instruments overlap briefly. The old unit leaves for service. The station remains the same place.

Act 4: Retrospective review

A later verification strengthens concern about part of the older record. Researchers identify a candidate review interval. Several measurements receive different outcomes: unchanged, corrected, unsuitable for one use or unresolved.

Act 5: Public consequence

A civic display, old news story or conservation decision had used one of the questioned results. Owners review their own downstream state rather than letting Science rewrite their history.

Act 6: Durable world memory

Months later the new sensor is ordinary. The old instrument may sit in a workshop or museum. One disputed interval remains ambiguous. Players can encounter the consequences naturally in later quests.

## Cross-domain consequences

Measurement Continuity can emit bounded handoffs without taking authority from other layers.

Science may receive a result-lineage revision.

Air Quality or Weather may receive evidence that a monitoring interval needs quality review.

Digital Systems may receive a record-correction or data-import issue.

Material Culture may receive an instrument retirement, repair or museum transition.

Place Reference may preserve a technical landmark name after equipment removal.

Public Information may preserve a corrected notice or publication history.

Case/Authority may consume provenance records as evidence.

No handoff automatically creates the receiving owner's conclusion.

## Canon questions raised by these seeds

These proposals intentionally leave open whether Ouros has formal calibration laboratories, reference standards, citizen monitoring, institutional accreditation, standardized unit systems, recurring technical inspections or specialized measurement professions.

They also leave unnamed every region, laboratory, station, instrument model, technician and Pokémon participant.

Those details require explicit canon approval before any seed becomes world history.
