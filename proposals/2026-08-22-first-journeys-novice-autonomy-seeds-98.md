# Pass 98 Proposals — First Journeys, Novice Autonomy & Supported Independence

Status: NON-CANON proposals. These are candidate Ouros concepts only.

## 30 candidate seeds

1. The First Night Away — a novice plans one overnight stay on a familiar route, then a transport disruption forces a decision between returning, waiting or rerouting.
2. The Route Everyone Calls Easy — locals describe a path as beginner-friendly, but a recent washout has made that reputation obsolete.
3. The Check-In That Never Arrived — a missed message triggers concern; the real problem may be communications rather than the traveler.
4. One Pokémon, No Backup — an early encounter becomes tense because the Trainer has only one available partner and must decide whether disengaging is smarter than pressing on.
5. The Marker Beyond the Orchard — a supervised field task sends a novice only slightly beyond home, but the marker has been moved by unrelated public works.
6. The First Voluntary Retreat — a character returns safely without completing an objective and must face an institution that may value judgment over success.
7. The Starter Who Already Had a Life — a first partner comes from an institutional or community role and has prior routines, relationships and locations that do not disappear after association with a Trainer.
8. Older Beginner — an experienced adult with work and travel history begins a first formal Trainer journey and is competent in many areas while still new to Pokémon battling.
9. The Safe House Is Closed — a planned overnight stop is unavailable, forcing route and timing changes without turning the problem into combat.
10. First Solo Survey — a learner receives permission for a narrow independent survey while larger actions remain supervised.
11. The Friend With Different Permission — two PCs want to travel together, but only one has access to a restricted route.
12. The First Lost Item — an important but ordinary piece of equipment goes missing; recovery teaches provenance, contacts and route memory rather than punishment.
13. The Mentor Is Out of Range — the player can continue, wait or turn back when advice is temporarily unavailable.
14. The Partner Does Not Want the Shortcut — a Pokémon repeatedly refuses one route; the system records the behavior without inventing its reason.
15. The First Return Home — the main event is the debrief, reactions and changes to routines after several days away.
16. The Route Report Nobody Expected — a novice notices a small change that experienced travelers stopped paying attention to.
17. Borrowed Gear, Real Responsibility — a club or institution loans equipment for a first expedition, creating custody and return obligations without granting ownership.
18. First Capture Offered, Not Required — a wild encounter creates a valid capture opportunity, but observation, aid or withdrawal are equally acceptable outcomes.
19. The Journey Paused for Care — a partner needs recovery and the character must decide whether to delay rather than force progress.
20. The Wrong Departure Day — the chosen date collides with a festival, migration, maintenance closure or weather window.
21. The First Stranger Who Helps — a recurring future NPC first appears as an ordinary traveler who provides practical help without becoming an instant mentor.
22. The First Person They Help — a novice discovers another traveler with a small problem and chooses whether to spend time assisting them.
23. The First Bad Map — a learner discovers that an official map is outdated and later sees the correction enter the public record.
24. The Home Route Changes — a route considered part of childhood/local routine changes after construction, ecology or a crisis.
25. The First Rival Without a Battle — two beginners repeatedly reach the same destinations by different methods and begin comparing decisions before ever fighting.
26. The First Battle That Ends Early — weather, withdrawal, surrender or changing priorities end a confrontation without a full knockout sequence when authoritative rules allow it.
27. The First Independent Purchase — a small supply decision teaches budgeting and provenance without becoming an economy simulator.
28. First Journey Archive — an institution preserves route notes, photographs and debriefs from generations of beginners, including mistakes and obsolete advice.
29. The Beginner Who Knows the Town Better — a local novice guides an accomplished visiting Trainer through social/geographic details the visitor does not know.
30. The Second First Journey — a character who once traveled under another role begins a new path as battler, researcher, performer, Ranger-like worker or expedition member.

## Long arc A — The First Hundred Kilometers

Year/phase 1: home district tasks and supervised route legs.

Phase 2: first overnight travel, first missed objective and first return/debrief.

Phase 3: wider permissions based on authored institutional review, not automatic XP.

Phase 4: the character begins choosing destinations instead of only accepting assigned ones.

Phase 5: a later novice asks this character for help, creating a mentorship opportunity without forcing acceptance.

The arc is complete when the character's original journey contract closes, even if their broader life remains open-ended.

## Long arc B — Three Beginners, Three Paths

Three characters begin from the same settlement but enter different trajectories: one League-oriented, one research-oriented and one community/service-oriented.

Their routes overlap periodically. The world remembers different discoveries, contacts, failures and permissions for each character.

No path is the canonical “real Trainer” route.

The arc can later produce club formation, rivalry, professional collaboration or simple divergence.

## Long arc C — The Route That Raised Them

A single nearby route appears repeatedly over several in-world years.

Early phase: supervised errands and first wild observations.

Middle phase: construction, ecological change, transport updates and new hazards alter the route.

Later phase: the original novice returns as an experienced traveler and sees a new generation learning on a place that is physically and institutionally different.

The route becomes a persistent narrative landmark rather than a disposable tutorial map.

## Encounter contract A — First Night Route Incident

FULL intent: a temporary route problem occurs during a first overnight leg. Wild Pokémon may be moving through the same corridor and may attempt withdrawal rather than fight to KO.

Dependencies:
- targeting/footprints/range/LoS — VERIFIED;
- base movement legality — VERIFIED;
- complete movement/interception/forced movement — BLOCKING for moving corridors/withdrawal;
- core calculations — VERIFIED;
- action economy/initiative — VERIFIED;
- full lifecycle — PARTIAL;
- full stateful damage — PARTIAL;
- status lifecycle — PARTIAL;
- terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions — BLOCKING if route/weather state enters battle;
- move-specific behavior — PARTIAL;
- abilities — PARTIAL;
- items — PARTIAL;
- Trainer Features/perks — PARTIAL;
- AI legal-action infrastructure — VERIFIED;
- AI tactical policy — BLOCKING;
- Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback — BLOCKING.

REDUCED version: route closure and weather remain overworld state. Freeze one static safe arena. Any battle is conventional. The important choice comes after the fight: continue, wait, reroute or return.

## Encounter contract B — Starter Partnership Field Test

FULL intent: Trainer and newly associated Pokémon complete a real field objective under distant supervision. The Pokémon may need objective-aware behavior rather than pure combat scoring.

Main blockers: AI tactical policy and adapter/playback. Any exact Move/Ability/Item/Feature used must have its own implementation evidence.

REDUCED version: field objective resolves outside battle. A conventional wild battle may occur separately. Debrief stores observed cooperation without assigning Loyalty.

## Encounter contract C — Return Before Dark

FULL intent: time pressure, route choice and possible withdrawal from an encounter interact.

Main blockers: complete movement, terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions, tactical AI, adapter/playback.

REDUCED version: local time, light, shelter and route feasibility resolve before battle. Any combat uses a static arena with no invented darkness or fatigue effects.

## Promotion gates

Before any seed becomes canon, resolve its actual region, sponsoring institution, travel scope, route state, partner source, custody, communication plan and PTU/Caelo mechanics.

Do not assign a PC's age, parents, guardians, family expectations, legal status, starter origin or emotional reaction unless the player has explicitly authored them.
