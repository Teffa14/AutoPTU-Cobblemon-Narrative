# Marea / Sendero controlled-comparison investigation loop — Pass 271

Status: PROPOSED NARRATIVE / ECOLOGY CANDIDATE. No canon promotion.

## Premise

A player has already observed a change in when a local wild source uses a micro-site. The tempting interpretation is that a recent disturbance caused the schedule shift. The story asks the player to test that explanation rather than rewarding the first plausible correlation.

All named sites, schedules, disturbance details and source behavior in this proposal are placeholders until separately approved.

## Reduced loop

First visit: establish a baseline observation window and record what was actually detectable.

Second visit: observe a changed temporal-use pattern at the focal site after a local disturbance. The journal can record the change and the causal hypothesis, but cannot label the disturbance as the cause.

Comparator attempt: visit another plausible micro-site/window. If visibility, effort, weather or observation method differs materially, the comparison remains insufficient. This is useful narrative failure: the player learned why the test was weak rather than merely failing a hidden roll.

Repeat observation: a familiar observer may redetect the same source more easily after learning its routine. The sighting strengthens the descriptive history but is not treated as a new independent source.

Matched comparison: survey focal and comparator windows with sufficiently similar method/effort/context. If the focal schedule changes while a plausible comparator remains stable and the exposure contrast is documented, the hypothesis can gain narrow causal support.

Follow-up: later nondetection under poor conditions remains a nondetection. It does not erase the source or reduce the authoritative population.

## Player-facing value

The investigation becomes environmental storytelling rather than an omniscient quest marker. The player sees why evidence can be strong, weak or confounded. A field researcher NPC can disagree legitimately with another observer because each has different methods or source lineages.

The same structure can later support questions about resource pulses, misleading cues, habitat modifications, facilitation cascades and shared-substrate pressure without creating a universal ecology score.

## Consequences

A narrowly supported disturbance effect may change a local working hypothesis, management recommendation or follow-up observation plan. It does not create a global species behavior rule.

Removing the disturbance does not guarantee immediate recovery. A later comparison must test recovery separately because different ecological responses can have different horizons.
