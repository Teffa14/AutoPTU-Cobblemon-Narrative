## Candidate 1 — Saltline Repair Morning

Primary sites: ferry landing, repair row
Primary residents: Lia Morn, Mina Cors, Teo Lark, Pia Min
Status: PROPOSED

Premise:
A ferry-landing fixture is recorded as unserviceable after an authored off-screen condition check. One loading position remains restricted while ordinary traffic uses an alternate arrangement.

Player-facing sequence:

- Lia's operating notice identifies the restricted position without claiming a cause she cannot verify;
- Mina explains how the workaround changes embarkation timing;
- Teo inspects/replaces ordinary hardware within his established maintenance role;
- Pia carries the revised notice/record to Tideglass if preservation is warranted;
- a later visit shows replacement material and the normal berth arrangement restored;
- the old part can remain tagged for inspection or teaching rather than disappear.

Why it matters:
This proves persistent repair state with no battle and no new institution.

Battle dependency: none.

Implementation priority: HIGH. Good first slice because it exercises site restriction, work progression, calendar progression and visible recovery using canon roles.

## Cross-system reuse

These proposals intentionally reuse existing layers:

- service dispatch can expose work that genuinely needs help;
- local knowledge determines who knows which condition/cause claim;
- communications carry notices and corrections;
- public memory preserves important past states;
- calendar advances ordinary work;
- relationships may alter access or cooperation, never physical truth;
- quests can structure player participation;
- world-map anchors remain fixed;
- AutoPTU owns any battle;
- Minecraft/Cobblemon only projects the current authoritative world state.
