# The Wall Remembers the Repair — Pass 412

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Canon effect: NONE
Date: 2026-09-10

## Premise

A reused piece of field infrastructure contains several visible repair layers. They may belong to different dates, crews or causes. The current surface is therefore evidence of a sequence, not proof of one culprit or one explanation.

The exact site, owners, repair cause, Pokémon involvement, institutions and dates remain unset until canon review.

## Encounter structure

A player or persistent NPC can inspect ordinary spatial evidence such as patched joins, replaced fasteners, mismatched material, drainage marks, wear direction, relocated fixtures, blocked sight lines or later construction covering an older repair.

An observation records what was actually noticed, where it was noticed and by whom. A hypothesis remains separate from the observation. Another actor can later inspect the same site and reach a different conclusion because they notice another trace or possess different prior knowledge.

A communicated interpretation becomes information received by explicit recipients. It does not become global truth. If nobody observes a particular trace, the world does not retroactively grant that evidence to the investigation.

Possible outcomes include establishing a repair sequence, identifying a missing time interval, discovering that two apparently contradictory reports describe different physical revisions, determining that evidence is insufficient, or generating a follow-up obligation to locate records or another witness.

## Reduced implementation

The reduced version requires no AutoPTU battle.

It can run through persistent site revisions, inspectable evidence records, semantic time, actor knowledge, memory, communication, archive lookup, travel and obligations. Minecraft can present the structure and expose explicitly authored inspection interactions. It must not synthesize PTU Skill results or infer hidden history from block state.

The narrative premise remains intact even if every dangerous change happens between persistent world revisions rather than during tactical play.

## Design value

The site can tell a history without a narrator and without requiring one predetermined criminal explanation. The same location remains useful after the first visit because later repairs, new records or newly communicated knowledge can change what the existing traces mean.
