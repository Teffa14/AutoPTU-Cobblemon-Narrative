# The Message That Asks for New Terms — Pass 442

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Canon effect: NONE
Location assignment: UNRESOLVED
Named NPC assignment: UNRESOLVED

Premise

Two actors already have a valid assistance agreement. The accepted window begins under an adverse condition. The responder decides that the original arrangement should be reopened rather than improvised around.

The important story beat is communication. The responder's private decision does not reach the requester automatically. A message must travel. Until it arrives, the requester may still expect the original arrangement.

Reduced version

The responder records `REQUEST_RENEGOTIATION` after the adverse start assessment.

They author one explicit message tied to the exact commitment and disposition. The payload carries the original window, location, scope and alternative references so the requester can identify which agreement is being discussed.

Possible world-level outcomes after delivery include:

- requester agrees to hear new terms;
- requester refuses and keeps the old expectation;
- responder later offers a new time;
- responder offers remote assistance instead;
- another actor solves the underlying problem before new terms exist;
- the original window expires while negotiation is still unresolved.

None of those outcomes is created by the message itself.

Full encounter version

If later renegotiated terms lead into a hazardous field operation, the same premise can support an escorted survey, retrieval under access restrictions, protection during inspection, containment support or extraction.

The objective remains the negotiated work. Combat, if present, is one possible resolution pressure rather than the narrative definition of the scene.

Narrative consequences

A delivered request can preserve goodwill because the responder communicated rather than disappearing, but no automatic relationship delta is implied.

A failed or delayed channel can produce a credible misunderstanding because each actor possesses different information.

A later replacement proposal must preserve the old commitment as history instead of rewriting it.

If the underlying problem disappears before new terms exist, the renegotiation message still remains part of the actors' history.

Open canon questions

No region, institution, route, species, permit system, NPC, faction or social consequence is established here. The proposal also does not decide who is normally responsible for initiating replacement terms or how long an unresolved renegotiation remains open.
