# The Pickup That Failed Before the Save — Pass 357

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-08

## Premise

A field team has an accepted request and a valid authorization to collect a survey instrument from a service point. The receiving worker arrives during the authorized window, but the provider is absent. The worker records the failed pickup and leaves to handle another obligation.

The session ends before the team can retry.

After restart, the authorization may still be active. The failed attempt must also still exist.

## Investigation surface

The player can reconstruct the accepted request, exact instrument, authorization, intended participants, authored pickup site, failed attempt time, failure outcome and observation provenance. If a later retry succeeds, both events remain visible: the earlier attempt failed and the later transfer succeeded.

A legacy save that predates attempt persistence has no such record. Ouros must present that as missing history rather than infer a no-show from current custody or NPC memory.

## Consequences

Ordinary absence can lead to a retry, reschedule, alternate provider, communication attempt or rerouted work. Explicit refusal has a different consequence because Pass 344 treats it as terminal for that authorization.

Repeated failures can later become evidence for staffing, access-hour or process changes, but this proposal does not assign blame or automatically alter relationships.

## Reduced version

No structured combat is required. The case uses semantic time, Pass 342 requests, Pass 343 handoff authorization, Pass 344 attempts, Pass 357 persistence, travel, communication, memory/belief and selective replanning.

## Mechanically rich version

A later retry may occur while the pickup location is temporarily obstructed by wild Pokémon or another world event. The narrative objective is to complete or safely abandon the pickup according to current conditions rather than necessarily defeat every opponent.

Capability dependencies are explicit:

Targeting / footprints / range / LoS is required for exact tactical target/perception rules.

Base movement legality is required for ordinary approach and repositioning.

Complete movement including push/pull/knockback/interception/forced movement is required only if the encounter permits physical interception, rescue or forced displacement.

Core calculations and action economy / initiative are required for ordinary structured PTU resolution. A tactical pickup/drop/handoff action still requires its own legal-action contract.

Full turn / round lifecycle is required for a round-bound pickup window or delayed arrival.

Full stateful damage pipeline is required if battle damage matters to actors or equipment.

Status lifecycle is required for persistent battle conditions.

Terrain / weather / hazards / zones / reactions is required only for the exact families authored into the obstruction.

Move-specific behavior, Abilities, Items and Trainer Features/perks remain individually gated.

AI legal-action infrastructure needs explicit cargo/handoff actions if those become tactical choices.

AI tactical policy remains required for retry, protect-carrier, delivery-first, retreat and disengage-after-objective behavior.

Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback may display authoritative events but cannot decide that proximity means pickup, attempt or custody transfer.

## Canon boundary

No named NPC, institution, place, instrument assignment or historical event in this proposal becomes Ouros canon without separate approval. Existing Marea or other local fixtures may be used only as regression bindings.
