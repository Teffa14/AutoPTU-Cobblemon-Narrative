# Marea Community Education and Field-School Seeds

Status: PROPOSED / NOT CANON
Pass: 192
Date: 2026-09-01

These seeds use the canon Loma Clara field school, Jo Venn, and existing Marea residents. They do not define Caelo schooling law, ages, credentials, or PTU advancement.

## 1. The Note That Changed

Jo prepares a field-school session using an older public Mirador observation summary. Nerea has since issued a revision that changes one interpretation while preserving the underlying observations.

Play:
- retrieve both versions;
- identify what changed;
- preserve the old handout as historical material;
- update the lesson without pretending the earlier class never happened.

Useful continuity:
Learners who attended the older session may still repeat the earlier interpretation. Correcting them is a world event, not a retcon.

Dependencies: none beyond ordinary Narrative records and presentation.

## 2. Oren's Prevention Hour

Oren visits the field school for a narrow prevention session tied to his already-canon clinic role.

The lesson can cover only practices verified as ordinary care/prevention knowledge. It cannot teach a mechanical Medic Feature unless PTU authority and implementation explicitly support that effect.

Quest surface: CLASS / SETTLEMENT / CHARACTER.

## 3. One Plot, Three Records

Jo, Alba, and Brin present three records concerning the same cultivation period:
- one producer's field note;
- a cooperative intake record;
- a teaching summary.

The learning objective is source comparison. The correct outcome may be that the evidence does not support a single exact explanation.

No invented Agriculture roll is required.

## 4. Pia Teaches the Copy, Taro Reviews the Claim

Pia demonstrates how a public copy is prepared and circulated. Taro retains review authority over historical interpretation.

The useful distinction is procedural knowledge versus substantive authority.

A learner may correctly reproduce a document while still being unable to approve its caption.

## 5. The Equipment Lesson With the Missing Step

Teo demonstrates ordinary maintenance on a non-mechanical field instrument. A printed checklist omits a step present in the current workshop procedure.

Possible outcomes:
- Teo catches the discrepancy;
- a learner notices it first;
- nobody notices until a later routine inspection.

The lesson record and the procedure correction remain separate.

## 6. Sela's Rules Without the Battle

Sela visits the field school to explain how Bruma Battle Yard challenge contracts are read before a match.

No battle is required. The session can teach where legal conditions are published, which facts belong to the contract, and why a public result does not authorize unrelated relationship conclusions.

This seed reuses existing battle-institution design without exercising unverified combat content.

## 7. The Question Jo Cannot Answer

A learner asks a question about regional law, Pokémon behavior, or historical origin outside Jo's evidence.

The successful educational outcome is Jo recording the question and routing it to the appropriate source rather than inventing an answer.

This seed reinforces CORRECT_UNCERTAINTY as useful learning behavior.

## 8. The Lesson Happens Without the Player

The player returns to Loma Clara after a scheduled field-school session has already occurred.

Evidence in the world:
- revised board;
- used materials;
- a question awaiting answer;
- one corrected handout;
- a note about a future follow-up.

The world advances without requiring player presence.

## 9. Mirador Guest Session

Nerea and Ema lead a source-aware lesson on how an observation moves from field note to reviewed record.

Important boundary:
They teach process and uncertainty. Attendance does not grant Researcher class content, Pokémon Education ranks, or authority to publish Mirador findings.

## 10. The Old Learner Returns

A resident who attended an earlier session returns with a practical question after encountering a similar situation elsewhere.

The system may surface prior learning evidence, but it must not assume perfect recall. The returned learner may remember one distinction correctly and another incorrectly.

This gives the field school persistence across time without inventing a formal alumni system.

## 11. Open Field-School Session at Glass Bend

Jo holds a public field lesson near a familiar section of Sendero del Vidrio. A wild incident develops before the group completes the exercise.

Full version:
Learners, instructor, route geometry, wild actors, withdrawal corridor, and tactical pressure coexist in one mechanically rich scene.

Required permanent capability families:
- targeting/footprints/range/LoS;
- base movement legality;
- complete movement including push/pull/knockback/interception/forced movement when displacement/corridor protection enters BattleSpec;
- core calculations;
- action economy/initiative;
- full turn/round lifecycle;
- full stateful damage pipeline;
- status lifecycle where selected content uses it;
- terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions if route conditions become tactical;
- move-specific behavior;
- abilities;
- items;
- Trainer Features/perks when present;
- AI legal-action infrastructure;
- AI tactical policy;
- Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback support.

Current disposition: FULL VERSION BLOCKED.

Reduced version:
Narrative resolves the lesson interruption and moves learners to safety first. Any remaining wild threat becomes a separate audited battle on stable terrain. Battle outputs are limited to immediate tactical facts such as `IMMEDIATE_ROUTE_THREAT_WITHDREW`. Jo's later educational record is based on observed behavior, not on victory itself.

## 12. A Lesson With No Single Answer

Taro, Nerea, or Jo presents two plausible interpretations supported by different evidence quality.

The point is to distinguish:
- fact;
- source claim;
- inference;
- uncertainty;
- unresolved question.

A learner can succeed by explaining why the issue remains unresolved.

## Longer arc: What Marea Teaches Next

This arc follows the field school across ordinary seasons rather than building toward a grand academy plot.

Potential progression:
- Jo reuses a stable lesson;
- a source revision requires correction;
- Oren or Nerea contributes a guest session;
- an old learner returns with a question;
- a lesson occurs while the player is elsewhere;
- a field session is interrupted by a real event;
- materials and teaching practice improve afterward.

Persistent physical traces can include revised boards, dated handouts, borrowed maps, learner questions, source references, replaced checklists, and a growing shelf of archived lesson versions.

The narrative premise remains small-scale and local. No graduation, license, school rank, or regional educational bureaucracy becomes canon without separate evidence.
