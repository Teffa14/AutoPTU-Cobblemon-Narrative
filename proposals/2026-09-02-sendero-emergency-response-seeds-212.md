# Sendero Emergency Response Seeds 212

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-02

## Purpose

Apply scan 212 to existing Marea Interior people, Sendero del Vidrio and established field-work structures. These seeds do not establish the cause of Thin Delivery Season, create a new faction, assign new Pokémon species, or add unverified PTU mechanics.

## Warning Before Certainty

A route observer reports a physical condition near one Sendero anchor that may make normal passage unsafe. Mara can issue a temporary operational restriction before the cause is understood. The restriction records who observed the condition, what was actually seen, which edge or site is affected, why access is limited, and what must be checked before reopening.

The player can carry the warning to an existing destination, confirm that another resident received it, inspect a second anchor under already-authorized world interactions, or help document which travelers have already passed the affected point. The warning is an administrative world-state object. It does not create PTU difficult terrain, weather, damage or status effects by itself.

Reduced implementation: server-authored `INCIDENT_RECORD` plus `ACCESS_RESTRICTION`, explicit acknowledgement writes and ordinary Minecraft traversal around the restricted edge.

## One Name Still Unconfirmed

After a field window closes, the response ledger confirms several known workers safe but leaves one existing field participant unconfirmed because the last authenticated visit record predates the incident. The episode goal is to resolve accountability rather than defeat an enemy.

The player can follow the last confirmed site, check a handoff record, ask an existing resident with relevant local knowledge, or reach the last work area. Finding the person updates only their accountability state. Their absence cannot be treated as evidence for Thin Delivery or for the incident's cause.

No new named NPC should be created solely to occupy the missing-person slot. Use an established resident only when current schedules and canon make the participation valid; otherwise keep this seed dormant.

## Wild Patient, Open Exit

A persistent wild Pokémon is found inside the affected area after access has been restricted. Welfare and evacuation are separate objectives. If the canonical lower-shelf Fletchling is eligible by its current authoritative location/state, it may be observed or assisted as that same individual; this proposal does not move or clone it for dramatic convenience.

The player can create space for departure, report the sighting, request qualified field assistance, or disengage. Helping a wild Pokémon reach a safe route produces no ownership, party membership, friendship score, obedience or capture right. A later re-observation must use persistent identity evidence rather than species alone.

Reduced implementation: authenticated wild identity plus field-assistance record and explicit release/departure transition. No forced movement is simulated unless the battle engine authorizes it.

## Reopen Only After the Check

Once the immediate response ends, a route does not automatically return to normal. Mara or another canonically appropriate field worker needs an inspection record that addresses the original restriction condition. The player can assist with documentation, carry the result, or discover that only part of the path is ready.

A reopening creates a dated history: restriction issued, response performed, inspection made, restriction lifted or narrowed. Later residents can remember the disruption, and Tideglass can retain both warning and reopening records without rewriting either.

This gives emergencies aftermath instead of treating them as disposable quest instances.

## Full encounter candidate: Last Crossing Before Closure

Intended version: an authenticated incident is already active when several legitimate objectives converge at the seasonal crossing. One actor must leave the affected zone, another needs a route or observation handoff, a persistent wild presence may complicate movement, and the access window is narrowing because of an authored world process. The player may need to protect movement lanes, disengage, reposition participants or choose which objective receives immediate attention.

The incident cause remains outside the battle outcome. If environmental pressure has mechanical effects, those effects must come from verified terrain/weather/hazard/zone/reaction contracts. If an actor is pushed, pulled, knocked back or intercepted, complete movement authority belongs to AutoPTU. If a Move, Ability, item or Trainer Feature prevents or changes displacement, the Java engine must emit and reconcile the semantic result; Minecraft renders it.

The rich version depends on all 16 permanent capability families: targeting/footprints/range/LoS; base movement legality; complete movement including push/pull/knockback/interception/forced movement; core calculations; action economy/initiative; full turn/round lifecycle; full stateful damage pipeline; status lifecycle; terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions; move-specific behavior; abilities; items; Trainer Features/perks; AI legal-action infrastructure; AI tactical policy; Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback support.

## Reduced encounter: Account, Warn, Reopen

This preserves the emergency premise without simulating a tactical disaster. The incident begins from an explicit authored observation. The player carries or acknowledges a warning, checks one or more known accountability records, travels using ordinary world geometry, performs only already-authorized inspections, and returns enough evidence for the restriction authority to lift, narrow or keep the closure.

Optional wild combat remains a separate BattleSpec. The response layer may read only audited battle reconciliation fields. Victory cannot establish that a route is safe, an evacuated Pokémon trusts the player, an environmental cause ended, or an NPC received the warning.

## Capability readiness boundary

Current evidence supports targeting/footprints/range/LoS, base movement legality, core calculations, action economy/initiative and AI legal-action infrastructure within their audited contracts. Complete movement remains partial despite new forced-movement prevention semantics. Full turn/round lifecycle, stateful damage, status lifecycle, move behavior, abilities, items and Trainer Features remain partial. Terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions and AI tactical policy still block the rich encounter. Adapter/playback support remains partial/blocking end-to-end.

## Canon boundary

Every episode title, incident record, restriction and consequence in this file remains proposed. Existing people, locations, responsibilities, wild identities and prior records keep their established canon. Thin Delivery Season remains unresolved. No weather event, disaster type, Injury, status, Skill check, Move effect, Ability effect, equipment effect or Trainer Feature is canonized here.
