# Marea / lower Sendero indirect-sign exploration loop — Pass 249

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Canon effect: NONE

## Premise

A player revisits lower Sendero during a period when disturbance pressure suppresses direct Fletchling presentation. The ecology still indicates local use of the shelf, so the presentation arbiter can expose weak indirect evidence instead of forcing a Pokemon entity into view.

This proposal deliberately avoids defining the still-unresolved physical forage resource.

## Reduced version

The reduced version contains no tactical battle.

During a suppressed direct-presentation window, the player can receive a fixture-only acoustic presence cue. The cue becomes one Pass 240 observation with one provenance root. It supports only a low-confidence claim that Fletchling may be present locally.

A relay from an NPC who heard about the same observation keeps the same root and therefore does not independently corroborate it.

If the player returns during a quieter eligible window, Ouros may select an already-counted Fletchling source, reserve a Pass 239 lease and then request direct Cobblemon materialization. Seeing the Pokemon directly can create a genuinely new observation root.

The exploration premise survives if no battle ever occurs: indirect evidence led to a better-timed revisit and a direct sighting of a living member of the existing population.

## Full version

If the direct sighting turns into an attempted pursuit, interception or contested capture, Pass 242 decides whether to remain overworld, open AutoPTU, use a reduced supported version or block unsupported tactical behavior.

The full version may include a Fletchling retreating through eligible microhabitats while the player tries to maintain sight or cut off a route. Nothing about that behavior is canonized until species behavior and exact map geometry are approved.

If the current tactical engine cannot support the intended pursuit, the reduced outcome is simple: the Fletchling leaves direct presentation, Ouros records the observation, and the player can continue investigation from later signs or another projection window. The Pokemon does not need to be duplicated or teleported into a tactical encounter to preserve the story.

## Uncorrelated native entity case

A deliberately adversarial implementation branch is part of this proposal. If Cobblemon creates a native Fletchling entity in the same area without an Ouros lease, it cannot be assumed to represent another population member.

The correct narrative outcome is quarantine semantics: no population write, no persistent identity promotion, no automatic observation of a known individual and no AutoPTU manifest entry. The exact adapter operation used to prevent interaction remains an implementation question.

## Player-facing evidence design

The exploration loop should prefer several independent routes to the same broad ecological conclusion rather than one hidden trigger. Candidate roots can include direct visual observation, a separately generated acoustic cue, and an independently sourced field report.

The important property is provenance independence. Rendering one sound twice, repeating one rumor through several NPCs or reloading the same chunk cannot manufacture corroboration.

## Capability dependencies

Reduced version:

Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback support: PARTIAL/BLOCKING and directly required.

No AutoPTU battle family is required.

Full pursuit version:

Targeting/footprints/range/LoS is required if maintaining tactical sight or range becomes adjudicated. Base movement legality is required. Complete movement is required when interception, forced movement or advanced movement interactions matter. Action economy/initiative and full turn/round lifecycle are required if the pursuit becomes turn-structured. AI legal-action infrastructure and AI tactical policy are required for autonomous tactical evasion. Adapter/playback support is required end-to-end.

Terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions, damage, statuses, move-specific behavior, abilities, items and Trainer Features/perks become dependencies only when the authored encounter invokes those exact mechanics.

## Canon questions intentionally left open

The proposal does not decide the physical forage resource, the exact call behavior of Marea Fletchling, the exact lower Sendero microhabitat geometry, or whether individual birds can be identified acoustically. Those require separate canon or research decisions.
