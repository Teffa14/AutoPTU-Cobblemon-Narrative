# Proposal — The Warning That Never Came — Pass 301

Status: PROPOSED / NOT CANON
Date: 2026-09-06

Premise: a named traveler enters a route after a dispatcher learned that a crossing had become unsafe. The traveler had previously registered for route-safety notices, creating a durable disclosure expectation. The dispatcher had a real communication opportunity and chose silence.

The important mystery is not merely whether the route was dangerous. Later investigation can separate five facts: the dispatcher possessed the closure evidence; a disclosure expectation existed; the expectation was active for this traveler; the communication decision was silent; and no warning receipt exists. That supports a trust consequence without declaring that the dispatcher lied or acted maliciously.

Possible motives remain open. The dispatcher may have protected a secret, prioritized another obligation, feared retaliation, believed the danger tolerable, been overwhelmed by another commitment, or intentionally accepted the risk. Future evidence can change how other NPCs judge the same breach.

Reduced executable version: a direct route observation creates the dispatcher’s private claim. A role, promise, faction duty or explicit registration creates `DisclosureExpectation`. Pass 300 evaluates the communication opportunity after Pass 301 raises the real cost of silence. If SILENT still wins, Pass 301 records an observable expectation breach. The traveler’s directional trust can fall once, with provenance. Subsequent agenda/replanning can respond through existing world-agent systems. No tactical battle is required.

Full encounter version: the missing warning leads the traveler into a storm-damaged ravine where rescue becomes urgent. The player can reach the location by ordinary route planning, investigate damaged markers and help evacuate the traveler. The full intended scene may use line of sight through elevation, difficult terrain, forced displacement from water or wind, weather-driven zones, reactions/interception during rescue, damage/status consequences from hazards, Pokémon Moves or Abilities that alter terrain, Trainer Features that interrupt actions, autonomous tactical rescue choices and Minecraft playback.
