# Marea Identity, Credential and Delegated Authority Seeds — Pass 201

Status: PROPOSED CONTENT / NOT CANON
Date: 2026-09-02
Depends on: `design/identity-credential-delegated-authority-continuity-layer.md`

These seeds use only established Marea locations, institutions and residents unless a visitor or historical actor is explicitly left anonymous/proposed. They do not create a civil-ID system, government, police authority, Trainer licensing regime or external settlement.

## 1. The Access Note Outlives the Visit — recommended first slice

A repeat visitor returns to Tideglass or Mirador. A previous temporary-access note is still physically present in a copied schedule or old folder. Pia recognizes the visitor from the earlier visit, but the old window has ended.

Play:
- preserve the visitor's stable identity history;
- show that recognition and current access are separate;
- verify the new purpose;
- Taro or Nerea decides any new bounded grant using existing institutional responsibility;
- preserve the stale note as historical evidence rather than silently editing it.

No battle. No new NPC is required if the visitor is represented as a proposed generic actor during prototyping. No legal credential needs to be invented.

Primary lesson: `PRIOR_ACCESS != CURRENT_ACCESS`.

## 2. Same Name in Two Tideglass Records

Two older records contain the same display name. Their dates and contexts are compatible with either one person appearing twice or two different people sharing a name.

Pia must not merge them. Taro preserves separate identity candidates until stronger provenance appears.

Useful later consequence:
a third document can establish continuity for one record without proving anything about the other.

## 3. Lia's One-Task Delegation

During a busy unloading window, Lia asks another known resident to record one specific arrival or relay one berth change.

The delegate can perform that named action. Lia retains dock coordination responsibility. A copied instruction that survives later becomes useful evidence of why the delegate acted.

No generalized dock rank is created.

## 4. Ema May Observe, Nerea Must Review

Ema is authorized to execute a specific Mirador observation procedure. A visitor incorrectly assumes that this means Ema can approve an interpretation or publish a station conclusion.

The scene distinguishes:
- field-task assignment;
- observation authorship;
- review authority;
- publication/circulation.

It integrates directly with Mirador provenance and pass 200 information circulation.

## 5. Pia Carries the Copy, Taro Owns the Archive Decision

Pia legitimately carries an archive copy to another local institution. Someone asks her to authorize access to a related restricted or unresolved source.

Her courier role supports possession/custody of the copy but does not automatically grant Taro's decision authority.

The request can remain pending instead of forcing Pia to become omnipotent or refuse for invented legal reasons.

## 6. Pickup for Teo

A repaired ordinary field instrument is ready. The owner/requestor cannot collect it and sends a known person instead.

Pass 198 owns the work order. Pass 201 records delegation to collect. Existing custody/ownership systems remain authoritative for the item itself.

Possible states:
- delegation confirmed before arrival;
- claim presented but verification still needed;
- pickup succeeds;
- pickup waits for confirmation;
- requestor cancels or replaces the delegate.

No currency or Item mechanic needs to be introduced.

## 7. The Familiar Battle Yard Face

A visiting Trainer previously observed or participated in a Bruma Battle Yard session. On return, Jace recognizes them.

Recognition can speed ordinary social interaction but does not establish:
- an active booking;
- ranking;
- rival status;
- League standing;
- a right to use the yard immediately;
- a mechanical reward.

Sela still owns the current session decision.

## 8. The Genuine Credential from Somewhere Else

A visitor presents an apparently genuine professional or Trainer-related credential from outside currently canonized geography.

The artifact can be genuine while its local meaning remains unresolved. Marea actors may accept the visitor's stated background as an attributed claim and only grant the narrow access justified by local evidence.

This seed deliberately does not name the external institution or settlement until canon expands outward.

## 9. The Old Uniform

A resident or visitor still possesses clothing, a badge-like object or other visible marker associated with an earlier role.

The object is authentic as an artifact. The current role may have ended or changed.

The scene teaches that visual presentation and active authority require separate records. It also gives the Minecraft adapter a concrete stale-projection test.

## 10. Market Proxy Collection

Ivo has a reserved or expected lot. Another person arrives saying they were asked to collect it.

Pass 199 owns offer/reservation/transaction history. Pass 201 only decides whether the person has sufficient authority for the pickup action.

Payment, ownership and commercial liability remain outside this layer.

## 11. One Surname, No Family Shortcut

A new document or visitor repeats a surname already held by a Marea resident. The system must not manufacture a family link.

This directly exercises an existing canon rule already demonstrated by Alba Serrat/Ivo Serrat, Pia/Taro Min and Jace/Sela Orrin relationship cautions.

A later explicit canon promotion may establish kinship. Until then, identity similarity is merely a research lead.

## 12. Duplicate Actor at the Dock

A Minecraft/Cobblemon projection bug briefly causes two visual instances of the same persistent NPC to exist around the ferry landing.

Narrative must bind both projections to the same canonical actor ID or reject the duplicate. The bug cannot create:
- two Lia Morns;
- duplicate observations;
- duplicate authority;
- contradictory departure histories;
- a clone plot.

This is primarily an adapter test packaged as world-continuity protection.

## 13. The Disguise Question

A future authored scene contains a person deliberately trying to pass as somebody else or as a harmless bystander.

Narrative stores:
- underlying actor ID when the GM/system legitimately knows it;
- presented identity claim;
- observer-specific beliefs after adjudication;
- any authority record that the impersonator attempts to exploit.

If detection is contested, PTU Guile/Perception or another authoritative Caelo rule must adjudicate it. Narrative does not compare custom identity scores.

This seed remains mechanically contingent and should not be an early implementation slice.

## Longer arc — Who Speaks for Whom

Across ordinary Marea activity, small cases accumulate:

- Lia delegates a dock check;
- Pia carries material without gaining Taro's whole authority;
- Ema performs fieldwork that Nerea later reviews;
- a repeat visitor's old access expires;
- an outside credential is genuine but only partly understood;
- a proxy collects a repaired object;
- stale visual markers persist after roles change.

The arc gradually teaches that institutions operate through people and handoffs rather than generic quest flags. It can later support larger factions, expeditions and regional governance without prematurely defining that governance now.

No global `authority_score`, `trust_score` or universal credential tier is required.

## Mechanically rich concept — Authorized Recovery at Mirador Perimeter

Premise:
A Mirador instrument package or bounded record bundle must be recovered from a route-side observation point after ordinary field conditions make retrieval inconvenient. Nerea authorizes one actor to retrieve the package and another to carry it back. Wild activity creates an immediate withdrawal problem.

Narrative stakes:
- identity of the authorized actors;
- exact task scope;
- package custody;
- time window;
- route purpose;
- later return/handoff.

The battle does not decide any of those records.

### Full intended version

If the scene uses protected withdrawal, corridor control, Interception, displacement, tactical weather/terrain, reactions or objective-aware wild behavior, dependencies are:

- targeting/footprints/range/LoS;
- base movement legality;
- complete movement including push/pull/knockback/interception/forced movement;
- core calculations;
- action economy/initiative;
- full turn/round lifecycle;
- full stateful damage pipeline;
- status lifecycle where selected content requires it;
- terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions;
- move-specific behavior;
- abilities;
- items;
- Trainer Features/perks;
- AI legal-action infrastructure;
- AI tactical policy;
- Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback support.

Current readiness: full version BLOCKED while required partial/blocking families remain incomplete.

### Reduced runnable premise

Narrative verifies delegation before combat and moves noncombatants/package semantics to a safe state. If one immediate wild actor still blocks withdrawal, AutoPTU receives one ordinary audited battle on stable geometry using only verified/audited content.

Allowed handoffs:
- `IMMEDIATE_RECOVERY_ROUTE_CLEAR`
- `IMMEDIATE_ROUTE_THREAT_WITHDREW`
- `IMMEDIATE_TEAM_CAN_WITHDRAW`

The battle cannot establish:
- identity;
- credential validity;
- delegation validity;
- package ownership;
- permanent Mirador access;
- employment/membership;
- disciplinary fault;
- local recognition of an external credential;
- future authority.

## Promotion priorities

First implementation target: `The Access Note Outlives the Visit`.

It exercises stable visitor identity, stale physical projection, expiry, current verification and new scoped access without battle or new regional law.

Second target: `Ema May Observe, Nerea Must Review`.

It reinforces already-canon roles and prevents operational competence from becoming unlimited institutional authority.

Third target: `Pickup for Teo`.

It connects service-request continuity to delegation and custody while keeping economy and Item mechanics separate.
