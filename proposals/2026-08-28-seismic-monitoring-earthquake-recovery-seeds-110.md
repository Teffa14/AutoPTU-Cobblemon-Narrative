# Ouros Seismic Monitoring & Recovery Seeds — Pass 110

Status: NON-CANON PROPOSALS. These are reusable situation seeds, not established lore.

All concepts preserve provenance separately from world truth. None assumes a canon seismic agency, technology, region, magnitude scale or species-level sensing ability.

## The Alert Reached the Port First

A settlement receives contradictory stories about when an event warning was issued. The port halted one activity before the central square heard anything.

The investigation resolves alert authorization, communications handoff and receipt timestamps separately. The mystery can end with every witness being correct within their own scope.

## The Station Was Offline, the Village Was Not Blind

A monitoring node failed before a seismic event, but residents, field workers and Pokémon behavior reports still produced useful observations.

The story asks which evidence can reconstruct the event without treating any witness as an omniscient sensor.

## The Road Reopened Before the Archive Updated

Travel resumes along a route while an old public record still lists it as restricted.

The conflict comes from asynchronous owner systems: inspection and reopening happened, but the information layer did not catch up.

## The Pokémon Left the Ridge Yesterday

Several people remember a wild collective leaving a familiar area before a tremor. Some residents call it prediction. Others think another disturbance caused both behaviors.

Ouros preserves observations and beliefs separately. The quest can investigate food, habitat, noise, construction, weather, earlier tremors or unknown causes without granting a species-wide supernatural rule.

## The Old Crack Is Older Than the Event

A visible crack becomes evidence in public debate after an earthquake. Archival photographs show that part of it already existed.

The narrative question shifts from "what caused the crack" to "what changed, when, and what evidence supports each claim."

## The Temporary Sensor Became Permanent

A temporary observation post established after a damaging event becomes socially important. Travelers use it as a landmark, workers maintain a path to it and local Pokémon become familiar presences around the site.

Years later, removing or relocating the equipment affects more than monitoring.

## The Bell Rang After the Shaking

Residents remember a warning bell, but records show it sounded after local shaking had already begun.

The bell may still have helped people elsewhere. The story distinguishes detection, delivery latency, spatial scope and local experience rather than declaring the system a failure or success with one flag.

## The House Passed Inspection, the Lane Did Not

A residence receives permission for limited access while the street serving it remains restricted.

Housing, Roads and Maintenance hold different valid states. The player may help coordinate a safe temporary routine without changing any owner's authority.

## The Observatory Recorded Nothing at Noon

A widely felt tremor has no record at the closest monitoring site because that node was offline or delayed. A more distant station and several human reports contain usable evidence.

Absence of a record is not evidence of absence.

## The Shop Reopened, the Delivery Route Did Not

A business is structurally usable but its normal logistics corridor remains restricted. Temporary supply patterns change who visits the district and which other shops benefit.

The earthquake becomes a trade and neighborhood story after the immediate crisis ends.

## Five Times, One Event

Five records show different timestamps: first felt motion, first sensor detection, alert authorization, end of local shaking and first inspection.

The puzzle is solved by identifying what each timestamp actually measures. No hidden truth score is required.

## Four Maps, Three Revisions

Several maps place the same event differently because estimates were updated as observations arrived.

The oldest map may be historically authentic without being the best current interpretation. Public memory, archive preservation and scientific revision can all remain valid at once.

## The Second Map Under the Station

Exploration concept.

An old monitoring building was expanded several times. Its surface plan, maintenance plan and archival plan disagree because each describes a different era. A sealed lower records room may contain observations from a historic event that later reconstruction obscured.

Current executable version:

Players investigate maps, labels, repair records, photographs and accessible passages. Access state changes through noncombat inspection and records work. Any battle occurs in a reviewed static area.

Full future version may support dynamic structural restrictions, falling debris, timed evacuation or aftershock zones only when the exact engine families exist.

## A Valley Learns Its Tremors

Long-term arc.

Phase one establishes ordinary routes, businesses, households, monitoring habits and wild-Pokémon routines before any major event.

A seismic episode then creates several independent consequences. A road closes, one monitoring node fails, a temporary observation point appears, some households relocate temporarily and several actors disagree about what happened first.

Recovery occurs by owner system rather than by one global percentage. Rescue may finish before road access. A building may reopen while a service remains unavailable. A detour can become economically important. A temporary sensor can become a landmark.

Months or years later, a smaller event makes old records relevant. The story can compare what the valley learned, what it forgot and which temporary adaptations became permanent.

No `seismic_recovery_level` is used.

## Encounter seed — Monitoring Ridge Withdrawal

Full intended form:

A live or recently renewed seismic episode complicates withdrawal from a ridge. Protecting staff and keeping a corridor open can require Intercept, forced movement, generalized reactions, active unstable-ground zones, objective-aware tactical AI and semantic playback.

Required permanent families include targeting/footprints/range/LoS; base movement legality; complete movement including push/pull/knockback/interception/forced movement; core calculations; action economy/initiative; full turn/round lifecycle when timed changes exist; damage/status if hazards inflict them; terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions; move-specific behavior; abilities; items; Trainer Features/perks; AI legal-action infrastructure; AI tactical policy; and adapter/playback.

Reduced current form:

Active shaking ends before combat. Staff and equipment withdraw first. Unsafe slope cells are excluded. The fight occurs in a static reviewed clearing with explicit combatants. Victory secures the immediate approach only.

## Encounter seed — Post-Event Plaza Perimeter

Full intended form:

A controlled plaza must remain accessible while assessment work continues nearby. Loose debris, route protection, interception and secondary shaking could change tactical priorities.

Reduced current form:

The battle takes place after evacuation and immediate hazard isolation. Buildings and debris are noninteractive scenery outside the tactical contract. Winning protects the perimeter but does not certify or reopen any structure.

## Encounter seed — Sensor Vault Diversion

Full intended form:

A conflict occurs while specialists try to reach a monitoring vault. A future implementation could involve timed access, technical-object protection or an after-event environmental phase.

Reduced current form:

The vault is inert and excluded from the grid. The battle occurs outside. Records, repairs and data validation happen afterward through their owner systems.

## Consequence palette

Possible persistent consequences include changed detours, inspection backlogs, temporary households, business relocation, new observation posts, archived contradictory reports, altered wildlife routes, public arguments about cause, repaired landmarks, new emergency routines and old infrastructure becoming heritage.

None of these consequences requires the earthquake itself to remain an active tactical hazard.

## Explicit exclusions

Do not generate earthquake damage rolls, aftershock schedules, structural-collapse chances, species-level predictive behavior, automatic Ground-type interactions, Minecraft block destruction as damage, or real-world seismic measurements unless future canon and PTU/Caelo implementation explicitly govern them.
