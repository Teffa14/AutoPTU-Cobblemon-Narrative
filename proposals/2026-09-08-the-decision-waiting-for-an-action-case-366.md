# The Decision Waiting for an Action — Pass 366

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-08

## Canon boundary

This case is a reusable Ouros candidate. Names, institution, exact location, species, rewards and regional placement remain unset. Nothing here overrides existing canon or PTU/Caelo rules.

## Premise

Two field teams need the same survey instrument during overlapping windows. A historical conflict has been assessed. A scheduler with valid authority selects the proposed later window for one team. The world is saved immediately after the decision and before Pass 349 applies any operational displacement.

After recovery, the allocation decision still exists with its authority and conflict provenance. The reservation ledger has not been silently rewritten. A field technician who only sees the currently operative booking can continue preparing the old plan. A scheduler can correctly remember having made the decision. Both behaviors remain rational because decision, application, communication and private knowledge have separate owners.

## Player-facing investigation

The player can discover that a decision exists but has not yet become operational state. Evidence may include the historical conflict record, authority check, allocation resolution, unchanged booking and communications that have or have not reached the affected actors. The interesting question is where the causal chain stopped.

Possible outcomes include delivering the missing instruction, asking the scheduler to confirm or revise the choice, helping one team switch instruments, preserving the original booking if the decision is withdrawn by an authorized process, or allowing the conflict to proceed into the later Pass 349 application/notice chain. No automatic blame or relationship penalty follows from the mismatch alone.

## Reduced implementation version

The reduced version requires no AutoPTU battle. Semantic time, reservations, reschedule lineage, conflict admission, allocation authority, allocation resolution, communications, private knowledge, travel and checkpoint recovery carry the entire premise.

The checkpoint must preserve the decision without executing Pass 349 during restore. This is the core narrative invariant.

## Mechanically rich version

One team has already departed toward a field observation before the missing operational update is discovered. The player can try to reach or contact them before the observation window closes. Wild Pokémon may complicate the route, but success remains objective-based: inform the team, preserve the instrument plan and reach or safely abandon the observation as appropriate.

## Design value

The scene turns administrative latency into story without requiring corruption, villainy or omniscient quest flags. It also gives Ouros a clean reason for NPC disagreement after restart: one actor knows a valid decision, another still acts on unchanged operational state, and the player can determine which link in the chain remains incomplete.
