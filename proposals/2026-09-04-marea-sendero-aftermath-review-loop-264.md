# Marea / Sendero aftermath review loop — Pass 264

Status: PROPOSED. No canon promotion.

## Premise

A known wild Pokémon can leave an AutoPTU encounter with an aftermath result that Ouros cannot yet admit for durable mechanical state. The world should continue instead of either forgetting the encounter or inventing the missing rules.

The player can revisit the habitat and study what appears to have changed. Observation creates knowledge and ecological hooks while the private semantic result remains quarantined until its original production path is actually admissible.

## Marea/Sendero fixture story

The existing persistent Fletchling of the lower Sendero shelf is used only as the canon subject anchor. No new Injury, nickname, territory, NPC or battle event is canonized.

Fixture-only sequence:

A structured encounter ends. AutoPTU-shaped data contains an Injury-like aftermath result, but the current Java evidence does not verify the exact full lifecycle + stateful damage path required for production persistence. Ouros records the result in quarantine and returns the habitat to normal world operation.

On a later field visit, the player sees the same persistent individual moving differently and records `APPARENT_MOVEMENT_IMPAIRMENT`. The journal can retain this as an observation with source/time/place provenance. It cannot display a PTU Injury count.

A second visit may find the Pokémon resting, feeding normally, avoiding a usual perch, or absent. Each observation extends the field history without voting the mechanical result true or false.

If the exact historical AutoPTU producer path later becomes certifiable, Ouros re-evaluates the original immutable result. An admitted result can then open the appropriate persistent aftermath horizon. If the path never becomes certifiable, the observations still remain valid records of what the player saw.

## Exploration loop

The useful gameplay rhythm is encounter → return to habitat → observe → compare prior evidence → choose a low-impact follow-up → revisit.

Pokémon Legends: Arceus demonstrates the broader value of repeated survey trips and discoveries at different times. Ouros adapts that rhythm to a persistent ecology: repeated visits inspect consequences rather than filling a fixed checklist.

A post-encounter observation can therefore become a reason to revisit Sendero without creating another fight. The player may choose a distant observation point, compare feeding/site-use against older records, or wait for a different time window.

## Quest hooks

An ecological researcher can ask whether the apparent impairment persists across two quiet observation windows. Success improves the knowledge record; it does not repair or create PTU state.

A local report can claim that the Pokémon was badly hurt. The player may discover weaker evidence: it was merely absent from a usual perch, or it moved differently once. The discrepancy becomes a small evidence mystery instead of a hidden retcon.

A conservation-oriented task can ask the player to reduce disturbance around the observation area while awaiting another sighting. That reuses the disturbance contracts from Passes 255–256 without requiring battle mechanics.

A future admitted aftermath result can unlock a recovery-monitoring arc. The recovery duration must use a registered semantic horizon from Pass 261 rather than a generic timer.

## Consequences and failure

Poor observation does not erase the battle result and does not create recovery.

A false rumor can lower knowledge quality or create contradictory reports without mutating the private result.

Repeated close approaches may increase disturbance pressure even if they produce no useful observation, using the Pass 255 contract.

If the player never returns, the quarantine remains an audit concern and the world continues. The system does not require every unresolved mechanical record to become content.

## Reduced version available earlier

The narrative premise survives without a durable PTU Injury.

The encounter can end with only a correlation/completion receipt. Later visits use ordinary observation, site-use, disturbance and individual-response systems. The player can truthfully know “this Fletchling appeared to move differently after that encounter” while Ouros makes no hidden Injury claim.

This version needs persistent ecology + observation + adapter presentation. It avoids duplicating unfinished PTU mechanics inside Minecraft.
