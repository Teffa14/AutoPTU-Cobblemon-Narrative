# Marea / Sendero ecology memory retention loop — Pass 260

Status: PROPOSED. No new species, NPC, injury, marker, site or quest fact is canonized here.

## Premise

Ouros should remember consequences, not every rendered Pokémon.

The player can observe multiple Fletchling from the already-established Sendero population. Most observations become durable field history without forcing Ouros to maintain a permanent hidden actor for every sighting. Private individual linkage survives only when a real ecological consequence still depends on the same biological individual.

## Reduced playable loop

A first Fletchling is observed using a fixture-only micro-site. The observation remains in the field record. Once the site-use correlation stops having future semantic value, the private source linkage expires. Later seeing another Fletchling in the same place does not resurrect the old linkage or imply it is the same individual.

A second already-counted source has an active individual disturbance-response history. Its provisional linkage survives restart while the recovery horizon is still active because future exposure would otherwise be evaluated incorrectly. The player is not told that Ouros is retaining a private source correlation.

A third visible Fletchling receives a generic Minecraft damage signal in the fixture. The player may have seen an impact animation or another ambiguous presentation event, but the ecology layer refuses to create a persistent injury because no authoritative PTU/AutoPTU semantic injury result exists.

The result is a world that can remember meaningful ecological consequences while discarding low-value hidden bookkeeping.

## Full version

The same premise can later support a field-study sequence in which the player returns after different gaps and learns that some information is historical, some is still behaviorally predictive, and some requires a structured encounter before it can become mechanical truth.

A tracked individual whose response history remains active can be followed again. If the player merely observes it, ecology/adapter paths remain sufficient. If the player blocks, intercepts or battles it, the encounter hands off to AutoPTU and declares the exact permanent capability families used.

A future authoritative battle result could return a durable individual consequence such as an injury only after the damage/lifecycle path is verified. At that point the retention layer would preserve the semantic result and create durable-identity pressure rather than relying on a Minecraft animation.

## Narrative value

This supports environmental storytelling without turning the landscape into a collection of quest flags. An empty perch can remain meaningful history. A previously wary individual can retain an unresolved recovery trajectory. A dramatic-looking overworld event can remain uncertain until the authoritative rules layer resolves it.

NPC disagreement can emerge naturally from the same structure. One observer may remember the location, another may remember a behavioural response, and neither automatically knows the hidden individual identity. The system can preserve all three facts without forcing a false merge.

## Canon boundary

The existing twelve-Fletchling population remains unchanged. All extra anonymous sources, micro-sites, recovery horizons and damage observations in the Pass 260 fixture are fixture-only.

No Fletchling receives a canon injury, marker, territory, nickname, relationship or persistent actor identity through this proposal.

## Dependency boundary

Reduced version: Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback is the production blocker for presenting and observing the correct already-counted source. No tactical AutoPTU family is required by the retention lifecycle itself.

Full field-follow version: targeting/footprints/range/LoS, base movement legality, action economy/initiative, full turn/round lifecycle, AI legal-action infrastructure, AI tactical policy and adapter/playback. Complete movement is additionally required for interception, blocking, push/pull, knockback or forced movement.

Future injury import: full stateful damage pipeline plus every lifecycle/status/move/Ability/Item/Trainer Feature family actually used by the encounter. The ecology layer does not emulate those missing rules.

## Open design questions

Species-specific recovery horizons still need evidence-backed policy. The persistent world service still needs a restart-safe time basis. AutoPTU semantic returns need a typed authority envelope so ecology can retain genuine mechanical consequences while rejecting Minecraft presentation noise.
