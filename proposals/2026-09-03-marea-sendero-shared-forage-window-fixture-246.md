# Marea / Sendero shared forage window — Pass 246

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-03

This candidate exists to exercise the coupled ecology ledger. It does not approve Squawkabilly or a new physical resource at Sendero.

## Premise

During a short feeding window, several bird Pokémon concentrate near one bounded resource patch. The already-canon persistent Fletchling uses the area. A proposed Squawkabilly group also appears in the fixture.

Players first see coexistence, not a fight. One group takes a larger share of the concentrated patch. The known Fletchling shifts position and becomes more cautious around that site. Later observations can reveal whether access separates by time, remains shared, or develops into repeated territorial pressure.

Useful observations include arrival order, time spent at the patch, yielding behavior, alternative-patch availability, whether visible abundance changed or only visibility/access changed, whether warnings occur without contact, and whether the pattern repeats.

The player-visible story can progress from “two groups are here” to “they use the same patch” to “scarcity is changing access” without exposing hidden pressure values.

## Full version

If Squawkabilly and the resource are later approved, actors can approach from distinct routes, choose alternate access, warn, block, withdraw or escalate. The known Fletchling retains its prior encounter history and can respond differently from another member of the same population.

This rich version requires complete movement for live interception/displacement, lifecycle for timed access, terrain/zones/reactions if the patch is mechanically bounded, tactical AI for resource-access and withdrawal goals, and adapter/playback support. Any actual Move/Ability/Item/Trainer Feature used must be audited individually.

## Reduced version

Keep the patch and all access/consumption in Ouros state. Project actors for observation only. A confirmed resource-use event decrements the finite patch; pressure changes are explicit semantic writes. No battle is required.

If escalation must occur before rich support exists, isolate only the immediate hostile participants into a conventional audited AutoPTU encounter. Resource ownership and ecology-event resolution stay outside BattleSpec.

## Consequences

Possible later consequences include a Fletchling activity-window shift, temporal partitioning by the proposed second population, stable population totals despite rising pressure, an observer incorrectly reporting “fewer Fletchling” because access/visibility changed, or a future traffic-management response if human overlap compounds the conflict.

None of these becomes canon through this proposal.
