# The Empty Slot That Used to Be Taken — Case 364

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-08

## Premise

A field crew needs one shared survey instrument during a narrow observation window. Their original pickup is rescheduled. When the replacement window is assessed, another legitimate reservation overlaps it, so the crew redirects toward a secondary observation site while the conflict remains unresolved.

Hours later the other reservation is cancelled for an unrelated reason. By the time the world is saved, the calendar now looks open.

After restart, the historical admission record still explains why the crew rerouted. Current availability does not erase the earlier evidence.

All names, institutions, locations and policy details remain intentionally unresolved until canon review can bind the premise to an approved Ouros place.

## Narrative use

Different actors may hold different but compatible views. The scheduler can know that a conflict existed at the assessment time. A later-arriving technician can see only the now-open calendar. A field researcher can remember being sent to the alternate site without knowing that the competing reservation was later cancelled.

This creates investigation and relationship play without requiring deception. The player can reconstruct chronology, compare evidence, decide whether to continue the alternate survey, request the original instrument again, or explain why the prior choice was reasonable when made.

The admission record does not establish priority, fault, negligence or entitlement. Those require later policy/canon and, when relevant, Pass 348 allocation authority.

## Reduced implementation

The reduced version requires no AutoPTU battle.

Required world systems: semantic time; reservations and request history; appointment/reschedule lineage; communication delivery; private knowledge/belief; travel; historical admission persistence; checkpoint recovery.

A successful outcome can be gathering the alternate observation, preserving a sample chain, warning another crew, or reaching the revised rendezvous before the environmental window closes.

## Full encounter version

The alternate observation site can become tactically active while the crew works. A wild encounter or environmental interruption occurs while the instrument or collected sample must be preserved and the team must still satisfy the observation objective.

The narrative objective remains task-first. Defeating every opponent is optional when safe withdrawal, completion of the reading, rerouting or protecting the carrier resolves the scene.

### Capability dependencies

Targeting/footprints/range/LoS: required for ordinary spatial targeting if a battle occurs. Current evidence supports ordinary audited contracts; do not infer universal move coverage.

Base movement legality: required and usable for the reduced tactical version using ordinary movement.

Complete movement including push/pull/knockback/interception/forced movement: required only by the richer version if currents, shoves, carrier interception or forced separation are authored. Keep those elements disabled until verified end-to-end.

Core calculations: required for ordinary battle arithmetic within audited scope.

Action economy/initiative: required for battle sequencing within audited primitives.

Full turn/round lifecycle: required if the observation or escape window advances by rounds, or if round-bound effects matter. Treat as partial.

Full stateful damage pipeline: required for any damaging battle interaction. Treat as partial outside specifically verified contracts.

Status lifecycle: required only if the encounter relies on persistent conditions. Treat as partial.

Terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions: optional in the reduced battle; required by the full environmental version if weather phases, unstable zones or reactive terrain are used. Gate each exact family.

Move-specific behavior: gate every authored move individually.

Abilities: gate every authored Ability and interaction individually. Intimidate now has specific additional evidence for Mirror Armor, Defiant and Competitive interactions, but that does not establish universal Ability coverage.

Items: gate battle-item behavior individually.

Trainer Features/perks: gate any interrupt or feature-specific effect individually.

AI legal-action infrastructure: ordinary audited action legality can be used; resource-carrying or objective actions need explicit contracts.
