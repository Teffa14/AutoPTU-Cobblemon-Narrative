# The Place Has More Than One Custodian — Pass 415

Status: PROPOSED / NON-CANON
Date: 2026-09-10
Canon effect: NONE

## Premise

A field location matters to several existing Ouros institutions at once. One actor maintains access. Another records observations. Another preserves older documentation. A fourth may depend on the site for ordinary work.

No single institution receives automatic ownership of the complete explanation.

The site can contain a current physical problem, an older repair, an ecological observation and an access restriction at the same time. Each fact can have a different custodian and provenance chain.

## Candidate use in Marea Interior

The proposal can bind to existing canon without adding geography. Sendero del Vidrio, Estación Mirador, Marea Field Office, Tideglass Archive, Teo Lark, Mara Veyra, Dr. Nerea Sol, Ema Rey and Taro Min already provide plausible access, maintenance, observation and archival roles.

No new incident, hidden structure, faction or historical fact is canonized here.

A future authored case could begin when two legitimate records about the same site appear incompatible. One may describe a physical repair. Another may describe an ecological observation from before or after that repair. A route restriction may have altered who could inspect the area. An archive copy may preserve an older site description.

The investigation asks which claims refer to the same revision of the place, who actually observed each condition, who had access, and what changed between observations.

## World-agent behavior

A persistent NPC can investigate off-screen only when that actor knows about the issue, has enough time, can travel to the location, has the required permission and has a motive or obligation that makes the work eligible.

Inspection creates evidence for that observer. It does not broadcast knowledge to every member of the observer's institution.

A report can later move through existing communication systems. A second observer can disagree because they saw a later site revision or because they possess different contextual records.

Refusing or delaying a request does not freeze the site. Another actor may inspect it, conditions may change, or the original question may become harder to answer.

## Reduced implementation

The reduced version needs no new AutoPTU mechanic.

It uses existing semantic time, world travel, schedules, obligations, permissions, private knowledge, durable memory, explicit communications, provenance and authored site revisions.

Minecraft may expose authored visual clues or interaction points. Those interactions may create an observation event only through the world contract. Block appearance alone does not author a PTU check, hidden cause or battle result.

## Full encounter version

A later version can place inspection under tactical pressure. Possible objectives include reaching a safe observation point, protecting an observer, recovering a record or withdrawing after sufficient evidence is secured.

The narrative premise survives if those mechanics are unavailable. The reduced version remains valid and does not require Minecraft to duplicate PTU rules.
