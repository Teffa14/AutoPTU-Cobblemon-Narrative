# Proposal — Correction Race

Status: PROPOSED / NOT CANON
Date: 2026-09-05
Scope: reusable Ouros quest and world-event structure

## Premise

A trusted public service publishes a warning about a route, venue, Pokémon sighting, tournament condition, supply failure or evacuation. Some named NPCs receive the first version and act. Later evidence causes the publisher to issue a correction. The corrected version reaches a different audience because coverage, travel, subscriptions, service availability or attention changed in the interval.

The adventure grows from the divergence between publication history and individual knowledge history.

## Core loop

1. A world event produces an initial claim.
2. A publisher releases a public notice.
3. Explicit named recipients receive it according to current audience state.
4. Their schedules, travel or obligations can change.
5. Later evidence produces an update/correction/retraction.
6. The revision independently expands to the audience that exists at that later time.
7. Some actors update their plans; others continue from older knowledge.
8. The player encounters the resulting consequences and can reconstruct the information path.

## Character and faction hooks

A conscientious official can become an ally because they issue a correction quickly even when it is embarrassing.

A rival can appear reckless while actually having missed the later notice. The rivalry can deepen or soften once the information history becomes known.

A faction can fracture over accountability: one member received the correction and failed to relay it, while another genuinely never had access.

A journalist, Chronicler-style researcher, courier, ranger, organizer or local coordinator can become valuable because they preserve provenance and timelines rather than merely supplying exposition.

These are archetypes only. No external character or plot is imported.

## Mystery structure

Useful evidence objects include publication timestamps, scope/coverage records, per-agent receipts, message failures, travel departures, eyewitness claims, revision rationale and downstream actions.

The mystery question can move beyond “what happened?” toward “what was reasonably knowable to this person when they acted?”

That supports consequences with more nuance than a global quest flag.

## Reduced implementation version

The reduced version uses only Ouros semantic systems:

publication -> receipt -> knowledge -> replanning -> travel/schedule consequence -> investigation

Example consequence: a courier takes the old road because the correction never reached them, arrives late, and forces the player to locate a delayed package or missing traveler. No tactical battle is required.

## Full mechanically rich version

A late or missed correction can place an NPC/player party inside a structured encounter before updated information arrives. The narrative premise stays identical while the scene gains tactical pressure.

Possible full-version modules:

- route obstruction with line-of-sight choices;
- forced relocation or interception during a chase;
- weather or hazard escalation;
- status-bearing environmental exposure;
- Ability/Item/Trainer Feature reactions;
- autonomous allied or hostile tactical actors;
- visible Minecraft/Cobblemon playback and acknowledgement.

Every selected module inherits its exact engine capability family. Omit unsupported modules without changing the information-history premise.

## Consequence families

The correction can arrive:

- before departure, preventing the incident;
- during travel, producing a reroute;
- after the incident, creating retrospective accountability;
- to only part of a group, producing disagreement;
- to someone who never received the original, creating a context gap;
- after a rumor derived from the original has already moved through private channels.

## Canon guard

No place, publisher, technology, faction, species distribution, route status or institution in this proposal is canon-approved. Region authors can bind the structure to canon only through normal Ouros approval.
