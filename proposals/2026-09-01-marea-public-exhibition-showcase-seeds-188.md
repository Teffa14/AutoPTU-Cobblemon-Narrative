# Marea Public Exhibition and Showcase Seeds — Pass 188

Status: PROPOSED. NOT CANON.
Date: 2026-09-01

These seeds apply `design/public-exhibition-judging-continuity-layer.md` to existing Marea institutions and residents. They do not canonize a new festival, Contest circuit, league, ranking body, fame system, judging council or regional Caelo format.

The existing questline taxonomy already provides `COMPETITIVE`, `SERVER_EVENT`, `SETTLEMENT`, `CHARACTER`, `RELATIONSHIP`, `CLASS`, `POKEMON`, `ITEM` and `SECONDARY` surfaces.

## 1. Bruma Battle Yard Open Exhibition

Premise:

Sela schedules a small public exhibition session at the existing Bruma Battle Yard. The purpose is to demonstrate a published battle format and let residents watch an audited match without treating it as a ranked championship.

Participants:

- Sela owns the session within her canon Battle Yard responsibility;
- Jace can assist with setup or participate if the authored episode chooses him;
- Teo may handle ordinary fixture readiness when appropriate;
- spectators can attend without gaining judging authority.

Persistence:

- the event notice exists before the session;
- the challenge contract revision is preserved;
- attendance and battle result are separate records;
- a posted result can remain after the event;
- later discussion can reference what was publicly observable.

Recommended first competitive implementation candidate if the selected matchup is inside audited AutoPTU capability contracts.

## 2. Mirador Field-Method Demonstration

Premise:

Nerea and Ema present a short public demonstration of how a transect observation becomes a field note and then a reviewed record.

There is no winner.

Useful interaction:

The player can follow two sample observations through preparation, recording, review and correction. If Ema intentionally includes an incomplete field-note example for instruction, Nerea can explain what evidence is missing without granting an abstract Research bonus.

Purpose:

Make Mirador's evidence discipline visible through action rather than text exposition.

No BattleSpec dependency.

## 3. Tideglass Caption Review

Premise:

Taro and Pia prepare two possible captions for a small public display drawn from the same historical source set. One is concise but overstates certainty. The other preserves uncertainty but is harder for visitors to parse.

The player can inspect sources and provide feedback. Taro retains review authority.

Possible outcome:

A final caption may combine clarity and provenance, while the rejected drafts remain archived as work history if useful.

No contest score is created.

## 4. Ferry Load-Securing Demonstration

Premise:

Lia and Mina conduct a scheduled demonstration of ordinary loading coordination at Ferry Landing using only procedures already established or explicitly authored for the episode.

Constraint:

This seed cannot invent maritime law, safety certification or universal loading rules. It can show roles, handoffs and visible sequence.

A late real arrival may force the demonstration to pause because ordinary work has priority.

Narrative value:

Public events can yield to institutional responsibilities instead of freezing the world around the player.

## 5. Teo's Repair Bench Open Hour

Premise:

Teo shows residents how he documents an ordinary equipment problem, distinguishes visible function from readiness and records a maintenance handoff.

Possible complication:

A participant brings an unrelated item expecting an immediate repair. Teo can decline or schedule it without turning the demonstration into a universal crafting service.

No new crafting mechanic is implied.

## 6. Popular Choice, Formal Result

Premise:

At a future approved exhibition, the crowd reacts most strongly to one entrant while the published procedure yields another formal result.

Design rule:

Both facts persist.

The system does not rewrite the adjudication from applause, and it does not suppress audience preference merely because the formal result differs.

This seed should be used only after the event's governing procedure is explicitly defined.

## 7. The Empty Slot

Premise:

A registered participant withdraws before a public event.

World behavior:

- the event remains scheduled;
- the printed or posted running order may still show the original slot until corrected;
- organizers update the authoritative entry record;
- spectators can notice the discrepancy;
- withdrawal produces no humiliation or relationship penalty by default.

Purpose:

Test procedure revision and visible stale information without villainy.

## 8. Result Board Correction

Premise:

A posted result contains a transcription mistake even though the underlying adjudication record is correct.

Gameplay:

Pia or another authorized courier notices the mismatch when transferring a copy to Tideglass. The board is corrected and the correction retains provenance.

Important boundary:

The event outcome never changed. Its public projection was wrong.

This is a strong Minecraft persistence test because breaking/replacing a board must not become result authority.

## 9. Jace Watches Instead

Premise:

The player expects Jace to participate in an exhibition, but he has an ordinary maintenance or assistant duty during that session.

Jace watches some or all of the event and can later remember only information legitimately public to him.

Purpose:

Continue his competitive character arc without forcing every appearance into a rematch.

## 10. A Pokémon Demonstrates One Useful Skill

Premise:

A named companion Pokémon participates in a small noncompetitive demonstration relevant to its resident's ordinary work: carrying a light object, locating a marked station, following a route cue or another behavior already supported by world simulation.

Boundary:

Visual success does not grant a Move, Capability, Ability, Feature or permanent personality trait.

If the action depends on a PTU mechanic, that exact mechanic must be audited first.

## 11. Two Rule Sheets

Premise:

An old handbill survives at the Battle Yard after Sela has issued a revised exhibition procedure.

The discrepancy matters because one visitor prepared from the stale version.

Possible resolution:

The event uses the actual effective revision while Sela documents why the old notice remained visible. A later episode can show improved posting practice.

No participant is automatically blamed for relying on information the institution left public.

## 12. What Marea Chooses to Show

Longer-term arc concept.

Across several seasons, residents occasionally make ordinary expertise public: a Battle Yard exhibition, Mirador field-method session, Tideglass display review, repair demonstration or other institution-owned event.

The arc builds a public history through:

- dated notices;
- changing procedures;
- photographs or display objects;
- corrected captions;
- remembered spectators;
- participants who become more competent through their already-authorized progression systems;
- invitations that reference earlier events;
- occasional cancellations or interruptions that remain part of the record.

The district develops a visible culture of showing work without requiring one giant festival or a universal popularity statistic.

## Mechanically rich encounter: Battle Yard Demonstration Under Live Disruption

### Narrative premise

A scheduled Bruma Battle Yard exhibition is already underway when an unrelated operational problem creates pressure around the venue. Spectators, staff and the published event procedure matter to the world, but they must not be turned into improvised tactical units merely to make the encounter more complex.

### Intended full version

The rich form could include:

- an audited exhibition battle;
- spectators and staff with safe-space requirements;
- a changing access corridor;
- interruption or suspension procedure;
- selected displacement/interception mechanics when exact content uses them;
- tactical zones or hazards only when exact rule families are verified;
- a decision to continue, suspend or terminate the exhibition after the immediate threat;
- persistent public and archival aftermath.

Permanent capability categories required when present:

- targeting/footprints/range/LoS;
- base movement legality;
- complete movement including push/pull/knockback/interception/forced movement;
- core calculations;
- action economy/initiative;
- full turn/round lifecycle;
- full stateful damage pipeline;
- status lifecycle for selected content;
- terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions if the venue disruption becomes tactical;
- move-specific behavior for every selected Move;
- abilities for every selected Ability;
- items when the challenge contract permits them;
- Trainer Features/perks when Trainers participate mechanically;
- AI legal-action infrastructure;
- AI tactical policy if actors must reason about objectives, retreat or audience-safe corridors;
- Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback support.

Current classification: BLOCKED for the intended rich form.

### Reduced version

The public event remains the same story while tactical authority is narrowed.

1. Registration, spectators, event procedure, result board and audience response remain world state.
2. Any operational interruption first moves spectators and noncombatants through authored RPG/world orchestration.
3. The exhibition is suspended if the world-state procedure requires it.
4. If an immediate combat threat remains, compile a separate ordinary audited battle on stable Battle Yard terrain with exact roster/content review.
5. AutoPTU returns only battle facts and a narrow approved handoff such as `IMMEDIATE_YARD_THREAT_WITHDREW` or `EXHIBITION_MATCH_COMPLETE` when appropriate.
6. Narrative decides whether the event resumes, stays suspended or is rescheduled according to the published procedure and world facts.
7. Audience reaction, archival publication and social consequences are evaluated separately.

The battle cannot decide:

- whether spectators enjoyed the event;
- whether Sela approves of a participant personally;
- whether Jace becomes a rival;
- whether a participant gains social prestige;
- whether a result board is correct;
- whether an interrupted exhibition should resume;
- whether the district is safe in the future;
- whether a participant receives a PTU Contest award;
- any Caelo-wide rank or qualification.

Current classification: REDUCIBLE if the battle itself is selected entirely inside audited capability contracts.

## Formal PTU Contest candidate — future only

A genuine Pokémon Contest could eventually become a strong Marea or wider-Ouros activity because the project source set contains Contest Types, Contest Effects, Appeal Rolls and Coordinator Features.

It is not implementation-ready from this pass.

Required next evidence includes:

- authoritative PTU/Caelo Contest procedure for the project's pinned source set;
- exact engine ownership for Contest rounds and state;
- exact implementation status of Coordinator Features used by participants;
- exact move Contest Effects;
- persistence contract for Contest outcomes;
- adapter/playback design that presents rather than recomputes results.

Do not convert a future Contest into a battle merely because battle infrastructure exists.
