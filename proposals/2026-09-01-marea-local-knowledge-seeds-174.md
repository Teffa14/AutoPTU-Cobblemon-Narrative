## Candidate 1 — The Bell and the Crossing

Primary actors: Mara Veyra, Ema Rey, Mina Cors
Sites: Sendero del Vidrio seasonal crossing, Estación Mirador, Puerto Bruma Field Office
Questline tags: REGION / EXPLORATION / CHARACTER / FACTION

Initial claim:
Several residents repeat that the seasonal crossing became unreliable after an old route bell stopped being heard from the upper junction.

Important separation:
- the timing correlation may be real;
- the bell may be broken, removed, muffled or simply not heard from some positions;
- the route condition may have a separate cause;
- the local phrase can remain culturally meaningful without becoming causal truth.

Player work:
- inspect the current crossing from more than one approach;
- ask Ema what Mirador recorded and when;
- compare Mina's lower-route observations;
- inspect the bell site without assuming sabotage;
- report which statements are observations and which are interpretations.

Durable outputs:
- a corrected route-warning packet;
- updated Field Office posting;
- a Tideglass copy of the old and revised reports;
- possible later maintenance request if a physical bell issue is independently established.

No battle dependency required.
