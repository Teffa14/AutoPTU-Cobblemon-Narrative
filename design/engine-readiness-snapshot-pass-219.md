## Rich concept trace

`Occupied Shelf Under Shared Use` can remain mostly world/ecology gameplay until the Trainer chooses a mechanically meaningful intervention.

Observation and rerouting can work through world-state, spatial inputs and normal traversal. Direct pursuit or containment can introduce footprints/range/LoS and complete movement. Capture/control can activate Items, Status, Moves, Abilities and Trainer Features. A defensive battle activates initiative, lifecycle and damage. Interception or protective reactions require those exact movement/reaction contracts and cannot be approximated in Minecraft.

This conditional dependency model lets the narrative premise ship in reduced form while retaining a richer future version.

## Reduced-form readiness

`Mark, Yield, Recheck` needs:

```text
authoritative site record
+ observation provenance
+ time/human-activity context
+ generic Cobblemon spawn projection where appropriate
+ persistent identity when explicitly bound
+ normal traversal and withdrawal
+ access/stewardship decision persistence
-> later re-observation and review
```

It deliberately rejects:

- `distance < X = attack` hard-coding;
- invented parental bonuses or aggro radii;
- unattended = abandoned;
- proximity = parentage;
- egg/juvenile discovery = ownership;
- Minecraft collision or animation deciding PTU harm;
- fake reaction/interception systems;
- off-screen tactical battle simulation.

If structured combat begins, use the normal authoritative BattleSpec path.

## Readiness conclusion

The ecological and institutional premise is not blocked by incomplete battle systems. A first implementation can support observation, retreat, rerouting, temporary access management and repeated evidence collection.

The rich autonomous version remains limited by complete movement when interception/forced movement is invoked, full lifecycle and damage/status when combat occurs, Move/Ability/Item/Feature coverage for specific tactics, AI tactical policy for competent protective choices, and end-to-end adapter/playback support.
