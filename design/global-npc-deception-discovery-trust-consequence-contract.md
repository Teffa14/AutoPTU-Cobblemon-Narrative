# Global NPC deception discovery and trust consequence contract — Pass 298

Status: PROPOSED EXECUTABLE FOUNDATION. This file defines world-agent behavior, not canon lore and not PTU rules.

Ouros already stores actual information provenance, subjective source attribution, deliberate deceptive statements and directional relationship state. Pass 298 connects those systems without creating an omniscient lie detector.

A receiver may corroborate that a received deceptive assertion conflicts with evidence they actually possess. That creates `FALSEHOOD_CORROBORATED`. This alone does not prove that the speaker intended to deceive and therefore cannot automatically reduce trust.

`INTENT_ATTRIBUTED` requires explicit intent-evidence claim references already present in the discoverer's private `KnowledgeLedger`. The runtime validates that those claims exist and are not from the future, but does not invent what counts as proof. Authored investigations, institutional records, admissions, witnessed preparations or future executable evidence systems must create those evidence claims through their own provenance-preserving paths.

False-content corroboration also requires same-subject contradictory evidence from an independent provenance root at or above an authored confidence threshold. Same-root repetition cannot corroborate itself. This protects against a rumor cascade manufacturing independent proof merely by being repeated.

Only an `INTENT_ATTRIBUTED` finding may apply the Pass 298 trust consequence. The relationship mutation is directional from discoverer to speaker, provenance-backed and idempotent by finding ID. It does not modify affinity, respect, fear, rivalry or faction standing automatically. Those consequences require separate authored policies or later contracts.

The trust delta is gameplay policy supplied by the caller. Pass 298's default is a bounded negative adjustment, not a claim about human psychology. Repair, apology, restitution, third-party verification and repeated violations remain future work.

False-source-only deception is not promoted to this path yet. Establishing that someone lied about who supplied information needs attribution-specific evidence, not the false-content contradiction test.

Reduced narrative dependency: none of the tactical AutoPTU capability families are required. The loop can run entirely through private evidence, communication, social state and world-agent replanning.

If a downstream confrontation enters structured combat, declare only the families it actually exercises. Forced movement requires complete movement. Delayed/status effects require lifecycle/status plus the owning Move/Ability/Item/Trainer Feature family. Mechanical weather or hazards require terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions. Autonomous tactical choice requires AI tactical policy. Minecraft-visible authoritative execution requires Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback support.
