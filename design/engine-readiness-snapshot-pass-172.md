## Vector ecology is not PTU Status

The following world-state facts never write a battle Status automatically:

- vector species detected;
- biting/feeding observed;
- host contact observed;
- pathogen target detected in a pooled vector sample;
- suspected transmission;
- confirmed clinical infection under some future health authority;
- public health alert;
- control programme active.

Likewise, battle facts do not establish epidemiology:

- `Poisoned` or `Badly Poisoned` does not prove infectious disease;
- being hit by Poison Fang does not establish vector transmission;
- Leech Life does not create blood-borne infection;
- Poison typing does not imply contamination or vector competence;
- Bug typing does not imply vector competence;
- status immunity does not imply biological immunity to arbitrary pathogens.
