## Boundary with Civic Governance

Civic Governance already owns collective proposals, decision authority and public-works sponsorship.

Pass 130 begins only after a valid project basis exists.

## Boundary with Facility Maintenance

Facility Maintenance owns faults, work orders, repairs, verification and deferred maintenance after a facility exists.

Pass 130 owns active construction/renovation execution, incomplete-scope corrections, handover and project history.

After ordinary operation begins, future faults normally transfer to Facility Maintenance while preserving references back to the original project.

## Why moving construction hazards remain excluded

A cinematic worksite can visually suggest cranes, vehicles, open edges, moving loads, dust, heat, debris or unstable surfaces. None should enter tactical mechanics until its exact family is supported.

For example:

- moving machinery that pushes a combatant needs complete forced-movement coverage and environmental authority;
- falling debris needs a governed damage source, lifecycle/timing and likely zone/reaction behavior;
- dust that changes LoS needs exact targeting/LoS and environmental contracts;
- unstable cells need terrain/hazard lifecycle;
- protected evacuation lanes need zone/reaction semantics and tactical policy;
- delayed collapse needs lifecycle plus stateful damage/environmental rules.

The reduced pattern keeps the narrative premise while moving those effects to pre/post-battle world state.
