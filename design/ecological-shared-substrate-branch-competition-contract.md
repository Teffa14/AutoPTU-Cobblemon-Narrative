# Ecological shared-substrate branch competition contract

Status: PROPOSED contract. Pass 269. No branch actor, species behavior, substrate, competitive relation or ecological outcome becomes Marea/Sendero canon through this document.

Record: `ECOLOGICAL_SHARED_SUBSTRATE_BRANCH_V1`.

A shared-substrate interaction contains a substrate/resource node plus two or more independently represented branch claims. Each branch claim must record branch id, claimant, substrate id, mechanism class, evidence refs, temporal scope, local access state, pressure state, interpretation state, provenance and canon status.

Allowed mechanism classes: `PHYSICAL_OCCUPANCY`, `RESOURCE_WITHDRAWAL`, `SUBSTRATE_MODIFICATION`, `ACCESS_OBSTRUCTION`, `TEMPORAL_PREEMPTION`, `RECIPROCAL_FACILITATION`, `UNKNOWN`.

Allowed interpretation states: `OBSERVED_OVERLAP`, `LIMITATION_HYPOTHESIS`, `DIRECT_LIMITATION_SUPPORTED`, `FACILITATION_SUPPORTED`, `MIXED_CONTEXT_DEPENDENT`, `UNCERTAIN`, `REJECTED`.

Core invariant: shared site, shared resource label, temporal precedence, visible crowding, Minecraft adjacency or repeated co-occurrence do not establish competition. `DIRECT_LIMITATION_SUPPORTED` requires evidence that one branch materially reduces another branch’s access, persistence opportunity or usable share through an identified mechanism.

Arrival order is evidence, not authority. An earlier branch may matter through niche preemption or niche modification only when that mechanism has evidence. `ARRIVED_FIRST` must never become `DOMINATES` automatically.

Interaction sign is local and contextual. The same claimant pair may be facilitative in one condition, limiting in another and unresolved elsewhere. Do not promote one global positive/negative species relationship from a local branch record.

No ecosystem scalar is authoritative. Do not derive a single habitat-health, branch-dominance, competition-intensity or carrying-capacity score that silently replaces branch-local state. If a future simulation uses numeric pressure internally, the number must preserve its causal inputs and may not by itself create canon facts.

Closing one branch triggers re-evaluation only. It cannot automatically: increase another population; create immigration or births; restore the substrate; promote another branch to ownership; prove ecological benefit; establish habitat recovery; delete historical evidence.

Pass 266 cue-quality separation remains authoritative for habitat-quality claims. Pass 267 habitat-modification rules govern physical persistence. Pass 268 facilitation-cascade rules govern direct dependency edges and continue to forbid automatic transitive closure.

Population authority remains separate. Projection may make already-counted individuals more or less visible around the substrate. A branch conflict cannot manufacture new persistent actors or demographic events.

Minecraft/Cobblemon geometry may present crowding, occupancy, obstruction or substrate change. It does not author `COMPETES_WITH`, `DISPLACES`, PTU cover, blocker, difficult terrain, hazard, zone, reaction, forced movement or damage.

Reduced encounter: present a shared substrate, project already-counted sources, record branch-local observations, expose evidence for or against direct limitation, and preserve history across restart. No AutoPTU handoff required.

Full encounter dependencies: targeting/footprints/range/LoS applies when actors detect or target each other or the substrate. Base movement legality applies to ordinary approach/traversal. Complete movement applies to interception, push/pull/knockback, forced displacement or tactical blocking. Core calculations apply only when a mapped PTU rule requires them. Action economy/initiative and full turn/round lifecycle apply to structured contest sequencing. Full stateful damage pipeline applies to destructive or injury-producing outcomes. Status lifecycle applies to persistent statuses. Terrain/weather/hazards/zones/reactions applies when substrate state has admitted battlefield semantics. Move-specific behavior, abilities, items and Trainer Features/perks apply exactly when those mechanisms alter access or contest state. AI legal-action infrastructure is required for legal autonomous actions; AI tactical policy is required to choose whether to contest, yield, wait, reroute or exploit a branch. Minecraft/Cobblemon/Craftics adapter/playback support is required for live projection and feedback.

Fail closed: unresolved overlap stays unresolved; temporal precedence does not imply dominance; branch closure does not imply recovery; no tactical displacement is inferred from visual crowding; no population change occurs without its separate authoritative transaction.
